How this quick MBTI quiz works

Choose one answer for each of 16 everyday situations. Each choice adds one point to one of the four preference pairs: E/I, S/N, T/F, and J/P. The majority score in each pair forms the four-letter result; ties select the first letter.

Use it for entertainment and personal reflection. This short quiz is not a scientific, clinical, hiring, or diagnostic assessment.

Your answers are counted in this page and are not submitted with your result. The page loads analytics, but advertising is disabled on this interactive quiz.
