How this 16-type quiz works

Choose one answer for each of 20 original everyday situations. There are five questions for each preference pair: E/I, S/N, T/F, and J/P. The majority score forms the four-letter result; ties are impossible in this five-question design.

The four preference pairs

E/I describes outward engagement or inward recharging. S/N contrasts concrete details with possibilities. T/F contrasts analytical criteria with people-and-values considerations. J/P contrasts structure with flexibility.

How scoring works

One answer equals one vote. There is no hidden weighting or random tie-breaking. A 3–2 axis is labeled Close result because this short quiz showed only a narrow answer majority.
