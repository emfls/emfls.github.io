  • summer:It is gentle and high in sunshine, so it is perfect for outdoor activities

🚌 Precautions on public transportation
  • Copenhagen connection:Frederick Sber is an independent autonomous district that is completely surrounded by Copenhagen
  • Use of subway:Visit the city using the subway at the Frederiksberg allé station or forum station.
  • Mobile ticket:Public transportation tickets can be purchased as a mobile app

💰 High price precautions
  • Northern European prices:Michelin restaurants and high -end cafes have a lot of food costs.

  • Family travel:There are many attractions that are good to visit in families such as zoo, museum, and park
