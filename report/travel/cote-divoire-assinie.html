🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever, bowl

  • malaria:Prevention drug required, mosquito repellent regular use, long -sleeved clothes
  • yellow fever:Implied vaccination before entering the country, bringing in a certificate of inoculation
  • Water quality:Only drink bottled water, avoid ice, peel off raw fruit peel
  • Marine safety:Swimming with strong birds, waves, and local guides
  • Wildlife:Guide order in the national park, not feeding animals

  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, deterioration of road conditions during rainy season

  • Night movement:Late time moving late at night, caution
