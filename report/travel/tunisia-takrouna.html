Day 1: Introduction to Desert and Berber Culture Experience

Arrival of Takrona → Desert Camp → Camel Trekking → Berber Village

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:In the Sahara Desert, Takrona, the traditional Berber culture, the camel trekking feels the grandeur of the desert, the night sky and the traditional Berber music performances at the Desert Camp, and the local Berber village observes the traditional handicraft and carpet production process, understanding the life of the desert nomads.

Day 2: Oasis exploration and desert activity

Pod Nasa Oasis → Desert ATV → Zeville National Park → Desert Sunset Tour

Recommended time:10 hours

Takrona Traditional Market → Desert Museum → Berber Cooking Experience → Return to Tunis

Desert Museum:2 hours, the history of the Sahara Desert and the Berber Cultural Exhibition
Cooking experience:3 hours, traditional Berber dishes such as Cuscus and Tajin
Return to Tunis:Takes 4 hours, bus or taxi from Takrona to Tunis

⚠️ Takrona travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Southern desert area, near border

  • Terrorism:Encouraging terrorist activities in the southern desert area, powerful use of package tours
  • Desert District:Absolutely prohibited to explore deserts alone, must go with guides
  • Emergency situation:In the desert
  • Wildlife:Desert snakes, scorpions, etc.
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Desert traffic:4WD vehicles are required, unable to enter a general passenger car desert
  • GPS limit:GPS signal instability in the desert, local guide required
  • Fuel supply:Desert gas station rarely prepared for fuel
  • Tunis connection:4 hours bus to Takrona, 3 hours of taxi
  • Desert Road:Surrying road conditions during sandstorms, the weather checks required
🌡️ Desert weather precautions
  • Daily crossroads:Day 40 degrees and 5 degrees at night
  • Sand Storm:Sudden sand storm can occur
  • ultraviolet rays:Desert strong ultraviolet rays, burn risk
  • dehydration:High risk of dehydration due to dry desert climate
  • Optimal period:October-April is a desert travel timely
