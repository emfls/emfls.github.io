🚨 Political safetyism

Main precautions:Prohibition of political remarks and behavior

  • Prohibition of shooting:Military facilities, political symbols, and poor shooting
  • Political remarks:Prohibition of critical mention of North Korean political system or leader
  • Religious activities:Absolute prohibition of religious activities or missions
  • Contact with locals:Prohibition of individual contact with unauthorized locals
  • Guide accompanied:Make sure to move with the official guide and prohibit alone
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Mobile restriction:Prohibition of moving out of the designated sightseeing course
  • Public transportation:Unable to use public transportation, use of dedicated vehicles
  • Border pass:Dandong, China, Sinuiju or Yanji-Rasun Path
  • Search for checks:Strict check -up search when entering and extracting countries
  • Communication Restriction:Restrictions on using mobile phones, cannot be connected to the Internet
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Winter (12-2):Drops to -14 degrees, preparation for visit to Korea
  • Summer (June-August):The temperature is around 25 degrees, humidity is high
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and windy
  • Precipitation:770mm per year, required for rainy season
  • Coast climate:Low temperature from sea wind
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash payment:Credit cards cannot be used, only cash
  • Exchange restriction:Can exchange only at the official exchange office
  • Hotel fee:Using hotels for foreigners, high accommodation fees
  • Meals:Dining only in a designated restaurant, limited menu
  • souvenir:Only souvenirs without political colors can be purchased
