⚠️Schwitz travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Train stations, downtown, and tourist attractions

  • pickpocket:In the crowded places such as train stations, museums, markets, etc.
  • Stranger Border:Leave it, and take it calmly when approaching, etc.
  • Loss of passport:Passport copy and photographs separately storage, cash dispersion
  • Travel Insurance:Required for subscription, preparation for emergency situations
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Use the Swiss Travel Pass, check the train and bus schedule in advance
  • Rent -a -car:Need for international driver's license, parking and speed violations
  • Mountain terrain:Wear safety equipment for hiking and climbing, check the weather forecast
  • Cable Car · Train:It is possible to stop operating during strong winds and bad weather
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):12-21 degrees in temperature, large over the day, UV strong, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -7 ~ 0 degrees (minimum), 0 ~ 5 degrees (best), winter clothes required
  • Spring/Autumn:5-14 degrees, rain and snow potential, umbrella and outerwear preparation
  • Alpine Zone:Body pressure and temperature change severity, the elderly refrain from climbing
  • Lake and River:Swimming attention because the water temperature is low and the flow rate is fast
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Rigi Mountain, Museum, Café and Restaurants around Lake Lake 2-3 times expensive
  • Museum entrance fee:Schwitz Museum, Bundesbrif Museum, each CHF 10-15
  • Tip Culture:5-10%restaurant, cafe 1-2 franc, hotel housekeeping 2-3 franc
  • Cheese, chocolate:Local specialty prices are high
