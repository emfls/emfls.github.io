What is the recommended travel course for visiting Carmelo, Uruguay?

The recommended travel course for Carmelo is a 2-night, 3-day itinerary that includes visiting Narbona Wine Logi, Bodega El Regiono, and enjoying water activities on the Rio de La Plata, along with experiencing the local town center.

What are the main attractions in Carmelo for wine lovers?

Wine lovers can enjoy world-class wine tasting and vineyard tours at Narbona Wine Logi and experience high-end Uruguayan wines at Bodega El Regiono, both located in Carmelo.

What outdoor activities are available along the Rio de La Plata in Carmelo?

Visitors can engage in various water activities such as kayaking, fishing, and yachting on the Rio de La Plata, which borders Argentina and offers scenic riverside experiences.
