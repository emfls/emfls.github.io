Major areas:Outside the city center, a rare place for night human

  • Night movement:Avoid dark alleys, secluded places.
  • Airport/Station:Don't leave your luggage alone, report it when you find an object without a master
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Transferth bus, tram, ferry, and smart relay card
  • Taxi/Rent -a -Car:Check the meter, care for the valuables in the vehicle
  • Long distance movement:Preparation of enough fuel, water and safety equipment for inland and outland travel
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (December -February):18 ~ 40 degrees, very hot, dry, UV protection required
  • Winter (June -August):8 ~ 20 degrees, rains often come, and at night, chilly, thick clothes are prepared
  • Spring/Autumn:15-25 degrees, a large, thin outerwear
  • Sea breeze/ultraviolet light:Sun cream, hats, and sunglasses required for beaches and outdoor activities
