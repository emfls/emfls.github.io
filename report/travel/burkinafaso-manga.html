⚠️ Manga travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Mali and Niger border areas (travel restrictions), outing at night

  • Terrorism:Northern border areas (Odalan/Soum/Senoju) Limited to 3rd stage of travel, and other regions of other regions
  • Political anxiety:After independence in 1960, coups occurred frequently, avoiding political rally or protests
  • Night going out:Don't go alone after sunset, accompany you with local guides
  • Supplies Management:Valuits are kept in hotel safe, only minimal cash
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:There are many unpaved roads, and in the rainy season, it is impossible to pass
  • Public transportation:Wear seat belts when using buses or taxis, speed attention
  • Rent -a -car:4WD car recommended, local driving customs
  • fuel:There are rare gas stations, so the fuel is fully prepared
  • Night driving:Prohibition of night driving due to lack of road lighting
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-2):Armatang Desert sand breeze, temperature 15-30 degrees
  • Heat (March-May):Rising to up to 40 degrees, enough water intake
  • Rainy (June-October):The temperature is 23-32 degrees, and it can be flooded with intensive heavy rain
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat
  • Daily crossroads:Preparation of warming clothing because it is large during the dry season
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Over the year, prevention drugs and mosquito repellent use required
  • yellow fever:Essential submitted when entering a vaccination certificate
  • drinking water:Drink only bottled water and prohibit ice intake
  • food:Intake only enough cooked food, street food attention
  • Medical facilities:Lack of medical facilities, moving to Dugu -gu in case of emergency.
💰 Economic precautions
  • money:Use Franc Sepa (XOF), euro and fixed exchange rate (1 euro = 656 theater)
  • Credit card:Almost impossible to use, cash transaction
  • Exchange:Exchange the euro to Franc Sepa from the local area
  • price:Basic daily necessities are cheap, but imports are expensive
  • tip:Small tips in the service industry
