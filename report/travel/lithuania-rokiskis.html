What is the recommended itinerary for the Lithuania Loki Shukis Tour?

The recommended itinerary is a 2-night, 3-day travel course beginning with Day 1 focused on history and culture exploration, including visits to the Rokiškis Regional Museum and St. Matthias Shingdick Church, with an estimated 8 hours dedicated to these activities.

What historical significance does Rokiškis hold in the Lithuania Loki Shukis Tour?

Rokiškis is significant for its connection to the hunter Locas's legend, its 19th-century architecture such as St. Matthias Shingdick Church built by Count Tizen House, and its role in the Polish-Lithuanian history dating back to 1499, all of which are showcased in the Rokiškis Regional Museum.

What should travelers prepare for when visiting Rokiškis on this tour?

Travelers should prepare for a culturally rich experience involving classical architectural sites and museums, plan for extended visits of around 8 hours on the first day, and be ready to explore the aristocratic and historical heritage of the region.
