⚠️Sharja travel precautions

🕌 Islamic cultural courtesy

Dress Regulations:Conservative costumes required in mosque and public places

  • Moscow:Both men and women wear long sleeves, long pants, women are required for head scarfs
  • Public location costumes:Prohibit clothes that reveal your shoulders and knees, avoid clothes that stick to your body
  • Ramadan period:Prohibition of eating food and drinks from public places from sunrise to sunset
  • Friday prayer:Most of the shops 12-2 o'clock, traffic congestion around the mosque
  • Ban:Sharja is completely prohibited from drinking and drinking alcohol and drinking
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Summer (May-September):The temperature is 35-48 degrees, extremely hot and humid, recommended before or after 6 pm
  • Winter (11-3):Temperature 15-30 degrees, tourism optimism
  • Desert:Drying throughout the year, enough moisture intake and moisturizing
  • Sandstorm:Spring winds can occur, preparation of masks and sunglasses
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Bus system development, women -only seats
  • Taxi:Confirmation of the use of meters, prohibit the front seat when boarding alone
  • Dubai connection:Sharja-30 minutes for Dubai, more than 1 hour for traffic jams
  • Rent -a -car:Need for international license, right traffic, speeding speed
  • Walking:Summer daytime walking risk
💰 Shopping related precautions
  • Succulent Rag:Beginning at 30-50% of the first presentation price
  • Fake product:Purchase of counterfeit goods such as luxury bags and watches
  • Gold purchase:18K, 22K purity confirmation, calculation of international gold prices
  • Carpet purchase:Confirmation of handmade, request for certificate of origin
  • Spice:Check the seal packaging status, check the expiration date
📸 Precautions for shooting
  • Mosk shooting:Check the interior shooting zone, prohibit the shooting of people who pray
  • Female shooting:Absolutely prohibited from shooting local women
  • Government building:Palace, military facility shooting
  • museum:Prohibition of flash shooting, no exhibit shooting
