⚠️ Woodon Tani Travel Precautions

🌡️Checks related to weather

Tropical climate:Udon Tani is a hot and humid tropical climate all year round, especially in summer

  • High temperature and humidity:Summer temperature is very high and humidity is high, so there is a risk of dehydration.
  • Strong ultraviolet light:High SPF UV blockers required to prevent burns due to strong ultraviolet rays in the tropics
  • Preparation for the rainy season:During the rainy season in May-October
  • Mosquitoism:Due to the nature of the tropics, there are many mosquitoes, so prepare mosquito eradication and long -sleeved clothes
  • Dry dust:There are many dust during the dry season from November to April
🌺 Red lotus sea viewing precautions
  • Time limit:Red lotus sea can only be seen from December to February, so it is required to check the time of travel.
  • Early morning view:Lotus blooms most beautifully at around 6 am to 10 am, so early morning departure
  • Boat Tour Reservation:Boat tour reservation is difficult during the peak season, so reservations are required in advance.
  • Check the weather:When it rains, the lotus does not bloom properly, so visit the weather after checking the weather
  • Shooting manners:Be careful not to break or damage the lotus and consider other tourists
🚗 Precautions when using transportation
  • Access from Bangkok:1 hour and 20 minutes from Don Muang Airport to Udon Tani Airport
  • Train use:It takes 8-10 hours by train from Bangkok, recommended a bed compartment when using a night train

  • City traffic:Various transportation means such as 쏭 Tau (red truck), tuk tuk, and taxi
