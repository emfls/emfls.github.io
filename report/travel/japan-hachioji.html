What is the recommended itinerary for a 2-night, 3-day trip to Hachi Oji, Japan?

The recommended itinerary includes Day 1 with nature and history exploration at Mount Takao, Yakuoin Temple, Hachi Oji Ramen Restaurant, and Gomiya Park, and Day 2 focusing on cultural and art experiences at the Tokyo Fuji Museum of Art, Hachi Oji Castle, Tama Forest Science Garden, and Sogo Recreation Park.

Travelers should plan for approximately 8 hours on Day 1 and 9 hours on Day 2 to comfortably visit all recommended sites and enjoy the activities without rushing.

Key attractions include Mount Takao for hiking and scenic views, Yakuoin Temple, Hachi Oji Ramen Restaurant for local cuisine, Gomiya Park, Tokyo Fuji Museum of Art, Hachi Oji Castle, Tama Forest Science Garden, and Sogo Recreation Park.
