What are the main attractions included in the Sri Lanka Tala and Kellerine Dura travel itinerary?

The itinerary includes visiting Lifton Tea Farm to taste fresh Ceylon tea, exploring Baker Falls which is 263 meters high, trekking Horton Plains National Park located 2,100 meters above sea level, and enjoying the panoramic views at World’s End.

How long should travelers plan for the first day of the Tala and Kellerine Dura trip?

Travelers should plan for about 9 hours on the first day to comfortably visit the tea farm, waterfall, and Horton Plains, allowing ample time to experience each location without rushing.

What preparations or precautions are recommended for visiting the Central Highlands in Sri Lanka?

Visitors should prepare for high-altitude conditions, bring appropriate clothing for cooler temperatures, and be ready for moderate hiking. It is also advisable to stay hydrated and wear sturdy footwear for exploring natural sites like Horton Plains and Baker Falls.
