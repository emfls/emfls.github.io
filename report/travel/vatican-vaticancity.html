🚨 Admission and dress regulations

Strict outfit regulations:Regulations that must be observed as a religious sacred place

  • Prohibited costumes:Sleeveless, nashi, short skirts or shorts that come up 15cm above your knees
  • Essential outfit:A neat dress that covers the shoulders and knees, men also prohibit shorts
  • Hat wear:Prohibition of wearing a hat inside the cathedral
  • shoes:Sneakers or oral recommended rather than slippers or sandals
🎒 Search for prohibited items and security
  • Big bag:Large backpacks, travel bags must be left to the storage (free)
  • Shooting equipment:Selfie stick, tripod, camera stand, video camera prohibition
  • Dangerous goods:Knife, scissors, weapons, spray, big umbrella
  • prog:Prohibition of bringing food and drinks (excluding water)
  • security check:Strict security search at the airport level

  • Pre -reservation required:The Vatican Museum must be reserved online pre -reservations
