It is recommended to allocate about 8 hours on the first day to thoroughly experience the historical and cultural highlights of Brussels’ center, including sightseeing, shopping for Belgian chocolate and lace, and visiting key landmarks.

Travelers can enjoy the Royal Gallery, Europe’s first shopping arcade from the 19th century, where they can experience traditional Belgian culture through high-end chocolate and lace shopping in a historic and elegant setting.
