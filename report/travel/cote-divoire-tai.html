⚠️ Thai travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Main risk factors:Malaria, yellow fever, wildlife, jungle environment

  • malaria:Thai areas are malaria dangerous areas, required to take prevention drugs, always use mosquito repellents.
  • yellow fever:Essential certificate of evasion of yellow fever at the time of entry, completed in vaccination 10 days before departure
  • Wildlife:Dangerous wildlife formats such as chimpanzees, elephants, and leopards
  • Jungle Environment:Toxic plants, insects, snakes, etc.
🌧️Chires and weather precautions
  • Woogi (May-October):Heavy rain and flood risks, road conditions deterioration, possibility of changing schedules when traveling in the rainy season
  • Dry (11-4):The optimal travel time, but the daytime temperature is over 35 degrees, enough water intake
  • humidity:More than 80% of the year, fast -dried clothing required
  • Daily crossroads:Daily and night temperature differences of 10 degrees or more, preparation
🚗 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road situation:There are many unpaved road sections to Thai, 4WD vehicles required
  • Guide required:Duty of accompanying local guides when entering Thai National Park
  • fuel:Lack of gas stations in Thai area, supplemented with enough fuel in Avijang
  • communication:Thai National Park Instable mobile phone signal instability
  • Emergency situation:Far from medical facilities, first aid supplies required
💰 Price and cost precautions
  • National Park Admission fee:Foreigners 50,000 XOF (about 100,000 won)
  • Guide cost:30,000-50,000 XOF per day (6-10,000 won)
  • lodgment:Restrictions on accommodations in Thai area, required for advance reservations
  • Food expenses:Lack of local restaurants, camping foods in advance
  • Cash use:Card payment areas, enough cash preparation
