⚠️Gaoua travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the market, late at night

  • Terrorism:Burkina Paso, Terrorist Dangerous Area, Foreign Ministry Travel Alert Confirmation required
  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing and valuables in the market and tourist attractions
  • Night going out:Prohibition of going alone after sunset, recommending local guides
  • Photo shooting:Government building, absolute prohibition of shooting military facilities
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Buses and taxis are poor, wearing seat belts
  • Road situation:Road flooding and traffic sections occur during the rainy season
  • Fuel supply:Lack of gas station, plan to secure fuel
  • Waga Dugu connection:It takes about 4-5 hours to the capital
  • Border movement:Ghana, Courtbuire border unstable
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):Temperature 25-38 degrees, very dry, hot, enough water intake
  • Woogi (May-October):Temperature 22-35 degrees, high humidity and heavy rains
  • Harmatan:December-February Sahara Desert sand breeze, respiratory attention
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet, UV protection required throughout the year
  • Daily crossroads:Preparation of warming clothing because it is large during the dry season
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash payment:Most of them are limited to cash payment, card use
  • Exchange:Recommended US dollars to CFA francs
  • Tip Culture:Tip customs for guides and service workers
  • negotiation:Price negotiations in the market
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Malaria dangerous areas throughout the year
  • yellow fever:Required vaccination required, confirming certificate when entering Korea
  • drinking water:Only drinking water, no ice intake
  • food:Eat only completely cooked food
  • Medical facilities:Lack of medical facilities, plan for emergency situations
