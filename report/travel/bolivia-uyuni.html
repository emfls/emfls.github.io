⚠️ Uyuni travel precautions

🏔️ altitude disease and healthism

Altitude altitude:Uyuni 3,653m, tour area up to 4,850m

  • History symptoms:Headaches, nausea, dizziness, shortness of breath, etc.
  • Adaptation period:The first day of arrival is enough rest, no excessive activity
  • Moisture intake:Over 3-4 liters per day required for water intake
  • An alcoholic ban:Drinking alcohol in alpine areas danger of worsening alpine disease
  • Emergency situation:In case of severe altitude disease, it is necessary to immediately descend
🌡️ Extreme climate precautions
  • Daily crossroads:Day 20 degrees and -15 degrees at night
  • ultraviolet rays:Very strong ultraviolet rays with altitude and salt reflection
  • Dryness:It is extremely dried with less than 10% humidity
  • gale:Sand wind occurs due to a sudden strong wind
  • Woogi (12-3):Road blocking and cancellation of tours
🚗 Traffic and Tour precautions
  • Tour required:Unable to travel, be sure to use local tours
  • Vehicle status:Old 4WD vehicles, the risk of failure
  • Road situation:Unpaved roads, tough mountain trails
  • Communication:Many sections without cell phone signals
  • Lack of fuel:Difficulty securing fuel due to lack of gas station
  • Preparation for motion sickness:Motion sickness is required by traveling for a long time
💰 Economic and living precautions
  • Cash Society:Limited card use, enough cash preparation
  • ATM lack:ATM is also very limited in downtown Uyuni
  • Prices:Due to the nature of the tourist destination, all prices are high
  • Electric instability:Frequent power outage, limited charging opportunities
  • Toilet paid:Most of them are separate toilet fees
  • Food restriction:Vegetarian, meat choice
