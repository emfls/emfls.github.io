  • Crime for tourists:In many places, you have to deal with bags and belongings carefully.
  • Night movementism:At night, special careful and safe places
  • Storage of valuables:Important objects are kept close to the body and always stored in a safe place
  • How to deal with:If you are a stranger approach, you refuse firmly, wearing pickpockets prevention items
