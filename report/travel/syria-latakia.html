  • Ministry of Foreign Affairs Travel Alert:Designated as a prohibition area, required to collect sufficient information in advance
  • Local guide:Accompanied by a reliable local guide
  • Political remarks:Absolutely prohibited to speak sensitive remarks related to politics and religion
  • Photo shooting:Military facilities and government buildings prohibited
  • Night going out:Resisting out after sunset
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Limited and irregular operation, recommend using taxis or exclusive vehicles
  • Road situation:Some road damage and checkpoints exist
  • Lack of fuel:Intermittent fuel shortage occurs
  • Border movement:Requires additional documents when passing the Turkish border, Lebanon
  • flight:Damascus airport, no direct flights
🌡️Checks related to weather
