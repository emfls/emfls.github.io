⚠️ Darn travel precautions

🚨 Religion and culturalism

Islamic conservative countries:Required religion and customs

  • Prohibited item:Punishment when importing alcohol, pork, religious books, etc.
  • Dress Regulations:Conservative costumes in public places, wearing female Abaya
  • evensong:Stop the operation of the store during the prayer time 5 times a day
  • Ramadan:Prohibition of public food intake during fasting
  • Photo shooting:Local people, especially women, require permission when shooting women
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Extreme heat:Rising to 50 degrees of summer temperature, enough water intake
  • Dry climate:Skin care and moisturizing required
  • Sand breeze:Caution of respiratory diseases due to sandstorms of seasonal seasons
  • Daily crossroads:Fall down 10 degrees or less in winter in winter
  • ultraviolet rays:Seon cream and hat mandatory with strong sunlight
🚗 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Drinking driving:Drinking itself is illegal, deportation
  • checkpoint:Installation, headlights and reductions are required for each highway section
  • Wildlife:Camellia crash on the desert highway
  • Night driving:There are many sections without street lamps,
  • Sand Storm:Stop driving if it is difficult to secure visibility
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • MERS:Prohibition of visiting camel contact farms or milk processing factories
  • Medical insurance:Travel insurance is required because medical expenses are very expensive
  • Emergency situation:Emergency phone 997 (ambulance), 999 (police)
  • Drug:Problems can occur when importing drugs without prescription
