⚠️ Jeju Island travel precautions

🌪️ Weather and natural disasters

Majorism:Typhoon season (July-September), winter strong winds

  • gale:Jeju Island has a strong wind all year round, making it difficult to use umbrellas, windbreak jackets required
  • typhoon:In the summer typhoon, flight can be canceled and Udo ship operation can be stopped
  • Sudden weather change:When climbing Halla Mountain
  • ultraviolet rays:UV rays and sunscreen are required due to altitude and sea reflection.
🚗 Traffic and rental precautions
  • Rent -a -car reservation:Peak season (July-August, year-end and New Year holidays)
  • Parking problem:Popular tourist attractions such as Seongsan Ilchulbong and Udo
  • Road situation:Hallasan entry and freezing in winter, preparing a chain
  • Public transportation:Intercity bus dispatch interval is long and recommended to use car rental
  • flight:Lack of alternative shortage, secure relaxation schedule in case of cancellation due to weather
🏔️ Hallasan climbing precautions
  • Climbing time:Seongpanak Course 8-10 hours, Gwaneumsa Course 8-9 hours
  • Mountain time:Seasonal entry time restrictions, preliminary confirmation required
  • Weather change:Rapid temperatures near the summit, preparing for winter clothes
  • Health management:Altitude diseaseism, sufficient water intake at 1,950m altitude
  • Safety equipment:Climbing, stick, head lanterns required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Jungmun Tourism Complex, the price of restaurants around Seongsan Ilchulbong is high
  • Rent -a -car fee:Peak season rental car rates 2-3 times higher
  • Accommodation fee:Beach resort peak season prices soar
  • Specialty:The traditional market is lower than the airport such as Hallabong and citrus fruits
