자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What is the recommended travel course for visiting Jeju Island in Korea?

The recommended travel course for Jeju Island is a 2-night, 3-day itinerary. On Day 1, visitors explore eastern nature sites including Seongsan Ilchulbong, Udo Island, Seopjikoji, and Woljeongri Beach, spending approximately 9 hours enjoying these locations.

What are the highlights of Seongsan Ilchulbong on Jeju Island?

Seongsan Ilchulbong, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is famous for its spectacular sunrise views, volcanic crater, and panoramic scenery. It is a must-see natural landmark on Jeju Island offering unique geological and visual experiences.

What should travelers prepare for when visiting Jeju Island’s eastern nature sites?

Travelers should prepare for extended outdoor activities lasting around 9 hours, including walking and coastal exploration. It is advisable to bring sun protection, comfortable footwear, water, and weather-appropriate clothing to fully enjoy the natural attractions.
