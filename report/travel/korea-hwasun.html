⚠️ Hwasun travel precautions

🚨 Safety precautions

Major areas:Mudeungsan Climbing, around Hwasun Red Wall Street

  • Climbing safety:Prohibition of leaving the trail when climbing Mudeungsan, preparing for sufficient water and snacks
  • Price safety:Safety accident care during swimming and water play in Hwasun Red Wall and Dongbokcheon
  • Weather change:Preparation for clothing in preparation for sudden weather changes in the mountainous region
  • Wildlife:Maintain safe distance when aims at wild animals such as wild boars and snakes while hiking
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Hwasun city bus dispatch interval is long, so it is necessary to check the timetable in advance.
  • Rent -a -car recommendation:It is convenient to use a car rental due to the distance between tourist destinations
  • Parking Space:Narrow parking lot of major tourist attractions, recommending arrival early in the peak season
  • Road situation:Safe driving is required in the mountains and narrow sections.
  • Navigation:Some tourist attractions may be inaccurate, so you recommend search by phone number
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):The temperature is 25-35 degrees, humid, hot and showery often occurs
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -5-10 degrees, eye and ice attention near the summit of Mudeungsan
  • Spring/Autumn:Daily intersection is large and required for temperature control when climbing
  • Rainy season:Mid-July-in early August, risk of hiking and water play with heavy rains
  • ultraviolet rays:UV rays strong in Mudeungsan, which is high
💰 Price related precautions
  • Restaurant price:Restaurant prices around tourist attractions
  • Accommodation:Hot Spring Resort Peak Season price 2-3 times higher
  • Parking fee:Most of the free parking, some private parking lots,
  • Specialty:Purchase after comparing the price of specialty products such as bokbunja and Korean beef
