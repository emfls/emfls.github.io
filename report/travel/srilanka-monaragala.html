⚠️ Monara Gala Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Night at night, around the national park, a secluded temple area

  • Wildlife:Dangerous wildlife formats such as elephants, leopards, and vipers
  • Political situation:Possibility of demonstrations due to the Sri Lankan economic crisis, local news check
  • Religious conflict:Hinduism-Buddhist religious tension between Buddhism, prohibition of photography in religious rituals
  • Going out late at night:After sunset, refrain from going out due to an increase in wild animal activity
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Difficult moving due to dirt roads and mountainous terrain, recommended 4WD vehicles
  • Public transportation:Bus operation is irregular and there are many if there is no air conditioner
  • Tuk Tuk Fare:Most of the preliminary fees are required, if there is no meter
  • Lack of fuel:Lack of fuel due to economic crisis, securing sufficient fuel
  • Rainy movement:May-September Monsun Season Road flooding and landslide risk
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (12-3):25-32 degrees, hot and dry, enough water intake
  • Woogi (May-September):Temperature 23-28 degrees, heavy rains and flood risk
  • Sogi (10-November):Intermittent showers, umbrellas and raincoats required
  • Tropical Climate:High humidity and high temperature
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, UV protection required near the equator
