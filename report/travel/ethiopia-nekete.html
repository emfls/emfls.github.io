⚠️ Nechee travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, typhoids, hepatitis A

  • malaria:Prevention drug required, mosquito repellent regular use, long -sleeved clothes
  • Altitude disease:Area 2,000m above sea level, slowly moving with enough moisture intake
  • drinking water:Only drinking water, avoid ice, avoid raw fruit
  • Emergency situation:Limited to local medical facilities, transfer to Adisava
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, unable to pass in the rainy season
  • Public transportation:Wearing overloading, seat belt when using minibus
  • Rent -a -car:International driver's license required, local guide recommendation
  • flight:Domestic flight connection from Adis Ababa, delayed due to weather
  • Night movement:Absolute prohibition, risk of road condition with wild animals
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (10-5):The temperature is 15-25 degrees, dry and dusty
  • Woogi (June-September):12-20 degrees, heavy rains and flood risk
  • Plateau climate:15 degrees or more, very cold at night
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen required
  • Rainy Travel:Road blocking, trekking is impossible
💰 Price related precautions
  • Exchange rate fluctuations:Ethiopia Vir exchange rate instability, dollar exchange recommendation
  • Price bargaining:It is essential for bargaining in the market, foreign gourds
  • Tip Culture:Guide $ 10-15 a day, driver $ 5-10
  • Accommodation fee:$ 20-50 eco-rogue, $ 10-25 guest house
