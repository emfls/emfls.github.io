⚠️ Um Alku Wine Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:In general, it is very safe, but attention to outdoor activities

  • Desert activity:Mandatory guides during riding or desert tours, prohibited entry into the desert alone
  • Water Activities:Comply with safety rules in the beach and water park, wearing life jackets
  • Wildlife:Maintain a proper distance when observing birds in wetlands, prohibit feeding
  • Emergency situation:Emergency Telephone 999, Tourism Police 901, Korean Consulate Contacts Store
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Restrictions on bus routes, recommend using taxi or car rental
  • Taxi fee:About 150-200 Dirham from Dubai to Um Alku Wine
  • Rent -a -car:International driver's license required, right traffic, speed limit compliance
  • Dough Cruise:Telegraph Island Tour is required for advance reservations
  • distance:About 50km from Dubai, 1 hour by car
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-September):35-45 degrees of temperature, very hot and humid, restricted outdoor activities
  • Winter (12-2):The temperature of 15-25 degrees, the best time to travel
  • Spring/Autumn:20-35 degrees of temperature, appropriate for outdoor activities
  • Desert:Big and dryness, enough water intake
  • ultraviolet rays:Very strong, sunscreen and hat required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Water Park:Dreamland Aqua Park admission fee 150 Dirham
  • Museum entrance fee:UAQ National Museum 10 Dirham, Fort 5 Dirham
  • Dough Cruise:Telegraph Island Tour 300-500 Dirham
  • Tip Culture:Restaurant 10-15%, Taxi Round, Hotel 5-10 Dirham
