📅 Back 2 nights and 3 days travel courses

Day 1: History and Culture Walk

Lu River Old Street → Yongsan Temple → Cheonhu Palace → Folk Art Museum

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:Enjoy a variety of snacks and handicrafts at the Lu River Old Street, where the mood of Taiwanese tradition is alive, experience the culture of Taiwan in Yongshansa and Cheonpung Palace, which retains the beauty of wooden architecture, and see the artifacts and lifestyles of the Qing Dynasty at the Folk Art Museum.

Day 2: Nature and Outlook Experience

Bagua Mt.

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:At the Great Buddha at the top of Bagua Mountain, see the outlook of the city and the plains, and see the train performance in the linear cars built during the Japanese colonial period. You can feel the history of boots and the railroad culture of the Changhwa Temple and the round locomotive.
