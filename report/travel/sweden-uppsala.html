⚠️ Uppsala travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around Uppsala Station, some parts of the city center late at night

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing in the station, tourist attractions, and cafes, the bag is carried forward and the zipper lock
  • Local impersonation:Fake guides, ticket sales attention at tourist attractions
  • Going out late at night:Be careful to go alone after 10 pm near the station
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Train movement:Stockholm-40 minutes of Uppsala train, ticket pre-booking, station belongings
  • Public transport strike:Due to the nature of Sweden, strike without notice, alternative transportation check
  • Taxi fee:Airport-about 500 ~ 700sek, meter check required
  • Walking:The city center is enough to walk, be careful with sliding in winter
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):Meeting of 15-25 degrees, long, sunny, UV protection required
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -5 ~ 3 degrees, very cold, winter clothes required
  • Spring/Autumn:Daily crossing, sudden rain and wind, preparation of outerwear and umbrella
  • White night phenomenon:Recommended for light until late at night
💰 Price related precautions
  • Food prices:Restaurant and cafe prices are high, simple dishes and lunch boxes recommended
  • Admission fee for tourist attractions:Cathedral and Museum free or 50 ~ 150sek
  • Tip Culture:Tips are not essential, about 5-10% for good services
  • shopping:Purchase after checking traditional crafts and food prices
