자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main attractions to visit in Nursultan on the first day?

On the first day, travelers should visit the Baiterek Tower, a 105-meter-high symbol of Kazakh life, the Palace of Peace and Reconciliation designed by Norman Foster, the Arc Orda Presidential Palace, and enjoy the fountain shows along Nurzhol Boulevard.

How much time is recommended to explore the key landmarks in Nursultan?

It is recommended to spend about 8 hours exploring the core landmarks on the first day to fully appreciate the architectural and cultural significance of sites like Baiterek Tower, the Palace of Peace and Reconciliation, and the Arc Orda Presidential Palace.

What cultural or architectural features make Nursultan’s landmarks unique?

Nursultan’s landmarks are notable for their symbolic designs, such as the Baiterek Tower representing the life tree of Kazakh culture, the pyramid-shaped Palace of Peace and Reconciliation hosting the World Religious Leaders' Conference, and the futuristic style of the Arc Orda Presidential Palace.
