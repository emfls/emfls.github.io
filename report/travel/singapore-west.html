⚠️Singapore West Regional Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:In general, it is very safe, but attention when using night park

  • Drug crime:In case of distribution of more than a certain criterion, it is absolutely prohibited by the death penalty and no exception.
  • Park safety:Use of lighting zones for night using West Coast Park
  • Nature Reserve:Wildlife attention by using only the specified paths for mangrove exploration
  • Emergency situation:999 (police), 995 (fire department/ambulance)
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • MRT use:West area is recommended by using the EW line (east -west) to off -peak time
  • Usage of buses:Exact charges required, no change
  • Taxi/Grab:Peak time premium application, grab app recommendation recommendation
  • Bicycle rental:Use of bicycle rental stations in West Coast Park
  • Transportation card:Recommended for purchasing ez-link cards or nets flashpay cards
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • High temperature and humidity year -round:Average maximum 31.4 ℃, minimum 25.1 ℃, humidity 70%
  • Monsoon period (11-1):Relatively cool but high rainfall
  • Sudden scall:Frequently occurred in the afternoon, an umbrella required
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, sunscreen and hat required
  • air conditioning:Preparation of thin coat
💰 Price related precautions
  • Zoo entrance fee:Adult 39 SGD, Children 28 SGD
  • Hawker Center:One-person meal 5-15 SGD, cheap local food
