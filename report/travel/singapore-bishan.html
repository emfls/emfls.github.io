⚠️ Bishan travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Bishan is a very safe area overall

  • General security:Bishan is one of the safest residential areas in Singapore and has a very low crime rate.
  • Police patrol:Maintaining high security level with efficient and rapid response from Singapore police
  • Night safety:Relatively safe even in late night, but keep the basic precautions
  • Emergency situation:Report the ambulance and fire department with the police, 999, and 995.
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • MRT system:Bishan Station is very convenient as a transit station where North-South Line and Circle Line intersect.
  • EZ-LINK Card:Use of public transportation cards required, available at convenience stores
  • Taxi fee:About 25-35 Singapore dollars from Changi Airport to Bishan
  • Grab Car:Southeast Asia Uber service is cheaper and more convenient than taxis
  • Bicycle rental:Vishan-Bicycle rental store in Anmokio Park can be used
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Tropical climate:The year-round temperature 24-32 degrees, high humidity (80-90%)
  • Scol:Sudden tropical shower occurs frequently, umbrella required
  • ultraviolet rays:Very strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat required near the equator
  • air conditioning:Temperature difference caused by an indoor air conditioner
  • Moisture supplement:Destructive risk of dehydration due to high humidity and temperature
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:Singapore is one of the most expensive cities in the world
  • Hawker Center:Inexpensive local foods at the Hawker Center, 5-10 Singapore dollars
  • Shopping Mall Restaurant:The restaurant in the mall is 20-50 Singapore dollars
  • Tip Culture:In general, it does not give tips, but 10% service is occupied in high -end restaurants.
📋 Laws and regulatory precautions
  • Prohibited gum:Prohibition of bringing and selling gum, fines
  • Non -smoking area:Smoking can only be smoked in most public places and only in designated areas
  • Garbage speculation:Up to 1,000 Singapore dollars fine for unauthorized speculation
  • Food in the subway:Prohibition of eating food within MRT
  • J -Walking:Penalty and designated crosswalks in case of unauthorized crossing
