자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What is the recommended itinerary for a 2-night, 3-day trip to Villadu Maiu?

The recommended itinerary includes spending Day 1 exploring Villadu Myu Port, Porto Inglês Old Town, and the salt fields, with an estimated 8 hours to cover these core historical and cultural sites.

What historical significance does Porto Inglês hold in Maio Island's history?

Porto Inglês was formed by British ships exporting salt until the 19th century and features colonial-era traces, reflecting its importance as a salt export hub during that period.

What notable historical event took place near Villadu Maiu in 1814?

On January 23, 1814, during the Napoleonic Wars, the British and French fleets engaged in the Maiu naval battle near Villadu Maiu, marking a significant historical event in the area.
