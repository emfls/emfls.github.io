What are the main attractions to visit in Bern on the first day?

On the first day, visitors should explore Bern’s medieval landmarks such as the Cheatglogge (Clock Tower), Prison Tower, Einstein House, Bern Cathedral, and Bear Park, all located within the UNESCO World Heritage old town.

How much time should I plan for the Day 1 itinerary in Bern?

The recommended time to complete the Day 1 itinerary is about 9 hours, allowing ample opportunity to appreciate the 16th-century fountains, arcade walks, and climb the Bern Cathedral Observatory.

What unique experiences does Bern offer related to history and science?

Bern offers unique experiences such as viewing the Doll of Cheatglogue in the Clock Tower and exploring the Einstein House, which celebrates the life and work of the famous scientist Albert Einstein.
