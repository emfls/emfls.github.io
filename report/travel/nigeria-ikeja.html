⚠️ Ikeza travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Late -night hours around the airport, market area, outskirts

  • Strength and theft:Reliable taxi use and distribution of valuables when moving from the airport to the city
  • fraud:False police or civil servants, who are impersonating a public official, contacts the embassy in case of requiring ID cards
  • hijacking:Don't go alone, always go to the group, prohibit going out late at night
  • Terrorism:Avoid large -scale rally or protest areas, check safety notes for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • traffic jam:Serious traffic jams between Ikaja section, enough time to spare time
  • Taxi:Official taxi company use, meter check or pre -fee negotiations
  • Public transportation:Careful pickpocketing when using BRT (Bus Rapid Transit)
  • Road situation:Road flooding during the rainy season
  • Airport movement:It takes about 1 hour from Martala Mohamed Airport to downtown
🌡️ weather and health precautions
  • Rainy (4-10):Rain and flood risks, waterproof products required
  • Dry (11-3):Wearing dust, mask due to the wind
  • malaria:High risk of mosquito -mediated diseases, required to take preventive drugs
  • yellow fever:Refusal to enter the country when you have a certificate of vaccination,
  • Water -person disease:Avoid food after drinking bottled water
  • heatstroke:Sufficient moisture intake and rest in the shade
💰 Price and economic precautions
  • Exchange rate fluctuations:Naira exchange rate can change rapidly, recommended dollar cash
  • Rewards:Excessive charges for foreigners
  • Tip Culture:10-15% tips for service workers
  • blackout:Frequent blackouts, auxiliary batteries required
