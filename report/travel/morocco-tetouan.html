자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main attractions included in the Tetuan 2-night, 3-day travel course?

The main attractions include Tetuan Medina, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its 15th-century Andalusian architecture and traditional markets; the Grand Mosque with its impressive 15th-century Minaret; the 17th-century Royal Palace featuring traditional Moroccan architecture and gardens; and the Spanish Mosque, which highlights the unique convergence of Iberian and Maghreb architectural styles.

How much time is recommended to explore Tetuan’s key historical sites?

A recommended time of approximately 9 hours is suggested to thoroughly explore the core points of Tetuan, including the Medina, Grand Mosque, Royal Palace, and Spanish Mosque, allowing travelers to experience the cultural and architectural richness of the city.

What architectural styles can travelers expect to see in Tetuan?

Travelers can expect to see a blend of 15th-century Andalusian Islamic architecture within the Medina, traditional Moroccan architecture in the Royal Palace and gardens, and a unique fusion of Iberian and Maghreb styles in the Spanish Mosque, reflecting the city’s historical Spanish Protection era influences.
