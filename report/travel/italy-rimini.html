Major areas:Central Station, near the beach, late at night

  • Motorcycle theft:Walking inside India,
  • Fake guide:Fake ticket sellers and forced guides at tourist destinations
  • Going out late at night:Station, Beach, Old Town to go alone after 10 pm
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transport strike:Due to the nature of Italy, sudden traffic strikes can occur, alternative means of transportation
  • Ticket check:Bus and train ticket smiling fines, must be checked before riding
  • Taxi fee:Airport-about 20 euros in the city, the meter check required
  • Train connection:Connect with Florence (3 hours), Rome (5 hours), Bologna (1 hour)
  • parking:Recommended for parking in urban parking, recommendation of parking lots
  • San Marino Movement:Limini -San Marino section (National Road No. 72)
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):Temperature 22-31 degrees, moist and hot, sufficient moisture intake, UV protection
  • Winter (12-2):The temperature is 5-12 degrees, but it rains often
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and sudden showers, umbrellas and outerwear preparation
  • Coastal Wind:Sea breezes can be low in temperature.
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, sunscreen required
