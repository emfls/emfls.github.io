⚠️Saragosa travel precautions

🚨 General safety precautions

Overall risk:Low -Saragosa is quite safer than other Spanish cities like Barcelona and Madrid, so tourists can travel without worrying about crime.

  • Pickpocket attention:Pickpocketing and bag snatches may occur in crowded areas where tourists frequently visit, but the incidence rate is low compared to other tourist cities.
  • Public transportation safety:Trams and buses are regularly monitored and patrolled and safe, and there are few crimes.
  • Taxi:Only a license and a registered taxi are used, checking whether it is an official taxi with the green light of the taxi roof.
  • Night walk:If you are traveling alone, don't walk alone at night, especially avoid dark lights
🌧️ Natural disaster precautions
  • Flood risk:Saragosa has many floods caused by storms and hurricanes, and floods are the most common natural disasters in Spain.
  • Check the weather:Check the weather before the trip, and floods occur mainly in the warm season.
  • Preparation for winter:In winter, there may be strong winds and heavy rain, so prepare umbrellas and waterproof clothing
🍷 Drinking and fraudulent precautions
  • Beverage management:When you drink alcohol, always watch the drink, do not leave the drink, and you do not receive a drink that you do not know.
  • Fraud:Since there is a fraud for tourists, careful passers or fake police officers are careful, and the real police officer does not ask for an ID card.
  • Environmental recognition:Wherever you are, you will always recognize the surrounding environment, and do not slow down in the quiet area.
🏛️ Cultural precautions
  • Respect for local law:Saragosa is a diversity city that avoids problems by respecting local laws.
  • Dress Regulations:Wear a neat dress when visiting a conservative or religious place
  • Public location courtesy:Control of excessive affection expression in public places
  • Photo shooting:Confirmation of permission when shooting inside religious facilities
