📅 Okakara 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: Exploration of Wildlife Protection Area and Savana

Okakara Wildlife Protection Zone → Savana Sapari → Traditional Village → Sunset Point

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:Observing elephants, giraffes, zebras, lions, etc. in the Okakara wildlife protection zone, the vast Savannah plains experience the natural African ecosystem through the game drive, learn about the African native culture and traditional lifestyle in the traditional villages of Herero and the Obanbo, Finishing the impression of the first day while watching the sunset
📍 Okakara Wildlife Protection Zone 📍Savana Sapari 📍 Traditional village 📍 Sunset Point

Day 2: Cultural Experience and Natural Examination

Bushman Cultural Center → Baobab Tree Forest → Okakara Market → Star Observation Tour

Recommended time:11 hours
Core points:At the Bushman Cultural Center, you can experience traditional hunting, herbal knowledge, and ancient rock painting of the Aboriginal desert of the Kalahari Desert, and shoot certification shots with symbolic trees of the African continent in hundreds of years old Baobab Tree Forest. Enjoy the night sky of Africa with a special star observation tour that observes the Southern Cross and the Milky Way in the clear sky
📍Bushman Cultural Center 📍Baobab Forest 📍 Okakara Market 📍 Star observation tour

Day 3: Adventure and Finishing (optional)

Bush Working Safari → Traditional Craft Experience → Okakara Museum → Souvenir Shopping

Bush Working Safari:Takes 4 hours, explores Savana on foot with local guides, and traces animal footprints and plants observations.
Traditional craft experience:3 hours, Heroo traditional costumes, wood carcraft crafts, beads jewelry experiences
Okakara Museum:Takes 2 hours, Namibia Independence History and Okakara Cultural Heritage Exhibition
Souvenir Shopping:It takes 1 hour, local handicrafts and traditional African souvenirs
📍Bush Working Safari 📍 Traditional craft experience 📍 Okakara Museum 📍 Souvenir shopping

⚠️ Okakara travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever

  • Malaria prevention:Start taking malaria prevention drugs two weeks before departure, mosquito bite prevention required
  • Wildlife:Never get out of the car during the safari, the guide order strict compliance
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, avoid drinks with ice
  • food:Avoid street food and eat only enough food
  • Emergency situation:It may be far from the nearest hospital, so first aid supplies are required
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Dry (5-10):Daily crossroads, day 30 degrees/night 5 degrees, warm clothes required
  • Woogi (11-4):Sudden heavy rain, road conditions worsening
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays near the equator, sunscreen occasionally
  • Dryness:Very dry climate, sufficient water intake and moisturizer
  • Sand breeze:Sand winds in the desert, snow and respiratory protection
🚗 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road situation:Many unpaved roads, recommended 4WD vehicles
  • drive:Traffic on the left, the crosswalk of the wildlife road
  • fuel:Gas station spacing, well prepared for fuel
  • GPS:Signal weak areas, preparation of offline maps
  • Night driving:Prohibition of night driving due to wildlife risk
💰 Security and economic precautions
  • Cash management:Avoid mass quantity of cash, and store distributed
  • valuable:Hotel safe use, at least only one of safari
  • Tip Culture:Guide, $ 10-20 a day to the driver
  • price:High prices of tourist attractions, recommending local markets
  • communication:Internet connection unstable, important contact is processed in advance
