  • Police:The police are patrolling at the tourist destination, so it is relatively safe, but it is always careful.
  • How to deal with:My belongings are always on my body, and especially in places where people are crowded
🚖 Traffic and airport precautions
  • Milwaukee Airport Taxi:Official taxi at the airport, caution
  • Uber/Lift:Used at a safe pickup place
  • Public transportation:Use MCTS bus system, prepare accurate charges
  • Rent -a -car:Winter eye driverism, chain preparation
  • parking:Downtown parking fee expenses, recommended public transportation
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):Temperature 25-30 degrees, high humidity, sunscreen and hat required
