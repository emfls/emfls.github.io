⚠️ Medina travel precautions

🕌 Religion and culturalism

Main precautions:Need to comply with strict religious norms as an Islamic holy place

  • Dress Regulations:Both men and women are required to wear long sleeves, long pants, women recommend covering hair
  • evensong:Pause shops and tourist attractions for 5 times a day
  • friday:Friday afternoon restrictions on traffic and commercial activities during group prayer time
  • Ramadan:Prohibition of open food intake during the day during the day during the Ramadan period
  • Photo shooting:Prior permission is required when shooting inside and local and locals
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Haramine High Speed Railroad:Pre-reservation required when using high-speed railway between Mecca-Medina
  • Taxi:Recommended for using app -based taxi services such as Uber and Karim
  • Public transportation:Checking the separate seat zone for men and women when using buses
  • Walking:High temperature due to desert climate, enough water intake
  • Rent -a -car:Both international driving licenses and local licenses are required
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-September):Temperature 35-45 degrees, extremely hot and dryness, refrain from going out afternoon
  • Winter (12-2):The temperature is 15-25 degrees, mild but chilly at night
  • Spring/Autumn:20-30 degrees of temperature, the best time for sightseeing
  • Desert:Daily cross -crossed and very dry, moisturizer required
  • Sand breeze:Recommended intermittent sand breeze, mask wearing
💰 Price related precautions
  • Accommodation fee:2-3 times higher accommodation costs during the pilgrimage season
  • food:Halal food -oriented, pork and alcohol completely prohibited
  • souvenir:Religious souvenir prices are high, bargaining available
  • Transportation cost:Taxi fare is relatively inexpensive, but the pilgrimage season
