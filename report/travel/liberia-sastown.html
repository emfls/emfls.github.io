What is the recommended itinerary for visiting Sasu Town in Liberia?

The recommended itinerary for Sasu Town is a 2-night, 3-day travel course. On Day 1, visitors explore the harbor city, central streets, traditional markets, and coastal trails, spending about 8 hours to fully experience the local culture and scenery.

What are the key attractions to see in Sasu Town on the first day?

Key attractions on the first day include Sasu Town’s port, the lively fishing activities, the city’s central street with traditional and colonial architecture, the traditional market featuring local products like palm oil, cassava, and rice, and the coastal trail offering views of the Atlantic Ocean and beautiful sunsets.

What preparations and precautions should travelers consider when visiting Sasu Town?

Travelers should prepare for a full day of walking and outdoor exploration, bring appropriate sun protection for coastal areas, and be ready to engage respectfully with local markets and cultural sites. It is advisable to plan for an 8-hour tour on the first day to cover all major points of interest.
