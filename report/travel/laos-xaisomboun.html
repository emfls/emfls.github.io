🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Outside Mountain area, city late at night

  • Trekking Safety:Be sure to go with local guides, prohibit access to mountainous areas alone
  • Wildlife:Maintain safe distances with wild animals in nature protection zones
  • Emergency situation:As medical facilities are limited, first aid supplies are required
  • communication:Mobile phone signal weakness in mountainous areas, preparation in advance how to contact emergency contact
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:As the mountain road is rough, it is necessary to prepare for motion sickness.
  • Public transportation:Use of city buses and 쏭 Taewoo, check in advance
  • Rent -a -car:International driving license required, experience of driving on mountain roads
  • Vientian connection:It takes about 3-4 hours by bus
  • Rainy movement:It is possible to deteriorate road conditions in the rainy season in July-September
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):11-34 degrees, large and dry daily
