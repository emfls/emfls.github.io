⚠️ Castet Jonde de La Plana Travel Precautions

🚶‍♂️ walking precautions

Casteon has a 5-6 km distance from the city to the beach, so you need to be careful when you walk.Especially in the hot weather in the summer, long -distance walks can be difficult.

  • Beach moving:It takes 1 hour and 30 minutes on foot from the city to Plana Beach, recommended tram
  • Eat enough water:It is necessary to take water bottles when walking on long distance
  • Comfortable shoes:Mandatory wearing shoes suitable for walking for a long time
  • Use shade:Move along the shade of the building in hot weather
🚗 Traffic safety precautions
  • Recommended for using train:43 minutes in Valencia, the most efficient and comfortable vehicle
  • Tram:It can be conveniently moved from the city to the beach.
  • Bus schedule:Local buses can be irregular, so check
  • Taxi:Use only official taxis and check the meter
🏖️ Beach safety precautions
  • Swimming safety:Use of places where safety personnel are swimming only in designated beaches
  • UV protection:Burnism, sunscreen due to the strong sunlight of the Mediterranean,
  • Supplies Management:Recommended for loss of valuables, waterproof bags on the beach
  • Sunset time:When viewing sunset on the beach, check in advance
🍽️ Food and restaurant precautions
  • Market experience:Local food experiences such as Mercado Central to Tortiya Espanyola
  • mealtime:Lunch 2-4:00, 9-11 o'clock in Lunch for Spanish meals
  • Seafood freshness:Taste fresh seafood near the port
  • Tip Culture:5-10% tips are common in the restaurant
🌤️Chires and costumes
  • Seasonal dress:April-October travel optimal, light clothes in summer, and warm clothes in winter
  • September weather:It can still be hot, so enough water intake and shade rest
  • Beach dress:In addition to swimsuit
  • Daily crossroads:It can be chilly in the morning and evening, so prepare a light outerwear
