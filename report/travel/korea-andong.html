⚠️ Andong travel precautions

🚨 Safety precautions

Major areas:Around Andong Dam, Nakdong River Promenade

  • Price safety:Safety accident care when playing in the water around Andong Dam and Nakdong River
  • Trail safety:Wearing safety equipment when climbing the vessel
  • Night activity:Cautions that lack lighting when watching the night view of the Wolyeonggyo Bridge
  • Traditional building:Comply with the Rules of Cultural Property Protection in Hahoe Village and Dosan Seowon
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Andong city bus dispatch interval is long, so it is necessary to check the timetable in advance.
  • Hahoe Village Shuttle:Recommended use of shuttle buses from Hahoe Village entrance to village
  • parking:Lack of parking spaces in peak season for tourism, recommendation of public transportation
  • KTX connection:It takes about 2 hours and 30 minutes from Seoul to Andong Station
  • Rent -a -car:Convenience to rental cars when moving out of the outskirts such as Dosan Seowon and Bongjeongsa Temple
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):25-32 degrees, moist and hot, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -5-5 degrees, very cold, winter supplies required
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and dryness, moisturizers and outerwear preparation
  • Rainy season:In mid-July-early August, intensive rainfall, umbrella required
  • Fine dust:High concentration of fine dust in spring, wearing masks
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Hahoe Village, Restaurant price around Wolyeong Bridge is somewhat high
  • entrance fee:Hahoe Village 3,000 won, Dosan Seowon 1,500 won, Bongjeongsa Temple 1,300 won
  • Parking fee:1,000-2,000 won per hour
  • Andong Specialty:Reasonable prices of local specialties such as liver mackerel and Andong shochu
