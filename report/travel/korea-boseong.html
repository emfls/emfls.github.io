⚠️ Boseong travel precautions

🚨 Safety and healthism

Major areas:Jiamsan Mountain Trail, coastal tidal flat area

  • Climbing safety:Jiamsan Mountain Climbing Province Preparation of Climbing Roads, Proper Climbing Equipment
  • Coastal safety:Compliance with safety rules when using Yulpo Beach, check the tide time when experiencing tidal flats
  • Bugs:Ingreen tea fields and natural recreational forests such as mosquitoes and ticks
  • Health disease prevention:Sufficient water intake and rest for long -term outdoor activities in summer
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation limit:Boseong is a rural area, limited number of public transportation operations
  • Bus schedule:Intercity bus and city bus schedule required in advance
  • Taxi:Reservations are recommended in advance because taxi waiting time may be longer
  • Rent -a -car recommendation:Recommended for using car rentals for free movement
  • Parking Space:Avoid congestion time for major tourist attractions
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):Temperature 25-32 degrees, moist and hot, UV protection required
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -5-10 degrees, coastal winds strong, preparation of winter supplies
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and sudden showers, umbrellas and outerwear preparation
  • Rainy season:The possibility of restrictions on outdoor activities during the rainy season in June-July
  • typhoon:Check the weather information of the typhoon season in August-September
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Use after comparing the price of major tourist destinations
  • entrance fee:Korea Dawon 4,000 won, Korea Tea Museum 3,000 won
  • Specialty price:Boseong Green Tea Product Price Comparison Purchase
  • Accommodation fee:Peak season accommodation costs increase, recommendation in advance
