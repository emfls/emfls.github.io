자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main historical sites to visit in Accra on the first day?

On the first day, visitors should explore Independence Square, which commemorates Ghana's independence in 1957, the Ghana National Museum showcasing Ashanti Kingdom treasures and West African culture, and the Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park honoring Ghana's first president.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect at Makola Market in Accra?

At Makola Market, the largest market in West Africa, travelers can experience traditional Ghanaian crafts such as Kente fabrics, Adinkra symbols, and purchase local cocoa products and spices, offering an authentic cultural immersion.

How long is the recommended time to explore the Day 1 travel course in Accra?

The recommended time to complete the Day 1 travel course, including all key historical and cultural sites, is approximately 8 hours.
