What are the main attractions to visit in Makarska on the first day?

On the first day in Makarska, visitors should explore the harbor area, St. Marco Cathedral known for its 18th-century altars and frescoes, Kachi Square which dates back to the 15th century, and the Francesco Monastery featuring exotic gardens and religious artifacts.

How much time is recommended for the Day 1 travel course in Makarska?

The recommended time for the Day 1 itinerary in Makarska is about 8 hours, allowing travelers to fully experience the port city’s historical and cultural highlights.

What kind of atmosphere and scenery can travelers expect in Makarska?

Travelers can expect a vibrant coastal atmosphere with colorful fishing boats, palm trees, and a blend of historical architecture and religious sites surrounded by exotic plants and flowers, typical of the Dalmatian region.
