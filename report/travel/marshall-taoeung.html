⚠️ Tao -eung travel precautions

🌊 Marine safetyism

Main precautions:Strong bird, coral reef wound, marine life

  • Algaeism:Strong algae around the condolences, mandatory accompanying guides
  • Coral reef protection:Prohibition of coral reef contact, compliance with rules for the protection of ecosystems
  • Marine life:Toxic jellyfish, sea snakeism, bright swimsuit wearing
  • Health disease prevention:Eat enough water, rest in the shade
🚤 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Airlines limit:Essential diesel, limited number of flights
  • Boat traffic:The possibility of stopping operations according to the weather
  • Road situation:Many unpaved roads, recommended 4WD vehicles
  • Fuel supply:Gas station limited, confirmation of fuel in advance
  • Emergency situation:Limitative medical facilities, familiarity with emergency contacts
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Tropical climate:The year-round temperature 24-32 degrees, high humidity
  • Woogi (5-November):Scall and typhoon potential, weather forecast confirmation
  • Dry (12-4):Relatively dry, tourism optimal
  • ultraviolet rays:Very strong ultraviolet, sunscreen required
  • Dewatering:Risk of dehydration due to high temperature and humidity
💰 Description of living expenses
