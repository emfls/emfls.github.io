⚠️Pucon travel precautions

🌋 Caution related to volcanic activity

Volcanic alarm system:Blue (safety), yellow (attention), red (dangerous)

  • Climbing:In the yellow alarm, it is impossible to climb the top volcano, only up to the middle point.
  • Weather dependence:If the weather is not good, the full cancellation of the volcanic trekking, the preliminary confirmation required
  • Guide required:Personal volcanic climbing absolute prohibition, must be a certified guide
  • Equipment inspection:Duty to wear safety equipment such as cramp phones, pickels, helmets
  • Preparation of fitness:9-10 hours high-intensity trekking, enough physical strength
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Santiago connection:7 hours by bus from Santiago, recommend using night buses
  • Border pass:Fruits and vegetables when entering in Argentina
  • Local transportation:The city is available for walking, and the outskirts are required for tours or car rentals.
  • Road situation:Winter eye attention, need to prepare a chain
  • Public transportation limit:Decrease in Sunday and holiday buses
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (12-3):Temperature 15-25 degrees, trekking optimal time, UV strongness
  • Winter (June-August):Temperature 0-10 degrees, snowy, some activity restrictions
  • Daily crossroads:The temperature difference between the day is large, so it is required
  • Spreading weather:Sudden weather changes due to the nature of Patagonia climate
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong UV rays, sunscreen and sunglasses required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tour cost:Volcanic trekking 100-120 USD, Hot Spring Tour 80-100 USD
  • Cash payment:Most tourists receive only cash, preparing for enough cash
  • Equipment rental:Climbing equipment rental costs are included in the tour prices
  • Restaurant price:Due to the nature of the tourist destination, 2 times more expensive than the general Chile city
  • Tip Culture:Guide tip 10-15%, restaurant 10% recommended
