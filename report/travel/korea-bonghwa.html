⚠️ Bonghwa travel precautions

🏔️ Mountain safetyism

Main weekly section:Cheongnyangsan Climbing Road, Sky Bridge section

  • Hiking trail safety:Cheongnyangsan has a lot of rock formations, and recommends to use rockfalls, climbing sticks
  • Weather change:Mountain weather in the mountainous regions, required for raincoats and windproof clothing
  • Sky Bridge:Restrictions on access in case of strong winds in case of high phobia
  • Wildlife:Wild animals such as wild boars and snakes can appear loudly, warning loudly
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation limit:Bonghwa is a limited city bus line, recommended to use a car rental or taxi
  • V-Train Reservation:Peak season reservation required, difficulty on purchasing the same day
  • Parking Space:Cheongnyangsan Mountain, Baekdudaebang Arboretum Parking Lot, recommended early visits
  • Road situation:Mountain roads and motion sickness
  • Fuel supplement:Gas station spacing, supplementation in advance
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):25-35 degrees of temperature, moist and hot, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):Gion -10 ~ 5 degrees, attention to snow and ice
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and sudden showers, outerwear preparation
  • Mountain Climate:5-10 degrees lower than the flat, required for insulation clothing
  • fog:Early in the morning, it occurs frequently around the fog around Cheongnyangsa Temple
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination price:Cheongnyangsan and Baekdudaebang Arboretum Restaurant price is high
