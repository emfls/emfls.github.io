⚠️ Ennie Wetok Travel Precautions

🚨 Environment and safetyism

Main precautions:Radioactive pollution area, marine safety, lack of medical facilities

  • Radioactive contamination:Some regions visit only the designated safety zones due to the past nuclear test.
  • Marine safety:Strong birds and waves, prohibiting swimming alone, accompanying guides
  • Medical facilities:Lack of emergency medical facilities, preparation of sufficient medicine and first aid supplies
  • Communication Restriction:The Internet and telephone communication instability, preparation for emergency contact means
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Airlines limit:Marshall Islands flights very limited, pre -booking required
  • Island between islands:Use of boats or small aircraft, and stop operating according to weather
  • Local transportation:No public transportation, walking or using bicycles
  • Lack of fuel:Fuel supply instability, checking fuel on boat tours
  • Weather dependence:All traffic stops during typhoon and storm
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (5-November):Temperature 27-32 degrees, high humidity and scalls, typhoonism
  • Dry (12-4):The temperature 25-30 degrees, relatively dry and wind
  • ultraviolet rays:Very strong ultraviolet rays, sufficient UV protection required
  • typhoon:Occasionally checked weather information when traveling in a typhoon season
  • Sea level rise:Influence of sea level rise due to global warming
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:Very expensive prices due to dependence on imports of goods
  • Accommodation fee:High rates with limited accommodations
  • foodstuffs:Very high price mainly for imported foods
  • Fuel cost:Fuel costs such as boat tours
