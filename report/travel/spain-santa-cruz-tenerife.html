  • Public transportation:Titsa bus schedule check in advance, use traffic card

  • Walking:Wear the right shoes on the rough surface of the volcanic terrain

💡 Santa Cruz de Teneripe Travel Tips

  • Teide cable car reservation:At least 1-2 weeks ago, online pre-booking required, frequent operation due to weather
  • Eternal Spring Island:It can be visited in a gentle weather throughout the year, but March-May and September-November are optimal.
  • Rent -a -car recommendation:Attractions and beaches hidden by car rentals rather than public transportation are efficient
  • Local market use:Nuetra Semo Ra de African market, fresh tropical fruits and local specialties purchase
  • Canary specialty:Banana Li Chair, Moho Sauce, and Aloe Vera products can only be purchased locally
  • Dolphin whale observation:Dolphin and Whale Observation Tours are available throughout the year in the southwestern sea area of Teneripe
  • Las Teresitas Beach:If you visit early in the morning, there are few tourists and a relaxed time guarantee
  • Local language:Basic Spanish greetings, thanksgiving expressions, limited English communication
  • Carnival experience:Participation in the world famous Santa Cruz Carnival when visiting February-March
  • Star observation:Teide National Park is one of the world's best stars, night tour recommendation.
