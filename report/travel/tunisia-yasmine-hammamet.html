What are the main attractions on the first day of the Yasu Minhammet travel course?

On Day 1, travelers can enjoy Yasu Minhammet Beach known for its emerald Mediterranean waters and white sands, visit the modern marina with yacht and café culture, explore the traditional 15th-century Medina Hamatmet market featuring spices, ceramics, and carpets, and appreciate panoramic views from the Casba fortress.

How much time should I plan for the Day 1 itinerary in Yasu Minhammet?

What cultural experiences can I expect in Medina Hamatmet?

Medina Hamatmet offers a rich cultural experience with its traditional Arab market dating back to the 15th century, where visitors can see and purchase local spices, ceramics, carpets, and other artisanal goods, providing insight into Tunisia’s heritage.
