What are the main historical sites to visit in Kabitte on the first day?

On the first day, visitors should explore the Baby Nondo Shrine, the birthplace of Filipino independence; Fort San Felipe, a 16th-century fortress significant during the Spanish colonial era; and San Sebastian Church, a 19th-century structure showcasing Spanish colonial architecture.

How long should I plan to spend exploring Kabitte on the first day of the itinerary?

The recommended time to explore the key sites on Day 1 is approximately 8 hours, allowing ample time to visit the Baby Nondo Shrine, Fort San Felipe, Kabitte City Public Market, and San Sebastian Church.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect in Kabitte’s public market?

Travelers can enjoy local seafood and traditional Filipino foods at the Kabitte City Public Market, providing an authentic taste of the region’s culinary heritage alongside opportunities to engage with local culture.
