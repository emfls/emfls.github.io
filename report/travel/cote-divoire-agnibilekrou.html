🚨 Health and safetyism

Major disease risks:Malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever, bowl

  • malaria:High risk of annual occurrence, use of preventive drugs and mosquito repellents required
  • yellow fever:Essential vaccination 10 days before entering, if there is no certificate, refusal to enter the country
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, avoid ice and peel the fruit directly
  • hygiene:Ported to hand disinfectant, street food intake
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, deterioration of road conditions during rainy season
  • Public transportation:Bus operation irregularities, preliminary negotiations when using taxis
  • Avijang connection:270km distance, about 4-5 hours
  • Cana border:Passports and visas confirm, waiting time when passing the border
  • Night movement:Risk of security, restraint after sunset
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Annual average temperature:27 degrees, high temperature and humid tropical Sabana Climate
  • Woogi (June-August):Visiting non -recommendation, concentrated heavy rains and flood risk
  • Dry (February-April):Visit optimal, but very hot and dry
  • Annual precipitation:Low at 180mm, water shortage can occur
  • ultraviolet rays:Very strong, mandatory sunscreen and hat
💰 Economic and security precautions
  • Political situation:It has been stabilized since the civil war in 2011, but the difference in security by region
  • pickpocket:Caution and valuables in the market and border area
