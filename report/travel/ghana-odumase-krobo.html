⚠️ Odumase Crobo Travel Precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever, bowl

  • Malaria prevention:Prevention drug required, mosquito repellent regular use, long -sleeved clothes
  • Drinking water safety:Drinking only bottled water, avoiding ice, prohibiting food intake
  • Hygiene management:Hand disinfectant is carried out at all times, thoroughly washed hands after using toilet
  • Emergency situation:Local hospital location in advance, travel to travel insurance contact information
🌦️Checks related to weather
  • Rainy (4-10):Sudden heavy rain, the possibility of flooding roads
  • Dry (11-3):Dust, dryness due to the wind
  • Temperature:25-35 degrees throughout the year, high humidity temperature is high
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, sunscreen near the equator
  • garments:Breathable cotton material, long -sleeved clothes prepared
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Pickpocketing when using Trotro (minibus)
  • taxi:No meter, preliminary fee negotiations required
  • Road situation:Many unpaved roads, cannot pass in the rainy season
  • Acra connection:It takes about 1 hour and 30 minutes by bus
  • Night movement:Avoiding as much as possible, safety risk
💰 Price related precautions
  • Bead price:Recommended for tourists, accompanied by local people
  • Market bargaining:Bagin at 30-50% of the first presentation price
  • Tip Culture:Guide 10-20 Sedi, Restaurant 5-10 Sedi
  • Exchange:Banks or certified currency exchange centers, prohibit street currency exchange
🏛️ Cultural precautions
  • costume:Prohibition of exposure costumes, especially when visiting religious facilities
  • Photo shooting:Save permission before people shoot, prohibit the interior of the royal palace
  • Religious courtesy:Respect for Christian culture, consideration of church time on Sunday
  • Personnel law:When handshake, the right hand is used, the left hand is considered unclean
  • Defo consciousness:Something as a sacred ritual, quietly when watching
