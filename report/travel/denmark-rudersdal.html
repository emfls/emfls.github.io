⚠️ Luderdal travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the station, a rare park late at night

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing at the station, bus stop, and tourist attractions, the bag is carried forward and the zipper lock
  • Bicycle theft:Double lock required when using bicycles
  • Fake guide:Fake ticket seller attention in tourist destinations
  • Going out late at night:Late at night, rare place alone
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transport strike:Denmark can also be strike without notice, alternative means of transportation
  • DSB ticket:Train, bus integrated ticket use, fine attention to checks
  • Taxi fee:About 400-600 DKK, meter check from Copenhagen to LuderSdal
  • Train connection:Connect Copenhagen (30 minutes), Heler Loop (20 minutes), Healerurd (35 minutes)
  • Bicycle terrain:There are many bicycles, but be careful with the unpaved section
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):Temperature 18-25 degrees, sunscreen and hat required during the day
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature 0-7 degrees, waterproof jacket for wind and rain
  • Spring/Autumn:Preparation of large and frequent rain, umbrella and outerwear
  • Near the lake:Low body temperature due to sea breeze and humidity
  • ultraviolet rays:Northern Europe is also UV rays, so sunscreen is required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Café around the lake, restaurant price 2-3 times expensive
  • Museum entrance fee:Rudersdal Museum 60 dkk, Frederix Dal Garden Free
