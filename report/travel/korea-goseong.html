What is the recommended travel course for a 2-night, 3-day trip to Goseong, Korea?

The recommended itinerary includes exploring Hwajinpo Beach, visiting the Kim Il-sung European castle replica, enjoying panoramic views at Baekseom Marine Observatory, and tasting fresh seafood at Geojin Port Market, especially on Day 1 which takes about 8 hours.

What are the key attractions to visit in Goseong on the first day?

Key attractions on Day 1 include Hwajinpo Beach known for its harmony of mountain and sea, the historically significant Kim Il-sung castle replica, Baekseom Marine Observatory with panoramic east coast views, and Geojin Port Market offering fresh local seafood.

What preparations and precautions should travelers consider when visiting Goseong?

Travelers should prepare for outdoor activities such as trekking around Hwajinpo Beach and Eungbong, plan for an 8-hour day of sightseeing, and be ready to experience local food markets. It is advisable to wear comfortable footwear and check weather conditions for coastal exploration.
