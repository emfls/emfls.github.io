⚠️ Urbari travel precautions

🚨 Political safetyism

Major areas:Borders, around military facilities, political assembly areas

  • Political instability:Libya is politically unstable, so it is required to check the level of travel alerts in the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
  • Armed group:Since there are armed group activities in some areas, it is recommended to travel with local guides
  • checkpoint:Passports and visas are always portable because document tests are frequent at the military checkpoint.
  • Prohibition of photography:Military facilities, government buildings, and inspections are absolutely prohibited
  • Night movement limit:After 10 pm
🏜️ Desert Environmental Precautions
  • Extreme Daily Interior:It can drop to 50 degrees during the day and 5 degrees at night.
  • Sand Storm:In the event of a sudden sandstorm
  • Dewnity risk:At least 4-5L of water per day, supplemented with salt
  • Lostness danger:Absolutely prohibited from behavior in the desert, a compass for GPS and compass
  • Wildlife:Dangerous desert animals such as vipers and scorpions
  • ultraviolet rays:Risk of burns due to extreme ultraviolet rays, SPF 50+ sunscreen required
🚗 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • 4WD required:The desert area can only be moved to four -wheel drive vehicles.
  • Fuel:There is a rare gas station, so preparation for extra fuel tanks
  • Tire punk:Tire punk frequency during the desert, prepare more than 2 spare tires
  • Local driver:Recommended for employment of local drivers who are familiar with the desert road
  • Communication:Desert area mobile phone signal weakness, preparation for satellite phones
💰 Economic precautions
  • Cash -based economy:Limited credit card use, enough cash preparation
  • Exchange difficulties:Libya Dinar is difficult to exchange US dollars because it is difficult
  • Price fluctuations:Prices can change rapidly due to political instability
  • Fuel cost:The desert tour fuel cost is very high
🏥 Medical precautions
  • Lack of medical facilities:There are few medical facilities in the desert, a perfect first aid kit required
  • Vaccination:Propected yellow fever, hepatitis A, vaccination of vaccination
  • malaria:Some regional malaria risks, prevention drugs
  • Emergency evacuation:In case of serious emergency, the cost of evacuation of helicopters is very high
