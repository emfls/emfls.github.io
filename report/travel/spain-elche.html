⚠️ Elect travel precautions

🚨 General safety precautions
  • Pickpocket attention:Thorough management of bags and belongings in tourist attractions and markets
  • Beach safety:Encourage activities only in the safety zone when swimming, life jackets
  • Night activity:Do not go alone in late hours, but use bright lights
  • Drinkingism:Prohibit access to the beach or high places after excessive drinking
🚗 Traffic safety precautions
  • Rent -a -car use:Familiar with the Spanish traffic laws and parking zones required
  • Public transportation:Check the bus and train schedules in advance, use traffic cards
  • Taxi:Only official taxis are used, checking the operation of the meter
  • Walking:Only the promenade designated in the palm forest
🌡️ Climate and environmental precautions
  • Summer heat:Over 35 degrees in July-August, enough water intake
  • UV protection:UV rays, sunscreen, as a Mediterranean climate
  • Siesta time:Considering the store closed at 2-5 pm and scheduled schedules
  • Palm tree allergies:Pollen Season (March-May) Allergies
