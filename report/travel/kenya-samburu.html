⚠️Samburu travel precautions

🦁 Wildlife safety rules

Absolute prohibition:Go out of the car, feed the animal, and make a loud sound

  • Elephant attention:Near the elephant, the mother can be aggressive, so keep enough distance
  • Lion/Leopard:Do not turn off the engine when discovering large cats and animals quietly
  • Hasma/Crocodile:Never approach the water on the riverside
  • Snake/Scorpion:Always check and wear tents or shoes
  • Night movement:Absolutely prohibited from moving outside the lodge without a guide.
🏥 Health care precautions
  • malaria:Use of mosquito repellents, wear long -sleeved, prevention drugs
  • Moisture intake:3-4 liters of water per day
  • Foodism:Only drink bottled water, prohibit raw intake
  • ultraviolet rays:Applying strong sunlight, sunscreen from time to time
  • Altitude disease:Rest at the headache and dizziness of Mount Kenya
  • Emergency situation:Immediately contact the guide, takes 6 hours to Nairobi Hospital
🌡️ Climate and environmental precautions
  • Dry (June-October):25-30 degrees of temperature, dusty and dryness, enough water intake
  • Woogi (11-5):20-28 degrees, sudden showers, mud roads
  • Daily crossroads:10-15 degrees of daytime and night temperature differences, insulation clothing required
  • altitude:800-1,200m above sea level, required for alpine adaptation time
  • dust:Savannah's severe dust, masks and sunglasses wear
