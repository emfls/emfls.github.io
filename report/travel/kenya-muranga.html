Day 1: Coffee Farm and Averder Mountains Explore

Muranga Coffee Farm → Aberne Mountains → Maragua area → Traditional Kikuyu Village

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:At the Muranga Coffee Farm, located at 1,340 ~ 1,950m above sea level, you can experience the cultivation process of the world's finest Kenya AA coffee, explore the optimal coffee cultivation environment produced by the red volcanic soil and an average annual precipitation of 1,305mm at the foot of the Averder Mountains, and Maragua, Muranga In the cultivated area, Grapefruit Taste of Kenya Coffee, which has a smooth acidity, and learning the traditional culture and coffee cultivation of the Kikuyu tribes

Day 2: Cultural Experience and Natural Examination

Murangga Town → Kikuyu Culture Center → Averder National Park → Murangga River

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:In exploring the history of the Kenya Yusu, the center of the Kenya Independence Movement in Muranga Town, explores the meaning of the birthplace of Jomo Kenyata, experiences the history of the Mauum Mau Independence Resistance Movement and the traditional Kuyu culture at the Kikuyu Culture Center, observed African wild animals such as elephant, buffalo and leopard in Aberne National Park, and Kenya in Muga River Exploration of the natural environment rich in water resources and the source of fertile soil
📍Muranga Town 📍Kikuyu Culture Center 📍Aberdear National Park 📍Murangga River

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, typhoids, hepatitis A

  • yellow fever:When you enter Kenya, you will be required
  • drinking water:Drink only bottled water and avoid ice, fruits peel off and eat
  • Altitude disease:Sufficient water intake and rest at an altitude of 1,500m above sea level
