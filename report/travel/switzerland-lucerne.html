⚠️ Lucecern travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Train station, around tourist attractions, rare alleys at night

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing, bags of pickpockets in tourist attractions such as station, Capello, and old towns, and the zipper lock
  • Theft:Distributing cash and valuables, copies and photos prepared for passport loss
  • Going out late at night:Be careful to go alone at night
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Free use of buses and trains (provided by accommodation) and check before boarding
  • Rigisan/Cruise ship:Free when carrying a Swiss pass, checking weather changes and operating timetables required
  • Luggage storage:Instant storage time limited time limit, additional fee for exceeding
  • Walking:Most of the city center is required for comfortable shoes within a 20 -minute walk
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):15 ~ 28 degrees, high -day crossing, high showers, UV rays
  • Winter (12-2):0 ~ 8 degrees, snow and rain frequent, required for winter clothes
  • Spring/Autumn:Moody weather, outerwear and umbrella preparation
  • Alps trail:Alpine regional pressure, sliding attention, health status check
💰 Price related precautions
  • Food expenses:Recommended outfit (more than 100,000 won per person), supermarket · COOP recommendation recommendation
  • Admission fee for tourist attractions:Paid, such as glacier park and museum, Capello, lion, and old town
  • Cash use:Most of them can pay for cards, only small cash preparation
