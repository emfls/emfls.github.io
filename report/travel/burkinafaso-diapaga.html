⚠️ Diapu travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Mali-Niger border areas (travel restrictions), prohibit outing at night

  • Terrorism:It has been reported as an activity area for W-park armed group, and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Travel Alert 2-3
  • Armed intensity:Night move to night, absolutely prohibited, local guide accompanying
  • Mass shootout:November 2023 Attack Housing for Civilians
  • Local authorities cooperation:Prior consultation with local security forces when moving
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, unable to pass in the rainy season
  • Public transportation:Restricted, recommended personal or local guide vehicles
  • Fuel supply:Lack of gas station, need to secure sufficient fuel
  • Waga Dugu connection:It takes about 300km, 5-6 hours
  • Border pass:Strengthen security search when moving near Niger border
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-2):Meeting 15-35 degrees, Armatang sand breezeism
  • Hot dry season (March-May):Temperature up to 40 degrees, heat stroke attention
  • Rainy (June-October):Temperature 23-32 degrees, road flooding and malaria increase
  • Sahel Climate:Big and dryness, enough water intake
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight and sun protection year -round
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash -oriented:Almost impossible to use credit cards, fully prepared for cash
  • Exchange rate:1 euro = 656 Franc Sepa (fixed exchange rate)
  • lodgment:Basic accommodation is limited, preliminary reservation required
  • Food expenses:Local food, it is difficult to find Western food
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Occasional occurrence, prevention drugs and mosquito prevention required
  • yellow fever:Duty of vaccination, compliance with international health regulations
  • Water -person disease:Bottled water only drinking, prohibiting food intake
  • Medical facilities:There is only a basic health center.
  • Emergency situation:Difficulties in contact with medical staff, preparing for sufficient pharmacy
