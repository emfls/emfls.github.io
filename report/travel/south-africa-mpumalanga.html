What is the recommended itinerary for a trip to South Africa's Kruger National Park?

The recommended itinerary spans 3 nights and 4 days, with Day 1 dedicated to a safari experience in Kruger National Park, entering through Malera Gate, participating in a Big Five safari tour, and staying overnight at a safari lodge.

Which animals can travelers expect to see during the Kruger National Park safari?

Travelers can expect to see the Big Five animals: lion, elephant, buffalo, leopard, and rhino, among various other wildlife species native to one of Africa's largest and oldest wildlife protection areas.

What are the key preparations and precautions for a safari in South Africa's Kruger National Park?

Visitors should prepare for daytime safari tours by dressing appropriately, following professional guide instructions, and staying at recommended safari lodges to ensure safety and maximize wildlife observation opportunities.
