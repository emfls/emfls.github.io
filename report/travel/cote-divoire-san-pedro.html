What is the recommended itinerary for a 2-night, 3-day trip to San Pedro?

The suggested itinerary includes spending Day 1 visiting San Pedro Port, Central Market, the Beach Trail, and the Fishing Complex over approximately 8 hours, focusing on exploring the port's modern facilities, local markets, traditional culture, and coastal scenery.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect in San Pedro?

Travelers can experience traditional West African culture by visiting the central market to see local agricultural and aquatic products, observing fishermen's daily lives at the port, and enjoying the Atlantic Beach Trail's sunset views.

What are the key highlights of San Pedro as a travel destination?

San Pedro is notable for being Côte d'Ivoire's second largest port, featuring lively modern port facilities developed since the 1960s, a bustling central market, a scenic beach trail along the Atlantic Ocean, and a vibrant fishing complex.
