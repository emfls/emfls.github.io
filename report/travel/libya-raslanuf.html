⚠️Rasranuf travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around petroleum facilities, military checkpoints, tribal conflict areas

  • Political instability:Since the civil war, the security situation is unstable, the local guide accompaniment required
  • Petroleum facility:Never ban on petroleum refining stations and port facilities, prohibit access to security zones
  • Lack of conflict:Possibility of conflict between regional shortages, occasional confirmation of local conditions
  • Night movement:After sunset, restraint
  • checkpoint:Many military checkpoints, ID cards are always portable
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Lack of public transportation:Use of local guide vehicles, risk of individual movement
  • Road situation:Many desert roads and unpaved roads, 4WD vehicles recommended
  • Fuel supply:Gas station spacing, need to secure enough fuel
  • Dependence on GPS:Lack of road signs, GPS and local guides required
  • Emergency situation:Limitative medical facilities, familiarity with emergency contacts
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-September):35-45 degrees of temperature, extremely hot, outdoor activities restrictions
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature 10-20 degrees, gentle but chilly
  • Desert:Daily crossroads (more than 20 degrees), insulation clothing required
  • Sand breeze:Gibri Wind Season (March-May) Field Block
  • ultraviolet rays:High risk of burns due to desert reflective light
  • dehydration:Extremely dried, enough water intake required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash -oriented:Limited credit card use, enough cash preparation
  • Exchange:Official currency exchange, cancer market exchange risk
  • Price fluctuations:Rapid inflation
  • Fuel cost:Although it is a petroleum producer, fuel shortages occur due to distribution problems
  • Tourism Service:High cost with limited tourism infrastructure
🕌 Culture and religious precautions
  • Islamic culture:Conservative Islamic society, compliance with the norms of costumes and behaviors
  • Ramadan:During the daytime food intake during the daytime food
  • friday:Friday afternoon store and government offices
  • alcohol:Prohibition of alcohol sales and drinking
  • Photo shooting:Local people, especially women, required to shoot women
  • Religious facilities:Dress and courtesy when entering and exiting the mosque
