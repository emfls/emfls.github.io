자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What is the significance of the Bonifacio Monument in Caloocan?

The Bonifacio Monument in Caloocan commemorates Filipino Independence Hero Andres Bonifacio and marks the first battle between the Katipunan and Spanish troops on August 3, 1896. The bronze statue was created in 1933 by national artist Guillermo Tolentino.

What cultural sites are included in the Day 1 travel course in Caloocan?

Day 1 includes visits to the Bonifacio Monument, San Lake Cathedral to experience local religious culture, the Taito Taoist Temple which reflects the heritage of the Caloocan Chinese community, and the SM Center for modern shopping and cultural exposure.

How much time should travelers allocate for the Day 1 itinerary in Caloocan?

Travelers are recommended to allocate approximately 8 hours to fully explore the historical, cultural, and modern attractions included in the Day 1 itinerary.
