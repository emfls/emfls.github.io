⚠️ Port Elizabeth Travel Case

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:The city center, some areas of the late night time

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing at tourist attractions and shopping malls, packing bags in the future and locking zipper
  • robbery:Recommended taxi when you go out at night, don't go alone
  • Fake guide:Attention to those who sell fake tickets in tourist attractions or who are forced to guide
  • Beach front safety:Beach prices are relatively safe, but pay attention late at night
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Rent -a -car driving:South Africa is required for left -handed and international driver's license
  • Taxi:About 200-300 land, meter check from the airport to the city
  • Public transportation:Buses and minibus taxis are available, but rental cars are recommended for tourists
  • Airlines connection:Cape Town (1 hour), Johannesburg (1 hour 30 minutes) domestic flight connection
  • Road situation:The city is good, but the safari area is a dirt road.
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (11-3):20-28 degrees, marine sports optimal, enough water intake
  • Winter (June-August):Temperature 10-20 degrees, mild but windy
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and sudden showers, umbrellas and outerwear preparation
  • Indian Ocean Climate:Sea breezes can be low in temperature.
  • ultraviolet rays:African strong sunlight, sunscreen and hat required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Kings Beach, Café around the Boardwork Mall, Restaurant Price
  • Safari Tour:Kraga Kamagam Park admission fee about 200 lands, ADE Elephant 400 Land
