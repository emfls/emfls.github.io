⚠️ Chemie travel precautions

🌡️ Extreme cold attention

Winter temperature:-15 degrees ~ -30 degrees (to -40 degrees)

  • Statue risk:Exposed skin is required for frostbite risk, face and finger protection within 10 minutes
  • Respiratoryism:Lung damage can be damaged when breathing rapidly in extreme cold, slowly breathing in the nose
  • Clothing management:Immediately change the wet clothes, sweat, and be careful about body temperature
  • Indoor and outdoor temperature difference:Cold due to temperature differences over 40 degrees
🚨 Activity safety precautions
  • icebreaker:Note that slipped on the stomach, strict guide to safety guide
  • Snow Mobil:Speed ​​restrictions, maintenance of guides from a certain distance, and wearing helmet
  • Dog sled:After a guide permission when contact with dogs, sudden movement prohibits
  • Pedestrian walking:Move only the ice thickness confirmed zone, evacuate immediately when discovering cracks
  • Aurora observation:Maintaining body temperature for night activities, prohibiting departure from the group
🏥 Preparation for emergency situations
  • Emergency phone:112 (Police, fire department, emergency medical care)
  • Medical facilities:Chemi Central Hospital location check in advance
  • Travel Insurance:Insurance to include extreme sports
  • communication:Consideration of rental of satellite communication equipment, satellite communication equipment
  • companion:Prohibition of participation in activity alone, always acts together at least 2 people
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • flight:Delay due to deterioration of weather in winter, canceled frequent
  • Rent -a -car:Winter tires required, 4WD recommended, chain preparation
  • Public transportation:Reduce bus operation time, restriction on Sunday operation
  • Road situation:Ice -long attention, restrictions on traffic due to snow removal work
  • fuel:Restrictions on operating hours of gas station, fully prepared for fuel
