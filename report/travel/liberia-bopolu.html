⚠️ Bobolu travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the market, late at night

  • pickpocket:In the market and crowds, it is thoroughly managed by belongings, and the bag is carried forward and the zipper lock
  • Night going out:Prohibited to go alone after 8 pm, recommending local guides
  • Photo shooting:Preliminary permission required for government buildings, military facilities, and local shooting
  • Local law:Very strict, absolute prohibition of drugs related to drugs
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:The road condition during the rainy season, very bad, 4WD vehicle recommended
  • Public transportation:Wearing seat belts and speeding attention when using buses and taxis
  • fuel:Because the gas station is limited, the fuel is fully prepared
  • drive:Need to understand local driving culture, lack of road signs
  • Mobile time:It takes about 6-8 hours from Monrovia to Bobolu
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (May-October):Heavy rain and flood risk, possibility of restrictions on movement
  • Dry (11-4):Cats of 25-35 degrees, dust and dryness
  • humidity:Destructive risk, sufficient moisture intake with high humidity throughout the year
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, sunscreen near the equator
  • Tropical disease:Malaria, dengue fever, mosquito -mediated diseaseism
