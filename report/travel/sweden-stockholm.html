⚠️ Stockholm travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Gamrastan (Old Town), Jungang Station, Hotel, Night Alley Road

  • pickpocket:Longers and bags in Gamrastan, public transportation, cafes, restaurants, etc.
  • Techniques:Cloudy to come, hug, shed something on the clothes, and then wipe it, cover the map or newspaper to steal valuables, etc.
  • Night going out:Avoid dark alleys, rare people
  • Emergency situation:Police/first aid 112, the Embassy in case of loss of passport
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • SL Transportation Card:Integrated use of subway, tram, bus, and ferry can be used in station/convenience store/SL app
  • Transportation ticket:Penalty, ticket possession when free ride is caught
  • Operating hours:Most museums and shops are all required to finish at 17-18:00, schedule management
  • Winter:Snow and Ibing Pan Light

  • ultraviolet rays:Northern Europe is also UV strong, sunscreen required

자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main attractions to visit on the first day in Stockholm?

On the first day, visitors should explore Stockholm City Hall, known for the Nobel Prize dinner and its observation deck, stroll through Gamla Stan to experience its 13th-century medieval atmosphere, visit the Stockholm Palace, the official residence of the Swedish royal family, and enjoy coffee and photo opportunities at Stortorget Square.

How much time should I allocate for the Day 1 itinerary in Stockholm?

The recommended time to complete the Day 1 itinerary, which includes Stockholm City Hall, Gamla Stan, Stockholm Palace, and Stortorget Square, is approximately 8 hours to fully appreciate the historical and cultural highlights.

What cultural experiences are suggested during the Stockholm trip?

The guide suggests experiencing Swedish coffee time, known as fika, particularly at Stortorget Square, and enjoying the medieval ambiance of Gamla Stan, along with visiting royal and historic landmarks to immerse in Stockholm’s rich culture.
