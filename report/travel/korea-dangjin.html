⚠️ Dangjin travel precautions

🚨 Safety and precautions

Major areas:Tidal flat area, coastal, industrial complex

  • Tidal flat safety:Requirements for confirmation when experiencing tidal flats, prohibiting depths without guides
  • Coastal safety:Each coastal accessism, no safety fence on the day of high waves
  • Sunrise view:Safe driving when moving to Dwarmok Village, slippery rock attention
  • Industrial Complex:Dangjin Industrial Complex Regional Restrictions on Personal Personality and Tours are required for advance reservations.
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Dangjin city bus dispatch interval is long, checking the schedule in advance
  • Self -use:Use of the West Coast Expressway, Weekend Traffic Camp
  • parking:Lack of parking spaces in tourist attractions, recommending arrival early in peak season
  • taxi:Lack of rural taxis, reservations in advance or use of public transportation
  • Rent -a -car:When driving in the tidal flat area, the car car wash is required
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):25-35 degrees of temperature, moist and hot, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -5-5 degrees, West Coast wind stronger feeling temperature is low
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and windy, preparation for outerwear
  • West Coast Weather:Sea breeze, low and sudden weather changes
  • Fine dust:Check the concentration of fine dust due to the nature of the industrial complex
💰 Cost -related precautions
  • Accommodation fee:Peak season (summer vacation season) 2-3 times higher accommodation expenses
  • seafood:Wooga Village Seafood Price is high, price check in advance
