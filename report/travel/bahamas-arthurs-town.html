⚠️ Arthrestown travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Secret Beach, late at night, outside the village

  • Isolated environment:As a small island village, medical facilities are limited, and when emergencies are required to move to Naso.
  • Marine safety:Do not do it alone when snorkeling or swimming, and work with local guides
  • Wildlife:Do not enter alone without a guide when exploring bat caves
  • Communication Restriction:Mobile phone signals may be weak, so local contact information is secured in advance
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Limited transportation:There is almost no public transportation, so rental car or taxi required
  • Road status:There are many dirt roads and 4WD cars recommended
  • Fuel:Since the gas station is limited, the fuel condition is always checked
  • flight:Use of small aircraft from Nasso to Cat Island, and can be canceled according to the weather
  • Boat traffic:Some areas such as healing halls can only be accessible by boat
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Hurricane Season (June-November):Typhoon alarmism checks weather information before travel
  • Tropical climate:Desisement at 24-32 degrees throughout the year, high humidity
  • Woogi (May-October):Sudden scall, preparation of waterproof products
  • Strong ultraviolet light:Caribbean sunlight, sunscreen occasionally
  • Maritime weather:Check the waves and wind status during the boat tour
