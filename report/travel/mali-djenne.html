⚠️Jenne Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the market, late at night

  • Terrorism:Confirmation of travel alarms for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs due to the risk of terrorism in northern Mali
  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing attention from the market and tourist attractions, the bag will be carried in the future and the zipper lock
  • Night going out:Don't go alone after sunset, recommend guiding accompaniment
  • Photo shooting:Military facilities, prohibition of shooting government buildings, required to permit when shooting locals
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:There are many unpaved roads and difficulties in the rainy season
  • Public transportation:Buses and taxis are poor, wearing seat belts
  • Guide required:Risk of moving alone without local guides
  • Lack of fuel:Difficulty securing fuel due to lack of gas station
  • checkpoint:Many military checkpoints, ID cards are always portable
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):The temperature is 15-35 degrees, very dry and dusty
  • Woogi (May-October):Temperature 25-40 degrees, moist and flood risk
  • Harmatan:December-February Sahara Desert Sand Breeze Caution
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat
  • Daily crossroads:Daily and night temperature difference is large, so prepare the outerwear
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:High risk of mosquito -mediated diseases, required to take preventive drugs
  • yellow fever:Request for vaccination, testing certificate when entering Korea
  • drinking water:Drink only bottled water and avoid ice
  • food:Avoid street foods and eat only foods that are completely cooked
  • Medical facilities:Lack of medical facilities, insurance for emergency situations
