What are the main attractions included in the IT Carpur 2-night, 3-day travel course?

The main attractions on Day 1 include Carpur Central Market, where visitors can experience local life and tropical fruits; Saint-Pierre Church, showcasing French colonial architecture and Catholic culture; Carpur Beach, known for traditional fishing methods and Caribbean Sea views; and the Local Culture Center, which highlights traditional music genres such as Kongpa, Merende, and Voodoo culture.

How much time should travelers allocate for the Day 1 itinerary in Carpur?

Travelers should allocate approximately 8 hours to fully explore the Day 1 itinerary, which covers the central market, historic church, beach, and cultural center to gain a comprehensive understanding of Carpur's local lifestyle and heritage.

Visitors can expect to engage with Haitian traditional culture through observing local crafts and tropical fruits at the market, appreciating Creole architecture and Catholic influences at Saint-Pierre Church, witnessing traditional fishing at Carpur Beach, and exploring the history of traditional Haitian music and Voodoo culture at the Local Culture Center.
