What is the recommended itinerary for a 3-day trip to Rocha, Uruguay?

The recommended itinerary includes a 2-night, 3-day travel course starting with a hippie culture and beach tour visiting Rafaloma, Lafedra, Punta del Diablo, and Balisa on Day 1, focusing on local lifestyles, carnival culture, surfing, and hitchhiking experiences.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect in Rocha during the February Carnival?

Travelers can experience the vibrant party culture of young people during the February Carnival in Lafedra, along with the free-spirited hippie culture and local festivities that characterize the region.

How can travelers best experience the authentic charm of Uruguay in Rocha?

By spending time with local families, exploring secluded beaches, engaging with fishermen, and participating in local customs such as surfing and hitchhiking, travelers can discover the genuine lifestyle and cultural essence of Uruguay.
