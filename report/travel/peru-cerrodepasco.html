⚠️ Precautions for Vertical Pasco

🏔️ altitude disease and healthism

Altitude altitude:4,338m (one of the highest cities in the world)

  • History symptoms:Headache, nausea, dizziness, shortness of breath, etc.
  • preventive:On the first day of arrival, enough rest, moisture intake, drinking coca leaf, and prohibiting excessive activity
  • Emergency situation:In case of severe altitude disease, immediately move low and visit the hospital
  • An alcoholic ban:Exacerbation of alcohol when eating alcohol in alpine areas
  • Move slowly:Prohibition of rapid movement and jump
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Mountain rugged roads, sudden curves and cliffs
  • Public transportation:Bus operation irregularities, can be stopped according to the weather
  • Taxi:Reliable taxi company use, restraint night moving at night
  • Lima connection:6-8 hours from Lima to bus, no plane
  • Weather influence:Rainy (12-3) road closure and landslide risk
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Cold year:The average annual temperature is 5-10 degrees and falls below freezing at night
  • Daily crossroads:15-20 degrees during the day and night temperature, enough winter clothes required
  • Woogi (12-3):Rain, snow, and road condition deterioration
  • Dry (4-November):Dry and windy, moisturizer required
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong UV rays, sunscreen and sunglasses required
💰 Price and living precautions
  • price:It's cheaper than Lima, but imports are expensive
  • Cash use:Limited credit card use, enough cash preparation
  • drinking water:Drink only bottled water, avoid tap water and ice
  • food:Street foodism, only cooked food
  • language:Spanish required, not English
