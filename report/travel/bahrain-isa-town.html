⚠️ Isa Town travel precautions

🚨 Culture and religiousism

Main precautions:Respect for Islamic culture, compliance with dress regulations

  • Mosk visit:Long -sleeved, long pants required, women are required to cover their heads, take off their shoes
  • Ramadan period:Refringe on food intake during the day during public places, change the operating time of restaurant
  • drink:Only in hotels and licensed places, prohibiting drinking of public places
  • Photo shooting:Local people, especially women, permission when shooting women
🌡️ Climate and health precautions
  • Extreme heat:Summer (June-September) temperature 40-50 degrees, outdoor activities restrictions
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat
  • Dryness:Low humidity, risk of dehydration, sufficient water intake
  • Desert dust:Dust storms can occur, mask preparation
  • air conditioning:Indoor and outdoor temperature difference deep sea cold attention
🚗 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Rent -a -car:International driver's license required, right traffic, speed limit strict
  • taxi:Recommendation of use of meters, recommend using Uber/Cary/Carub app
  • Public transportation:Limit bus system, recommend using a car rental or taxi
  • parking:Shopping malls and tourist attractions paid parking
  • Traffic jams:Most commute time severe traffic jams
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:Overall, prices higher than Korea, especially food expenses
  • Tip Culture:Restaurant 10-15%, Hotel Service 1-2 Dinar
  • Shopping Mall:International brand prices are high, use of sale season
  • Oil value:Advantages when using cheap gasoline, car rental
