Day 1: Desert and traditional culture exploration

Boco Desert → Traditional Village → Islamic Moscow → Local Market

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the vast Boko Desert in the Sasabo area, you can feel the grandeur of the West African desert through the desert trekking and camel experiences, experience traditional architecture and living culture in traditional Hausa and Fullani villages, and enjoy the unique architecture of West African Islamic culture in the local Islamic mosque, and local markets such as traditional crafts, spices, and fabrics in local markets Buy special products and understand Niger culture by interacting with locals

⚠️ Boco travel precautions

🚨 Security and terrorism

Main dangerous areas:Near the Mali border, Burkina Faso Borders

  • Terrorism:Boko Haram and Islamic extremist organizations, the risk of kidnapping foreigners
  • Military Operations:Must -cooperative checkpoint cooperation during the counter -terrorism operations of the French and Nigerguns
  • Night traffic prohibition:Prohibition of moving after sunset, atmosphere in the accommodation
  • Prohibition of solo travel:Be sure to accompany local guides, recommend group travel
  • Government facilities:Military facilities, absolute prohibition of shooting government buildings
🏥 Health and medical precautions
  • malaria:Essential risk of annual occurrence, prevention drugs and mosquito avoidance required
  • yellow fever:Request for vaccination, testing certificate when entering Korea
  • Water -person disease:Bottled water only drinking, prohibiting food intake
  • Medical facilities:Medical infrastructure is extremely poor, and Niame transfer in emergency situations
  • MERS:Caution during camel contact, thorough hand disinfection
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Dry (10-5):15-45 degrees of temperature, severe drying, enough water intake
  • Woogi (June-September):25-35 degrees of temperature, risk of flooding, and deterioration of road conditions
  • Harmatan:Desert sand breeze, respiratory protection required in December-February
  • ultraviolet rays:Sahara desert strong ultraviolet, sunscreen and protective clothing required
  • Daily crossroads:Desert area large crossover, warming supplies preparation
💰 Economic precautions
  • Cash Society:Credit cards cannot be used, enough cash preparation
  • Exchange:Preliminary exchange in Name, limited local currency exchange
  • price:Daily necessities are expensive and difficult to obtain
  • fuel:Lack of gasoline and frequent blackouts
