📅 Bocolo 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: Traditional Market and Culture Experience

Bocolo Central Market → Traditional Craft Village → Baobab Tree Forest → Traditional Food Experience

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the central market where you can experience the vivid daily lives of locals in Bocolo, a small town in Togo, West Africa, you can see vegetables, fruits, and traditional fabrics, observe wooden sculptures, weaving, and ceramics in the villages of craftsmen with traditional craftsmanship, and in the sacred forest of hundreds of years of huge baobab trees Feeling the grandeur of the great nature, and experiencing the culture of West Africa by tasting Togo traditional foods such as Pufu, Banku, and Palm Nut Soup made from traditional recipes in local homes.
📍Bocolo Central Market 📍 Traditional craft village 📍Baobab Forest 📍 Traditional food experience

Day 2: Natural Exploration and Village Life Experience

Savana grassland trekking → Traditional agricultural experience → Riverside rest → Traditional dance and music performance

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:In the characteristic meadow of the African Sabana Climate, we explore the natural ecosystem with guides, exploring the natural ecosystem, with local farmers, experiencing the cultivation process of major crops such as corn, cassava, and yam, learning traditional agricultural technologies, and observing the local water use culture of local people on the riverside that penetrates the village. In the evening, in the evening, you can enjoy the rich cultural heritage of Togo while enjoying the traditional dance performances of the EWANY and Kabi Tribes with traditional drum performances in the village plaza.

⚠️ Bocolo travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Major disease risks:Malaria, yellow fever, typhoids, hepatitis A

  • malaria:High risk of annual occurrence, required to take preventive drugs, and regular use of mosquito repellents
  • yellow fever:Request for vaccination, checking certificate of vaccination when entering Korea
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, avoid ice, fruits are peeled directly
  • food:Only intake of completely cooked food, street food attention
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Preliminary negotiations for buses or taxis, speeding driving attention
  • Road status:There are many unpaved roads, very bad road conditions during rainy season
  • Night movement:Prohibition of time travel late at night, risk of security
  • Motorcycle Taxi:Checking helmet wearing, request for safe driving
  • Rome connection:It takes about 4-5 hours to the capital Rome
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Dry (11-3):Gion temperature 25-35 degrees, dry and hot, Harmatan wind
  • Rainy (4-10):The temperature is 23-30 degrees, wet and rainfall
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat
  • dehydration:Risk of dehydration due to high temperature, enough water intake
  • Preparation for the rainy season:Umbrella, raincoat, waterproof bag preparation
💰 Economic precautions
  • Cash Society:Limited credit card use, enough cash preparation
  • Exchange:Exchange US dollars to CFA francs locally
  • Bargaining culture:Price bargaining in the market
  • Tip Culture:Provide the appropriate tips to the guide and the driver
