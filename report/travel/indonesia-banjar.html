⚠️ Banzar travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:At night, when driving scooters,

  • Scooter safety:Banja is a mountainous terrain and has a lot of steep slopes and curves
  • Night activity:Banja is a rural area, so there is a lack of night lighting, so recommended to return to accommodation before sunset
  • Water play safety:Wearing local guides and life jackets when swimming on waterfalls and lakes
  • Trekking Safety:The guide is required when trekking in the jungle, prohibit hiking alone
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Lack of public transportation:Banja recommends using personal vehicles or tours because public transportation is limited
  • Scooter Rent:7-10,000 rupiah a day, international driver's license requirement, duty to wear helmet
  • Road status:Due to the nature of the mountain area,
  • Rainy traffic:In the rainy season in October-April, the travel time increased due to deterioration of road conditions
  • Fuel confirmation:There is a rare gas station, so it is necessary to check the fuel status occasionally
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (4-10):20-30 degrees of temperature, low humidity, low waterfall quantity
  • Woogi (10-April):Temperature 22-28 degrees, high humidity and scall, waterfall optimal
  • Alpine Zone:Daily cross -dawn, dawn/chilly chilly, long -sleeved preparation
  • Hot Springs:The distinctive smell of sulfur components, sensitive skin attention
  • ultraviolet rays:UV strong, sunscreen is required due to the high altitude
💰 Price related precautions
  • Hot Springs admission:Foreigners 45,000 rupiah (about 4,500 won)
  • Guide Tour:A day tour of $ 50-150, individual/group tour price difference
  • Eating costs:Local Warong 2-50,000 Rupia, Tourist Restaurant 10-200,000 Rupiah
  • Accommodation cost:Guest House 20-5 million rupiah, resort 100-3 million rupiah
  • Transportation cost:Scooter Rent 7-10,000 Rupia/Sun, Personal Driver 50-800,000 Rupia/Sun
