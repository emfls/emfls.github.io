⚠️ Louwe Travel Note

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, cholera, bowl

  • malaria:Constant use of risk areas of the year, required to take preventive drugs, and regular use of mosquito repellents
  • yellow fever:Essential submitted when entering a vaccination certificate
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, avoid ice, fruits are peeled directly
  • Wildlife:In the Safari Tour, the guide order strictly observed
  • Emergency situation:Local hospital information confirmation in advance, and communicate with medical staff.
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, deterioration of road conditions
  • Public transportation:Overcrowism, determined rate check when using minibuses
  • taxi:In case of no meter, preliminary fee negotiations are required
  • Rent -a -car:4WD car recommended, international driver's license and local license required
  • Night movement:Avoiding as much as possible and using a reliable driver if it is inevitable
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (5-10):The temperature is 15-28 degrees, dry and clear, dusty
  • Woogi (11-4):20-30 degrees of temperature, wet and non-omin
  • Daily crossroads:Preparation of clothing for insulation because it is large
  • ultraviolet rays:UV strong, sunscreen is required due to the high altitude
  • Optimal visits:The best thing to travel in May-September
💰 Economic precautions
  • money:Zambia Kwacha (ZMW), the dollar is also widely used
  • Exchange:Banks or certified currency exchange centers, prohibit street currency exchange
