⚠️Sokcho travel precautions

🌊 Weather and naturalism

Main precautions:Due to the nature of the East Coast

  • Strong windism:Sokcho has a strong sea breeze, so be sure to take the outerwear
  • Daily crossroads:It can be chilly in the morning and evening, so a thin coat is required
  • ultraviolet rays:Sun cream and sunglasses required with strong sunlight in the east coast
  • Seoraksan Weather:Mountain weather changes in mountainous areas
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Peak season traffic jams:Summer and autumn autumn leaves are expected to be severe traffic jams
  • Bus dispatch interval:Use of bus apps because the interval between the city bus may be long.
  • Taxi tribes:Difficult to catch taxis on weekends evening or peak time
  • Rent -a -car reservation:In the peak season, you must make a reservation in advance
  • Parking problem:Possibility of parking lot of major tourist attractions
🏔️Cautions for climbing and outdoor activity
  • Seoraksan Cable Car:If the wind blows a lot, it will be stopped.
  • Climbing Preparation:Sufficient water and snacks, appropriate hiking boots required
  • National Park Regulations:Prohibition of importing garbage, required for environmental protection
  • Beach safety:Avoid bathing on the day of windy day
  • Sunset time:The sun is early, so you need to plan outdoor activities
🍽️ Food and accommodation precautions
  • Peak season accommodation:Summer and autumn maple foliage accommodation required in advance
  • Restaurant weighting:Famous restaurants may have long waiting time
  • Market visit time:The morning or late afternoon is less crowded
  • Seafood allergies:Sokcho Special Seafood Cookingism
  • Cash preparation:Some shops in the market are only cash.
