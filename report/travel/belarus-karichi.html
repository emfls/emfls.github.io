⚠️Carichi travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Late in the late night, rural areas with rare people

  • General security:Karichi is a relatively safe area, but be careful to go alone at night
  • Language Wall:There are few English speakers, so basic Russian or Belarusian needs
  • Emergency situation:Emergency rooms and hospitals are limited, so prepare enough
  • communication:Mobile phone signals may be weak in some areas
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:The bus operation interval and the timetable may be irregular
  • taxi:If you do not use the meter, you need to negotiate the preliminary fee.
  • Rent -a -car:The road signs are made of Kiril characters, so pre -learning is required.
  • Road status:Some rural roads can be incomplete in packaging
  • Winter driving:Riskism caused by eyes and ice roads
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -10 degrees to -20 degrees, very cold, so it is required for winter products
  • Summer (June-August):Cats of 15-25 degrees, gentle but sudden showers
  • Spring/Autumn:The crossover can be large and the wind can be strong
  • humidity:Unpleasant due to high humidity in summer
  • Sunshine time:Winter sunshine time is very short
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash use:Card payment is limited, so prepare enough cash
