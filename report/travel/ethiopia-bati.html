자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What is the main attraction of the Bati Monday Market?

The Bati Monday Market is Ethiopia's largest market where up to 20,000 people gather, showcasing a unique cultural intersection of the Amara, Oromo, and Afar peoples, with vibrant cattle and camel trading reflecting both desert and highland lifestyles.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect in Bati?

Travelers can experience the cultural convergence of the Amara region and the Oromian zone, observe traditional desert life of the Afar people, and enjoy historical sites like the Sabah Hotel, built by Italians in the 1930s, offering a cool mountain atmosphere.

What does the 3-day travel course in Bati include?

The 3-day itinerary includes Day 1 at Bati Monday Market and city tour, Day 2 exploring nature and history at Anneva Forest, Degan Village, and Gerba, and opportunities to dine at local restaurants and stay overnight in Bati.
