Major areas:Around Iksan Station, some parts of the old city center late at night

  • Personal belongings:Bag zipper lock at tourist attractions, valuables are in close contact with the body
  • Vehicle use:When parking, prohibits the exposure of valuables in the vehicle, thoroughly in the paragraph
  • Night going out:Be careful to go alone after 10 pm of Iksan Station and some old towns
  • Emergency situation:112 (Police), 119 (Fire Department) Fire
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Iksan City Tour Reservation System Operation, Pre -Reservation Required
  • City Bus:Recommended for using transportation cards, preparation for change when riding cash
  • Taxi:About 10,000 won from Iksan Station to downtown
  • KTX connection:Connect Seoul (1 hour 30 minutes) and Busan (2 hours) from Iksan Station

  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, sunscreen from spring to autumn
