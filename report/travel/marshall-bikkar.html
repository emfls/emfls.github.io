⚠️ Bikar travel precautions

🚨 Note that safety and emergency situations

Main precautions:Lack of medical facilities, restriction on response to emergency situations

  • Medical facilities:Very limited and serious emergencies in local medical facilities need to be transferred to Guam or Hawaii
  • Marine safety:The possibility of strong birds and sharks, always active with guides
  • communication:Unstable on the Internet and telephone connections, preparing satellite communication equipment
  • Risk of isolation:The possibility of long -term stay due to cancellation of flights in case of deterioration of weather
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • flight:AIR Marshall Islands, and delayed by weather
  • Move in the island:Walking or using bicycles, there are few public transportation
  • Boat movement:Use small boats when moving between islands, requiring weather and algae confirmation
  • fuel:Fuel supply instability, the movement plan is carefully established
  • Directions:GPS signal weakness, local guide recommendation
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (5-November):Typhoon and heavy rain risks, travel plans carefully established
  • Dry (12-4):Temperature 24-30 degrees, high humidity, sufficient moisture intake
  • typhoon:Sudden weather change during the typhoon season
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and protective clothing near the equator
  • Sea level rise:Influence of sea level rise due to climate change
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:Price is very expensive due to dependence on imports of goods
  • foodstuffs:Lack of fresh food, canned food and frozen food
  • Fuel cost:Expensive electricity bills due to the high generator fuel cost
  • Cash payment:Limited credit card use, enough cash preparation
