Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In Garden Route's core city of Mosel Bay, in 1488, Bartholomeu Dias experienced the caravel ship when Bartolo Meu Dias discovered the Hope Peak, left a special letter in the first shoe box in South Africa, relaxed with a seagull on the Golden Beach in the Indian Ocean, and a beautiful coastline panoramic at the Cape St. Blaze Lighthouse. Day 2: Safari and Wildlife Experience

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:In the 4,200 hectares of Bottlerscov Private Game Protection Zone, 26 kinds of wild animals, including rhino, lion, giraffe, buffalo, zebra, and elephant, experience the heaven of bird observers who can meet 200 kinds of birds, and 3 hours of game drive with a professional guide in an open game viewing vehicle and 3.5 -hour sunset game Enjoy the essence of the wild Africa with the drive

🚗 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Left traffic:South Africa is noted when driving on the left or car rental
  • Road status:Garden Root N2 road is good, but wildlifeism during night driving
  • Fuel supply:Supply enough fuel before long distance moving, checking the operating hours of gas stations on Sunday
  • Public transportation:Encourage bus operation irregularities, rental cars or tours recommend
  • Cape Town Connection:4-5 hours by car from Mosel Bay to Cape Town
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (12-2):The temperature of 15-28 degrees, dry, hot, ultraviolet rays very strong
  • Winter (June-August):The temperature is 8-20 degrees, mild, but cold and rainy at night
  • Spring/Autumn:Daily cross -crossed and windy, outerwear required
  • Indian Ocean Current:Attention at swimming because the water temperature may be low
  • Dry climate:Eat sufficient water and use moisturizer
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Mosel Bay is not a malaria dangerous area, but it is necessary to prevent the visit to Kruger National Park
  • drinking water:It is possible to drink tap water but recommend bottled water
  • Marine Activity:Safety rules for shark diving thoroughly observed
  • Altitude disease:Sufficient rest for highland activities such as dragon dunes
  • Solar disease:Wearing hats and replenishing moisture during safari
