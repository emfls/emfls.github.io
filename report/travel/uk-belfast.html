What are the main highlights of the Belfast travel course on Day 1?

Day 1 of the Belfast travel course includes the Black Cab Tour to learn about political murals and the city's division history, viewing the Grand Opera House exterior, a free inner tour of Belfast City Hall, exploring the Ulster Museum with exhibits like the Northern Ireland Dinosaur, Egypt Mummy, Troubles Exhibition, and Victorian Gallery, followed by a relaxing walk in the Botanic Garden.

How long should I allocate for the Day 1 itinerary in Belfast?

The recommended time to complete the Day 1 itinerary is approximately 9 hours, allowing ample time to explore each site thoroughly and enjoy the cultural and historical experiences.

What preparations or precautions should travelers consider for visiting Belfast?

Travelers should prepare for a mix of outdoor and indoor activities, dress comfortably for walking tours, and be ready to engage with Belfast’s complex political history through the Black Cab Tour. Booking tours in advance and checking opening hours for museums and landmarks is advisable.
