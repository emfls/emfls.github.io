⚠️Take travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Mekong River Night, Loan Cave Area

  • Motorcycle safety:Mandatory helmet wearing when driving loops, accompanying speed restrictions
  • Cave exploration:Enter with a guide, wear head lanterns and life jackets
  • Night going out:Be careful to go alone after 9 o'clock in the Mekong riverside
  • Cash management:The large amount is stored in the accommodation, only a small amount
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Motorcycle rental:International driver's license required, insurance coverage confirmation
  • Road status:In the rainy season, some sections can be flooded, and the weather is confirmed after checking the weather
  • Fuel supply:Lack of gas station in the middle of the Takek loop, checking fuel in advance
  • Thai border:Nakon Pannom connection bus 4 times a day (9 o'clock, 11 o'clock, 14 o'clock, 16 o'clock)
  • Vientian connection:Direct bus takes about 11 hours twice a day
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):Meeting 15-30 degrees, cave tour optimal period
  • Woogi (May-October):Temperature 25-35 degrees, humid and scallop
  • october:The best time to travel to the rainy season, less and cool
  • Jungle area:Many mosquitoes and insects, insect repellent
  • Inside the cave:Cool and humid, light coat is prepared throughout the year
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cave admission fee:50,000 kip, beanor cave 65,000 kip
