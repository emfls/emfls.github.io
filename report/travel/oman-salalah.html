📅 Salala 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: History and Culture Exploration

Salala Suk → Al Huson Palace → Mirbat → Francine Sense Land

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:In Dopar, Salala, a traditional Arab market, Salala Sook, a traditional Arab market, experiences Francine Sense, spices, and traditional crafts, enjoys the Oman Royal Culture in Alfuson Palace, the summer palace of Sultan Kabus, and explores the 16th -century Portuguese fortress and traditional fishing village in Mirbat, an ancient Francine sense trade port. At the World Heritage Site Francine Sense Land, the history of ancient flavor trade and the Boswellia tree farm
📍Salala suk 📍Alhuson Palace 📍 Mirbat 📍 Francine Sense Land

Day 2: Nature and Beach Experience

Wadi Darbat → Ein Large → Al Balid Beach → Marnev Cave

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:At the Wadi Darbat Falls, which can only be seen in the Monsoon season, the Arabian Peninsula's only waterfall and emerald lake, the oasis and palm farms with the groundwater rising from the natural hot spring Ain Laji, and enjoy the coconut palms and the Turkish sea cave at the Albalid Beach, the white beach of the Arabian Sea. Exploration of mysterious underground sea and natural fountains that change according to the assistant in the Marnev Cave
📍 Waddarbat 📍Ain Large 📍Al Balid Beach 📍Marnnev Cave

Day 3: Desert and Mountain Exploration (optional)

Louval Harley Desert Tour → Zhang Kara → Taka → Salala Museum

Louval Harley Desert:It takes 4 hours, red sand dunes and Bedouin cultural experience in the world's largest continuous desert
Zhang Kara:It takes 3 hours, appreciates Monsoon Green Scenery and Baobab Tree at 1,000m above sea level
Taka:Takes 2 hours, ancient Frankin Sense Trade City Ruins and Arabian Sea Prospects
Salala Museum:It takes 1 hour, Dopar region history and Francine sense trade exhibition

  • Public transportation:Restricted city bus operation, recommend using taxi or car rental

  • drive:Right traffic, speed limit
