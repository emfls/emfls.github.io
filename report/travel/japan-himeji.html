⚠️ Himeji travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Himeji area, near tourist attractions, night alley

  • pickpocket:In station, shopping streets, and tourist spots, the bag is carried forward and the zipper lock
  • Inside:Taking off the shoes and entering the stairs, the stairs are very steep and slippery, so socks, dressing attention
  • Drone shooting:Himeji Castle Commercial Drone Flight and Shooting (Violation of Cultural Property Protection Act)
  • The elderly/children:The cheonseo staircase is narrow and high, so be careful when moving
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transport strike/delay:Japan is rare, but trains/bus delays,
  • Transportation card:Nationally compatible transportation cards such as icoca and suica can be used
  • Taxi fee:The basic 700 yen ~, the meter check is required
  • parking lot:Many paid parking lots in the city, confirmed when using private use
  • Walking:Last walking, such as sex, garden, museum, etc.
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Spring/Autumn:Pleasant weather, large -day crossing, thin outerwear preparation
  • Summer (June -August):25 ~ 33 degrees, moist, hot, water intake required
  • Winter (December -February):2 ~ 10 degrees, snow is rare but the wind is strong
  • Cherry blossoms/autumn leaves:Tourist Boom Boom Beanie/Traffic Pre -Reservation Mandatory
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Near the castle, the price of shopping restaurants 1.5 ~ 2 times expensive
  • entrance fee:Himeji Castle 1,000 yen, Gokoen 310 yen, integrated ticket 1,050 yen
