🚨 DMZ related precautions

Major restrictions:Inside DMZ, around military facilities

  • Pre -booking:DMZ Tour is a must -see reservation required, not visited on the day.
  • Bring your ID:Resident registration card or driver's license must be brought and passports are also available.
  • Prohibition of shooting:Military facilities and some district shooting in the DMZ absolute prohibition
  • Dress Regulations:Wearing a neat dress, prohibiting slippers or sandals
  • No drinking:DMZ Tour The Day of Drinking
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:2 hours from Seoul to Cheorwon, 4 times a day
  • Rent -a -car:About 1 hour 20 minutes from Seoul to Cheorwon, enough parking space
  • Local bus:Movement between tourist attractions in Cheorwon is recommended for taxis or car rentals
  • Road situation:In winter, Ice -Pan -Gil Caution, Chain Preparation recommended
  • Parking fee:Most of them are free parking, some paid sections
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):Temperature 20-30 degrees, moist and hot, cool near the waterfall
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -10 ~ 5 degrees, very cold, waterfall freezing
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and windy day crossovers, the outerwear
  • Woogi (July-August):Restrictions on access to waterfalls in heavy rain
  • Fine dust:Spring yellow dust and fine dust attention
💰 Price related precautions
