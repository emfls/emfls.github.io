⚠️ Wonju travel precautions

🚨 Safety and precautions

Major areas:Chiaksan Climbing Road, Salt Mountain Rock Bridge, Valley area

  • Climbing safety:Pay attention to changes in the weather when climbing Chiaksan, prohibiting the departure of trails, preparing for sufficient moisture and snacks
  • Rock Legs:Comply with safety rules when using the salted bridge, prohibit running or shaking
  • Valley safety:Watch out for water, watch out to be slippery when using Ganhyeon tourist attraction valley
  • Wildlife:The possibility of wild animals such as wild boars and snakes in the mountainous areas
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:When using Wonju City buses, use T-Money cards, prepare change when paying cash
  • Taxi:Taxi fare up to tourist attractions, check use meters
  • Rent -a -car:Preparation of the chain in winter when driving on the mountainous roads
  • parking:Popular attractions such as Museum Mountain and Salt Mountain Rock Bridge are limited to parking spaces.
  • City Tour Bus:Stop on Monday, Museum Monday Monday Closed
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):Temperature management in the temperature of 25-30 degrees, moist, hot, valley water play
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -10 ~ 5 degrees, very cold, winter supplies required
  • Spring/Autumn:It is recommended to wear a big and even more severe and overlapping mountains.
  • Gangwon -do characteristics:The temperature is 5-10 lower than Seoul, sudden weather changes
  • Mountain Weather:The higher the altitude, such as Chiaaksan and Salt Mountain, the higher the temperature.
