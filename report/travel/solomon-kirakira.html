🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • flight:Kirakira Airport can be stopped in small scale and deterioration of weather.
  • Local transportation:When using taxi or buses, check in advance, no meter
  • Boat use:When moving between islands, safety equipment wearing, weather status check
  • Road status:There are many unpaved roads and traffic difficulties in rainy season
  • Rent -a -car:International driver's license required

  • Woogi (11-4):Strong scalls and cyclone risks, weather information before travel check

  • Marine weather:Snorkeling or diving before waves and algae status
  • earthquake:Located in the Pacific Rim Earthquake, Tsunami Caution in the event of an earthquake
