⚠️Precipalism

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever, bowl

  • malaria:Essential to take preventive drugs as mosquito -mediated diseases, wear long -sleeved clothes and mosquito repellents
  • yellow fever:Implied vaccination before entering the country, bringing in a certificate of inoculation
  • drinking water:Drink only bottled water and avoid ice, raw fruit and vegetable attention
  • hygiene:Wash your hands thoroughly and avoid street food
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road situation:Many unpaved roads other than N6 roads, deterioration of road conditions during rainy season
  • Public transportation:It is recommended to use a taxi or car rental because it is limited
  • Taxi:Meter check and preliminary negotiations
  • Night movement:Avoid as much as possible and recommend the daytime travel
  • fuel:Preparation of fuel because it is limited to gas stations
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Woogi (October-May):Occupation, high humidity, raincoats are required
  • Dry (June-September):Relatively dry but still wet
  • Temperature:22-32 degrees throughout the year
  • humidity:Very high at 77-82%per year, dehydration
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, UV protection required near the equator
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:The price of daily necessities is high due to high dependence on imports
  • Cash use:Limited credit card use, fully prepared for cash
  • Exchange:Using banks or official currency exchange, avoiding street currency exchange
  • Tip Culture:Tips in the service industry, about 10%
