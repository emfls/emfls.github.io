⚠️Aruro travel precautions

🚨 altitude disease and healthism

Altitude altitude:Oururo is 3,700m above sea level

  • History symptoms:Headache, nausea, dizziness, shortness of breath, etc.
  • preventive:On the first day of arrival, prohibition of radical activity, sufficient water intake, coca leaf tea drinking
  • Emergency situation:In case of severe altitude disease, immediately move low and visit the hospital
  • An alcoholic ban:Drinking in alpine areas worsens alpine disease
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Daily crossroads:Daily crossover 20 degrees and minus 5 degrees at night
  • Dryness:It is extremely dried, less than 20% humidity, and requires enough water intake
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong UV rays, sunscreen and sunglasses required
  • Woogi (12-3):Sudden showers, hail, and umbrella preparation
  • Dry (4-November):Recommended dust and wind strong, mask recommendation
