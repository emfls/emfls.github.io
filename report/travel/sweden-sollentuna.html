⚠️Solene Tuna travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the station, late at night, residential and rare parks

  • Pickpocket / theft:Respectance of belongings in station, shopping malls, and tourist attractions
  • Night movement:Be careful to go alone after 10 pm, avoid rare people
  • Prohibition of neglect of goods in the vehicle:Do not leave valuables in the vehicle
  • Local kind use:When you get lost, contact the station attendant and police
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:15 ~ 20 minutes by train from Stockholm Central Station, use SL Transportation Card
  • Bicycle · Kickboard:Can be used for shared bicycles and kickboards, compliance with traffic laws
  • Taxi fee:Meter check required, official taxi use
  • Winter road freezing:Wearing Anti -Ibing Panism, Anti -Sids Prevention Shoes
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):On average, 17 ~ 20 degrees, day crossing, umbrella compared to showers
  • Winter (12-2):Average -2 ~ 1 degrees, snow and ice, and winter clothes required
  • Spring/Autumn:5 ~ 15 degrees, sudden rain, preparation
  • Long White Night/Short Comrade:The sun is late in the summer, and the sunshine time is short in winter
  • ultraviolet rays:Summer sunscreen required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Shopping mall and tourist attraction cafes, restaurant prices are high
  • Museum entrance fee:Edsbury Castle Free, some exhibitions
  • Tip Culture:The tip is not compulsory, 5-10% autonomous when the service is satisfied in a restaurant or hotel
  • Card payment:Most of the card payments, cash only prepares a small amount for emergency
