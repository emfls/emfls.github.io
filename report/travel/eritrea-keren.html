What is the recommended itinerary for a 3-day trip to Keren, Eritrea?

The recommended itinerary for a 2-night, 3-day trip to Keren includes spending Day 1 exploring the Keren Market, St. Antonio Cathedral, Barentu Square, and the Keren Museum, focusing on history and culture with an estimated 8 hours of activities.

What cultural and historical sites are must-see in Keren?

Must-see sites in Keren include the lively traditional Keren Market, the St. Antonio Cathedral showcasing Italian colonial-era architecture, Barentu Square which is the city center reflecting daily local life, and the Keren Museum that highlights the traditional culture of the Viren and Kunama peoples as well as Eritrea's independence struggle.

What should travelers prepare for when visiting Keren, Eritrea?

Travelers should prepare for a culturally immersive experience that includes walking through busy markets, appreciating a unique blend of African and European architecture, and learning about local traditions and Eritrea’s independence history. Planning for an 8-hour day of exploration and appropriate travel precautions is advised.
