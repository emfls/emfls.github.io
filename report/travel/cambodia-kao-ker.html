Recommended time:10 hours

Observe everyday life, rice fields, and the beautiful scenery of traditional houses

⚠️Kaoker travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Borders, secluded sites, late night rural areas

  • Mine risk:Do not get out of the displayed road, and prohibit exploration alone without a guide
  • pickpocket:Put a zipper lock in front of a bag in tourist attractions and markets
  • Fake guide:Check that the guide is officially approved, and require ID card
  • Night movement:Late time to move to a secluded area
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • 4 -wheel drive required:Recommended for using a four -wheel drive vehicle for access to Kaoker
  • Road status:Unpaved roads, rugged mountain roads, and traffic difficulties
  • Guide Tour:Recommended for professional guided tours rather than individual trips
  • Fuel preparation:Preparation of sufficient fuel and preliminary fuel when moving long distances
  • Communication equipment:Mountain mobile phone signal weakness, consideration of satellite phones
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):Temperature 25-35 degrees, very hot, dry, enough water intake
  • Woogi (May-October):Temperature 24-30 degrees, moist and heavy rain, and worsening road condition
  • Tropical Climate:High humidity and strong ultraviolet rays
  • Mosquito activity:Many mosquitoes throughout the year, malaria and dengue fever
  • heatstroke:Rest is required in the shade for a long time outdoor activities
