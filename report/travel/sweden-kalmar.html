The main attractions in the Kalmar travel course include Kalmar Castle, known as the best Renaissance castle in the Nordic region built in the 12th century, the 17th-century Baroque Kalmar Cathedral, the historic Karun Holmen Old Town, and the Kalmar Art Museum showcasing modern Swedish art.

Kalmar Castle is a significant Renaissance castle built in the 12th century, representing the historic city of Småland and the Kalmar Union’s history, as well as the palace culture of King Gustav Vasa.
