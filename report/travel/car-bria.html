⚠️ Bria travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Main precautions:All areas, armed group activities, extremely dangerous security situations

  • Armed group:Conflict between armed groups such as Seleka and Antivalaca, civilian damage occurred
  • Wagner Group:Human rights violations of Russian mercenaries and massacre of civilians
  • Night traffic prohibition:Absolutely prohibited to go out after sunset, active armed group activities
  • UN Camp:Emergency evacuation with the UN Peaceful Camp in case of emergency
  • Local guide:Accompanied by a reliable local guide
🏥 Health and medical precautions
  • malaria:High risk of occurrence of year, required for preventive drugs
  • yellow fever:Required vaccination required, confirming certificate when entering Korea
  • drinking water:Bottled water only drinking, integer filter recommendation
  • Medical facilities:Need to transfer overseas in case of extremely poor medical facility and serious emergency situations
  • Hygiene status:Overall hygiene, poorly managed personal hygiene
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Most of them are unpaved roads, no traffic in rainy season
  • Public transportation:There are few public transportation facilities, use of personal or chartered vehicles
  • flight:Bria airport flight irregularities and cancellation according to weather conditions
  • fuel:Unstable fuel supply, need to secure sufficient fuel
  • checkpoint:Many of the armed group checkpoints, ID cards and documents always carry
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):Temperature 25-40 degrees, very hot and dry
  • Woogi (May-October):20-30 degrees of temperature, road passage due to heavy rains
  • Harmatan:Dry wind blowing from the Sahara Desert in December-February
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet and sunscreen required throughout the year
  • dehydration:Risk of dehydration due to high temperature, enough water intake
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash payment:Most of them are limited to cash payment, credit card use
  • Price level:Basic daily necessities are expensive and difficult to obtain
  • Fuel cost:Fuel price is very high
  • Accommodation fee:High accommodation expenses due to limited accommodations
