Essential vaccination:Hwangyeol disease (15 days before departure), taking malaria prevention drugs

  • yellow fever:At least 15 days before traveling in Retisia
  • malaria:After consulting with a doctor, taking malaria prevention drugs, thoroughly preventing mosquitoes
  • Dengue:Amazon area dengue fever can occur, mosquito repellents required
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, avoid drinks with ice
  • food:Eat only enough food, raw fruit peeled and eat

  • Toxic plants:Do not touch plants without guide instructions, many toxic plants
