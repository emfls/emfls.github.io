Main risk factors:Malaria, sleep disease, crocodile, tropical disease

  • malaria:Constant use of risk areas of the year, required to take preventive drugs, and regular use of mosquito repellents
  • Tanganica Lake:No swimming in a crocodile format, only water play in designated safety zones
  • drinking water:Only drinking water, avoid ice, raw fruitism
  • yellow fever:Request for vaccination, testing certificate when entering Korea
  • sleeping sickness:Recommended for wearing long -sleeved arms

  • Road status:In the rainy season, the road condition is poor, 4WD car recommended
