Day 1: Verification of Panda City and Culture Exploration

St. Joseph Cathedral → Panda Port → Local Market → Explore colonial buildings

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the Hapi Islands' administrative center, explores an attractive village on the west coast of Ricuka, experiences the religious culture of Tongga in St. Joseph Cathedral (Shiashi Catolika) in the center of the village, observes the daily lives of local fishermen in the port along the Holopepeca Road, and traces of history through traditional barrels and colonial buildings. Discover, visit fresh agricultural products and handicrafts in the local market and exchange with locals who are friendly.

East beach → snorkeling → traditional fishing experience → Coast walk

⚠️Pan Gai Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Late at night, around the port, a secluded beach

  • General security:Tongga is relatively safe, but be careful to go alone at night
  • Marine safety:Do not go to the sea alone with local guides during snorkeling.
  • Emergency situation:Because medical facilities are limited,
  • Natural disasters:Cyclone Season (November-April) Check the weather information occasionally
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • flight:30 minutes as a small aircraft from Nukualopa to Pan Guy
  • perry:Monday and Tuesday from Nukualopa, 7-12 hours
  • Local transportation:Limited to taxi and car rental, walking main
  • Island between islands:Boat tour reservation required, canceling according to the weather
  • Airport access:5km from Pandai to Pillore Buve Airport, use taxi
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (5-10):20-26 degrees of temperature, low humidity and comfortable
  • Woogi (11-4):Temperature 24-30 degrees, wet and cyclone risk
  • ultraviolet rays:Very strong, sunscreen and hat required
  • Marine weather:Participation in marine activities after checking the wind and waves status
  • Tropical Climate:Preparation of umbrella for sudden showers
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:The price of daily necessities is high due to dependence on imported goods
  • Cash -oriented:Limited credit card use, fully prepared for cash
  • Tip Culture:Service Tips 10-15% customs
  • Exchange:Only one exchanged exchange in Nukualopa, only one Pan Guy Bank
