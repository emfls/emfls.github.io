📅 Arden Recommended Travel Course

Day 1: Nature and History Exploration

Dinang Village → Han Cave → Bastonew War Museum → Arden Forest Walk

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the picturesque dinang village on the Mews River, we appreciate medieval architecture and citadel, explore the stalactite and underground rivers in a mysterious cave, and experience the history of the Battle of World War II at the Bastonew War Museum. In the afternoon, enjoy hiking and nature in the dense Arden Forest.
📍Dinang 📍Han Cave 📍Bastonho War Museum 📍Arden Forest

Day 2: Middle Ages and Village Tour

Buiyon Poetry → Francsimon Castle → La Rocheen Arden Castle → Mal Medi Festival Experience

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:He strolls on Buion's medieval and alleys, soaks up in history, learns the Middle Ages in Francimon, and feels the magnificence of Goseong in La Rocheen Arden. In the spring, you can participate in the traditional festival in Mal Medi to experience the culture of Arden.
📍 Buayon 📍 Francimon Castle 📍La Rocheen Arden 📍Mal Medi

Day 3: Outdoor activities and rest

Spa Hot Spring → Anble River Kayak → Starblo Bicycle Tour → Local Food Experience

Recommended time:7 hours
Core points:Enjoy natural hot springs at the spa, experience kayaks in the Anbleb River, and enjoy nature along the Bike path around the Starblo. In the evening, take a break with local Walonia cuisine and trapist beer.
📍 Spa 📍Ableb River 📍 Starblo 📍 Orval Monastery

⚠️Arden travel precautions

🚨 security and safety
  • Recommended for wearing and wearing safety equipment and guides during outdoor activities
  • Avoid the remote place late at night
  • Pickpocketism, especially around tourist destinations and markets
🚌 Transportation and movement
  • Preliminary confirmation of public transportation schedules and operating conditions
  • Familiar with road conditions and parking regulations when using a car rental
  • Mountain roads are narrow and winding, so be careful
🌡️ Weather
  • Spring and autumn are large and rainy and raining frequently, umbrellas and outerwear preparation
  • Summer is mild, but mountainous areas can be chilly in the morning and evening.
  • Winter can control some trails due to snow and ice
💰 Price and cost
  • Restaurants and cafes around tourist attractions may be somewhat high.
  • Recommended for fresh agricultural products and cheese in the local market
  • Tip culture is about 5-10% recommended in the restaurant
