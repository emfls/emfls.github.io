📅 Bejaia 2-Night 3-Day Travel Course

Day 1: History and Coastal Culture Exploration

Bejaia Port → Kasbah → Sidi Touati Mosque → Bejaia Beach

Recommended Duration:9 hours
Key Points:Experience the history of North African maritime trade at Bejaia Port facing the Mediterranean Sea. Admire the unique architectural style blending Berber and Arab cultures at the Kasbah fortress built during the Ottoman Empire era. Explore the beauty of Islamic architecture and Bejaia's role as a religious center at the 16th-century Sidi Touati Mosque. Enjoy the distinctive North African coastal scenery with the blue Mediterranean Sea and white sand at Bejaia Beach.
📍 Bejaia Port 📍 Kasbah 📍 Sidi toister mosque 📍 Bejaia Beach

Day 2: Nature and Scenic Experience

Gouraya National Park → Pic des Singes → Bejaia Museum → Old Town Market

Recommended Duration:10 hours
Key Points:Explore North Africa's unique ecosystem with Berber monkeys and various Mediterranean plants at Gouraya National Park. Admire the spectacular views of Bejaia city and Mediterranean coast from Pic des Singes at 660m elevation. Understand Bejaia's multilayered history through Berber culture, Roman artifacts, and Islamic art at Bejaia Museum. Experience local daily culture and try Algerian specialties including Berber handicrafts, spices, and olive oil at the old town traditional market.
📍 Gouraya National Park 📍 Pic des Singes 📍 Bejaia Museum 📍 Old Town Market

Day 3: Nearby Exploration and Coastal Experience (Optional)

Jijel → Tizi → Souma → Corniche Coastal Road

Jijel:3 hours, Experience Kabylia culture and mountain scenery in traditional Berber village
Tizi:4 hours, Explore historic city where ancient Roman ruins and Berber fortresses coexist
Souma:2 hours, Experience traditional fishing culture in small Mediterranean coastal fishing village
Corniche Coastal Road:1 hour, Panoramic drive of cliffs and sea along Bejaia coastline
📍 Jijel 📍 Tizi 📍 Souma 📍 Corniche Coastal Road

⚠️ Bejaia Travel Precautions

🚨 Safety and Security Precautions

Main caution areas:Old town at night, outskirts, sparsely populated beaches

  • PickPocketing:Beware of pickpockets at markets and tourist spots, carry bags in front and zip them up
  • Night outings:Avoid traveling alone after sunset, especially female travelers
  • Photography:Absolutely prohibited to photograph military facilities, government buildings, and police stations
  • Alcohol:Islamic country with public drinking prohibited, only allowed inside hotels
🚌 Transportation and Travel Precautions
  • Public transport:Irregular and crowded bus service, taxi recommended
  • Taxi fares:Check meter use or negotiate fare in advance
  • Rental cars:International driving license required, mountain road driving experience needed
  • Algiers connection:About 3 hours by bus from Bejaia to Algiers
  • Mountain terrain:Guide recommended when traveling to mountain areas like Gouraya National Park
🌡️ Weather Precautions
  • Summer (Jun-Aug):Temperatures 25-35°C, very hot and dry, stay hydrated
  • Winter (Dec-Feb):Temperatures 10-18°C, mild but frequent rain
  • Spring/Fall:Large temperature differences and strong winds, prepare outerwear
  • Mediterranean climate:Dry summers and mild winters
  • UV rays:Strong North African sunlight, sunscreen and hat essential
💰 Price-related Precautions
  • Tourist price gouging:Beware of inflated prices for foreigners, bargaining essential
  • Museum entrance fees:Bejaia Museum 200 Dinars, Gouraya National Park 500 Dinars
  • Tipping culture:10% at restaurants, 1000-2000 Dinars for guides
  • Cash payments:Mostly cash payments, limited credit card use

💡 Bejaia Travel Tips

  • Free attractions:Bejaia Beach, Kasbah exterior, old town street walks are free
  • Berber food:Must try North African traditional dishes like couscous, tagine, brik, and mint tea
  • Best visiting time:April-June and September-November are best for weather and sightseeing
  • Language:Arabic and Berber are official languages, French is also widely used
  • Berber culture:Experience unique Berber culture and traditions of the Kabylia region
  • Islamic culture:Follow dress code when visiting mosques, avoid prayer times
  • Mediterranean culture:Unique cultural fusion where North Africa meets the Mediterranean
  • Local market:Most active Friday mornings, buy fresh produce and handicrafts
