⚠️ Monthenes travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever, Zika virus

  • malaria:Start taking prevention drugs two weeks before departure, thoroughly prevention of mosquito bites
  • yellow fever:Immigration for immigration, can be entered after 10 days after vaccination
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, avoid ice, fruits are peeled directly
  • Wound management:Small wounds are immediately disinfected and risk of infection
  • Emergency situation:It can take hours for the nearest medical facilities
🐍 Wildlife precautions
  • venomous snake:Beware of fatal viper such as Bush Master and Ferdance
  • jaguar:Rarely, but special attention for formats and night activities
  • Piranha:Strictly observed local guides when swimming in the river
  • Poison copper:Never touch the gorgeous frogs
  • Spider/Scorpion:Be sure to check before wearing shoes and clothes
  • mosquito:Disease medium, insect repellent occasionally
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Woogi (April-July):Heavy rain and flood risk, river level surge
  • Dry (8-November):Forest fire risk, dehydration
  • humidity:80-90% high humidity, mold prevention
  • Temperature:25-32 degrees all year round, less than daily but high temperature temperature
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays and sun creams near the equator
🗺️ Traffic and communication precautions
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, unable to pass in the rainy season
  • Mobile phone signal:Signals are lost in the jungle deep inside
  • First aid:Satellite phones or satellite communications required
  • fuel:Gas station spacing, extra fuel preparation
  • Guide required:Absolutely prohibited jungle exploration alone
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:2-3 times more expensive living expenses than the mainland of France
  • Guide cost:Professional jungle guide 150-300 euro a day
  • Equipment rental:Trekking equipment, canoe, etc. can be rented, but expensive
