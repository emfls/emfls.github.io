What are the main attractions included in the Kuwait Alara travel itinerary?

The main attractions on the Day 1 itinerary include Alzara Oasis, Alzara Traditional Market, Alzara Fort, and the Kuwait Agricultural Museum, which together showcase the natural desert oasis, local spices and handicrafts, Bedouin culture, historical defense sites, and agricultural practices.

How long is the recommended tour for exploring Alzara and its cultural sites?

The recommended tour duration for the Day 1 itinerary is approximately 8 hours, allowing travelers to thoroughly explore the oasis, market, fortress, and museum.

Travelers can experience the mysterious natural landscape of the desert oasis, explore traditional Bedouin culture through local spices and handicrafts at the market, learn about 18th-century northern Kuwait defense history at Alzara Fort, and understand Kuwait's agricultural heritage at the Agricultural Museum.
