What are the main cultural sites to visit in Osogbo according to the travel guide?

The main cultural sites include the Oshovo Palace, Oshovo Central Mosque, and the Oshobo Market, with a special focus on the Osun Osogbo Sacred Grove, a UNESCO World Heritage Site representing traditional Yoruba religion and culture.

The recommended time to explore the key sites on Day 1, including the palace, mosque, and market, is approximately 8 hours to fully appreciate the cultural and historical significance.

Travelers can experience the rich Yoruba royal culture, traditional architecture, the spiritual ambiance of the Osun Osogbo Sacred Grove, West African Islamic heritage at the mosque, and local daily life through traditional crafts at the market.
