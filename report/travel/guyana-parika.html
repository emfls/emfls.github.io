⚠️ Parisa travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around Parisa Port, late at night, riverside area

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing in the port and the market, the bag is carried in the future and the zipper lock
  • robbery:Don't go alone in the night, and cash is distributed
  • fraud:Fake tour guide or boat driverism that requires excessive rates
  • Wildlife:Possibility of dangerous animals such as viper and jaguar in tropical rainforests
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Perry:Essecibo River ferry can be stopped depending on the weather
  • Road status:Difficult to move because the roads are muddy during the rainy season
  • Boat safety:Wearing life jackets for steel cruise
  • Lack of fuel:Frequent fuel shortages occur in inland areas
  • GPS signal:GPS signal weakness in the rainforest area, local guide required
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (May-August):Flood and traffic paralysis caused by heavy rain
  • Dry (September-April):Strong sunlight and high humidity, dehydration
  • Sudden shower:Sudden heavy rains due to tropical climate characteristics
  • High humidity:More than 80% of humidity, mold caution
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays and sun creams near the equator
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:High risk of mosquito -mediated diseases, required to take preventive drugs
  • yellow fever:Can't enter the country without a certificate of vaccination
  • Dengue:Use of mosquito repellents and long -sleeved wear
  • drinking water:Prohibition of drinking tap water, only bottled water intake
  • Medical facilities:Lack of medical facilities in Parika, transfer to George Town in case of emergency
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:The price of daily necessities is high due to import dependence
  • Tour cost:200-300 USD per person for Kai Tour Falls Tour
  • Accommodation fee:The price is high with limited accommodations
  • Transportation cost:Need to negotiate boat rates with ferry
