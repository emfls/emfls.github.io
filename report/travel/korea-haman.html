Enjoying the beauty of the beauty of Hammijeong and enjoying the perfect harmony of the traditional architecture of Haman and the natural landscape.

⚠️ Haman travel precautions

🚨 Safety precautions

Major areas:Yeohang Mountain Trail, around the reservoir reservoir

  • Climbing safety:Sufficient preparation and climbing experience when climbing Yeohang Mountain (770m) needs to experience, start after checking the weather
  • Water play safety:Wearing safety equipment in water leisure reservoirs
  • Tomb Mounds:Only use roads designated to protect cultural property, and prohibit climbing tombs
  • Night Festival:Maintaining a fire safety distance during the Nakhwa Nori Festival, special attention when accompanied by children
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Haman is a rural area, so there is a long interval for bus dispatching.
  • Rent -a -car recommendation:The distance between major tourist destinations is far from the recommendation of a car or a car rental
  • parking:Free parking lots in Malcosan Tombs and Ipokgokgun Park
  • Public transportation connection:Masan (30 minutes), Changwon (40 minutes), Busan (1 hour 30 minutes) bus connection
  • taxi:Due to the nature of rural areas, it may be a taxi waiting time
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):Temperature 25-35 degrees, very hot and humid, enough water intake and shade rest
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -5-10 degrees, required for winter products when watching the burial mounds
  • Spring/Autumn:Daily crossing, dressed in body temperature control when climbing
  • Rainy season:Restriction of outdoor activities during heavy rains in June-July
  • Fine dust:Outdoor activities after confirming the concentration of yellow dust and fine dust in the spring
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination prices:Café in Ipokgun Park, 20-30% expensive than the general city of restaurants
  • Museum entrance fee:Haman Museum Free
  • Experience:20,000 won for electric boats, 15,000 won for Skybycle
  • Haman Watermelon:Steel (July-August) local purchases are 30-50% cheaper than Seoul
