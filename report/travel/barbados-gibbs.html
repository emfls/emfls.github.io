What is the recommended itinerary for a 2-night, 3-day trip to Gibbs Beach in Barbados?

The recommended itinerary includes Day 1 exploring Gibbs Beach, Merlins Beach, Hol Town, and Spit Town over approximately 8 hours, focusing on calm beaches and local culture. Gibbs Beach is known as one of the calmest beaches on Barbados' Gold Coast, featuring Turkish Sea waters and white sand. Nearby Merlins Beach offers a livelier atmosphere, while Hol Town provides diverse dining and shopping options. Spit Town offers a glimpse into traditional culture and local life.
