⚠️Pooban travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Major disease risks:Malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever, bowl

  • Malaria prevention:Prevention drug required, wearing long -sleeved clothes, using mosquito repellents
  • yellow fever:Required vaccination (confirmation of certificates when entering Korea)
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, avoid ice
  • food:Only intake of completely cooked food, street food attention
  • Emergency situation:Check local hospital information in advance
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, traffic difficulties in rainy season
  • Public transportation:Bus safety confirmation required, speeding attention
  • taxi:No meter, preliminary fee negotiations required
  • Rent -a -car:Need for international driver's license, knowing local traffic laws
  • Night movement:Avoiding as much as possible and using a reliable driver
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-3):The temperature is 15-28 degrees, dry and dusty
  • Rainy (4-10):Temperature 18-25 degrees, wet and non-frequent
  • Highlands:Big and chilly at night
  • ultraviolet rays:UV strong, sunscreen is required due to the high altitude
  • Preparation for the rainy season:Reading and waterproof bag preparation
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash Society:Limited credit card use, fully prepared for cash
  • Exchange:Moving Powuban after exchange in Younda Duala
  • Price negotiation:Price negotiations are required in the market
