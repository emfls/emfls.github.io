What are the main attractions included in the Gaziantep 2 nights 3 days travel course?

The travel course includes visiting the Zeugma Mosaic Museum, Gaziantep Castle, Emine Yoş Kitchen Museum, and Elmacı Bazaar, offering a blend of ancient art, history, culinary culture, and local market experiences.

What is special about the Zeugma Mosaic Museum in Gaziantep?

The Zeugma Mosaic Museum houses the world's largest collection of mosaics, featuring sophisticated Roman-era works including the famous Gypsy Girl Mosaic, making it a key cultural highlight of Gaziantep.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect at the Emine Yoş Kitchen Museum and Elmacı Bazaar?

At the Emine Yoş Kitchen Museum, visitors explore traditional cooking ingredients and tools, while Elmacı Bazaar offers a lively atmosphere with local spices, handicrafts, and authentic cultural interactions.
