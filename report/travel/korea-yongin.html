자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main attractions included in the Yongin 2 nights 3 days travel course?

The main attractions on the Yongin travel course include Everland, Korea's largest theme park with rides like T Express and Amazon Express, the Korean Folk Village showcasing traditional Joseon Dynasty culture, Yongin University Janggeum Park known for the drama Daejanggeum filming location, and the Nam June Paik Art Center featuring modern media art.

How much time is recommended to spend on Day 1 of the Yongin travel itinerary?

It is recommended to spend about 10 hours on Day 1 to fully enjoy the theme park rides, cultural performances at the Korean Folk Village, the historical setting of Janggeum Park, and the art exhibits at the Nam June Paik Art Center.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect in Yongin?

Travelers can experience traditional Korean culture through performances such as traditional wedding ceremonies, nongak play, and tightrope walking at the Korean Folk Village, explore historical architecture at Janggeum Park, and engage with contemporary media art at the Nam June Paik Art Center.
