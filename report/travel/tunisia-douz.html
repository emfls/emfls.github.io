⚠️Duzu travel precautions

🚨 Desert safetyism

Main precautions:Desert climate, sandstorm, dehydration risk

  • Prevention of dehydration:In the desert, water intake is required for more than 3-4 liters of water per day.
  • Health disease prevention:Minimization of direct sunlight exposure, relaxing in the shade
  • Sand Storm:In the event of a sudden sandstorm
  • Lost prevention:In the desert, you must go with a guide, prepare for GPS and compasses
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Luaji use:It takes about 6-7 hours from Tunis to Doz, a seat reservation required in advance
  • Desert Tour:Reliable tour company selection, safety equipment check
  • 4WD vehicle:It is impossible to use a general passenger car when moving to the desert area, and required for four -wheel drive vehicles
  • Fuel preparation:In the desert area, there are rare gas stations, securing enough fuel
  • Communication:In the depths of the desert, the mobile phone signal instability
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):40-45 degrees daytime temperatures, 25-30 degrees at night, extreme heat attention
  • Winter (12-2):Day 15-20 degrees, 5-10 degrees at night, and very big
  • Spring/Autumn:Day 25-30 degrees, 10-15 degrees at night, the best time to travel
  • Desert Daily:Daily and night temperature differences of 20 degrees or more, warm clothing required
  • Dryness:The humidity is also very low, moisturizer and lip balm required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Desert Tour, camel experience price pre -negotiations required
  • Desert Tour Cost:1 night 2 days Safari 150-250 Dinar, camel experience 20-30 Dinar
  • Tip Culture:Guide 10-20 Dinar, camel trainer 5-10 Dinar
  • Water price:2-3 times the price of bottled water in the desert area
