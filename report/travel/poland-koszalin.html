What are the main attractions to visit on the first day of the Coshalin travel itinerary?

On the first day, visitors should explore Coshalin's Old Town, including the Gothic St. Mary's Ascension Cathedral built in the 14th century, the vibrant Market Square with its colorful merchants and fountains, and the Coshalin Museum, which offers insights into local history and Baltic Sea culture.

How much time is recommended for exploring Coshalin's Old Town and its cultural sites?

The guide recommends dedicating approximately 8 hours on the first day to thoroughly explore the Old Town, including the cathedral, market square, and museum, to fully experience the medieval architecture and cultural heritage.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect in Coshalin's central city area?

Travelers can expect to experience the remnants of medieval alliances through red brick Gothic architecture, lively market activities with colorful merchants and fountains, and educational exhibits at the Coshalin Museum that highlight the local history and Baltic Sea culture.
