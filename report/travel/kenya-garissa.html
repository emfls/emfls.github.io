⚠️Garisa travel precautions

🚨 Security and terrorism

Main dangerous areas:Near the border of Somali, outskirts, late night downtown

  • Al Shabab Terror:Somalia's terrorist risk of Islamic extremist militants, university and government agencies
  • Border area:Somalia's border within 150 km of the area is very dangerous, prohibited from traveling alone
  • Strength and theft:Violent crime frequency, cash and valuables at least enough to be called by age
  • Motorcycle theft:Cautions using motorcycles, bags in front of the body
  • Prohibition of going out at night:After sunset, it is absolutely prohibited to go out alone, only inside the hotel
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Matotu Bus:It is a local public transport, but it is prohibited to use it due to risk of violent driving and pickpockets
  • Taxi:Only taxis calls from the hotel, JIM CAB, JATCO recommendation
  • Uber use:Use only before sunset, prohibit use of night time
  • Road situation:There are many unpaved roads and cannot be traffic in the rainy season
  • Fuel supply:There are rare gas stations, so the fuel is fully prepared
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (12-3):Temperature 25-35 degrees, very hot, dry, enough water intake required
  • Woogi (April-May, October-November):Flood risks, road blocking, travel schedules required
  • Strong ultraviolet light:Very strong in UV rays near the equator, sunscreen and hat required
  • Daily crossroads:Daily and night temperature differences of 10 degrees or more, preparing for insulation coat
  • Sand breeze:Preparation of sand breeze, glasses and masks
💰 Price related precautions
  • Foreigners:Rewards for tourists, preliminary prices required
  • Market bargaining:Negotiation in the central market, negotiations at 30-50% of Jesse
  • Tip Culture:Guide 1-20 a day, $ 10-20 hotel staff $ 1-2
  • Cash payment:Limited credit card use, cash -oriented transactions
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Malaria's onset areas, prevention drugs and mosquito repellents required
  • yellow fever:Essentials for vaccination of yellow fever disease, certificate when entering Korea
