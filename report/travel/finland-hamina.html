자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What is the recommended itinerary for visiting Hamina, Finland?

The recommended itinerary for Hamina is a 2-night, 3-day travel course. On Day 1, visitors should spend about 8 hours touring key sites including Hamina Fortress, the Old Town, Hamina City Hall, and St. John Church to experience the city’s historical and architectural highlights.

What makes Hamina’s urban planning unique?

Hamina is famous for its unique circular urban planning, which is centered around a star-shaped fortress built during the Swedish era. This design is rare and reflects the city’s historical military and administrative significance.

Which historical and architectural sites are must-sees in Hamina?

Must-see sites in Hamina include the star-shaped Hamina Fortress, the traditional Finnish architecture of the Old Town, the centrally located Hamina City Hall representing local administration history, and St. John Church, known for its religious legacy and beautiful architecture.
