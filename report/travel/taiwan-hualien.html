📅 2 nights and 3 days travel courses

Day 1: Nature and Night Market Experience

Tyrugger Gorge → Shakadang Trail → Changchunsa → Dongdamen Night Market

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:Taiwan's best natural spots in Tyruger National Park, the marble canyon, shakadang trails, and the view of Changchunsa Falls, and in the evening, you can enjoy local food in Taiwan at Dongdamen Night Market.

Day 2: Coast and Valley, Culture Experience

Chicingan Beach → Qing Sue Cliff → East Lift Valley → Aboriginal Culture Experience

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:Sunrise at the Chicingan Beach with the Pacific Ocean, the Mingle Sugi Cliff Drive and East Lift Valley Natural Hiking, and in the afternoon, traditional crafts and cooking in Aboriginal villages

⚠️This time caution

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around Hualien Station, Night Market, Night Sleeping Alley

  • pickpocket:Self -defenseism in night markets, train stations, and tourist attractions
  • Night movement:Avoid rare people in late night, only official taxis when using taxi
  • Natural disasters:Woogi (June -September) Typhoon, heavy rain, landslide attention
  • Trekking Safety:Only a designated trail is prepared for sudden weather changes
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Train reservation:Peak season/Weekend pre -booking required, ticket sold out quickly
  • Bus/Taxi:Easy card use, taxi destination is prepared in Chinese characters.
  • Driverism:Caution, mountain roads, when driving on the cliff road
  • Traffic strike:Rarely bus/train strike, alternative transportation check
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June -September):25 ~ 33 degrees, wet and showers frequent, UV strength, moisture intake and sunscreen required
  • Winter (December -February):15 ~ 20 degrees, mild but raining, umbrella/raincoat preparation
  • Spring/Autumn:Daily intersection, morning evening chilly, packing outerwear
  • Typhoon/Earthquake:Meteorological forecast check, safety information compliance
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Night market, major tourist attraction cafes/restaurant prices 2-3 times expensive
  • Transportation cost:Train (Taipei ~ Huan) One way about 18,000 ~ 20,000 won
  • Taxi Tour:On the 1st vehicle, 1 to 9 ~ 310,000 won for the number of people (sharing according to the number of people)
  • Tip Culture:Taiwan has no tip culture (hotel/taxi autonomous)
