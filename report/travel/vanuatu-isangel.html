⚠️ Lee Sang -gel travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, dengue fever, Zika virus

  • malaria:Malaria dangerous areas all areas of Tanna Island, use of preventive drugs and mosquito repellents required
  • Volcanic safety:Mandatory guides when visiting the Yasu Volcano, volcanic ash and toxic gasism
  • Marine safety:Barebar ban on coral reefs, toxic marine lifeism
  • drinking water:Drink only bottled water and avoid ice intake, avoid food
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • flight:Port Villa Essentials, Calls in case of worsening weather
  • Local transportation:Taxi and buses, rental cars require international license
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, deterioration of road conditions during rainy season
  • Boat use:Use after checking the weather and wearing life jackets
  • Walking:Prohibition of going alone in the jungle area, recommending guides
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (4-10):20-28 degrees of temperature, low humidity, tourism optimal
  • Woogi (11-3):Temperature 24-32 degrees, high humidity and cyclone risk
  • Volcanic ash:Volcanic ash flying, respiratory protection according to the wind direction
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays near the equator, sunscreen occasionally
  • Sudden showers:Preparation
