⚠️ Bata travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the market, late at night

  • pickpocket:Central market, pickpocketism at the night market, and valuables are stored in the hotel safe.
  • Night going out:Risk of going alone after 9 pm, recommended to use taxi
  • Photo shooting:Prohibition of photography of government buildings, military facilities, airport photography
  • Local guide:Be sure to accompany a certified guide when visiting the national park
  • Political Assembly:Prohibition of access to political protests or rally
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:The bus facility is poor and irregular, and recommends using taxis or car rentals
  • Road status:There are many unpaved roads, and the road condition is very deteriorated during the rainy season
  • Taxi:There are many cases in the absence of a meter, preliminary fee negotiations required
  • Domestic flights:Domestic flights between Malabo (capital) and Bata
  • Border movement:Visa checks when moving borders with Cameroon and Gabon
🏥 Health and medical precautions
  • malaria:Malaria dangerous areas throughout the year, taking prevention drugs and mosquito prevention
  • yellow fever:Request for immigration certificates when entering Korea, refusal to enter the country in case of unacceptable species
  • drinking water:Prohibit tap water, be sure to use bottled water or boiled water
  • food:Street foodism, ingest only enough cooked food
  • Medical facilities:Lack of medical facilities, overseas transfer in case of serious illness
  • Emergency situation:No Korean consulate, contacted Gabon or Cameroon Consulate General
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (May-October):Heavy rain and flood risks, restrictions on road moving
  • Dry (11-4):25-35 degrees of temperature, high humidity, enough water intake
  • Equatorial climate:Over the year, hot and humidity, heat stroke
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat required
  • Scol:Sudden showers, umbrellas and waterproof products
💰 Price related precautions
