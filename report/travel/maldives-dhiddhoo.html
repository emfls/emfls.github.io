⚠️Du travel precautions

🌊 Marine safetyism

Main precautions:Birds, marine life, weather changes

  • Algaeism:The sea area around the two can be strong, so it is prohibited from swimming far without guides
  • Marine life:Prohibit coral reefs, come out of the water immediately when discovering the sea snake or toxic jellyfish
  • Weather change:Sudden scalls or waves change immediately on land
  • Snorkeling safety:Be sure to work together with two or more people with a birdie system
🕌 Culture and religious precautions
  • Islamic culture:Maldives is a Islamic country and is banned from wearing a bikini in a local residential area.
  • An alcoholic ban:Alcohol sales and drunk on local islands
  • Dress Regulations:In the village, a neat dress that covers the shoulders and knees
  • evensong:Large noise restraint for 5 times a day
  • Ramadan period:Ramadan is not allowed to eat public food during the daytime
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Dry (12-4):Temperature 24-30 degrees, high humidity and very strong UV rays
  • Woogi (5-November):Limited to marine activities, strong winds and strong winds
  • Monsoon Season:In June-August, the waves are high and wind strong in southwest monsoon
  • Moisture supplement:High humidity and strong sunlight are risk of dehydration
  • Heat deathism:10 am-4 pm direct sunlight exposure
💰 Cost -related precautions
  • Cash payment:Most of them are limited to cash payment, credit card use
  • Tour cost:Snorkeling Tour $ 25-40, Dolphin Tour $ 35-50 Per Person
  • Food expenses:Local restaurant $ 8-15, guest house meal $ 10-20
  • Transportation cost:Speed ​​Boat $ 35-50, Public Ferry $ 3-5
  • Tip Culture:Guide $ 5-10, boat captain $ 3-5 per day
