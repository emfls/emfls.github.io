What are the main historical sites to visit in Västerås on the first day?

On the first day, visitors should explore the Gothic-style Västerås Cathedral built in the 13th century, Anundshög—the largest prehistoric Viking-era burial mound in Sweden, Västerås Castle which reflects the religious reforms of King Gustav Vasa, and enjoy the natural scenery at Lake Mälaren.

How much time should I allocate for the Day 1 itinerary in Västerås?

The recommended time for the Day 1 itinerary, which includes visiting key historical and cultural sites as well as Lake Mälaren, is approximately 8 hours to fully appreciate each location.

What should travelers prepare for when visiting Västerås according to the guide?

Travelers should prepare for a mix of cultural and outdoor activities by wearing comfortable walking shoes, planning their schedule to cover all key sites within the suggested timeframe, and being ready to experience both historical landmarks and natural beauty.
