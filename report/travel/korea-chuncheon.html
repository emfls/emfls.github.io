⚠️ Chuncheon travel precautions

🚨 Safety and precautions

Major areas:Around the lake, mountainous areas, night time zones

  • Water play safety:Chuncheon Lake, Water Play only in the safety agent when swimming around Uiam Lake
  • Mountains:Wearing hiking boots and sunset warfare when visiting the mountainous areas such as Cheongpyeongsa and Gugok Falls
  • Rail bike:Wearing seat belts and compliance with safety rules when using Gangchon Rail Park
  • Night activity:Use a walkway with lighting when walking at night around the lake
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Yongsan-Chuncheon 1 hour 20 minutes, pre-booking recommendation when using ITX-Youth
  • City Bus:Chuncheon City Bus Rate 1,370 won, recommended use of transportation cards
  • Taxi fee:About 15,000 won from Chuncheon Station to Nami Island, Meter Check
  • Rent -a -car:Weekend and peak season rental reservations required, parking lot check in advance
  • Nami Island Transportation:When you enter Nami Island
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):20-30 degrees of temperature, moist and hot, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -10-5 degrees, very cold, winter supplies required
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and sudden showers, umbrellas and outerwear preparation
  • Lake Wind:Chuncheon Lake, Preparation of Wind Bridge around Uiam Lake
  • ultraviolet rays:UV strong, sunscreen required with lake reflective light
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Nami Island, Myeong -dong Chicken Ribs Alley is high
