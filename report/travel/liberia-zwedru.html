📅 Jorzor 2 nights 3 days travel courses

Day 1: Explore city and traditional culture

Zoror Central → Central Market → Culture Center → Traditional Village

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the central city of Grand Gede County, we experience the West African traditional culture, see the traditional Liberian traditional agricultural products and handicrafts such as Kasaba, Rice, and Palm Oil in the local central market, enjoy the traditional music and dance of the Crew and Grebo in the cultural center, and experience traditional African houses and lifestyles in nearby traditional villages
📍Jorzor Central Street 📍Angang Market 📍 Culture Center 📍 Traditional village

Day 2: Exploration of nature and wildlife

Sapo National Park → Kabala River → Tropical Woorim Trekking → Observation of Wildlife

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:In Sapo National Park, we explored the West African tropical rainforest ecosystem, observed rare wildlife such as Pygmehama, Diana Monkey, and Forest Elephant, experienced the mangrove forest and riverside ecosystem in the Kabala River, and learned how to hunt for medicinal plants and traditional hunting through tropical rainforest trekking with the guide. Watch the diversity of west African birds
📍Sapo National Park 📍Cavala River 📍 Tropical rainforest trekking 📍 Wildlife observation

Day 3: History and Local Experience (optional)

History Museum → Rubber Farm → Traditional Craft Experience → Local Food Experience

History Museum:Takes 2 hours, Liberian Independence History and Granded District Learning Traditional Culture Learning
Rubber Farm:3 hours, Liberia's major industries, rubber production courses, farm experience experiences
Traditional craft experience:2 hours, weave a basket with local craftsmen
Local food experience:2 hours, traditional dishes such as Zolopris, Casaba Leaf, and Palm Butter

⚠️Joror Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Night going out at night, moving alone in the outskirts

  • Night going out:After the sun, it is recommended to go out alone, recommend moving with local guides
  • Supplies Management:Valuits are kept in the accommodation, only at least cash
  • Local guide:Must be accompanied by local guides when observing wild animals
  • Emergency situation:Local police station and hospital location in advance
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:In the rainy season, it is recommended to use 4WD vehicles in the rainy season
  • Public transportation:Wear seat belts when using buses and taxis, speed attention
  • Fuel:Securing enough fuel when moving long distances
  • communication:Unstable unstable cell phone signals in the outskirts
  • Mobile time:It takes about 6-8 hours from Monrovia to Zororzor
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (May-October):Rain and flood risks, umbrellas and waterproof products required
  • Dry (11-4):High temperature drying, enough water intake
  • humidity:Wearing high humidity and gout in the year
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat required
  • Temperature:25-35 degrees throughout the year, tropical climate
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Use of mosquito repellents, required to take preventive drugs
  • drinking water:Drink only bottled water, ice care
  • food:Only eat enough food
  • Medical facilities:Limited to local medical facilities, preparation for emergency medicine
  • hygiene:Washing hands, thorough, hand disinfection
