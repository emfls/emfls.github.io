📅 Tronheim 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: History and City Walk

Nida Ross Cathedral → Old Town Bridge (Gamle BYBRO) → Barlandett Old Town → Christian Sten Fort

Recommended time:8 ~ 9 hours
Core points:Northern Europe's largest Gothic -style Gothic Ross Cathedral, experiencing the history of the Norwegian royal family, crossing the Old Town Bridge, called the Lucky Gate, and walking through the colorful wooden houses and cafes of the old town of Barlandette, and watching Christian Sten Sten on the hills on the hills.

Day 2: Fiord and Modern Culture Experience

Tronheim Fiorded Walk → Rockheim (National Popular Music Museum) → Tronheim Museum of Art → Lingbe Museum

Recommended time:8 ~ 10 hours
Core points:Enjoy the Nordic natural landscape on the fiord riverside trails, experience the history of Norwegian popular music in Rockheim, and enjoy art and instrument culture at the Tronheim Museum of Art and Lingbe Museum

🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Buy bus/tram ticket with ATB app, 1 day ticket 129nok
  • Airport bus:FB73 requires a separate ticket
  • Train connection:Oslo-Tronheim takes about 6 hours
  • Rent -a -car:Snow tires required in winter, navigation/charger preparation
  • bicycle:Bicycle Lift experience available, winter slides
🌡️Checks related to weather

  • Spring/Autumn:Preparation
  • ultraviolet rays:Summer sunlight strong, sunscreen required
