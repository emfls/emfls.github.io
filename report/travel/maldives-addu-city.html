⚠️ Ardu City travel precautions

🚨 Marine safetyism

Main precautions:Strong bird, marine life, diving safety

  • Algaeism:Ardu Colonial is famous for its strong birds, so it is prohibited by diving alone without a guide.
  • Marine life:Maintain safe distances when observing large marine life such as sharks and stingrays
  • Diving Safety:Be sure to dive with a guide at the PADI certification center
  • Emergency situation:In the event of an emergency during the dive, do not rise to the surface immediately, follow the guide instructions.
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Domestic flights:Male-Liver International Airport Connection Review required, delayed due to weather
  • RTL Perry:20kg of luggage limit when using the Maldives Local Island Connection Ferry (no actual measurement)
  • Boat movement:Boats are used when moving between islands.
  • Bicycle Tour:Comply with traffic rules when using bicycles in the island
  • Resort pickup:Most resorts offer airport pick -up services
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):Temperature 24-30 degrees, low humidity, diving optimal time
  • Woogi (May-October):26-32 degrees of temperature, high humidity and scores
  • Monsoon Season:In May-October, the waves are high and wind strong in the southwest monsoon
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, underwater, UV protection required
  • cyclone:Sudden weather change in the rainy season
💰 Price related precautions
  • Resort cost:$ 200-800 per night for accommodation of Ardu City Resort
