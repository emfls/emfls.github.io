What is the recommended itinerary for a trip to Benin Okaka?

The recommended itinerary is a 3-day, 2-night trip starting with a 9-hour Day 1 course that includes visiting Okefara Falls, trekking the Atakora Mountains, exploring traditional villages of the Betamaribe and Somaba tribes, and experiencing the local market.

What natural attractions are featured in the Benin Okaka travel guide?

The guide highlights Okefara Falls, a three-stage waterfall with natural swimming pools, and the Atakora Mountains, known for trekking and observing West Africa's primitive nature and wildlife.

Travelers can experience authentic African culture by visiting traditional villages of the Betamaribe and Somaba tribes, observing traditional architecture, and engaging with locals at markets offering specialties like shea butter and traditional fabrics.
