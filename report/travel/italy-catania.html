📅 Catania 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: Volcano and History Center

Etna Mountain → Catania Cathedral → Piazadel Duomo → La Piscaria Market

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:In Etna, the most active volcano in Europe, you can explore the crater on the cable car, enjoy the unique scenery of the volcanic terrain, enjoy the beauty of Baroque architecture at the UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Catania Cathedral, and experience the symbol of the city in the Piazadel Duomo with elephant fountains.

Day 2: Ancient ruins and arts experience

Core points:In the second century, the Roman round theater, experienced ancient Rome's theater culture, enjoyed a unique architectural style that combines black basalt and marble, enjoys the essence of Italian art in an opera house named after the composer Bellini, and explored the history of Sicily in the Middle Ages.

⚠️ Catania travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around Central Station, some alleys in La Piccaria market, late at night

  • pickpocket:Central Station, Piazadel Duomo, pickpocketed at tourist attractions
  • Motorcycle theft:Suddenly, a motorcycle is caught a handbag or a cell phone.
  • Fake guide:Attention to those who sell fake tickets in tourist attractions or who are forced to guide
  • Going out late at night:Be careful to go alone after 10 o'clock in the central station and the old town after 10 pm
🌋 Volcanic related precautions
  • Etna Mountain Activity:Depending on the volcanic activity situation, the cable car can be stopped, the preliminary confirmation required
  • Volcanic ash:Respiratory problems due to volcanic ash during volcanic activity, preparation of masks
  • Weather change:As the altitude increases, the temperature drops sharply, so it is required for winter products.
  • Safety equipment:Recommended for wearing helmets and safety equipment for volcanic trekking
  • Guide Tour:Recommended tours with professional guides rather than individual climbing
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transport strike:Due to the nature of Italy, sudden traffic strikes can occur, alternative means of transportation
  • AMT ticket:Buses and regional traffic integrated tickets use, fines at checkpoints
  • Taxi fee:Fixed plan, meter check from the airport to the city
  • Rent -a -carism:Lack of narrow alleys and parking spaces
  • Perry:Depending on the weather
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):30-38 degrees of temperature, very hot and dry, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature 10-18 degrees, mild but raining often
  • Spring/Autumn:Daily crossroads, strong winds, preparation of outerwear
  • Shiroko Wind:Temperature rises with the hot wind blowing from Africa
  • Strong ultraviolet light:UV rays are very strong due to the Mediterranean climate
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Café around major tourist attractions, restaurant prices 2-3 times expensive
  • Market bargaining:La Peacaria Market can bargain when purchasing souvenirs
  • Tip Culture:Restaurant 10%, Cafe 1-2 euro, Hotel House Keeping 2-3 euro
