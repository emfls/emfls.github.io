Recommended time:10 hours

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:No access area around the mine, city late at night

  • Mine security:Rossing uranium mine has a strict security and legal punishment when approaching without permission
  • Radioactive exposure:In the past, reports of health problems related to radioactivity, compliance with safety rules on mining tours
  • Wildlife:Dangerous animals such as desert areas, scorpions, etc.
  • Risk of isolation:Life risk, GPS required if you get lost in the desert area
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Lack of public transportation:Arandis recommends public transportation limited, rental car or tour
  • B2 National Highway:Wint Hook-Subakovmund Connection road, wildlife
  • Fuel supply:Rarely prepared for fuel in the desert area
  • Road status:There are many unpaved roads, recommending 4WD cars
  • Communication:Neglect of mobile phones signal, satellite calls, consideration
