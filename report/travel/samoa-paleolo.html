⚠️Paleolo travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Dengue, Zika Virus, Malaria

  • Anti -mosquitoes:Early morning and evening time mosquito activity, long -sleeved clothes and evacuation required
  • Water qualityism:I only drink bottled water, ice attention, raw fruit peel
  • Marine safety:Strong birds, waves, and snorkeling accompanied by guides
  • Prevention of heat stroke:Sufficient moisture intake and rest in the shade
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Rent -a -car:Leave the left side, international driver's license required, mountain road attention
  • Public transportation:Bus operation irregularities, time spare
  • taxi:In case of no meter, preliminary fee negotiations are required
  • Boat Tour:The possibility of cancellation of flights according to the weather
  • Road situation:Road flooding and landslife attention during the rainy season
