Day 1: Border City Culture and History Examination

Largo Central → Jimani Market → San Fernando Fort → Malecon Coast

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:Largo Central, the center of Dominica and Haiti borders, experiences colonial buildings and local culture, tasting Caribbean specialties and local foods in the traditional market, and exploring traces of the history and border defense of the Spanish colonial era in the San Fernando fortress built in the 18th century, Appreciating the unique landscapes of beautiful sunsets and borders

Day 2: Nature and Lake Experience

Enriki Yo Lake → Cabaral National Park → Jimani Beach → Onsen District

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:Caribbean Sea Lake Enriki Lake, Lake Enrikiyo, observes unique wildlife such as Flamingo and Iguana, experiences a mysterious natural environment in a lower area than sea level, explores the dried tropical cactuses and rare plants in Cabaral National Park, and enjoys the clear sea and white sandy beaches of the Caribbean on the beach of Jimani. Experience relaxation and healing with hot spring water rich in hot springs
