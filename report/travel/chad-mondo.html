⚠️Mondo travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Borders, night time

  • Political anxiety:Chacha is a political unstable area, so it is required to confirm the latest safety information.
  • Terrorism:Since it is near the terrorist organizational activity area such as Boko Haram, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Safety Notice is confirmed
  • Prohibition of going out at night:After the sun, never go out alone and accompany the local guide.
  • checkpoint:Passports and visas are always brought at the military checkpoint, and maintain a cooperative attitude
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road situation:There are few packaging roads and 4WD vehicles required when moving in the desert area
  • Public transportation:It is recommended to use private vehicles or tours because there are few public transportation facilities
  • Fuel:There are rare gas stations, preparing for sufficient fuel and preliminary fuel
  • Desert Water:GPS and compass required, recommending local guides
  • Vehicle inspection:Thorough inspection of vehicles before the desert movement, preparation of preliminary tires
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-3):15-35 degrees of temperature, dry and dusty, enough water intake
  • Woogi (June-September):Temperature 25-40 degrees, increasing risk of wet and malaria
  • Desert Daily:The daytime is required to fall to 40 degrees daytime and 5 degrees at night
  • Sand Storm:Evacuate immediately when a sudden sand storm occurs
  • ultraviolet rays:Sahara Desert UV UV, completely blocked
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:All goods prices are very expensive as inland countries in Africa.
  • Cash payment:There are many areas that cannot be used for credit cards, enough cash preparation
  • Exchange:Exchange the dollar with CFA franc, recommend using banks
  • Tip Culture:Payment of appropriate tips when using the service
