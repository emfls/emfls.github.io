⚠️Gyvanda travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Riverside rural areas late at night, rural areas late at night

  • River safety:Tita River, Bramaput River flow rate is fast, so be careful with local guides on the riverside.
  • Rural areas:Foreigners are rare, and politely responds to the approach of curious locals
  • Religious consideration:Respect for Islamic Culture, compliance with costume regulations when visiting Mosk
  • Female traveler:As it is a conservative area, special attention
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Buses and Rikisha (rickshaws) mainly used to negotiate preliminary negotiations
  • Road situation:The possibility of flooding in the rainy season, the dry season
  • Crossing the river:Check safety equipment when using ferry, wear life jackets
  • Taka Connection:It takes about 6-7 hours by bus from Gaibanda to Taka.
  • Local transportation:Wear helmet when using motorcycle taxi (CNG)
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Monsoon Season (June-October):Intensive heavy rains and flood risks, recommended to restrain travel
  • Dry (11-3):The temperature of 15-25 degrees, the optimal travel
  • Summer (April-May):30-40 degrees of temperature, extreme heat and humidity
  • River level:Monsoon season river water level rising, boat tour risk
  • Daily crossroads:Dry day and night, the temperature difference is large, so prepare the outerwear
💰 Price related precautions
  • Local prices:Very cheap, but attention to foreigners
  • lodgment:Guest House 1 night 500-2000 Taka ($ 6-24)
  • Food expenses:Local restaurant 50-200 Taka, more expensive restaurants for tourists
  • Transportation cost:Rikisha 20-50 Taka, CNG 50-150 Taka
