📅 Rose Hall 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: Examination of sugar farms and rural culture

Rose Hall Sugar Farm → Kanje Village → Verbis River → Rural Experience Center

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:3808 hectares -scale rose hall sugar farms in Eastern Burvis Correntine, 37 Series Witaker clay soil, 85% Dutch bed, 13% British bed, and 2% wide beds, and experience the sugar production process of historical farms that won the service medal in 1978. Experienced the traditional lifestyle of the community and the cultural diversity of various population composition, and observed riverside walking and local fishing activities on the Burbis River.
📍 Rose Hall Sugar Farm 📍 Kanje Village 📍Burvis River 📍 Rural Experience Center

Day 2: Cultural Festival and Traditional Craft Experience

Traditional Market → Regional Festival Participation in Local Festival → Artisan Workshop → Agricultural Tourism Tour

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:In the Rose Hall Traditional Market, local agricultural products and handicrafts are purchased and exchanged with Indian, African, and Aboriginal merchants that reflect Gaian's multicultural society, and participate in local festivals held throughout the year to experience vibrant atmosphere, traditional music, and dance, and learn traditional handicrafts in local artisans. Experienced the field of agricultural tourism promoted by the Ministry of Agriculture and Tourism by visiting practices and sustainable rural tourism development programs.
📍 Traditional market 📍 Regional festival 📍 Artisan Workshop 📍 Agricultural Tourism Tour

Day 3: Burvis Coast and Natural Exploration (optional)

Burvis Coast → Mangrove Forest → Bird Observation → Coast Village Tour

Burvis Coast:Takes 4 hours, unique freshwater-seawater ecosystem observation in the Burbis River Estuary
Mangrove Forest:3 hours, exploration of various wildlife in the Mangrove ecosystem unique to the Kaiana coast
Bird Observation:2 hours, migratory birds and indigenous bird observation tours on the northern coast of South America
Coastal Village:Takes 2 hours, traditional fishing culture and seafood cooking experience in Verbis Coastal Fishing Village
📍Burvis coast 📍Mangrove Forest 📍Birds observations 📍 Coastal Village

⚠️ Rose Hall Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Outside the rural areas, around the night hours of farm farms

  • Rural security:There is a Rose Hall Police Station, but due to the nature of rural areas, night patrol restrictions are limited.
  • Wildlife:Mangrove forests and farms around the farm can appear dangerous wildlife such as snakes and crocodiles
  • Agricultural machinery safety:Maintaining a safety distance from large farm machinery and machinery equipment when visiting sugar farms
  • Kang:Strong birds and crocodiles during swimming or boat activities on the Burbis River
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Lack of public transportation:Due to the nature of rural areas, public transportation restrictions, rental cars or local guide tours required
  • Road status:Mud road mud road, 4WD vehicle recommended
  • Fuel supply:Gas station spacing is spacious and fully prepared for fuel
  • Georgetown Connection:It takes about 2-3 hours from Rose Hall to the capital George Town
  • Rainy traffic:Traffic can be blocked by flooding roads in the rainy season in May-August
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (May-August):Temperature 24-30 degrees, high humidity and heavy rains, waterproofing equipment required
  • Dry (September-April):26-32 degrees, relatively dry but still high humidity
  • Tropical climate:High temperature and humidity year -round, enough water intake and rest
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet, sunscreen near the equator
  • mosquito:Caution of mosquito activity throughout the year, malaria and dengue fever
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash payment:Most of the rural areas, cash payment, enough cash preparation
  • Agricultural products price:Local agricultural products are cheap but expensive imported products
  • Accommodation fee:Rural guest housekeys 20-40 USD, cheaper compared to Georgetown
  • Transportation cost:Rent-a-Car daily 50-80 USD, local guide tour 100-150 USD
