⚠️ Bandiagara travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Northern Mali, when moving at night

  • Terrorism:Northern Mali and Border Terrorism Risk, Foreign Ministry Travel Alert Confirmation required
  • Night travel:Night move after sunset, stay in the accommodation
  • Local guide required:Bandiagara area is banned alone, local guides are required
  • Government Checkpoint:Passport, visa inspection, and cooperative attitude at the checkpoint
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • 4WD vehicle required:Four -wheel drive vehicles required for access to Bandiagara
  • Road status:Unpaved roads and ruins, no traffic in the rainy season
  • Fuel:Securing enough fuel when moving long distances
  • Communication:There is no mobile phone signal in some areas, consideration of satellite calls
  • Preparation for emergency situations:Difficulty access to medical staff, preparing for sufficient emergency medicine
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):The temperature is 15-40 degrees, very dry and dust storm occurs
  • Woogi (May-October):Temperature 25-35 degrees, heavy rains and flood risk
  • Harmatan:Dry wind blowing from the Sahara Desert in December-February
  • Extreme heat:Rising to 45 degrees in March-May, heat stroke attention
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays throughout the year, sufficient sunscreen required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash -based economy:Limited credit card use, enough cash preparation
  • Guide cost:Local guide $ 30-50 a day
  • Accommodation:Basic accommodations, $ 20-60 for 1 night
  • Transportation cost:4WD car rental $ 100-150 a day
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Malaria dangerous areas throughout the year
  • yellow fever:Proposal for vaccination, presentation when entering certificate
  • drinking water:Only drinking water, ice and raw vegetables
  • Medical facilities:Lack of local medical facilities, transfer of Bamako in an emergency
  • Hygiene management:Washing hands, thorough, personal hygiene products
