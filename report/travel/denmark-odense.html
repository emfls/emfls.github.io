  • Public transportation:Recommended use of official taxis or public transportation

  • Bicycle -friendly city:Odense is a city that rides a lot of bicycles, and the bicycle road is well developed.
  • Helmet wear:Be sure to wear a helmet when using a bicycle or motorcycle
  • Bicycle rental:It's a good way to rent a bicycle and look around the city
  • Traffic rules:Local traffic laws and regulations and safely driving
💰 High price attention
  • Northern European prices:Danish prices are very expensive

  • Museum holiday:Andersen Museum is closed on Monday (excluding summer)
