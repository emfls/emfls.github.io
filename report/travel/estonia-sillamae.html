⚠️Silamae travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Old industrial complex, part of the shore of late night

  • General security:Estonia is generally safe, but pay attention to it late at night.
  • Language Wall:Estonian, Russian, mainly used, English communication restrictions
  • Emergency situation:Emergency call 112, Korean consulate contact information in advance
  • Radioactivity:Since it is a uranium processing area in the past, only the designated tourist area visits
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Bus route limited, timetable check in advance
  • taxi:Pre -reservation required, meter check
  • Rent -a -car:Snow tires required in winter, bringing international driver's license
  • Talin connection:About 2 hours by bus, limited train connection
  • Road situation:Winter icy roads, sudden changes in weather in summer
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):The temperature is 15-22 degrees, and the white night phenomenon is bright until late at night
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -10 ~ 0 degrees, very cold and sunshine short
  • Spring/Autumn:Daily cross -crossing and raining often, windproof clothing required
  • Baltic Sea Climate:High humidity and windy due to marine climate
  • ultraviolet rays:Long -term exposure caused by summer white nights
💰 Price related precautions
  • Overall prices:It is cheaper than Western Europe, but tourist destinations are expensive
  • Museum entrance fee:Silamae Culture Center 5-10 euros, Museum 3-8 euros
  • Tip Culture:Restaurant 10%, Taxi Round, Hotel 1-2 euros
  • Cash use:Cash preference in small stores
