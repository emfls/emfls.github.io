📅 Lamu 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: Swahilli culture and history exploration

Ramu Old Town → Lamu Museum → Fruit Market → Central Plaza

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the Ramu Old Town, designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, explores the traces of Swahili civilizations that have flourished from the 14th century, enjoys traditional buildings made of coralstones and sophisticated wooden sculptures, learned the history of Swahili culture and Indian trade in the Lamu Museum, and tasted tropical fruits such as mango and coconut in the local fruit market. Observation of the lives of relaxed locals

Day 2: Beach and Natural Experience

Shira Beach → Donkey Riding Experience → Sailing Dan Sail Sailing → Seafood BBQ

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:On the beach, you can enjoy the emerald sea of ​​the Indian Ocean and the white sandy beaches, explore the coral reefs with snorkeling, enjoy the unique experience of exploring the narrow alleys on the Tangi, the only means of transportation, and enjoying the Indian Oceans on the traditional sailboat, and experiencing romantic sailing in the evening Watch the night view of Ramu by tasting fresh seafood barbecue and Swahili traditional food caught by local fishermen.

Day 3: Examination of surrounding islands and cultural experiences (options)

Manda Island → Siyu Fortress → Henna Experience → Traditional craft shopping

Manda Island:It takes 3 hours, moves to a boat to the island where Lamu Airport is located, and observes mangrove forest and wild algae.
Siyu Fortress:It takes 2 hours, and in the 16th century, Lamu Island's defense history and strategic importance learning
Henna experience:1 hour, traditional henna art with a beautiful pattern on your hands and arms
