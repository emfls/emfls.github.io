⚠️Arusha travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, typhoids

  • Malaria prevention:Prior to the trip, consult a doctor and take malaria prevention drugs, use mosquito repellents required
  • Yellow fever vaccination:Essential requirements for entry, completion of inoculation 10 days before departure
  • Drinking water:Drink only bottled water and avoid ice, raw fruit and vegetable attention
  • Wildlife:Don't go out of the car from Safari
  • Altitude disease:Sufficient rest and moisture when climbing Kilimanjaro
🚗 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road situation:There are many unpaved roads and worsening road conditions in the rainy season, required for 4WD vehicles
  • Driverism:Leave the left side, the crossroads of the wildlife road, the nighttime driving prohibition
  • Guide accompanied:Local guide accompanying time for Safari Tour
  • Fuel preparation:The spacing of the gas station is far away and securing enough fuel
  • GPS limit:Some regional GPS signal weakness, compass and map preparation
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Dry (June-October):15-25 degrees, dusty and dryness, enough water intake
  • Woogi (11-5):Temperature 20-30 degrees, sudden heavy rain, rainfall equipment required
  • Plateau climate:Big and cold at night, prepared for warm clothes
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight near the equator, applying sunscreen from time to time
  • Kilimanjaro:Sudden temperature change caution according to high level
💰 Local culture and economic precautions
  • Tip Culture:Guide $ 10-20 per day, $ 5-10 porter, restaurant 10%
  • Masai Culture:Get permission before taking pictures, to respect traditional
  • Bargaining culture:In general, price bargaining in the market, 30-50% of the first presentation price
  • Respect for religion:Cautions when visiting Islam, Christian mixes, and religious facilities
  • Photo shooting:Get permission when shooting locals, prohibit shooting military facilities
