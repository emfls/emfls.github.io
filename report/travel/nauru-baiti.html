⚠️The travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Night coastal, secluded areas

  • Public status:Nauru is relatively safe, but avoid staying alone at night and recommend accompanying locals
  • Medical facilities:Since the medical infrastructure is limited, thorough preparation is required for emergencies
  • Communication Restriction:The Internet and telephone communication can be unstable, so prepare in advance
  • Risk of isolation:Due to the nature of the small island, it is difficult to help external help in the emergency situation
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Lack of public transportation:Since public transportation facilities are very limited, rental cars or taxis are required
  • Airlines limit:Pre -booking required because flights to Naururu are very limited
  • Road status:Ireland ring rods are packed but some section roads are poor
  • Fuel supply:Since the gas station is limited, the fuel condition is always checked
  • Street sense:The entire island is small, so it can be moved on foot, but physical consumption is caused by tropical climate.
