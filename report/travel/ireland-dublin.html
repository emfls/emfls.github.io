📅 Dublin 3 -day travel course

Day 1: Dublin History and Culture Tour

Trinity College → Graph Tone Street → Dublin National Museum → Ireland National Museum → Temple Bar

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:Experience abundant history and culture in Dublin, the capital of Ireland. Founded in 1592, enjoy the beautiful libraries and Kels' books at the prestigious trinity colleges, and enjoy street performances and shopping at the lively graph tone street. Feel the essence of Celtic culture at the National Museum of Ireland, where the archaeological treasures of Ireland are on display, and taste the Guinness beer with traditional Irish music at the Temple Bar.

Day 2: Dublin Performance Cathedral Tour

Half Penny Bridge → Dublin Castle → Christchurch Cathedral → St. Patrick Cathedral → Guinness Store House

Recommended time:8 hours 30 minutes
Core points:Cross Dublin's symbolic Half Penny Bridge and enjoy the beautiful scenery of the Lipy River and explore the political history of Ireland in Dublin Castle in 700 years. Founded in 1028 Christchurch Cathedral and the largest underground cemetery in Ireland, and listen to the Irish guardians at St. Patrick Cathedral. At the end of the day, learn the history and manufacturing process of Irish national beer at the Guinness Store House and enjoy the 360 ​​-degree Dublin view.

⚠️ Dublin travel precautions

🚨 Safety precautions

Major areas:Ocornel Street north, Panel Street, Panel Square, Upper Doset Street

  • Night movement:In late night, only in well -illuminated areas and avoiding areas that are not familiar.
  • Valuable goods management:Do not carry a large amount of cash and do not show off expensive cameras or cell phones.
  • Tourists do not appear:Avoid outfits like large bags and tourists
  • Traffic safety:Use designated pedestrians and compliance traffic signals
🎫 Prior reservation recommended
  • Guinness Store House:In the peak season, tickets may be sold out, so recommended online pre -booking
  • Trinity College Library:Pre -booking required for Kels' s visit.
  • Dublin St. Tour:Shorten the waiting time by pre -booking when participating in the guide tour
  • Restaurant reservation:Popular restaurants are required for dinner time
🚌 Precautions on public transportation
  • Dublin Bus:Accurate fee preparation (no change) or LEAP CARD use
  • Luas tram:Be sure to take a checker before boarding after purchasing a ticket
  • DART train:Use of the coastal area, required to check the timetable
  • Taxi:Use only the official registered taxi and utilize the Uber or FREENOW app
🌧️Checks related to weather
  • Maritime Climate:It's gentle throughout the year, but raining often comes
  • In preparation for rain:Always portable umbrella or waterproof jacket
  • Summer:Preparing for a light coat because it is cool at around 20 degrees
  • Winter:It does not go down below freezing, but the temperature is low due to the high humidity
🗣️ Language communication

Since English is official language, there is no big problem in communication. It may be necessary to get used to the Irish English accent, but locals are very kind and tries to help.

  • Literature City:Recommended for literary tours as the hometown of world -class writers such as Joyce, Wild, and Yates
  • Pub culture:Dublin's true charm can be felt in the pub, so please hang out with locals.
  • City of Music:Hometown of famous musicians such as U2 and Double Reners, many live music performances
  • A good city to walk:Dublin is flat and compact to be optimized for walking trips
  • Free Museum:Many museums such as the National Museum and the National Museum of Art
  • Festival:Various cultural festivals and events are held throughout the year.
  • Friendly locals:Dublin people are very friendly, so don't hesitate to get help if you get lost.
