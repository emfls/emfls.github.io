⚠️Kira travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Kira City late night, outskirts

  • robbery:Don't go alone in the night, and keep cash and valuables distributed
  • Motorcycle Taxi:When using BODA-BODA
  • fraud:Watch out for localism, fake guides, or commodities who offer excessive kindness
  • demonstration:Avoid political protests or assembly areas
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, deterioration of road conditions during rainy season
  • Public transportation:Speed and overloading when using Matatu minibus
  • taxi:Recommended Uber or Safeboda app
  • Rent -a -car:License on the left side, the international driver's license required
  • Campala connection:30 minutes to 1 hour by car from Kira to Campala
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (March-May, September-November):Strong rain, flood risk, raincoats
  • Dry (December-February, June-August):20-28 degrees, dry and dusty
  • Equatorial climate:The year -round temperature change and humidity is high
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, sunscreen and hat required near the equator
  • Daily crossroads:It can be chilly in the morning and evening
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Mosquito -mediated diseases, prevention drugs and required use
  • yellow fever:Required certificate of vaccination, confirmation when entering Korea
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, avoid ice
  • food:Street foodism, ingest only enough cooked food
  • Medical facilities:Limited to medical facilities in Kira area, using Kampala Hospital
💰 Price related precautions
  • Exchange rate:$ 1 = about 3,700 Uganda sealing (change possible)
  • Tourist destination:Excessive charges for foreigners
  • Tip Culture:10%of the restaurant, $ 10-20 per day guide
  • Bargaining:Price bargaining in the market, starting from half of the first presentation
