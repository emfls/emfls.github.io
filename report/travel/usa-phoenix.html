⚠️ Phoenix travel precautions

🌡️ Desert climate and heat stroke

Sun Valley:Phoenix is very hot and dry in clear weather for more than 300 days a year

  • Summer (June-September):Since the temperature rises by more than 40 degrees,
  • Symptoms:In the case of nausea, fatigue, headaches, dizziness, rest in the shade immediately
  • preventive:Drink water frequently, wear hats, take regular rest
  • Hiking time:Activities early in the morning or evening, prohibit outdoor activities in the midday
  • UV protection:SPF 50+ UV blockers must be recurred every 2 hours
🐍 Desert dangerous animal precautions
  • rattlesnake:Hiking walks with footsteps, and beware of grass forests or rocks
  • message:Check the shoes or clothes at night and wear it
  • coyote:Banned to leave food, be careful when attending with pets
  • First aid:If you are bitten or fired, immediately report 911 and transfer hospitals
🚗 Rent -a -car and traffic precautions
  • Rent -a -car required:Recommended for using car rentals because public transportation is limited
  • Gas Station Location:Check the location of gas stations in advance when moving long distances such as Grand Canyon
  • GPS required:GPS must be prepared because it can get lost in the desert area
  • Emergency supplies:Water in the vehicle, emergency food, first aid box beach
  • Sky Harbor Airport:It is crowded as one of the top five airports in the United States, so securing free time
🏜️ Hiking and outdoor activity precautions
  • Camelback Mountain:It is a high difficulty hiking course, so you need enough preparation and experience
  • Water preparation:Recommended for eating more than 1 liter per hour
  • Companion required:Hiking alone, must go with more than 2 people
  • Schedule sharing:Hiking schedule and route to your family or friends
