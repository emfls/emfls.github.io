⚠️ Ronlab travel precautions

🚨 Safety and health care

Main precautions:Lack of medical facilities, required for emergencies

  • Medical facilities:Ron Gellab is only basic medical facilities, so it is required to be transferred to each other in case of serious emergency.
  • Marine safety:Strong birds and sharks can be accompanied by local guides
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays near the equator, sufficient UV protection required
  • dehydration:Dehydration caused by high temperature and humid climate, enough water intake
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • flight:Frequent use of domestic flights in Maju -ro, cancellation of flights according to weather conditions
  • Boat traffic:Movement between the rounds is mainly used by boats, waves and weather influence
  • Reservation required:All transportation reservations required, difficulty in booking on the day
  • Luggage limit:Limited weight by using small aircraft
  • Lack of fuel:Transportation is delayed due to fuel supply instability
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (5-November):Typhoon and heavy rain, restrictions on outdoor activities
  • Dry (12-4):Relatively stable but strong trade wind
  • Temperature:26-32 degrees per year, higher humidity, higher temperature
  • typhoon:Sudden weather deterioration during the typhoon season
  • Sea sleep:Rising sea level due to climate change, risk of flooding in some areas
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:2-3 times expensive compared to Korea due to dependence on imports of goods
  • Accommodation fee:$ 150-300 per night with limited accommodations
