⚠️ Niigata travel precautions

🚌 Traffic limited precautions

Public transportation limit:It takes about 2 hours to Shinkansen in Tokyo, but when you leave the city, public transportation is very rare.

  • Bus dispatch interval:Hot spring areas, rural villages, and beach tourist attractions have only a long distance between bus dispatching or a few units a day
  • Recommended car rental:It is highly recommended to use a car rental for free movement
  • Check the rail schedule:Thoroughly check the schedule when using the train and plan to relax
  • Cancellation of operation in heavy snowfall:The bus operation may be canceled in the winter heavy snowfall, so check the weather forecast.
❄️Leading regional precautions
  • Anti -slip equipment:Preparation
  • Check weather information:Check local weather forecasts and traffic conditions from time to time
  • Mobile time spare:The train is also reduced depending on the eye, so planning the travel time

  • Difficulty using English:It is not easy to communicate in English except for some hotels and popular facilities than the local cities.
