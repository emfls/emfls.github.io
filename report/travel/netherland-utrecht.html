⚠️Witrecht travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Central Station, Area around the Canal, Night Business Street

  • pickpocket:Liberation on the central station, canal, and downtown, carry the bag in the future and lock the zipper
  • Bicycle theft:Double lock required when using bicycles
  • Night movement:Late at night, use bright and human streets, taxi/Uber use
  • Emergency number:112 (Police/first aid/firefighting)
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:OV-Chipkaart required, bus, tram, train connection convenience
  • bicycle:Recommended for developing a helmet when developing a city bicycle road
  • Taxi/Uber:Check the meter, comparison of Uber app charges
  • parking:Recommended for urban parking fare, P+R (Park & ​​Ride) recommended
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Spring (April-June):8 ~ 18 degrees, tulip season, comfortable weather
  • Summer (July-August):15 ~ 25 degrees, rain frequent, umbrella required
  • Autumn (September-October):10 ~ 18 degrees, autumn leaves, cool
  • Winter (11-3):2 ~ 8 degrees, Umjungchung, many precipitation
  • Foody weather:Layered, waterproof jacket preparation
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Dom Tower, Café and Restaurant price around the canal 2-3 times expensive
  • Museum entrance fee:Dom Tower 12 euros, Retfeld House 15.5 euros, Central Museum 13 euros
  • Tip Culture:Restaurant 5-10%, Cafe Small Tips
  • Free tap water:Recommended water bottles instead of bottled water
