⚠️ Kindi travel precautions

🚨 Alpine zone safetyism

Main precautions:Alpine zones over 2,000m above sea level, rapid weather changes

  • Altitude disease:Headaches, nausea, and shortness of breath at 2,000m above sea level
  • Weather change:Sudden drops in the mountainous region, rainfall, and waterproof clothing required
  • Lost:GPS required when trekking or GPS required, prohibited from moving alone
  • Wildlife:Wildlife habitats such as bears and wolves, food closed storage
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Mountain road unpacking sections, 4WD vehicles recommended
  • Public transportation:No public transportation, car rental or tour use to Kindi
  • Fuel supply:Gas station spacing wide, fully supplying fuel before departure
  • communication:Mountain mobile phone signal weakness, consideration of satellite communication equipment
  • Emergency situation:Far from the hospital, first aid supplies required
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):The temperature is 15-25 degrees, the daily crossover and the afternoon showers frequently
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -15 ~ 5 degrees, heavy snow and road freezing risk
  • Spring/Autumn:5-20 degrees of temperature, strong wind and rapid temperature change
  • Alpine climate:10-15 degrees lower than the flat, required for insulation clothing
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong UV rays, sunscreen and sunglasses required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash payment:Most of them are limited to cash payment, card use
  • Tour cost:Guide Tour 1 daily $ 50-100
  • lodgment:Jurd's accommodation 1 night 30-50
  • Food expenses:Local food $ 5-15, no Korean food
