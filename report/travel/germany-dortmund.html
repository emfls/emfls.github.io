⚠️Dortmund travel precautions

🌧️Checks related to weather

Optimal travel period:This is the best month to travel to Dortmund in May -September

  • Xi'an Maritime Protection Climate:There are many days in the year, but there are many days of rain and cloudy days
  • Spring (March-May):The weather is so capricious and the rain is often raining, so the umbrella and outerwear are required
  • Summer (June-August):The average daytime temperature is pleasant with 20-25 degrees, but it is necessary for sudden showers
  • Winter (12-2):There are a lot of gentle but humid and cloudy days, so the temperature can be lower.
💳 Cash use precautions
  • Cash preference:Many of the stores and restaurants in Germany still prefer cash.
  • Credit card restriction:In some cases, credit cards are not available in traditional beer shops or small stores.
  • Cash preparation:To avoid unexpected situations during the trip, always hold the appropriate amount of cash
🚇 Public transportism
  • Transportation card:Unlimited use of buses, trams and subways by purchasing VRR daily or weekly passes
  • Inspection system:Germany is a trust -based check -up system and a high fine for free rides
  • Night transportation:In late -night hours, the interval interval is longer, so it is necessary to check the timetable in advance.
⚽ Caution for watching soccer game
  • Ticket reservation:BVB economy is very popular, so pre -booking required, especially Derby matches early sold out
  • Safety rules:Respect the cheering culture in the stadium and avoid collisions with the opposing team fans
  • Traffic congestion:Expected traffic congestion around the stadium on the day of the game, traveling over time
🏪 Note that Sunday and holidays
  • Commercial facility closed:On Sundays and holidays, almost all commercial facilities, such as department stores, are closed.
  • Restaurant and Museum:Tourist spots such as dining and museums are open on holidays and are not inconvenient to travel except shopping.
  • Nortine Best Palenzhou:Because of the strong Catholic culture, there are a lot of religious holidays, so confirmation before visiting the tourist destination
🗣️ Language communication precautions
  • English communication:English works well in tourist attractions and hotels, but if you know the basic German expressions, usefulness is useful.
  • Communication with locals:If you prepare a simple German greeting or order, you can experience more comfortable experience
