  • Border area:Near the Indian border, ID card is always possessed, absolute prohibition of illegal menstruation

🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Buses and train delay frequent, need to secure free time
  • Ricksha rate:Prior to negotiations, if there is no meter
  • Road situation:Road flooding during the rainy season, preparation of alternative paths
  • Train connection:It takes about 8 hours to Taka and 12 hours to Cheetatong
  • flight:Domestic flight from Sidepur Airport to Taka
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Monsoon Season (June-September):Recommended to avoid concentrated heavy rains, flood risks, and rainy season trips
  • Winter (12-2):10-25 degrees of temperature, a good time to dry and travel
  • Summer (March-May):Temperature 25-40 degrees, very hot and humid, enough water intake
  • Cyclone:April-June, September-November cyclone season and weather information confirmation required
  • mosquito:Many mosquitoes throughout the year, malaria and dengue fever
