📅 Bergamo 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: Exploration of the Old Town

Beckia Square → Santa Maria Marjore Cathedral → Colenie Chapel → Palacho Dela Lagione → Loka Citadel

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:Explore a unique city in the hidden jewelry Bergamo in Lombardia, where the charm of the Middle Ages and the modern sophistication are harmonized. At the Beckia Square, the center of the old town (Cheetah Alta), enjoy the beautiful scenery of the Renaissance and Gothic -style buildings, and take a look at the colorful frescoes and wooden choruses at the Romanesque Santa Maria Marjore Cathedral built in the 12th century. Visit the tombs of Bergamo's military leader Bartolomeo Coleni in the choleri chapel, which is impressed by the delicate sculptures and colorful exterior, and enjoy the fantastic view of the Alps and Bergamo in the Loca Citadel.
📍Beckia Square 📍Santa Maria Marjore Cathedral 📍Colloni Chapel 📍 Loka Citadel

Day 2: Visiting New Town and Culture

Gaetano Donisetti Theater → Central Park → Cable Car Experience → Palacho Moroni → Local Market

Recommended time:8 hours 30 minutes
Core points:Experience the charm of a lively modern Bergamo in the New Town (Chitabasa), and enjoy the magnificent and beautiful architecture in honor of the composers from Bergamo at the Gaedano Donisetti Theater, which boasts world -class opera performances and concerts. Take a cable car that connects the old town and the new town to enjoy the open view of Bergamo during the short travel time, and explore the sophisticated frescoes and two hectares from Moroni, the first urban Palacho, Italy, built in the 17th century.
📍Gaetano Donisetti Theater 📍Central Park 📍 cable car 📍Palacho Moroni

Day 3: Alps trekking and gourmet tour (optional)

Valve Rembo Valley → Balcerina Valley → Traditional Food Tour → Local Restaurant → Souvenir Shopping

Alps trekking:It takes 4 hours, enjoys hiking and trekking in the beautiful Alps around Bergamo, and experience the magnificent experience of nature
Traditional food tour:3 hours and 30 minutes, traditional Bergamo regions such as Kason Celly Pasta, Polenta and Talgesio Cheese
Local market:It takes 1 hour, fresh agricultural products, traditional cheese, wine and crafts at the market held every Saturday

⚠️ Bergamo travel precautions

🚨 Pickpocket attention

Major areas:Areas with many tourists such as Vergamo Train Station and Beckia Square

  • Storage of valuables:Keep your passport or big money in a safe place, and only small cash
  • Bag management:The bag is carried forward and locked the zipper, especially in the crowds.
  • Tourististism:Especially in famous tourist destinations and public transportation
  • When using a cable car:Thorough management of belongings when boarding a cable car
🎫 Prior reservation recommended
  • Palacho Moroni:In peak season, pre -booking recommendation, especially weekends and public holidays
  • Gaetano Donisetti Theater:Pre -booking required for watching opera performances
  • Traditional food tour:3 hours and 30 minutes of gourmet tours are popular and recommended in advance
  • Alps trekking:Trekking tours with the guide require pre -booking
🚌 Precautions on public transportation
  • Milan connection:Bergamo is about 1 hour by train from Milan Malpensa Airport
  • Cable car operation:Check the cable car operation time connecting the old town and the new town
  • walking tour:Old town branches can walk on a sightseeing size
  • Parking:Old towns are recommended to use public transportation because vehicle traffic is restricted.
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):High temperatures and strong sunlight, sunscreen and hat required
  • Winter (12-2):Cold and occasional eyes, warm coat preparation
  • Spring/Autumn:Preparation of outerwear because the crossover may be large
  • In preparation for rain:Preparation of umbrella in preparation for sudden rain
🗣️ Language communication

Italian is official language, but English can be communicated in tourist attractions. Vergamo is an international tourist destination, so English guidance is provided at most restaurants and attractions.
