Day 1: Visit to Lata City and Nendo Island

Core points:In Lata, Toptu, we experience the unique culture of the east end of the Solomon Islands, enjoy warm meetings with locals in a small village of 553 people, tours the main facilities of Lata, such as Themetu Hospital, Post Office, and Telecommunications Office, observe the traditional way of Melanesia in the Matamotu area, and the beautiful coastline of Nendo Island Watch coral reefs and experience traditional outboard canoes
