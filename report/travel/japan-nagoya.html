⚠️ Nagoya travel precautions

🚇 Precautions on transportation

Transportation convenience:Nagoya is located in the central region of Japan, so it is convenient to transportation from Tokyo and Osaka, but there are precautions for local transportation.

  • Nagoya Transportation Card Use:Economic movement with transportation cards available at subway, bus and convenience stores
  • Nagoya 1 -day subway pass:Convenient for sightseeing with unlimited use of subways all day long
  • Avoid commuting time:7-9 am and 5-7 pm are very crowded, so consider the moving plan.
  • Check the bus schedule:When using local buses, it is necessary to check the exact timetable and route
🏨 Precautions when booking
  • Reservation in advance:Nagoya Station and Saka are very popular near Saka, so there may be no rooms in the peak season.
  • Hotel selection:Various options from business hotels to luxury hotels
  • Location consideration:Nagoya Station, Sakae, and near Fushimi are convenient for sightseeing and transportation
  • Cherry Blossom Season:In late March-in the early April cherry blossom season, accommodation rates rose significantly
🌡️Checks and costumes
  • Spring (April-June):Travel recommendation, light jacket preparation as the most pleasant time
  • Summer (July-September):It is essential for clothing and sunscreen that are well ventilated with hot and humid weather
  • Autumn (September-November):Good to travel with cool and pleasant weather
  • Winter (12-3):It is cold and dry and required for winter products such as coats and shawls
🍽️ Food and cultural precautions
  • Cash use:In small shops and restaurants, there are many places where only cash is received.
  • Nagoya Food:Misokatsu 1,200-1,500 yen, Hitsumabushi 2,500-3,000 yen
  • Restaurant reservation:Popular restaurants are required for advance reservations, especially dinner time zones
  • Tip Culture:Japan does not have a tip culture, so you don't have to give it a separate tip.
🗣️ Language and manner precautions
  • Japanese translation app:Tourist destinations are English, but for detailed communication, the translation app is prepared
  • Garbage processing:There is almost no trash can on the street, so prepare a small plastic bag
  • Public transportation manners:Do not speak loudly on the train, to give up priority
  • Meeting tradition and modernity:The attitude of understanding and respecting Nagoya's cultural differences
💳 Payment and shopping precautions
  • Card payment:Large stores can be paid for cards, but small shops only receive cash.
  • Tax -free shopping:Duty -free tax exemption when purchased more than 5,000 yen, it is required to bring a passport
  • Business hours:Most of the shops are closed at 8-9 pm, so consider the shopping time
  • souvenir:Nagoya specialties include Matcha Keycat, Golden Nagoya Castle Magnetic, and Pottery.
