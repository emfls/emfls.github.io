  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing in the market and downtown, packing bags in the future and locking zipper
  • Motorcycle theft:Walking inside India,

🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Encourage locals when using public transportation in Avo
  • Taxi:Only official taxis are used, required to check the meter
