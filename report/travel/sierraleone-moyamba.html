📅 Moan bar 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: Examination of the city center and history

Moan Bar Central Market → Sewa River → Moan Bar Museum → Traditional Village

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the central market where local people can experience the daily lives of locals in the central city of Sirarion, West Africa, they can see local agricultural products and traditional crafts such as palm oil, rice, and cassava, see the fishing activities and riverside landscapes in Sewa River, the life ship of the Kojanba area, and preserve the local history and culture at the Moan Bar Museum. Explore traditional culture and colonial artifacts, and experience the traditional architectural style and tribal community culture of mud houses and toe roofs in the surrounding traditional villages
📍Mowanba Central Market 📍Sewa River 📍Mowan Bar Museum 📍 Traditional village

Day 2: Nature and Mine Experience

Diamond Mine Tour → Tropical Woorim Tracking → Visit Falls → Observation of Wildlife

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:Visiting the site of Diamond mining, which is the main export of Sierraion, understanding the reality and mining process of African mineral resources, observing the precious wood and various tropical plants such as Mahogani and Iroko through trekking in the dense nature of the West African rainforest, and enjoying the magnificence of nature in the local waterfall forming the minister of Woo -Gi -Cheol. Experiencing the diversity of ecosystems by observing West Africa's own wild animals such as Diana monkeys and various birds.
📍Diamond mine 📍 Tropical rainforest 📍 Falls 📍 Wildlife habitat

Day 3: Cultural Experience and Vaccinations

Traditional craft experience → tribal dance performance → Palm wine manufacturing tour → Coconut farm

Traditional crafts:3 hours, with local craftsmen, wooden sculptures, weaving fabrics, and making ceramics
Tribal dance:Takes 2 hours, tour and participation in traditional dance and music performances of Temnes and Mendes
Palm wine:2 hours, tour of traditional fermented stocks extracted from palm trees
Coconut Farm:3 hours, experience the field of tropical agriculture and fresh coconut water tasting
📍 Traditional Craft Center 📍 Culture Theater 📍Malm wine manufacturer 📍Coconut Farm

⚠️Mojanba travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:All areas of the night time, outskirts

  • Prohibition of going out at night:Restricted out after sunset, recommended rest in accommodation
  • Cash management:Prohibiting large amounts of cash, required for distributed storage
  • Prohibition of solo behavior:Must be accompanied by local guides, prohibiting walking alone
  • Political Assembly:Prohibition of access to political meetings or protest areas
  • Photo shooting:Government building, absolute prohibition of shooting military facilities
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:There are many unpaved roads, bad roads, very bad
  • Public transportation:Uncovered minibus, use of exclusive vehicles as much as possible
  • drive:Direct driving cost recommendation without local driving experience
  • fuel:Lack of gas station, difficulty in securing fuel
  • communication:Mobile phone signal instability, securing emergency contact means
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (May-October):Rain and flood risks, restrictions on moving
  • Dry (11-4):High temperature drying, dehydration
  • Hamatan:December-February Sahara Desert sand breeze, respiratory attention
  • humidity:High humidity, mold prevention
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, burnism near the equator
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:High risk of infection throughout the year, required for preventive drugs

  • drinking water:Prohibition of drinking tap water, only bottled water
