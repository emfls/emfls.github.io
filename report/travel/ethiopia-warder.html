⚠️ Warrader travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Borders around the border and night time market

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing at the market and bus terminal, carrying bags in the future and storing valuables
  • robbery:Don't go alone when you go out at night, accompany you with local guides
  • Lack of conflict:Evacuate to a safe place immediately when conflicts occur between regions
  • Border area:Near the somali border, it is unstable, so it prohibits access
  • Photo shooting:Absolute prohibition of shooting military facilities or government buildings
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Wearing speed and overload, seat belt when using bus or minibus
  • Road status:There are many unpaved roads and the road condition is very bad in the rainy season
  • Night movement:Night long distance movement is very dangerous, so avoid it
  • Rent -a -car:Recommended for using car rentals without local driving experience
  • flight:Use domestic flights in Adis Ababa, the possibility of cancellation due to weather
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (10-5):The temperature is 25-35 degrees, very dry and dusty
  • Woogi (June-September):Temperature 20-30 degrees, sudden heavy rain and flood risk
  • Daily crossroads:Daily and night temperature differences, so it is required
  • ultraviolet rays:UV rays are very strong and sunscreen required due to the high altitude
  • dust:In case of dry season, it is necessary to protect the respiratory system with severe dust
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:High risk of mosquito -mediated diseases, required to take preventive drugs
  • yellow fever:Required vaccination, checking certificate of inoculation when entering Korea
  • drinking water:Drink only bottled water and avoid ice, use integer tablets
  • food:Avoid street foods and eat only foods that are completely cooked
  • Medical facilities:Lack of local medical facilities, transfer to Adisa Baba in an emergency
💰 Price related precautions
  • Exchange rate fluctuations:Ethiopia Vir exchange rate fluctuations, dollar cash preparation
  • Bargaining:In the market, bargaining is common, 30-50% of the first price
  • Tip Culture:Guide, $ 5-10 a day to the driver
  • Card use:Limited credit card use, cash -oriented transactions
