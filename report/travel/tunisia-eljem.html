자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main attractions included in the Tunisia Elg Tour itinerary?

The main attractions include the Elgem Circular Stadium, known as the world’s third largest Roman amphitheater built in 238 B.C.E., the Elgem Museum showcasing Roman mosaics and relics, the Old Town where traditional Tunisian culture thrives, and the traditional market.

How much time should I plan for the Day 1 tour in Eljem?

It is recommended to allocate about 8 hours on Day 1 to fully explore the Elgem Circular Stadium, Elgem Museum, Old Town, and the traditional market to experience the historical and cultural highlights.

What unique historical experiences can travelers expect at the Elgem Circular Stadium?

Travelers can explore the ancient Roman gladiator history, including visiting the underground prison, beasts’ enclosures, gladiator waiting rooms, and learn about the stadium’s role as a filming location for the Gladiator movie, all within a structure that once accommodated 35,000 spectators.
