⚠️Dret travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Northern Desert area, late at night

  • Terrorism:Northern Marley Terrorist Dangerous Area
  • Armed group:Moving mandatory with local guides, armed group activities for the desert area
  • Night going out:Absolutely prohibited to go alone after sunset
  • checkpoint:Passports and visas are always available at the military checkpoint
  • Photo shooting:Military facilities and government buildings prohibited
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, recommend using 4WD vehicles
  • Public transportation:Bus operation irregularities, preliminary reservations required
  • flight:Domestic flights in Bamako, delayed due to weather
  • fuel:Gas station rarely prepared enough fuel
  • Desert Movement:GPS and compass required, accompanied by local guides
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):15-35 degrees of temperature, dry and sand breeze
  • Woogi (May-October):Temperature 25-40 degrees, moist and flood risk
  • Harmatan:December-February Sahara sand breeze, respiratory protection required
  • Daily crossroads:More than 20 degrees of desert area, preparing for warming clothing
  • ultraviolet rays:Desert strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat required
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Mosquito -mediated diseases, prevention drugs and insect repellent use
  • yellow fever:Request for vaccination, inspection when entering the certificate
  • Water -person disease:Drink only bottled water and eat only cooked food
  • Medical facilities:Lack of medical facilities, transfer of Bamako in an emergency
  • dehydration:Desert risk of dehydration due to desert climate, enough water intake
