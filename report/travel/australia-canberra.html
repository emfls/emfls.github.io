⚠️ Canberra travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Downtown downtown

  • pickpocket:Subtitleism in downtown, tourist attractions, and public transportation, the bag is carried in the future and the zipper lock
  • Bicycle/Vehicle theft:Bicycles must be locked, no valuables in vehicles
  • Going out late at night:Recommended to use taxi/shared vehicles, avoid moving alone
  • Emergency situation:Emergency call 000, police/medical/firefighting all corresponds to this
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Bus, tram (light rail) available, myway required
  • Rent -a -car:Leave the left side, international driver's license required, parking fee confirmation
  • Walking:Major tourist attractions can be walked away, but some streets can be far.
  • bicycle:Helmet required, duty to install night light
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (12-2):12 ~ 28 degrees, dry and sunlight, UV protection required
  • Winter (June-August):1 ~ 12 degrees, morning evening very cold, preparation for winter clothes
  • Spring/Autumn:6-20 degrees, a large day crossing, the outerwear is required
  • rainy season:November -March showers available, umbrella preparation
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Urban, the price of cafes and restaurants around the lake, 1.5 ~ 2 times expensive
  • Museum entrance fee:Major National Museum Free, some special exhibitions
  • Tip Culture:Basically, no tips, 5-10% can be selected when satisfied with the service
  • Wine and coffee:Local wine and coffee prices diverse, winery tours
