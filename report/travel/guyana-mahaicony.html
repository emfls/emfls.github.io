📅 Mahaikoni 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: Riverside culture and fishing experience

Mahaikoni River → Traditional Fishing Experience → Explore Riverside Village → Local Fish Market

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the Mahaikoni River in the Gaiana East Coastal area, the traditional fishing culture was experienced, and in a small fishing village of 2,000 people, learning net fishermen with local fishermen, looking at the traditional houses formed along the riverside, watching the vast water system from the Mahaikoni River Estuary to the Atlantic Ocean. See fresh river meat and sea fish and explore the center of local fishing economy

Day 2: Agricultural Tourism and Nature Exploration

Rice Farm Tour → Sugar Sorghum Farm → Tropical Fruit Farm → Mangrove Forest Exploration

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:In the Mahaikoni, a major agricultural area of ​​Giaiana, you can visit a large -scale rice farm, learn the rice cultivation and harvest systems in the tropical climate, experience the traditional sugar manufacturing process in sugar cane farms, understand the core of the Kaiana agricultural economy, and pick up mango, papaya, avocado, and guava at various tropical fruit farms. In the Mangrove Forest of the Mahaikoni Estuary, observed unique ecosystems and various birds and enjoys exploring nature by riding canoe.

⚠️ Machoni travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever, Zika virus

  • malaria:Prevention drug required, mosquito repellent regular use, long -sleeved clothes
  • yellow fever:Implied vaccination before entering the country, bringing in a certificate of inoculation
  • Anti -mosquitoes:Recommended for using especially at dawn and evening hours, mosquito nets
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, avoid drinks with ice
  • food:Tell us only completely cooked food, avoid raw or less ripe food
🌊 River activity safety rules
  • Swimming Prohibition Zone:In the Ganghae area, the algae are strong, so it is dangerous, and only in the designated area.
  • Wildlife:Kaman Crocodile, Piranha Formation, Local Guide Combined with
  • Life jacket:Must wear when using boats
  • Check the weather:Sudden heavy rain and strong windism
  • Emergency situation:Using a mobile phone waterproof case, familiar with emergency contacts
🌡️ Preparation for tropical climate
  • Woogi (May-August):It is possible to deteriorate heavy rain and flood risks and road conditions
  • Dry (September-April):Strong sunlight and high humidity, dehydration
  • Daily crossroads:30-35 degrees during the day, 22-25 degrees at night, prepare for proper clothes
  • ultraviolet rays:Very strong, applying sunscreen from time to time
  • heatstroke:Eat enough water, rest in the shade
💰 Local economic situation
  • Cash Society:Most of them are limited to cash transactions, credit card use
  • Exchange:There is no bank in George Town in advance, and in Mahaikoni
  • price:Basic household goods are expensive, and imports are very high
  • Tip Culture:10-15% tips for guides and boat drivers
