What is the recommended itinerary for a 2-night, 3-day trip to Encarnacion, Paraguay?

The recommended itinerary includes exploring Encarnacion Central Street, visiting the Jesus Association Ruins—a UNESCO World Heritage Site—touring the San Roque Gonzalez Cathedral, and enjoying the Costanera Promenade along the Parana River, with an emphasis on history, culture, and scenic views.

The guide highlights the Jesus Association Ruins from the 17th and 18th centuries, designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and the San Roque Gonzalez Cathedral, which honors the city's guardian and reflects Paraguay's Catholic heritage.

What should travelers expect when visiting the Costanera Promenade in Encarnacion?

Travelers can expect a unique landscape along the Parana River, with views of the border city facing Argentine Posadas, offering a scenic and relaxing promenade experience after exploring the city's historical and cultural sites.
