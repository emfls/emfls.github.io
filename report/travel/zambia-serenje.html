⚠️Serenje travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the bus stop, late at night

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing at the bus stop and the market, the bag is carried in the future and the zipper lock
  • Wildlife:Do not go alone without a guide in the national park, but keep the distance safe
  • Fake guide:Unofficial guideism at tourist destinations, confirming accredited guides
  • Going out late at night:Caution to go alone after the sun, especially the market and bus stop
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Tazara Railroad:It runs twice a week and takes 5 hours 46 minutes to Sukpica
  • Bus traffic:Zulki Motors Bus Operation once a day, 3 hours 18 minutes up to Sukpica
  • Road status:In the rainy season, the road condition is poor, four -wheel drive vehicle recommended
  • Fuel supply:Use total gas stations, check fuel before long distance moving
  • Great North Road:3 km from major main roads
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (11-3):Waterfall tourism optimal with a temperature of 22-27 degrees, a lot of precipitation
  • Dry (5-10):17-31 degrees, dry and sunny, wildlife observation optimal
  • The hottest moon:In October-November, up to 36 degrees
  • The coldest moon:July, you can lower up to 2 degrees
  • humidity:An average of 64% per year, 85% or more in rainy season
💰 Price related precautions
  • lodgment:Guest House $ 20-40, only basic facilities
  • Food expenses:Local restaurant $ 5-15, Western food is more expensive
  • Transportation cost:Bus $ 5-10, Taxi $ 15-25
  • National Park Admission fee:Foreigner $ 25-30
  • Tip Culture:Guide $ 5-10, hotel employee $ 2-5
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Essential risk of annual occurrence, prevention drugs required
  • yellow fever:Required vaccination required, confirming certificate when entering Korea
  • drinking water:Purchase of bottled water or boiled water
  • Medical facilities:There are only small hospitals, transfer to Rusaka in an emergency
