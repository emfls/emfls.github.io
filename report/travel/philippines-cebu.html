📅 Cebu 3 nights and 4 days travel course

Day 1: Exploring Cebu City History and Culture

Magellan Cross → Santon Nino Cathedral → San Pedro Fort → Coron Street → Mango Square

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:Experienced the historical meaning on Magellan's cross to announce the beginning of the Philippine Christianity, enjoys the best religious artifacts of the Philippines at the Santo Nino Cathedral, the oldest Roman Catholic Church in the Philippines, explores the history of the Spanish colonial era in San Pedro fortress, the oldest military fortress in the Philippines, and is the oldest national road in the Philippines. Experience life and enjoy the local food and drinks by enjoying bars, clubs and live music at Mango Square, Cebu's representative nightlife area.
📍Mazellan's cross 📍Santo Nino Cathedral 📍San Pedro Fort 📍 Coron Street

Day 2: Natural Landscape and Outlook Experience

Tops Observatory → Taoist Temple → Ayala Center Cebu → Larsian BBQ

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:At Tops Observation Deck, where you can enjoy the breathtaking view of Cebu City and surrounding islands, you can experience the perfect place of photography, and enjoy the colorful Chinese architecture and peaceful atmosphere in Taoist Temple, where you can see the influence of Chinese culture in detail, and modern shopping in Cebu's best shopping, meals, and entertainment destinations. Enjoy experiences and experience local food culture at Larsian BBQ, a popular street food market where you can enjoy various BBQ dishes and seafood.
📍Tops Observatory 📍Taoist Temple 📍Ayala Center Details 📍Larsian BBQ

Day 3: Kawasan Falls and Adventure Experience

Kawasan Falls → Cannearing Experience → Moaloal → Sarder Run
