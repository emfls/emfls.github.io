⚠️ Yangpyeong travel precautions

🚨 Safety precautions

Major areas:Yongmunsan Mountain Trail, Namhan River Bicycle Road, Night Time Zone

  • Climbing safety:Prohibit the departure of the trail when climbing in Yongmunsan, enough water intake and proper rest
  • Bicycle safety:Wearing helmets, speed control and pedestrian priority when using the Namhan River Bicycle Road
  • Water Play:Do not swim on the head and riverside, maintain a safe distance
  • Night activity:Wildflower Arboretum LED Festival Moves with dark spots, companions
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Use the subway Gyeongui -Jungang Line from Seoul to Yangpyeong, takes about 1 hour and 30 minutes
  • Usage of buses:Yangpyeong City Bus Interview 30 minutes-1 hour, timetable check in advance
  • Rent -a -car:Weekend and holiday traffic jams severe, secure free time
  • parking:Popular parking lot of popular attractions such as Dumul Head and Semi -Won, recommended use of public transportation
  • Bicycle rental:Check the operation hours of the Namhan River Bicycle Road Road, and recommend pre -booking
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):25-35 degrees of temperature, moist and hot, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -10-5 degrees, riverside wind cold, winter supplies required
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and sudden showers, umbrellas and outerwear preparation
  • Riverside climate:The Han River basin has high humidity and frequent fog
  • ultraviolet rays:Outdoor activities required for sunscreen, recommended wearing hat
💰 Cost -related precautions
  • Tourist attraction:Semiwon admission fee 5,000 won, Yongmunsa entrance fee 2,500 won
  • Experience cost:Strawberry picking experience 15,000-25,000 won, rail bike 25,000 won
  • Eating costs:15,000-30,000 won per local restaurant
  • Accommodation cost:Pension 10-200,000 won, hotel 15-300,000 won (per night)
