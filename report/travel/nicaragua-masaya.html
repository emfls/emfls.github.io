🌋 Volcano -related safety precautions
  • During the eruption time:Do not stay for more than 5 minutes in Santiago crater
  • Sulfur gas:Respiratory diseases are required to wear masks, gas concentrations are high immediately evacuated.
  • Safety Fence:Never be passed on safety fences around the crater
  • Check the weather:Volcanic tours can be canceled in strong winds or bad weather
  • Emergency situation:In case of an emergency in the volcanic area, you should inform the guide immediately.
