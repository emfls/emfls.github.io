⚠️Ricip travel precautions

🚨 Safety and healthism

Main precautions:Marine activity, tropical climate, restriction on medical facilities

  • Marine safety:Snorkeling with scuba diving, must accompany the guide, not entering the sea alone
  • Coral reef protection:Do not touch or step on coral, sunscreen uses coral safety products
  • Jellyfishism:Immediately first aid when contacting transparent jellyfish or toxic marine organisms
  • Medical facilities:Since local medical facilities are limited, sufficient medicine and travel insurance required
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Airlines limit:Marshall Islands access flights are very limited, preliminary reservation required
  • Local transportation:There are few public transportation, so you can walk, bicycle, or boat
  • Boat safety:Move after checking the weather when moving between islands, wearing a life jacket
  • Lack of fuel:Fuel supply may be unstable, so prepare for the possibility of changing the plan
  • Communication Restriction:The Internet and telephone connections may be unstable
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Tropical climate:Desisement at 26-32 degrees throughout the year, high humidity
  • Woogi (5-November):Restrictions on scalls and typhoons, marine activities
  • Dry (12-4):Relatively dry but still high humidity
  • Strong ultraviolet light:UV rays very strong, enough to block near the equator
  • Sea level rise:Influence of sea level rise due to climate change
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:Prices are very high by relying on imports of all items
  • Limited grocery:Fresh food shortage, canned food and frozen food
  • Fuel cost:Power and fuel costs are very high
  • Cash payment:Limited to use credit cards, preparing for enough cash
