⚠️Hentis Bay Travel Note

🚨 Natural environment and safetyism

Main risk factors:Strong currents, sharks, desert climate

  • Prohibition of beach:Cold water temperature (less than 23 degrees) and strong waves due to Bengal Korean Wave
  • Sharkism:It is rich in plankton, so there are so many sharks inhabiting and need to be careful when fishing on the beach
  • Desert:A large crossover and a sudden sand storm can occur
  • Risk of isolation:GPS and sufficient water and fuel required when moving to the desert area
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Lack of public transportation:Hentis Bay is very limited in public transportation, requiring a rental car or tour
  • 4WD required:In the desert area and some coastal roads, only four -wheel drive vehicles are accessible.
  • Fuel supply:Since the gas station is limited, the fuel condition is always checked
  • Road status:There are many sand and gravel roads, tire punk dangers
