⚠️ Mahaika travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, dengue fever, yellow fever, Zika virus

  • Yellow fever vaccination:Essential requirements for entry, completion of 10 days before departure
  • Malaria prevention:Essential to take preventive drugs and use mosquito repellents
  • Drinking water safety:Drink only bottled water and take care of ice
  • Wildlife:Dangerous animalism such as crocodiles and snakes during the river tour
  • Emergency situation:Restrictions on medical facilities, preparation
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, traffic difficulties in rainy season
  • Public transportation:Limited, minibus and taxi mainly used
  • Kang Transportation:Wearing life jackets when using boats
  • drive:List on the left side, the international driver's license required
  • fuel:Gas station spacing, secure enough fuel
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (September-April):Temperature 24-31 degrees, high humidity, tourism timely
  • Woogi (May-August):Temperature 23-29 degrees, heavy rains and flood risk
  • Tropical climate:High temperature and humidity, enough water intake
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, UV protection required near the equator
  • Sudden shower:Umbrella and raincoats are always ready
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash -oriented:Limited card use, enough cash preparation
  • Exchange:Local exchange in the US dollar with Gyana dollar
  • Tour cost:50-100 Gaiana Dollar per person
  • Tip Culture:10-15% tip for guides and boat drivers
