자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main historical attractions to visit in Kavala on the first day?

On the first day in Kavala, visitors should explore Kavala Castle, the Archaeological Museum, the Roman Songsuro waterway, the Ottoman-era Imaret complex, and the Old Town Panagia area, all showcasing Byzantine and Roman architectural marvels.

How much time should travelers allocate to explore Kavala’s historical center?

Travelers are recommended to spend about 8 hours on Day 1 to thoroughly explore the historical center of Kavala, including the castle, museums, and other key landmarks.

What unique experiences does the Imaret complex offer to visitors?

The Imaret complex in Kavala is a restored Ottoman-era building now serving as boutique hotels, allowing visitors to experience historical architecture combined with modern hospitality in a culturally rich setting.
