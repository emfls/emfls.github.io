⚠️Shuzle travel precautions

🚨 Safety and security attention

Major areas:Castress Night, Loan Beach Area

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketism in tourist attractions and markets, and valuables are stored in hotel safes.
  • Night going out:Avoid going alone after 9 pm and recommend using taxi
  • Beach safety:Do not swim alone on a secluded beach, wearing a life jacket
  • Fake Tour:Unofficial Tour Guideism, official license confirmation required
🚗 Traffic and driving precautions
  • Left traffic:British left pass, special care when driving
  • Mountain road:The winding mountain trails, steep slope roads, and low speed driving required
  • Rent -a -car:Need to issue a local temporary driver's license (30 xcd)
  • Public transportation:Preparation of accurate charges when using minibus
  • Taxi fee:No meter, preliminary fee negotiations required
🌡️ Weather and natural disasters
  • Hurricane Season:June-November Hurricane season, occasional weather information check
  • Scol:Sudden tropical showers, umbrella always portable
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays near the equator, sunscreen must be recurred every 2 hours
  • Current:Strong currents and waves of some beaches
  • Volcanic activity:Guide instructions when visiting Sulper Springs
💰 Price and tip culture
  • High prices:High prices due to import dependence, fully prepared for budget
  • Tip Culture:Restaurant 10-15%, Hotel House Keeping 2-5 USD
  • Tourist destination:Purchase after comparing the price of tourist attractions
  • Cash payment:Prefer cash payment in small stores
