🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Buses and taxis can be used, and there may be a long interval of dispatching.
  • Rent -a -car:Recommended for moving after car rental in nearby large cities such as Belgrad
  • Walking:Snown and natural attractions have a lot of walking, so you can prepare comfortable shoes

  • Summer (June-August):Temperature 20-30 degrees, hot day, cool night, and water intake required

  • Mountain area:The highlands are lower, so the layered outfit is recommended
