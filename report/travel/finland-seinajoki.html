⚠️Sina Yoki Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Some of the ports around the central station, late at night

  • Security Good:Finland is one of the safest countries in the world, but basic attention is needed.
  • Bicycle theft:Be sure to use lock when bicycle rental
  • Wildlife:Possibility to encounter wildlife such as bears and wolves in nature protection zones, recommended guides
  • Emergency situation:Emergency call 112, English communication with medical staff
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation limit:Due to the nature of small towns, public transportation routes and operating hours limit
  • Rent -a -car recommendation:Recommended for using car rentals for free movement
  • Winter driving:Snow tires required in winter, attention
  • Parking fee:Fresh parking and parking disk to be paid to the city center.
  • Train connection:About 3 hours in Helsinki, 1 hour and 30 minutes in Tampere
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):The temperature is 15-25 degrees, bright until 11 pm due to white night phenomenon
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -20 degrees ~ -5 degrees, darkness from 3 pm due to an extreme phenomenon
  • Spring/Autumn:Daily cross -crossing and raining often, waterproof clothing required
  • Aurora:Aurora observation can be observed on a sunny day in winter
  • ultraviolet rays:Long -term sunlight exposure caused by summer white nights
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:Due to the nature of Nordic, 2-3 times higher than Korea
  • Liquor price:Alcohol tax is high, so alcohol prices are very expensive
  • Tip Culture:Restaurant 10-15%, Taxi Round, Hotel House Keeping 2-5 euros
  • Card payment:Most of them are available for card payments, low frequency of cash use
