자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main attractions to visit on the first day in Bishakapatnam?

On the first day, visitors should explore Rama Krishna Beach for a scenic sunrise and beach walk, visit the iconic Bishakapatnam Lighthouse to learn about the city's port history, tour the INS Kurusura Submarine Museum to understand Indian Navy heritage, and end the day at Beach Park to enjoy a Mediterranean garden landscape and romantic sunset.

How much time should I plan for the Day 1 travel course in Bishakapatnam?

The recommended time to complete the Day 1 itinerary in Bishakapatnam is approximately 9 hours, allowing ample time to experience each attraction thoroughly and enjoy the coastal environment.

What makes Bishakapatnam a unique travel destination on India’s east coast?

Bishakapatnam is unique for its combination of golden sandy beaches and rich maritime history, including the world’s only submarine museum, a historic lighthouse symbolizing the port city, and scenic Mediterranean-style gardens, offering travelers a blend of natural beauty and cultural heritage.
