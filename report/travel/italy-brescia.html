⚠️ Brescia travel precautions

🚨 Pickpocket attention

Major areas:Areas with many tourists, including Brescia Central Station and Piacha Dela Logia

  • Storage of valuables:Keep your passport or big money in a safe place, and only small cash
  • Bag management:The bag is carried forward and locked the zipper, especially in the crowds.
  • Tourististism:Especially in famous tourist destinations and public transportation
  • 밤늦은 시간:Don't go around late at night and go around with your companion.
🎫 Prior reservation recommended
  • Santa Julia Museum:In peak season, pre -booking recommendation, especially weekends and public holidays
  • Brecia Castle:Open from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm, it can be closed depending on the weather
  • Lake Tour:Lake Lake and Lake Lee Se -oh are discounts for pre -booking
  • Local restaurant:Dinner is recommended for pre -booking, especially weekends
🚌 Precautions on public transportation
  • Milan connection:Brecia is about 1 hour by train from Milan
  • Verona connection:It is possible to hit the day from Verona to 40 minutes by train
  • City Bus:Modern and safe bus system, safe even if you travel alone
  • Night use:Caution when using public transportation late at night, especially around the train station
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Spring (March-May):Brescia travel optimal, temperature at 13-22 degrees
  • Summer (June-August):High temperatures and strong sunlight, sunscreen and hat required
  • Winter (12-2):Cold and occasional eyes, warm coat preparation
  • In preparation for rain:Preparation of umbrella in preparation for sudden rain
🗣️ Language communication

Italian is official language, but English can be communicated in tourist attractions. Brecia is a city where more locals live than international tourist destinations, so it is helpful to learn basic Italian greetings.
