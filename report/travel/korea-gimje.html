⚠️ Gimje travel precautions

🚨 Safety precautions

Major areas:Moaksan Mountain Trail, Mangyeong River Waterside Area

  • Climbing safety:Sufficient preparation and safety equipment when climbing Moaksan Mountain, and completed the sun mall
  • Waterside safety:Mangyeonggang and Gimje Soo -byeon Park
  • Rural areas:Maintain safe distances on farm roads working on agricultural machinery
  • Wildlife:Possibility of wild animals such as wild boars and snakes in the mountainous areas
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:There is a long interval between the city buses, so it is necessary to check the timetable in advance.
  • Self -use:Many farm roads and narrow roads, navigation required
  • parking:Geumsansa Temple, Wall Solvice, etc.
  • taxi:Reservations are recommended in advance because the number of taxis in the city is small
  • bicycle:Wearing helmets when using Mangyeong River Bicycle Road
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):25-35 degrees of temperature, high heat and humidity, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -5-10 degrees, plain winds strong
  • Spring/Autumn:Daily cross -day, yellow dust, fine dust care
  • Rainy season:Restrictions on outdoor activities due to heavy rains in June-July
  • typhoon:Outdoor activity risk due to the typhoon impact in August-September
💰 Price related precautions
  • lodgment:Peak season (festival period) 2-3 times higher accommodation expenses
  • food:The price of restaurants around the tourist destination is high, recommended to use the traditional market
  • Parking fee:Paid parking lot of major tourist attractions
  • entrance fee:Geumsansa admission fee 3,000 won
