⚠️Raspecia travel precautions

🚨 Pickpocket attention

Major areas:Areas with many tourists such as Las Pesian Central Station, Cinque Terre Train Station, and Tourist Site

  • Storage of valuables:Keep your passport or big money in a safe place, and only small cash
  • Bag management:The bag is carried forward and locked the zipper, especially in the crowds.
  • Train striker:Thoroughly, while lining up when purchasing Cinque Terre Card,
  • Beachism:Thoroughly managed belongings when swimming or sunbathing on the beach
🎫 Prior reservation recommended
  • Cinque Terre Card:In the peak season, we recommend that you book an online pre -booking because you have to stand long lines for the purchase of Cinque Terre Card.
  • Perry Tour:Ferry can be canceled depending on the weather, so it is required to confirm the day.
  • Hiking Tour:Prior reservation recommended for the Cinque Terre Hiking Guide Tour
  • lodgment:Hotels near the central station of Las Pesia are difficult to make reservations in the peak season, so booking in advance
🚌 Precautions on public transportation
  • Milan connection:Laseschia is about 3 hours by train from Milan
  • Cinque Terre Movement:5 Vinque Terre is connected by train and 10 euros one way, 38 euros of Cinque Terre Pass
  • Perry:Perry is operated only in summer and can be canceled depending on the weather.
  • Train reservation:In the peak season
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):High temperatures and strong sunlight, sunscreen and hat required
  • Optimal period:Spring (April-June) and autumn (September-October) are the most pleasant travel time
  • Beach activity:Sufficient water intake and shade rest during summer beach activities
  • In preparation for rain:Preparation of umbrella in preparation for sudden showers
🗣️ Language communication

Italian is official language, but English can be communicated in tourist attractions. Since Cinque Terre is an international tourist destination, there is a creating an environment where you can communicate in various languages.
