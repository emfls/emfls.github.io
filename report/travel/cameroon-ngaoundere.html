🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Borders around the border area,

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing at the central market, bus terminal, packing bags in the future and locking zipper
  • robbery:Prohibition of going out late at night, especially not alone, accompanied by local guides
  • fraud:Taxi driverism that requires fake guides or excessive charges
  • Border area:Chad and Central African Borders are prohibited from political instability
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Buses and taxis are poor, wearing seat belts
  • Road status:Road flooding and porthole attention during the rainy season
  • Taxi fee:No meter, preliminary fee negotiations required
  • Train connection:Yaound (8 hours), Duala (10 hours) Train connection
  • flight:Domestic flights can be used at the middle airport
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-3):The temperature is 15-35 degrees, very dry, hot, moisture supplement
  • Rainy (4-10):20-30 degrees of temperature, wet and heavy rains
  • Harmatan:Dry wind blowing from the Sahara Desert in December-February
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, sunscreen and hat required near the equator
  • Daily crossroads:The temperature difference between the day and night is 15 degrees or more
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Occasional occurrence, prevention drugs and mosquito prevention required
  • yellow fever:Request for vaccination, testing certificate when entering Korea
  • Water -person disease:Do not drink bottled water and prohibit food intake
  • Medical facilities:Lack of medical facilities, transfer to the case of emergency situations
  • pharmacy:Fake drug sales attention at local pharmacies
