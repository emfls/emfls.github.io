📅 Ferrara 2 nights 3 days travel courses

Day 1: Ferrara's core attractions

Estens Castle → Ferrara Cathedral → Palacho Day Diamanti → Corso Hercolle Deste → Old Town Walk

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:Explore the charm of the city designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in the Renaissance Jewelry Ferrara in Emilia-Romania. Take a tour of Ferrara's symbolic buildings built to protect Nicola II and his family in Estens Castle, which has a moat built at the end of the 14th century, and enjoy a variety of rooms decorated with high tower and exquisite murals. At the Gothic George Cathedral in Romanesque Gothic St. George Cathedral, you can enjoy the oil painting of the Virgin Mary and several Tura, and experience the essence of Renaissance in Palacho Day Manti, decorated with diamond -shaped stones.
📍Estensus 📍 Ferrara Cathedral 📍Palacho Day Diamanti 📍Corso Her Colle Desste

Day 2: Renaissance Urban Planning and Culture Examination

Adici One Erchulea → Masari Park → San Cristoporo Ala Cretosa → Ariosstea Square → Jewish Getto

Recommended time:8 hours 30 minutes
Core points:Experience the innovative Renaissance urban planning for modern city design in Adicione Erkulea, a planned urban expansion at the end of the 15th century, and enjoy the beauty of nature at the romantic Masari Park with hundreds of years of Lebanon Cedar and the University Botanical Garden. Tour the inside of the beautiful Renaissance Church in San Christoporo Ala Cretosa, an ancient temple of Duke of Borso Deste in the mid -15th century, and feel the fragrance of literature in Ariostea Square in honor of Ariosto, a great poet on the central pillar.
📍Adicione Erculea 📍Masari Park 📍San Christoporo Ala Cretosa 📍Ariostea Square

⚠️ Ferrara travel precautions

🚨 Pickpocket attention

Major areas:Areas with many tourists such as Ferrara Train Station and Estens Castle

  • Storage of valuables:Keep your passport or big money in a safe place, and only small cash
  • Bag management:The bag is carried forward and locked the zipper, especially in the crowds.
  • Tourististism:Especially in famous tourist destinations and public transportation
  • Lock use:Double lock recommendation with carrier and bag lock
🎫 Prior reservation recommended
  • Estensus:In peak season, pre -booking recommendation, especially weekends and public holidays
  • Guide Tour:Korean guide tours are popular and reservations are required in advance
  • Gourmet Tour:Tours that include Capella Chi Allah Zuka are recommended in advance.
  • lodgment:Ferra is recommended at least 46 days ago because the accommodation of Ferra is limited.
🚌 Precautions on public transportation
  • Ballo Nya connection:About 30 minutes by train from Bologna
  • Venetian connection:About 1 hour and 30 minutes by train from Venice
  • Train ticket:Be sure to check the ticket before boarding to check the ticket before the boarding machine to prevent fines.
  • Movement in the city:Ferrara city can be toured by walking or bicycles
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):High temperatures and strong sunlight, sunscreen and hat required
  • Winter (12-2):Cold and occasional eyes, warm coat preparation
  • Spring/Autumn:Preparation of outerwear because the crossover may be large
  • In preparation for rain:Preparation of umbrella in preparation for sudden rain
🗣️ Language communication

Italian is official language, but English can be communicated in tourist attractions. Ferra is a city where you can experience local culture rather than international tourist destinations, so it is helpful to learn basic Italian greetings.
