⚠️ Locoja travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism
  • Pickpocket and theft:Be careful with belongings at the market, downtown, bus stops, etc.
  • Night movement:Restricted alone in late night, recommended use of official taxis
  • Check local status:Fake guides, scammer attention in tourist destinations
  • Mosquitoes and malaria:Mosquito repellents, mosquito nets required
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road circumstances:Some road packaging conditions, attention when moving
  • Public transportation:Minibus and safety checks when using motorcycle taxi (Okada)
  • Taxi fee:If the meter is without a meter, preliminary negotiations are required
  • traffic jam:Most of commute time crowded, schedule
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (November -March):Hot, dry, water intake required
  • Rainy (April -October):Frequent showers, umbrellas and raincoats prepare
  • ultraviolet rays:Sunlight strong, sunscreen and hat required
