⚠️ Just travel precautions

🌡️Checks related to weather

Tropical climate:Perfect for the summer, especially in summer with hot and humid tropical climate all year round.

  • High temperature and humidity:Summer temperature is very high and humidity is high, so there is a risk of dehydration.
  • Strong ultraviolet light:High SPF UV blockers required to prevent burns due to strong ultraviolet rays in the tropics
  • Preparation for the rainy season:During the rainy season in May-October
  • Mosquitoism:Due to the nature of the tropics, there are many mosquitoes, so prepare mosquito eradication and long -sleeved clothes
  • Mountain Zone:Alpine areas such as national parks have a low temperature, so warm clothes are required
🚗 Precautions when using transportation
  • Access from Bangkok:Air conditioners run every day at 05: 00 ~ 22: 00 in the northern Terminal of Bangkok, 6 hours required time
  • drive:Phahonyothin (Phahonyothin), 32 Expressway in Bangkok via Ayutthaya
  • Border area:Borders are in contact with Myanmar, so we need to be careful when traveling to borders
  • Mountain road:Mt.
  • Local transportation:Various transportation can be used, such as 쏭 Tau and Tuk Tuk
🍽️ Food and hygiene precautions
  • Street food:Select a place with a good hygiene when eating local street food
  • Bottled water drinking:Do not drink tap water directly, drink only water or boiled water
  • Iceism:It is safe to avoid the ice of drinks sold on the street
  • Spicy food:Thai food can be very spicy, so you can order it at first
  • Local special food:Just local traditional food and alpine cuisine experience
🛕 Religion and cultural courtesy
  • When visiting the temple:Wearing clothes covering your shoulders and knees, taking off your shoes and entering
  • Buddhist shooting:Taking a picture with a Buddha statue is considered a rude behavior.
  • Prohibit the touch of the head:In Thailand, the head is sacred, so it prohibits the touch of others' heads
  • To the toes:It is rude to make the toes to the Buddha or the person
  • Taksin King Shrine:It is a place to honor an important person in Thai history, so a godly attitude is needed
🏨 Precautions for accommodation and convenience facilities
  • Small town atmosphere:It's a quiet and peaceful small town that is different from a big city
  • English communication:Basic English communication is possible, but it is helpful to learn simple Thai expressions
  • Convenience facilities:Modern amenities and traditional markets coexist
  • Less foreign tourists:There are few foreign tourists, so you can shop and eat at local prices
  • Quiet night:It becomes very quiet at night, so refer to the night activity plan for reference
🚤 Precautions for water activity
  • Mae Kong River Cruise:Wearing safety equipment for river cruise tours
  • Life jacket:Duty to wear life jackets when boarding boats
  • Check the weather:Check the weather and water level before the award activity
  • Pumi Phone Dam:Activities only in safety zones when watching around the dam
  • Swimming prohibition:Swimming in the dam and the river is not recommended for safety
🎭 Border regional precautions
  • Myanmar border:Perfect for the border with Myanmar, special care needs when visiting the border area
  • Travel alert:Miyawadi, Myanmar, is designated as a prohibited area.
  • Mae Soy area:Border areas such as soaring are unnecessary travel in the second stage of travel alerts.
  • Personal safety:In the border area, special care for personal safety
  • Passport portable:Passport portable when visiting the border area
🏌️‍♂️ Golf and outdoor activity precautions
  • Bumi Phone Dam Golf Club:Note that you may not be able to concentrate on the game because it is fascinated by the fantastic surrounding landscape.
  • Trekking:Recommended for guides when trekking at Thasin National Park
  • Proper dress:Wearing suitable shoes and clothes for mountainous areas
