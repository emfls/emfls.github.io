⚠️Prins George Travel Note

🐻 Wildlife and safetyism

Mainist animals:Black Bear, Grizzly Bear, Mousse, Cougar

  • Bearism:Hiking, carrying bear spray, storage of food sealing, and talking loudly
  • Mousseism:Cautions for mousse when driving on roads, especially dawn and twilight time zones
  • Outdoor activities:Hiking alone, always accompanied by 2 or more people
  • Emergency situation:911 Emergency Telephone, Mountain Rescue Contact Contact in advance
🚗 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Winter driving:November-March Snow Tire Duty, Chain Portable Essential
  • Fuel supplement:Full supplementation of fuel when moving long distances, spacing at gas station
  • Public transportation:Restricted city bus operation, recommended car rental or taxi
  • Airport access:20 minutes taxi from Prince George Airport (YXS) to downtown
  • Road situation:Winter roads and summer construction sections
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):The temperature of 15-25 degrees, sudden showers, mosquitoes
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -20 ~ -5 degrees, extreme cold, enough winter clothes required
  • Spring/Autumn:More than 15 degrees of cross -day crossing, preparation for layered clothing
  • Precipitation:Relatively dry year -round, but in summer, heavy rains
  • Sunshine time:Summer 19 hours, winter 7 hours
💰 Price related precautions
  • Accommodation fee:Summer peak season rises 2-3 times, pre-booking required
  • Food expenses:25-40 CAD per restaurant, tip 15-20% separately
  • Fuel cost:1.4-1.6 CAD per liter of gasoline
  • Admission fee for tourist attractions:Exploration Place 15 CAD, State Park 10 CAD
