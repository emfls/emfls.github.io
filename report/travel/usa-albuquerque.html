⚠️ Alberk key travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Some areas of downtown, outskirts of nighttime

  • Tourist destination safety:Old Towns and major tourist attractions are relatively safe, but it should be avoided to go to night alone.
  • Vehicle theft:Prohibition of storage of valuables in vehicles when using a car rental, always check the door lock
  • Emergency situation:911 Report, Tourism Police Station contact information in advance
  • How to deal with:Active in places with many locals, and avoid rare places
🚗 Traffic and rental precautions
  • Rent -a -car required:Recommended for using car rentals because public transportation is limited
  • International driver's license:Korean international driver's license is required
  • parking:Old Towns compliant with paid parking, meter time
  • highway:GPS required by using I-25 and I-40 major highways
  • fuel:Fuel sufficiently supplemented before driving in the desert area
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Desert:It is dry throughout the year and the crossover is very large (35 degrees in summer, 15 degrees at night)
  • ultraviolet rays:Because of the high altitude, the ultraviolet rays are strong, so sunscreen and sunglasses are required
  • Moisture supplement:Eat enough water because there is a high risk of dehydration
  • Altitude disease:At 1,600m above sea level,
  • winter:Prepare warm clothes because it can come to snow
🎈 Hot air balloon tour precautions
  • It can be canceled depending on the weather, so secure a spare schedule
  • It is common to sleep at 5 am, so enough sleep is required.
  • If there is a heights, prior notice
  • Wear comfortable shoes because it may be shocked when landing
