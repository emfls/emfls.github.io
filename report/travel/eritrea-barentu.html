⚠️ Barentu travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Sudan border area, late at night

  • Political situation:Eritrea prohibits politically sensitive countries, government criticism or political remarks
  • Photo shooting:Estimated photography, such as military facilities, government buildings, airports, etc.
  • Border area:Near the border of the Sudan, prohibits unauthorized access, cannot be moved without permission.
  • Local guide:Be sure to travel and restrict individual behaviors with government official guides
  • Foreigner Registration:Duty of reporting to local authorities during the period of stay
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Limited public transportation, mainly used for personal vehicles or taxis
  • Road situation:Many unpaved roads, deterioration of road conditions during rainy season
  • fuel:Fuel shortages frequent, required to confirm fuel when moving long distances
  • Asmara connection:It takes about 4-5 hours to the capital, Asmara
  • Mobile permit:It may be necessary when moving between regions
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Malaria dangerous areas, prevention drugs and mosquito blocking required
  • drinking water:Prohibit tap water, drink only bottled water
  • food:Avoid raw or less ripe food, check hygiene status
  • Medical facilities:Lack of medical facilities, moving to Asmara when serious illness
  • High temperature:High temperature, heat stroke more than 40 degrees
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, skin protection required
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (10-5):Temperature 25-40 degrees, very dry and hot
  • Woogi (June-September):Temperature 20-30 degrees, concentrated heavy rains and flood risk
  • Plateau climate:Big and chilly at night
  • Dust Storm:Sand storms can occur when dry season
  • Rainy movement:Difficulties in road traffic during the rainy season
💰 Economic precautions
  • Cash -oriented:Nearly impossible to use credit card, cash preparation required
  • Exchange:Exchange only at the official exchange office, risk of exchange of cancer markets
  • price:Inflation due to lack of daily necessities
  • Tip Culture:Tip Culture, appropriate tips for services
