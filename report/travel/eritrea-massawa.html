⚠️ Masawa travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Prohibition of all night outing, around the government building

  • Political situation:Eritrea is an authoritarian government to prohibit political remarks or government criticism
  • Photo shooting:Strictly prohibited photography at government buildings, military facilities, airports, and ports
  • Contact with locals:Clearly expressing political dialogue and tourism purposes
  • Night activity:After the sunset, refrain from going out outside the hotel and accompany the guide.
  • Passport management:Always hold your passport, copy separately
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Restricted and irregular, taxi or guided vehicle recommendation
  • Road situation:Lack of packaging roads, deterioration of road conditions in rainy season
  • Lack of fuel:Instability of gasoline supply, mobile planning carefully established
  • checkpoint:When moving between cities, pass through checkpoints, ID cards and permission
  • Asmara connection:It takes about 3 hours from Masawa to Asmara
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Desert:High temperature drying, daytime temperature 35-45 degrees
  • Woogi (July-September):Sudden heavy rain and flood risk
  • Dry (October-June):Extremely dried, enough water intake required
  • Daily crossroads:At night
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen, etc.
  • Sand breeze:Wearing a mask, wearing a wealth of sand breeze
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Red Sea Coastal Malaria Dangerous Area
  • drinking water:Drink only bottled water and avoid ice, use integer tablets
  • food:Intake only completely cooked food, peeled raw fruit peel
  • Medical facilities:Lack of medical infrastructure, move in Asmara in an emergency
  • yellow fever:Proposal for vaccination, presentation when entering certificate
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash economy:Nearly impossible to use credit cards, enough cash preparation
  • Exchange rate:The difference between the official exchange rate and the cancer market exchange rate
  • price:Import price is very expensive, purchase of local products
  • Tip Culture:Tips for guides and hotel staff
  • Fuel cost:Gasoline price is high and supply instability
