Day 2: Dome de Fabro Nature Exploration

Dome de Fabro → Rock Climbing → Observation Deck → Nature Exploration

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:Dome de Fabro is a unique terrain with huge rocks that are naturally formed, and they are like a domino. Climb to the top of the rock to enjoy the vast panorus, explore the rocks and observe the mysterious sculptures created by nature. This is one of the highlights of Banpoa Travel and is a perfect place to enjoy climbing and exploration.

Day 3: Lake Ten Grella and Hama Observation

Lake Ten Grella → Hama Observation Tour → Walk around the Lake → Singu Falls

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:Lake Ten Greella is a famous natural attraction near Banpoa where you can observe hippo. ⚠️ Banpoa travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Borders around the border area,

  • Terrorism:Confirmation of travel alerts from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs due to the risk of terrorism in the West Africa
  • Wildlife:Maintain safety distance when observing hippopotamus, comply with guide instructions

🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:When using buses and taxis, there is no bargaining, no meter
  • Motorcycle Taxi:Checking helmet wearing, request for safe driving
  • Road situation:Many unpaved roads, deterioration of road conditions during rainy season
  • fuel:Since there are rare gas stations, fuel confirmation required
  • Night movement:Late time moving late at night
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):Temperature 25-40 degrees, very dry and hot

  • Harmatan:Dry wind blowing from the Sahara Desert in December-February
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight and sun protection year -round
  • Moisture intake:Eat enough water to prevent dehydration
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Mosquito -mediated diseases, prevention drugs and mosquito repellents use
