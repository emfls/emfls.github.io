🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Outside area, late at night

  • Political instability:Security anxiety caused by Yemen's civil war, confirmation of government travel alerts
  • Terrorism:Al -Qaeda and ISIS activity areas and embassy contacts
  • Kidnapping:The abduction of the kidnapping for foreigners, prohibiting single behavior
  • Night traffic prohibition:Restriction on going out after sunset, accompanied by local guides
  • checkpoint:Many military checkpoints, ID cards are always portable
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Airlines limit:The possibility of stopping international flights, required for prior confirmation
  • Road situation:Road infrastructure defect, 4WD vehicle recommendation
  • Lack of fuel:Unstable fuel supply, securing sufficient fuel
  • Public transportation:Low bus and taxi safety, reliable driver use
  • Maritime Transportation:Pirate dangerous areas, special attention when moving to maritime
🏥 Health and medical precautions
  • malaria:High risk of mosquito -mediated diseases, taking prevention drugs
  • cholera:Sudden disease occurs, only drinking water
  • Medical facilities:Lack of medical infrastructure, foreign transfer in case of emergency
  • Dengue:Mosquito -mediated diseases and insect repellent required
  • Food poisoning:Sanitary condition is poor, only cooked food
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (April-October):35-45 degrees, extreme heat and humidity
  • Winter (11-3):Temperature 20-30 degrees, relatively comfortable
  • Sand Storm:Sand storm occurrence during dry season, protection of respiratory system
