🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the port late at night, the outskirts

  • Supplies Management:Valuates are kept in the accommodation and only at least cash
  • Respect for local customs:Save permission before taking a photo when visiting a traditional village
  • Female travelers:As a conservative culture,
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • flight:Solomon Airlines domestic flight from Honiara to Aooki (about 45 minutes)
  • Local transportation:Taxi, bus service limited, walking a lot
  • Boat use:Moving the weather when moving between the island, wearing a life jacket
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, restrictions on moving in rainy season
  • Lack of fuel:Occasionally, fuel supply stops, travel scheduled to be relaxed
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (November-April):It is good to avoid heavy rain and cyclone
  • Dry (May-October):Travel optimal, temperature 24-30 degrees, humidity is high
  • ultraviolet rays:Ultraviolet rays are very strong, sunscreen must be near the equator
  • Sudden shower:Always prepare for umbrellas or raincoats
  • Marine weather:Check the weather before using snorkeling or boats
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Mosquito -mediated diseases dangerous areas, prevention drugs and mosquito repellent use
  • Dengue:Mosquito -mediated diseases, medical staff counseling immediately when high fever
  • drinking water:Purchase of bottled water or boiled water, ice care
  • Medical facilities:Limited to Aooki Hospital Facilities, Honia Ra transfer in case of serious emergency
  • Marine safety:Jellyfish, toxic fishism, snorkeling guide accompanied by
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:The price of daily necessities is high due to dependence on imported goods
  • Cash Society:Limited credit card use, enough cash preparation
  • Exchange:Recommended to visit Aooki after exchange in Honiara
  • Tip Culture:Small tips for guides or boat drivers
