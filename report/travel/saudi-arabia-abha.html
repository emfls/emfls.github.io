⚠️ About travel precautions

🚨 Culture and religiousism

Respect for Islamic culture

  • Dress Regulations:Both men and women wear clothes covering their shoulders and knees, women cover their heads when they enter the mosque
  • evensong:In the 5th prayer time a day, shops and restaurants may be suspended.
  • friday:On Friday afternoon, many facilities are closed with major prayer days.
  • Ramadan period:In Ramadan, it is prohibited to eat public food from sunrise to sunset
  • alcohol:Saudi Arabia is completely prohibited by alcohol sales and consumption.
🌡️Chires and highlands
  • Daily crossroads:The temperature difference between day and night is 15 degrees or more
  • Summer temperature:Relatively cool at up to 32 degrees in June-August at 24 degrees at night
  • Winter temperature:It is required for winter clothes because it goes down to 15 degrees during the day and 5 degrees at night in December-February
  • Alpine adaptation:Difficulty breathing due to highlands or headaches can occur
  • ultraviolet rays:Sunscreen and sunglasses are required with strong ultraviolet rays
  • Dryness:Very dry climate requires enough water intake
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Promoting public transportation is recommended for rental cars or taxis because public transportation is limited
  • Mountain road:There are many winding mountain roads, so special attention is needed when driving
  • Taxi fee:Meter usage confirmation or pre -fee negotiation
  • International license:When using a car rental, both international driver's license and Korean license
  • fuel:Fuel is fully checked because there are rare gas stations in the mountainous region.
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Restaurants and shops around major tourist attractions are high
  • Cash payment:In the traditional market, cash payment is common
  • Tip Culture:Restaurant 10-15%, hotel service 5-10
  • Exchange:Recommended for exchange at airports or banks, street exchange offices
  • Specialty:Traditional honey and handicraft prices in the Asir area
