⚠️Sagamihara travel precautions

🌡️Checks related to weather

Seasonal features:Sagamihara has a relatively gentle climate near Tokyo

  • spring:When the lake is colored with cherry blossoms, it becomes more beautiful
  • summer:It can be hot and humid, so enough water intake and sunscreen required
  • autumn:The autumn leaves are beautiful, so it's just a good time to take a walk
  • winter:The Illumination Festival is held to enjoy the sparkling scenery under the night sky
🚇 Precautions when using transportation
  • Approach near Tokyo:Only one hour from Shinjuku can be traveled on a day -to -day trip
  • Public transportation:Convenient movement using JR Pass or transportation card
  • Bicycle rental:Recommended to rent a bicycle in the park and look back on the park
  • Transportation etiquette:Avoid telephone in silent mode and long calls
🍽️ Food and cultural precautions
  • Cash use:Japan has a lot of cash use, so enough cash is prepared
  • Tip Culture:Japan has no tip culture, so you don't have to give a tip.
  • Meal etiquette:How to use chopsticks and eat without sound
  • Local specialty:Local food experiences such as Sagami Hara Ramen and Smile Grilled Cuisine
🏞️ Precautions for natural activities
  • Lake activity:Comply with safety rules when using canoea kayak at Lake Sagami
  • Home line experience:Saga Miko Resort to experience safely in accordance with a professional staff guide when experiencing the long line of the Zip Miko Resort
  • Barbecue activity:Fire prevention attention when using a barbecue space near the lake
  • Environmental protection:Observing garbage separation and environmental protection rules for nature protection
🎢 Precautions when using theme park
  • Pleasure Forest:When using the rides
  • Operating time confirmation:Operating hours are different depending on the season and day of the week, so check
  • Illumination:Preparation of winter clothes when watching the Illumination Festival in winter
  • Preparation for weather:Outdoor activities can be stopped according to the weather
🏛️ Precautions when visiting museums and cultural facilities
  • Photo shooting:Inside the museum, shooting may be limited, so confirmation of guidance display
  • Traditional craft experience:The experience program of the Satake Museum is recommended in advance
  • Quiet view:In the museum and the temple, maintain a quiet and godly posture
  • Cultural property protection:Not touching historical artifacts or cultural assets
