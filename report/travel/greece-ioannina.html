What are the main historical attractions to visit in Ioannina on the first day?

On the first day, visitors should explore Ioannina Castle, built in the 6th century during the Byzantine era, which also reflects Ottoman influences. Additionally, the Silver Craft Museum showcases local specialty arts, and a boat trip to the island on Lake Pamvotis allows exploration of a monastery and traditional villages.

What nature and adventure activities are recommended for the second day in Ioannina?

The second day itinerary includes visiting Vikos Canyon, known for its stunning natural beauty, exploring Ferrama Cave, touring the Ali Pasha Museum, and walking through the historic Old Town, combining outdoor adventure with cultural insights.

How much time should travelers allocate for the Day 1 itinerary in Ioannina?

Travelers should plan approximately 8 hours to fully experience the Day 1 itinerary, which covers historical sites, museum visits, and leisure time around Lake Pamvotis including a boat trip to the island.
