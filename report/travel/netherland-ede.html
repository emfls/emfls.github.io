자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main attractions to visit in Ede on the first day of the travel course?

On the first day, visitors should explore Hohee Beluwe National Park to experience its forests, grasslands, and sand dunes by bicycle or on foot. They should also visit the Crawler Muller Museum, which houses the world's second-largest Van Gogh collection and a modern sculpture garden, and walk the Belluwe Trail to observe wildlife like wild deer, wild boar, and foxes.

How long should travelers allocate for the Day 1 itinerary in Ede?

Travelers should allocate approximately 9 hours for the Day 1 itinerary to fully enjoy the nature and art experiences at Hohee Beluwe National Park, the Crawler Muller Museum, and the Belluwe Trail.

What cultural sites are recommended to visit on the second day in Ede?

On the second day, visitors are encouraged to explore Ede's history and culture by visiting EDEDECE COENG, the Edesuke Concert Hall, the Dutch Tile Museum, and walking the Clampenpenpad trail.
