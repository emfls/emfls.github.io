What are the main historical sites to visit in Basseterre on the first day?

On the first day, visitors should explore Independence Square, St. George Anglican Cathedral built in 1670 with Gothic and British colonial stained glass, the National Museum showcasing local history, and the Victorian-style Berkeley Memorial Clock Tower built in 1883.

How much time should I allocate for the Day 1 travel course in Basseterre?

The recommended time to complete the Day 1 itinerary, which includes visiting key historical and cultural landmarks, is approximately 8 hours.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect in Basseterre according to the guide?

Travelers can experience Caribbean independence history at Independence Square, admire British colonial Gothic architecture at St. George Anglican Cathedral, learn about local heritage at the National Museum, and enjoy Victorian architectural views from the Berkeley Memorial Clock Tower.
