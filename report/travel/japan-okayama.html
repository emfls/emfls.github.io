자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main attractions to visit on the first day of the Okayama travel course?

On the first day, visitors should explore Okayama Castle, known for its striking black walls and Edo-era history, Gorakuen Garden, one of Japan's three largest gardens showcasing seasonal beauty, Gibizu Shrine, and the shopping streets around Okayama Station where local foods like Demikatsu and Fruit Parfa can be enjoyed.

How much time is recommended to spend exploring downtown Okayama on day one?

An estimated 8 hours is recommended to fully experience the historical and cultural sites in downtown Okayama, including the castle, garden, shrine, and shopping areas.

What local foods should travelers try when visiting Okayama?

Travelers are encouraged to try local specialties such as Demikatsu, a breaded pork cutlet with demi-glace sauce, and Fruit Parfa, a dessert featuring fresh seasonal fruits, especially around the shopping streets near Okayama Station.
