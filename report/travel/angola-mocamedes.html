🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the port, part of the city late at night

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing at the port, market, and tourist attractions, bags of bags and zipper lock
  • robbery:Don't go alone in the night, always go to the group
  • Fake guide:Attention to those who sell fake tickets in tourist attractions or who are forced to guide
  • Going out late at night:Port and some parts of the city, risk of going alone after 9 pm
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Lack of public transportation:Recommended for using taxi or car rentals because public transportation facilities are limited
  • Road status:Some road conditions are not good, so 4WD car recommended
  • Taxi fee:Negotiations from the airport to the city are required, if there is no meter
  • Railway connection:Mosamedes Railroads are suspended regularly due to the damage caused by civil war.
  • Fuel:Fuel enough for long distance moving
