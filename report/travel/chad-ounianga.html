⚠️ Ouniana travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Border area, late at night, city center center

  • Political instability:Chad is a political unstable area and requires the latest safety information confirmation.
  • Terrorism:Confirmation of travel alarms from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs due to the risk of terrorism in the Sahel area
  • Armed intensity:Risk of armed intensity in the outskirts, local guides mandatory
  • Prohibition of going out at night:After the sun, never go alone
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • 4WD required:Ouniangga must be accessible only by 4WD vehicle.
  • Local guide:The desert area is easy to get lost, so experience guides are required
  • GPS equipment:Satellite GPS and compass preparation required
  • Fuel:There is no gas station in the middle, so enough fuel is prepared
  • Emergency contact network:Preparation of satellite telephone or emergency signal
🌡️ Climate -related precautions
  • Extreme heat:Over 45 degrees of daytime temperatures, enough water intake and shade rest
  • Rapid temperature difference:At night, it falls below 10 degrees and is required
  • Sand Storm:Evacuate immediately when a sudden sand storm occurs
  • Dry Visit:November-February is optimal, avoiding rainy (June-September)
  • ultraviolet rays:Burnism caused by the strong UV rays of the desert
💊 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Use of mosquito repellents and taking prevention drugs required
  • Water -person disease:Do not drink bottled water and prohibit food intake
  • yellow fever:Can't enter the country without a certificate of vaccination
  • Dewnity risk:At least 4L of water per day
  • Medical facilities:Preparation of pills sufficient due to lack of local medical facilities
