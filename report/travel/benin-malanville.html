📅 Marangville 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: Niger River and Traditional Culture Examination

Niger River → Marangville Central Market → Traditional Village → Riverside Promenade

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the Niger River, the western milk line of the West Africa, observe the traditional fatigue experience and the daily lives of fishermen, visit the local agricultural products, handicrafts, and traditional fabrics in the Marangville Central Market, experience the culture of the Bargu and Pulani, and tour traditional houses and grains built with mud bricks in traditional villages. Understand and appreciate the calm landscapes of Savana in Africa and Niger River on the riverside promenade.
📍Niger River 📍Marangville Central Market 📍 Traditional village 📍 Riverside promenade

Day 2: Savana ecosystem and agricultural culture experience

Savana Sapari → Agricultural Cooperative → Traditional handicraft villages → Star observation

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:In the Sabana area around Marangville, observed West African wildlife such as nutrition, monkeys, and various birds, explores the Savana ecosystem with baobab and acacia trees, tours the Savana crop cultivation process such as sorghum, captains, and corn in local agricultural cooperatives, experiences harmony between traditional farming and modern technology, and a traditional handicraft village Learn Beenal traditional craft techniques such as weaving, ceramics, fabric dyeing, and observing the southern hemisphere constellation and the Milky Way in the clear sky of Savana at night.
📍Savana Sapari 📍 Agricultural Cooperative 📍 Traditional handicraft village 📍 Star observation point

Day 3: Exploration of borders and cultural exchanges (options)

Niger Border → Border Market → Culture Festival → Niger River Boat Tour

Niger border:Takes 2 hours, Bengin-Niger border, the two countries' cultural differences and commonalities observations
Border Market:It takes 3 hours, and the trading of the merchants of both countries and various products in the border trade market
Culture Festival:4 hours, local traditional music, dance, and consciousness participation
Niger River Boat Tour:3 hours, take a traditional boat to explore upstream of the Niger River and observe wild animals on the river.

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Borders around the border area,

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing at the mayor and bus stop, the bag is carried in the future and the zipper lock
  • Motorcycle theft:Walking inside India,
  • Fake guide:Attention to fake guides or forced help in tourist attractions
  • Going out late at night:Prohibited to go alone after sunset, recommend group activities
  • Border area:Bengin-Niger border security insecurity, local guide mandatory
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Buses and taxis are poor, wearing seat belts and speeding
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, the possibility of flooding roads during the rainy season
  • Taxi fee:Prior to negotiations, if there is no meter
  • Long distance movement:About 8 hours from Kotonu to Marangville, rest required
  • Lack of fuel:Lack of gas stations, confirming the situation in advance
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Mosquito -mediated diseases are high, prevention drugs and mosquito prevention required
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, avoid ice, use integer tablets
  • food:Street foodism, ingest only enough cooked food
  • Medical facilities:Lack of local medical facilities, in serious cases, go to Kotonu
  • Emergency situation:Korean consulate contact information in advance
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-3):Temperature 25-40 degrees, very hot, dry, enough water intake
  • Rainy (4-10):Temperature 22-35 degrees, moist and heavy rain, raincoat and waterproofing supplies
  • Harmatan:December-February Sahara Desert Sand Wind, Mask and Sunglasses Need
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, sunscreen and hat required near the equator
  • Daily crossroads:At night, the temperature drops, a light coat
💰 Price related precautions
