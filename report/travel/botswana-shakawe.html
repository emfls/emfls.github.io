Day 1: Delta and Traditional Village Experience

Shakawe Village → Okabango Delta → Mokoro Boat Tour → Traditional Village

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the world's largest inland deline, Okabango Delta, the traditional canoe mokoro explores the waterway, observes wild animals and aquatic plants, experiences the African native culture in the traditional villages of local Baye and Hambu Kushu, tours traditional dance and handicrafts, and explores the unique wetland ecosystem of the Kalahari Desert

Recommended time:10 hours

⚠️Shakawe travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever, sleep disease

  • malaria:Essential prevention medications, mosquito repellents are always used, long -sleeved clothes
  • yellow fever:Essential vaccination before departure, presented when entering a certificate of vaccination
  • Wildlife:In the Safari Tour, the guide order strict compliance, prohibit unauthorized leaving outside the vehicle
  • drinking water:Bottled water only drinking, ice attention, fruit peeled
  • Emergency situation:The nearest hospital is located in MAUN, recommended to subscribe to emergency helicopter service insurance
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road situation:Many unpaved roads, 4WD vehicles required
  • fuel:Gas station spacing, excess fuel preparation required
  • GPS:Offline map download, compass and paper map backup preparation
  • Border pass:Near the Namibia border, passport always portable
  • flight:Use of small aircraft at Machun Airport, the possibility of delayed by weather
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (5-10):Temperature 10-30 degrees, dry and dusty, enough water intake
  • Woogi (11-4):20-35 degrees of temperature, moist and mosquito polar, road flooding
  • Daily crossroads:The temperature difference between the day and night is 15 degrees or more, the warm clothing required
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, sunscreen near the equator
  • mosquito:Polarity, screening and avoiding in the rainy season and riverside
💰 Price related precautions
  • Safari Tour:One-day tour $ 150-300, guide tip $ 20-30 separate
  • lodgment:Luxury Safari Lodge $ 300-800/Park, backpacker lodge $ 50-100/Park
  • Food expenses:Local restaurant $ 10-20, lodge meal $ 30-60
  • traffic:4WD car rental $ 80-150/day, fuel cost
  • Tip Culture:Guide $ 20-30/Sun, Lodge employee $ 5-10/Sun
