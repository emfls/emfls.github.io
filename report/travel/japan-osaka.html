📅 Osaka 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: Osaka Castle and City Exploration

Osaka Castle → Osaka Castle Park → Shinsaibashi → Dotonbori → Hozenji Yoko Cho → Aveno Harukas

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In Osaka Castle, one of Japan's three largest reputation, Toyotomi Hideyoshi's history is experienced, photographed with Glyco signboards in Dotonbori, the symbol of Osaka, and tasted Osaka's representative foods such as Takoyaki and Oko Nomiyaki, and enjoys the view of Osaka from the highest building Aveno Harukas Observatory.

Day 2: Universal Studio Japan Experience

Universal Studio Japan → Harry Potter Magic World → Minion Park → Kuromon Market → Ambassador to Sumiyoshi

Recommended time:10 hours
Core points:The world's famous theme park Universal Studio Japan explores Harry Potter's magical world and Hogwarts, enjoys a good time with cute characters at the Minion Park, and tastes fresh seafood and fruit in the Kuromon Market called Osaka's kitchen.
📍 Universal Studio Japan 📍Kuromon Market 📍 Ambassador Sumiyoshi 📍 Harry Potter magic world

Day 3: Sinsekai and Culture Experience (optional)

Sinsekai → Tsuken Kaku Tower → Shiten Noji → Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan → Kansai International Airport

Shinsekai:It takes 3 hours, Kushikatsu and traditional game experience on a retro street where you can feel the old mood of Osaka
Tsuten Kaku Tower:Takes 2 hours, Osaka's symbolic tower

⚠️ Osaka travel precautions

🚇 Precautions on transportation

Transportation pass use:Osaka has a complicated connection with subways, JRs, and private railways, so it is economical to buy a traffic pass in advance.

  • Osaka Amazing Pass:2,800 yen for free use all day long and 40 tourism facilities free of charge.
  • Kansai Through Pass:2 days of 4,380 yen for free transportation of public transportation in the Kansai area
  • Avoid commuting time:7-9 am, 5-7 pm is very congested
  • Signs in History:It is recommended to prepare translation apps but English and English weapons
🏨 Precautions when booking
  • Reservation in advance:Dotonbori and Shinsaibashi are very popular, so there may be no rooms in the peak season.
  • Hotel selection:Osaka has a variety of options from business hotels to luxury hotels.
  • Location consideration:Lodges near Namba, Umeda, and Tennoji are convenient for tourism and transportation
  • Near USJ:Lodgings near Universal Studios are convenient for using theme parks but are high.
🌡️Checks and costumes
  • Spring (April-June):Travel recommendation as the most pleasant time, preparation of light outer clothes
  • Summer (July-September):Hot and humid weather needs enough water intake and UV rays
  • Autumn (September-November):Good to travel with cool and pleasant weather
  • Winter (12-3):It is required for winter products due to cold, dry and windy winds
🍽️ Food and cultural precautions
  • Cash use:In small shops and restaurants, there are many places where only cash is received.
  • Osaka food:Takoyaki 500 yen, 800 yen of Okonomiyaki, and 100-200 yen in 1 Kushikatsu
  • Restaurant reservation:Popular restaurants are required for advance reservations, especially dinner time zones
  • Tip Culture:Japan does not have a tip culture, so you don't have to give it a separate tip.
🗣️ Language and manner precautions
  • Japanese translation app:Tourist destinations are English, but for detailed communication, the translation app is prepared
  • Garbage processing:There is almost no trash can on the street, so prepare a small plastic bag
  • Public transportation manners:Do not speak loudly on the train, to give up priority
  • Photo shooting:Be careful not to interfere with others in many places
💳 Payment and shopping precautions
  • Card payment:Large stores can be paid for cards, but small shops only receive cash.
  • Tax -free shopping:Duty -free tax exemption when purchased more than 5,000 yen, it is required to bring a passport
  • Business hours:Most of the shops are closed at 8-9 pm, so consider the shopping time
  • souvenir:Osaka specialties include Momijimanju and Takoyaki sweets.
