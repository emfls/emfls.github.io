⚠️Awwla travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Main risk factors:Malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever, Zika virus

  • Yellow fever vaccination:Essential requirements for entry, completion of inoculation 10 days before departure
  • Malaria prevention:Essential to take preventive drugs and use insect repellent, wear long -sleeved clothing
  • Drinking water:Only drinking water, ice and fresh fruit intake
  • Emergency Medical:The nearest hospital is Saint Laurent Dumaroni (1 hour away)
  • Toxic creatures:Jellyfish, stingray, viper, etc.
🌦️ Climate -related precautions
  • Woogi (12-7):Monthly average precipitation 330mm, flood and road blocking risk
  • Dry (8-November):Relatively dry but still high humidity
  • Temperature:27-31 degrees per year, higher humidity, higher temperature
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat required near the equator
  • Sudden scall:Preparation of waterproof products compared to tropical scalls
🚌 Traffic and accessibility precautions
  • Road status:There are many unpaved roads, unable to pass through the rainy season
  • Public transportation:Restricted, pre -transportation reservation required
  • Rent -a -car:4WD car recommended, international driver's license required
  • fuel:Gas station limited and sufficient fuel secure
  • communication:Mobile phone signal weakness, satellite phone or satellite messenger recommended
🌿 Environmental protection precautions
  • Sea Turtle Observation:Prohibition of use of flash, at least 5m distance
  • Garbage processing:Be sure to take all garbage
  • Wildlife:Prohibition of feeding, habitat damage
  • Plant collection:Prohibition of unauthorized harvesting, compliance with protection zone regulations
  • noise:Minimize noise in wildlife habitat
🏛️ Cultural precautions
  • Aboriginal culture:Kalina tradition and respect for customs
  • Photo shooting:Be sure to get permission before shooting a local
  • sanctuary:A guide accompanied by entering the traditional consciousness place
