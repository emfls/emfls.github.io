What are the main attractions to visit on the first day in Yokosuka?

On the first day, visitors should explore Mikasa Park, which symbolizes the Japanese Navy, tour the interior of the battleship Mikasa, walk along the exotic Dobuita Street, and enjoy scenic port views at Berni Park.

How long is the recommended travel course for Yokosuka?

The recommended travel course for Yokosuka is 2 nights and 3 days, allowing ample time to experience both historical landmarks and natural attractions.

What should travelers prepare for when visiting Yokosuka?

Travelers should prepare for a mix of historical tours and outdoor activities, including walking through parks and coastal areas, so comfortable clothing and footwear are advised along with planning for weather conditions.
