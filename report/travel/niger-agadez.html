⚠️Agadez travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Main dangerous areas:Northern Agadez, Tuareg activity area

  • Tua Leg activity:Dangerous due to the Tua Leg activities in the northern regions such as the Ir Mountains, Iperuan, and Arlit
  • Armed intensity:Armed force in the desert area, must accompany local guides.
  • Kidnapping:Abductions for foreigners, absolutely prohibited from traveling alone
  • Desert District:Prohibition of entry into the desert without GPS and satellite communication equipment
  • Night going out:Resistant to go out in the city after sunset
🏥 Disease and health precautions
  • malaria:Mosquito -mediated diseases, prevention drugs and insect repellent
  • Meningitis:Respiratory diseases due to dry yellow dust, recommendation of vaccination
  • yellow fever:Required certificate of vaccination, confirmation when entering Korea
  • dehydration:Serious dehydration due to desert climate, enough water intake
  • typhoid:Precious water and food intake
  • HIV/AIDS:Adult population 0.8% infection, care
🌡️ Desert climate precautions
  • Extreme heat:Due to daytime temperatures over 45 degrees, morning/evening activities recommended
  • Extreme cold:Less than 5 degrees at night, required for winter products
  • Hwangsa (Armatang):November-February Sahara Dust Wind, Respiratory Protection Essentials
  • Dryness:Humidity less than 10%, moisturizer and moisture intake
  • Strong ultraviolet light:Risk of burns due to desert reflective light
💰 Economic and payment precautions
  • Cash Society:Credit cards cannot be used, cash required
  • ATM lack:Difficulties in withdrawing cash, preparing for sufficient cash
  • Exchange:Exchange dollars or euro to CFA franc in the field
  • Rewards:Excessive charges for foreigners
  • Tip Culture:Guide, appropriate tips to the driver
