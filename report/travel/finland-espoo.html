📍 Espo Popular attractions

Nuuk Shio National Park

National Park in northern Espo where you can enjoy nature and wild

Various hiking trails, lakes, and dense forests are open to activities such as camping, canoe, fishing, and berry picking. Durable shoes and mosquito repellent preparation.
📍Nukio National Park

Espo Cathedral

An old -fashioned cathedral where you can learn the history and culture of Espo

Visit to local history, visit the surrounding museum, and participate in local tours. It is also popular as a quiet walking course.
📍 Espo Cathedral

Seurasari Outdoor Museum

Historical buildings to experience Finnish traditional lifestyle

Located on islands with dense forests and beaches, you can experience Finnish life culture and customs for centuries.
📍Seurasari Outdoor Museum

Cello Shopping Center

Espo's representative shopping mall where you can enjoy shopping and meals in one place

There are a variety of shops, restaurants, and cinemas, so it's a good place to enjoy shopping and leisure.
📍Sello Shopping Center

Aalto University Campus

Attractions that can experience modern architecture and study

Campus walks, nearby museums, restaurants can be visited. Recommended for travelers interested in design and architecture.
📍A Alto University

Serena Water Park

Amusement Park popular for family visitors

A good place to have a good time with children with various rides and water park facilities.
📍 Serena Water Park
