⚠️ Naktali travel precautions

🌡️Checks related to weather

Features of Finnish weather:Change and difficult to predict

  • Summer (June-August):The temperature is 15-25 degrees, white night phenomenon, bright until 11 pm, mosquitoism
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -10 degrees ~ 5 degrees, and the sun is luggage at 3 pm
  • Spring/Autumn:Sudden showers, umbrellas and outerwear are required
  • Baltic Sea Wind:Experience temperature is about 5 degrees lower than the actual actual
  • ultraviolet rays:Long -term sunlight exposure, sunscreen required
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation limit:Naktali is a small city, and the bus dispatch interval is wide, recommended car rental
  • Moomin World Shuttle:Paid shuttle bus from Turku Hotel to Moomin World (only summer season)
  • Turku-Naktali:20-30 minutes by car, buses operate only in summer
  • Helsinki connection:2-3 hours by bus or train from Helsinki
  • Taxi fee:About 40-50 euros from Turku to Naktali
🏨 Lodging and reservation precautions
  • Peak season reservation:Summer in the summer of June-August, early sold out, pre-reservation required
  • Moomin World Operation:Operated only from mid-June-late August, accurate schedule confirmation
  • Sauna reservation:Popular saunas such as Heran Kukaro are recommended in advance reservations
  • Spa use:Advanced facilities such as Naktaly Spa are required for peak season.
  • Golf course reservation:Aurinko Golf, etc. need to make a tea time pre -booking
💰 Price related precautions
  • Nordic junk price:Finland has high prices in Europe
  • Moomin World admission fee:Adult 28 euros (on -site purchase standard)
  • Sauna fee:Traditional sauna experience 50-80 euro
  • Restaurant price:25-40 euros per person in a local restaurant
  • Tip Culture:10%of the restaurant, 5-10 sauna guide
🌿 Nature and safety precautions
  • Mosquitoes:Many mosquitoes near the summer forest and lake, insect repellent
  • Sauna Safety:Do not overdo enough water intake when using high temperature saunas
  • Beach safety:Baltic sea water temperature is low, attention to swimming
  • Nature protection:Finland is strictly protected in nature, prohibiting unauthorized dumping of garbage
  • Wildlife:Elk or bear witness in the forest area, maintaining a safe distance
