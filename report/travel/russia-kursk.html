What is the recommended travel course for visiting Kursk in Russia?

The recommended travel course for Kursk is a 2-night, 3-day itinerary starting with Day 1 focused on exploring historical and war memorial sites such as the Kursk Great Victory Memorial Hall, Victory Memorial Hall, Red Square, and Zunamenski Cathedral.
