⚠️Apabet travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Borders, all regions when going out at night

  • Political situation:Eritrea is a politically sensitive country and prohibits government criticism or political remarks.
  • Photo shooting:Estimated photography such as government buildings, military facilities, airports, etc.
  • Night activity:After the sun, it is prohibited to go out alone, always accompanied by local guides.
  • Border area:Prohibition of access to the border area with Ethiopia
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Bus operation is irregular and safety standards are low
  • Rent -a -car:Non -road condition, local driving culture is needed
  • taxi:No meter, preliminary fee negotiations required
  • Long distance movement:It takes about 3-4 hours from Asmara to Afabet
  • fuel:There is a rare gas station, so it is necessary to secure enough fuel
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (10-5):The temperature is 15-30 degrees, very dry and dust storm occurs
  • Woogi (June-September):20-35 degrees of temperature, sudden heavy rain and flood risk
  • Highlands:It is very large at day and falls below 10 degrees at night
  • ultraviolet rays:UV rays are very strong due to the high altitude
  • dehydration:Moisture shortage with a dry climate
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • malaria:Essential risk of annual occurrence, prevention drugs and mosquito prevention required
  • drinking water:Only drinking water, no ice intake
  • food:Intake of street food intake, only enough cooked food
  • Medical facilities:Limited medical facilities are limited and planned for emergency situations
  • Altitude disease:Note that altitude disease symptoms in the highlands
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash -oriented:Limited credit card use, enough cash preparation
  • Exchange:Exchange only at the official exchange office, prohibit the exchange of cancer markets
  • lodgment:Optional restriction, preliminary reservation required
  • Food expenses:Local food is cheap but western food expensive
  • Transportation cost:Add fuel costs when traveling long distances
