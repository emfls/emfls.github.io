What historical and industrial aspects can travelers expect to learn about in Lysychansk?

The Severo Netzk Connection Bridge symbolizes the geographical and historical connection between Lysychansk and its twin city across the Donets River. It serves as a key point to experience the intertwined industrial and cultural heritage of the two cities.
