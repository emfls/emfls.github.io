⚠️Telps travel precautions

🚨 Alpine zone safetyism

Major areas:Cable car over 2,000m above sea level

  • Altitude disease:Headaches, dizziness can occur at 2,000m above sea level, and slowly moves with sufficient moisture intake
  • Weather change:Alpine mountainous weather changes rapidly, waterproof clothing and insulation clothes are required
  • Glacier area:Be careful with slipping on Stubai glacier, required to wear dedicated equipment
  • Hiking Safety:Only the hiking trails indicated by the marked hiking trails
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Cable car operation:Can be stopped according to the weather, required for prior confirmation
  • VVT ticket:Tyrol area integrated transportation tickets use, fines at checkpoints
  • Rent -a -car:Winter Snow Chain Essentials, Mountain Driving Experience Needs
  • Train connection:Connection of Insbruck (20 minutes), Munich (2 hours), Vienna (4 hours)
  • Mountain road:A lot of steep slopes and curves, required for safe driving
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):The temperature of 15-25 degrees, afternoon showers frequently, umbrella required
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -10-5 degrees, heavy snowable, winter supplies required
  • Spring/Autumn:15 degrees or more, morning and evening very cold
  • Alpine Zone:10 degrees lower than the flat, required for insulation clothing
  • ultraviolet rays:Very strong due to alpine and glacier reflexes
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Cable Car upper restaurant, a cafe price in the ski resort 2-3 times expensive
  • Lift fee:Telps Cable Car Round trip 35 euros, Stubai Glacier 45 euros
  • Tip Culture:Restaurant 10%, Cafe 1-2 euro, Hotel House Keeping 2-3 euro
  • Ski equipment:40-60 euros per day
