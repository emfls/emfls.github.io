⚠️ Hangzhou travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Downtown, night market, public transportation congestion section

  • pickpocket:Pickpocketing on the downtown, night market, bus and subway, packing bags in the future and locking zipper
  • Counterfeit bill:Carefully checked when exchanged (100 yuan)
  • Fake guide/guest:Negative guide and ticket sales attention around tourist attractions
  • Crossing/Signal:Short signal, unauthorized crossing and vehicle signal violation, and always care
  • Going out late at night:Human rare alleys, late time to move alone
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Both subways, buses, and taxis are available for QR code payment (Alipay/WeChat Pay), transportation card and cash preparation
  • Traffic jams:Most commute time traffic congestion
  • Taxi fee:Meter check, app call (Dichusing) recommended
  • Bicycle rental:Can use shared bicycles around West Lake and Park
  • Walking:Walking and bicycle recommendation around the wide park and lake
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):25 ~ 35 degrees, moist, hot, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):2 ~ 10 degrees, moist cold, winter clothes required
  • Spring/Autumn:A large number of days, preparation of umbrella and outerwear
  • Lake / Wetland:Morning, evening fog, dew attention
  • ultraviolet rays:Sunlight strong, sunscreen and hat required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Café and restaurant prices around West Lake, Hepangje, Theme Park 2-3 times expensive
  • entrance fee:40 ~ 80 yuan in the West Lake cruise ship, 45 yuan in Youngeunsa, 40 yuan for brain peak tower, and more than 300 yuan
  • Tip Culture:No official tip culture (exception to hotels and luxury restaurants)
  • Local food:Local restaurants are cheaper, tourist attractions and hotels are expensive
