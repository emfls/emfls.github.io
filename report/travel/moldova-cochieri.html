⚠️Cochie Eri Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Night rural areas, secluded vineyards

  • Rural areas:Coach Eri may lack lighting when moving at night to rural areas.
  • Winery Tour:Excessive drinkingism, no operation during wine tasting
  • Farm experience:Safety accident care when contacting farm equipment and animals
  • Language Wall:Because English communication is limited, basic Romanian or Russian preparation
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation limit:Restricted public transportation in rural areas, recommending rental cars or taxis
  • Road status:Some rural roads may be unpaved or poor conditions
  • Kissino connection:About 1 hour from Kissino, use minibus or taxi
  • GPS signal:GPS signals may be weak in rural areas, so prepare offline maps
  • Fuel supply:There aren't many gas stations, so check the fuel in advance
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):Temperature 18-28 degrees, strong sunlight in vineyards, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -5-5 degrees, cold and snow, winter clothes required
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and rainy and rainy, umbrella and outerwear preparation
  • Farm experience:Experience activities may be limited depending on the weather
  • Wine harvest:In September-October harvest season, there may be more tourists.
💰 Price related precautions
  • Wine price:Cheap when purchasing directly from local winery, expensive at tourist attractions
  • Farm experience:Experience program participation fee check
  • Cash payment:In rural areas, cash payments are common
  • Tip Culture:10% tip for guided tours, small tips for farm experience
