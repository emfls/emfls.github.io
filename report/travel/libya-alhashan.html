🚨 Political safetyism

Current situation:Libya is a political unstable area, requiring a travel alarm before traveling before travel.

  • Travel alert:Korea Foreign Affairs Travel Alert 3-4 areas, prohibition of travel or withdrawal recommendation
  • Local guide:Be sure to accompany local professional guides, absolutely prohibit individual travel
  • agency:Secure required for contact information for the Korean consulate, duty to register for travelers
  • insurance:Special Travel Insurance Including War Risk
🏜️ Desert travel safety rules
  • Moisture intake:At least 4-5L of water per day, take care of dehydration
  • Health disease prevention:Restriction on outdoor activities at noon, rest in the shade
  • Sand Storm:Evacuate indoor immediately during sandstorm alarms
  • Wildlife:Desert Fox, Scorpion, Piphers, etc.
  • Lost prevention:GPS required, never falls with guides
  • Emergency situation:Preparation of satellite telephone or emergency signal
🕌 Culture and religious precautions
  • Dress Regulations:Conservative dress required, especially women cover hair and limbs
  • Ramadan period:Prohibition of public food during Ramadan period
  • friday:Friday afternoon prayer time store and tourist attractions

  • drink:Absolutely prohibited to drink as an Islamic state
  • pork:Prohibition of bringing pork and related products
