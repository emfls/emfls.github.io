📅 1 night 2 -day trip course

Day 1: Visiting near Drew Bark Village and Oslo

Drew Bark Village → Oslo Fiorded Cruise → Oslo City

Recommended time:8 ~ 9 hours
Core points:Drew Bac is a cute port village located about 40 minutes by car from Oslo and is also famous as a Christmas village. After a walk and a local cafe, you can take Oslo Fiorded Cruise to enjoy the beautiful Fiorded scenery of Norway. Afterwards, you can move to Oslo City and visit the Bigelan Sculpture Park and Oslo Opera House to experience Norwegian culture and nature.

Day 2: Oslo Nature and History Experience

Program Park → Akers Fort → Norwegian Folk Museum

Recommended time:7 ~ 8 hours
