What is the recommended itinerary for visiting Hammam Sousse in Tunisia?

The recommended itinerary for Hammam Sousse is a 2-night, 3-day travel course. On Day 1, visitors explore Sus Medina, Libat fortress, the Susu Archaeological Museum, and Casba, dedicating about 8 hours to experience the historical and cultural highlights.

What are the main attractions in Hammam Sousse mentioned in the travel guide?

The main attractions include Sus Medina, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its traditional Tunisian architecture and narrow alleys; Libat, an Islamic military fortress offering panoramic views; the Susu Archaeological Museum, which houses the largest Roman mosaic collection in North Africa; and Casba, where visitors can walk along ancient city walls.

What should travelers prepare for when visiting Hammam Sousse according to the guide?

Travelers should prepare for extensive walking through narrow, winding alleys and historic sites, allocate sufficient time (around 8 hours on the first day), and be ready to explore outdoor locations such as the fortress and archaeological museum to fully appreciate the cultural and historical significance of Hammam Sousse.
