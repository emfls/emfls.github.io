⚠️ Mecca travel precautions

🚨 Religious discipline and culturalism

Main precautions:Need to comply with strict religious discipline as an Islamic holy place

  • Dress Regulations:Men wear Ihram, women are conservative clothes (Abaya, hijab)
  • Caba shooting:Strictly prohibited photographs in the interior of the carba and in certain sacred areas
  • Prayer time compliance:Stop all commercial activities at 5 times a day
  • Ramadan period:During the sun, food and drinks are prohibited in public places
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Hajah season traffic:Extreme traffic jams during the pilgrimage period, securing sufficient travel time
  • Metro use:Compliance with Gender Separation Zone when using Mechametro and Bus
  • Taxi fee:King Abdul Aziz International Airport to Mecca, about 80km, about 150 riyal
  • Walking:There is a lot of walking from the holy site, so comfortable shoes are required
  • Crowd management:Extreme congestion due to millions of pilgrims during the period
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):35-48 degrees of temperature, extremely hot and dry, heat stroke
  • Winter (12-2):The temperature of 18-30 degrees, relatively gentle, but large
  • Spring/Autumn:The temperature of 25-35 degrees, the best time for pilgrimage
  • Desert:Extremely dried and enough water intake required
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong sunlight, sunscreen and hat required
💰 Price related precautions
  • I don't have a season:In the pilgrimage period, accommodation and food prices rose 2-3 times
  • Admission fee:Most of the holy lands are free, but some museums are paid
  • Charity donation:Preparation of charity donation culture in the holy place
  • Halal food:All foods are halal, but the price is relatively high
