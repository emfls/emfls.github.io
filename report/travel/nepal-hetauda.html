🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Local bus:It takes 4-5 hours from Kathmandu to Hatada, road situation experience.
  • Taxi fee:In case of no meter, preliminary fee negotiations are required
  • Woogi Road:Roasting and road closure risks in June-September
  • Public transport strike:Sudden traffic strike (Banda) can occur
  • Tough terrain:Need to spare enough time when moving to the mountainous area
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (June-September):Essentially risk of concentrated heavy rains and landslides, umbrella and waterproof clothing
  • Dry (10-5):Dry and dusty, recommended to wear masks
  • Winter (12-2):5-20 degrees of temperature, chilly in the morning and evening
  • Summer (March-May):The temperature is 15-30 degrees, the heat and humidity are high
  • Daily crossroads:Regardless of the season, it is large, so preparation
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:2-3 times higher price demand for foreigners
  • Accommodation fee:Basic Guesthouse 1,000-3,000 rupees, hotels 3,000-8,000 rupees
  • Food expenses:Local restaurant 200-500 rupees, 500-1,500 rupees for restaurants for tourists
  • Transportation cost:Local bus 50-200 rupees, taxi 500-2,000 rupees
🏥 Health -related precautions
  • drinking water:Drink only bottled water and avoid ice, use integer tablets
  • food:Street foodism, ingest only enough cooked food
  • malaria:Use mosquito repellents, wear long sleeve clothes
