⚠️ Greenville Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the port, the outskirts of the late night

  • Political anxiety:History that was greatly destroyed by the first Liberian civil war, now stable, but
  • Wildlife:Maintaining a safe distance from wild animals in the national park, and accompanying guides
  • Local guide:Trusted local guides, prohibit moving alone
  • Going out late at night:After sunset, refrain from going out, rest in the accommodation
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road situation:There are many dirt roads and traffic difficulties in rainy season, 4WD vehicle recommended
  • Public transportation:Restricted public transportation, pre -transportation reservation required
  • flight:Domestic flights from Monrovia to Greenville
  • Fuel supply:Gas stations are limited, securing sufficient fuel
  • communication:Consideration of unstable in the outskirts, satellite calls
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (May-October):Possibility of concentrated heavy rains and flood risks, restrictions on movement
  • Dry (11-4):The temperature is 25-35 degrees, dry but dusty with the wind
  • humidity:Destructive risk, sufficient moisture intake with high humidity throughout the year
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat required near the equator
  • Tropical disease:Prevention measures such as malaria, yellow fever, dengue fever, etc.
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:The price of daily necessities is high due to dependence on imported goods
  • Cash payment:Limited credit card use, enough cash preparation
