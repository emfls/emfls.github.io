⚠️ Song Kla Travel Precautions

🌡️Checks related to weather

Tropical climate:Songkla needs to be particularly careful in summer with hot and humid tropical climate throughout the year.

  • High temperature and humidity:Summer temperature is very high and humidity is high, so there is a risk of dehydration.
  • Strong ultraviolet light:High SPF UV blockers required to prevent burns due to strong ultraviolet rays in the tropics
  • Preparation for the rainy season:During the rainy season in May-October
  • Mosquitoism:Due to the nature of the tropics, there are many mosquitoes, so prepare mosquito eradication and long -sleeved clothes
🚗 Precautions when using transportation
  • Heart Yai via:Songkla is located 30-40 minutes away from Heart Yay to a mini van, and can be moved to 34 baht.
  • Local transportation:In Song Cla, moving using Song Tae -woo or tuk -tuk
  • Tokuan Hill access:Walk to the observation deck or use a lift for 45 baht per person
  • Island Tour:Boats to Rat Island and Cat Island Need to make a phone reservation in the room.
🍽️ Food and hygiene precautions
  • Street food:Select a place with a good hygiene when eating local street food
  • Bottled water drinking:Do not drink tap water directly, drink only water or boiled water
  • Iceism:It is safe to avoid the ice of drinks sold on the street
  • Spicy food:Thai food can be very spicy, so you can order it at first
  • Local special food:Starting from a small amount of special food experiences in Songla area
🛕 Religion and cultural courtesy
  • When visiting the temple:Wearing clothes covering your shoulders and knees, taking off your shoes and entering
  • Buddhist shooting:Taking a picture with a Buddha statue is considered a rude behavior.
  • Prohibit the touch of the head:In Thailand, the head is sacred, so it prohibits the touch of others' heads
  • To the toes:It is rude to make the toes to the Buddha or the person
  • Respect for Chinese culture:Respect for Chinese temples and culture in the region where many Chinese live
🐒 Monkey precautions
  • Tokuan Hill Monkey:Note that 20-30 monkeys appear on the stairs that go up to the observatory.
  • Safety distance:Maintaining the proper safe distance with monkeys and prohibiting food exposure
  • Alternative path:If the monkey is scary, access to the observation deck using a lift
  • Supplies Management:Note that monkeys can take bags or belongings
🏖️ Beach safety precautions
  • Swimming safety:Check the algae and waves when swimming on Samila Beach
  • Personal belongings:When playing on the beach, attention to personal belongings management
  • Health disease prevention:Rest in the shade for a long time in the beach activity
  • Beach facility:Comply with local rules when using facilities around the beach
