자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What is the significance of the Spitak Earthquake Memorial?

The Spitak Earthquake Memorial commemorates the devastating earthquake on December 7, 1988, which measured 7.0 in magnitude and completely destroyed the city. It serves as a solemn reminder of the tragedy and honors the lives affected.

What can visitors expect to see at the Spitak Central Church?

Visitors to the Spitak Central Church will experience Armenian Orthodox architecture that symbolizes the city's cultural heritage and resilience, as the church was rebuilt during the city's reconstruction after the earthquake.

What is the recommended itinerary for a 3-day trip to Spitak?

A recommended itinerary includes spending Day 1 exploring the historical and memorial sites such as the Earthquake Memorial Hall, Central Church, and Reconstruction Memorial Square, followed by a visit to the Caucasus Mountains Observatory to appreciate the natural scenery.
