⚠️ Ovoc travel precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Borders around the port late at night

  • Border dispute:Somalia, prohibit access to Ethiopia border, anti -government forces activity regionalism
  • Refugee problem:Somalia, security instability around Ethiopian Refugee Camp
  • Camera theft:Camera or cell phone robbery in a tourist destination
  • Night going out:Absolutely prohibited to go alone after sunset
  • Local guide:Required to accompany a reliable local guide
🌡️ Climate and health precautions
  • Extreme heat:Summer (June-August) temperature of 40 degrees or more, heat stroke care
  • Dry Visit:November-April is the optimal travel period, relatively cool
  • Dewnity risk:Full moisture intake, 3-4 liters of water per day
  • malaria:Mosquito -mediated diseases risks, prevention drugs and insect repellent use
  • yellow fever:Request for vaccination, testing certificate when entering Korea
  • Strong ultraviolet light:Recommended sunscreen, recommended to wear long -sleeved clothes
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Limited transportation:Lack of public transportation facilities, required to use rental cars or taxis
  • Road status:Many unpaved roads, recommended 4WD vehicles
  • Lack of fuel:Need to secure gas station limited and sufficient fuel
  • GPS required:Lack of road signs, navigation required
  • Emergency situation:Restrictions on medical facilities, pre -acquisition
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:The price of daily necessities is high due to high dependence on imports
  • US dollar:Essential to bring dollars, limited card use
  • Accommodation fee:High price due to limited accommodations
  • foodstuffs:Western foods other than local foods very expensive
  • Transportation cost:Taxi and car rental costs are high
