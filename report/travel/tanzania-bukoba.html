⚠️ Bucoba travel precautions

🚨 Health and safetyism

Majorism:Malaria, yellow fever, typhoids, hepatitis A

  • malaria:Bucoba is used at all times of malaria dangerous areas, prevention drugs, mosquito repellents permanently
  • yellow fever:Request for vaccination certificate, inspection when entering Korea
  • drinking water:Only drink bottled water, prohibit ice intake, avoid raw fruit
  • hygiene:Personal use of hand disinfectants, street food attention
  • Emergency situation:Local hospital information check in advance, travel insurance required
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Bucoba Airport:Only domestic flights, DarS Salam or Non -Well -Warge Needs
  • perry:Bucoba-Regular ferry between Bucoba-Maloin, can be stopped depending on the weather
  • Road status:In the rainy season, the road condition is poor, 4WD car recommended
  • Public transportation:Careful pickpockets when using Dala Dala (minibus)
  • Border pass:Near the Uganda border, check visa when passing the border
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (March-May, October-December):Rain and flood risks, road traffic difficulties
  • Dry (June-September):Dry and dusty, important water intake
  • Temperature:20-30 degrees all year round,
  • humidity:Humidity is high near the lake, mold prevention
  • ultraviolet rays:UV rays very strong near the equator
💰 Security and economic precautions
  • pickpocket:Caution and valuables at the market and bus terminal
  • fraud:Caution of fake guides or excessive rates
  • Exchange:Official currency exchange and prohibition of street currency exchange
  • Tip Culture:Appropriate tips to service workers
  • Bargaining:Price bargaining in the market
