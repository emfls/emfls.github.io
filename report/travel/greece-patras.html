⚠️ Patra travel precautions

🌡️ Mediterranean climate precautions

Summer:The temperature from June-September is high and dry, so enough water intake and UV protection required

  • Prevention of heat stroke:Outdoor activity restraint between 12 pm and 4 pm, rest in the shade
  • Dry climate:Low humidity, risk of dehydration, enough water
  • UV protection:Use high SPF sunscreen, hats and sunglasses are required
  • Winter:December-February is gentle, but it rains a lot of umbrella required
🏔️ Terrain and pedestrian safety
  • Hills and stairs:Patra is a city with many hills, a comfortable walking shoe
  • St. Nicholas Stairs:Sufficient rest and moisture when using steep stairs
  • Patra Castle:Be careful with safety because the road to the castle is steep.
  • water front:Note that the wind around Patras can be strong
🚗 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Rio Antirioism:It is expensive at 11.70 tolls, but the only way to connect to the mainland
  • Public transportation:The bus operating interval may be long, so check the schedule in advance
  • Taxi:Checking the use of meters, applying for night premiums
  • Rent -a -car:Care for driving narrow roads and hill roads
🏪 Business hours and holidays
  • Sunday Closed:Most shops and markets are closed on Sunday
  • Siesta time:At around 2-5 pm, many shops rest time
  • Museum confirmation:Operating hours and holidays prior confirmation before visiting tourist destinations
  • Winery:Aka Claus winery recommended pre -booking
🎭 Patra carnival season precautions

The largest carnival festival in Europe, which runs from January 17 to Greece Lent

  • Accommodation reservation:Increased accommodation and difficulties in booking during the carnival period
  • Traffic congestion:During the festival period, traffic control and congestion
  • Safetyism:Caution of pickpockets and lost items due to many crowds
  • noise:Night noise can be generated due to festivals
