📅 Helsinki 3 nights 4 days travel course

Day 1: Helsinki Central Station Central City Exploration

Helsinki Central Station → Helsinki Cathedral → Uspensky Cathedral → Market Plaza → Central Library OODI

Recommended time:5 hours 30 minutes
Core points:As it is the first day, take a look around the city of Helsinki. Walking around attractions close to Helsinki Central Station will welcome places with various charms such as parks, cathedrals and libraries. All walks are enough, and you can enjoy the unique architectural style of Finland and the neat design of Nordic.
📍Helsinki Central Station 📍Helsinki Cathedral 📍Ospensky Cathedral 📍 Market Square

Day 2: A day to meet design and nature

Design District → Splanadi Park → Suo Menrinna → Market Square Dinner

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:It is the day of meeting Helsinki, famous for its 'design city'. In the morning, it is time to explore various galleries of Design District, and then enjoy the beauty of nature. See the Baltic Sea Park and the UNESCO World Heritage Site, Suo Menrinna, and have dinner at the market. You can check the ferry time in advance and use the ferry as a ticket to Helsinki A and B.
📍 Design District 📍S Planadi Park 📍Suo Menrinna 📍 Market Square

Day 3: Unique Building and Sauna Experience

Campy Chapel → Tempelia Ukio Church → Sibelius Monument → Shopping → Sauna Experience

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:It's time to look at Helsinki's unique architecture. Tempelia Ukio Church, built in the Campy Chapel and Rock, is representative. Enjoy shopping in the middle and enjoy the time to enjoy the beach. Finally, Finland's pride, Sauna, is waiting.
📍 Campy Chapel 📍 Tempelia Ukio Church 📍Sibellius Monument 📍Sauna experience

Day 4: Finished with the morning market and shopping

Mori Market → Marie Meko Outlet → Stock Bay Department Store → Artech Design Shop → Airport Movement

Recommended time:5 hours
Core points:The last day starts with the morning market. If you are a fan of Marimetko, it is recommended to go to the outlet and enjoy shopping. If time is allowed afterwards, you can visit the department store Stokman and Design Furniture Shop Arstec. Meet the essence of Finnish design.
📍 Morning Market 📍 Marie Meko Outlet 📍 Stockman Department Store 📍Helsinki Airport

🚌 Precautions on public transportation
  • Helsinki Card:Since the entrance fee for major tourist attractions is 10-20 euros, it is recommended to purchase Helsinki Card.
  • Traffic schedule:When using public transportation, it is important to check the timetable and monitor the luggage during the ride.
  • Taxi:Avoid taxis that use official taxis and forced to use meters.
  • A · B area ticket:Ferry is also available as a ticket to Helsinki A and B
🛡️ Prevention of safety and crime
  • Safe city:Helsinki is a safe city, but travelers must prepare for crime.
  • Storage of Cherry products:It is a good idea to keep it in the hotel's safe and to go carefully on the street.
  • Nightism:It is a good idea to always choose a bright place at night.
  • Valuable goods management:Keep your passport or big money in a safe place, and only small cash
🍽️ Food and beverage precautions
  • Allergicism:Be careful with allergies or ingredients when you enjoy local food. In particular, attention to foods that contain dairy products and meat
  • Food safety:It is a good idea to buy fresh foods and use water purifiers or bottles for water.
  • High prices:The price of the Helsinki restaurant is very expensive compared to Seoul, so prepare the budget enough.
  • Lunch time:Many restaurants offer a relatively inexpensive menu at lunchtime, so please use it.
🗣️ Cultural courtesy
  • Respect for personal space:The Finns value personal space, so it's a good idea not to get too close to others.
  • Public place manners:In public places, it is polite to minimize the noise and use the mobile phone quietly.
  • Language communication:Finnish and Swedish are official language, but English communication is possible at tourist attractions.

💡 Helsinki Travel Tips

  • Compact city center:Helsinki is very small in the city, so it is enough to use public transportation or walk on foot.
  • Design City:Finland is a world -renowned design powerhouse, the home of famous brands such as Marimetko, Itala, and Artech.
  • Sauna Culture:Be sure to experience Sauna, a representative culture of Finland. It ranges from public sauna to hotel sauna
  • White night and extreme night:You can experience white nights in summer and extreme night phenomena in winter.
  • Free Museum:There are many free museums and parks for budget travelers.
  • Card payment:Card payment is common rather than cash, so prepare your credit card.
  • English communication:It is possible to communicate sufficiently in English in tourist destinations.
  • Harmony of nature and city:It is Helsinki's charm that you can easily meet nature in the city center.
