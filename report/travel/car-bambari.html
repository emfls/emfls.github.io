⚠️ Night Bari Travel Precautions

🚨 security and safetyism

Dangerous area:Outstream of the city, mining area, near border

  • Political instability:The Central African Republic is required to confirm the travel warning of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs before traveling as political turmoil continues.
  • Strength and theft:In urban areas, robbery, thieves, and police robbers are frequently occurred.
  • Armed group:Rural areas are insensitive to unlaw and armed group activity
  • Prohibition of going out at night:After the sun, never go out alone
  • Local guide:Accompanied by a reliable local guide
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Road status:Many sections that are bumpy and daggers that are impossible to pass during rainy season.
  • Public transportation:There is almost no public transportation system, so it is required to use personal vehicles or taxi
  • Fuel supply:There are rarely gas stations and frequent fuel shortages
  • Border closure:All land borders have been closed to foreigners
  • flight:Delay and cancellation of flights when using Bambari Airport
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-3):15-34 degrees, dry and hot, enough water intake
  • Rainy (4-10):19-31 degrees, wet and raining, malaria risk increased
  • Daily crossroads:Preparation of clothing for insulation because there is a large crossover during the dry season
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet, sunscreen and hat required near the equator
  • Preparation for the rainy season:Road flooding and traffic paralysis
🏥 Health and medical precautions
  • malaria:Malaria dangerous areas throughout the year, taking prevention drugs and mosquito prevention
  • yellow fever:Essentials for vaccination of yellow fever disease, certificate test when entering Korea
  • Water -person disease:Risks such as cholera and typhoids, only bottled water
  • Medical facilities:The medical infrastructure is very poor, and it is necessary to transfer to a nearby country in case of emergency
  • HIV/AIDS:High infection rates, attention when using medical instruments
