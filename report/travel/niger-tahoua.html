📅 Taua 2 nights 3 days travel course

Day 1: History and Market Exploration

Grand Marche → Sultan Palace → Taua Mosque → Taua Water Tower

Recommended time:8 hours
Core points:In the traditional market, one of the largest in the Sahel area, we toured the spices, fabrics, and livestock auctions, watched the architecture beauty of the Hausa -Tumerg mixed style in the Sultan's palace that came down since the 19th century, and experienced local Islamic culture in Taua Mosk, which was built with ocher brick, and viewed a panorama that touched the desert and Sahel in the Water Tower Observatory.
📍 Grand Marshe 📍Sultan Palace 📍Taua Mosque 📍Taua Water Tower

Day 2: Cultural Experience and Lake Landscape

Taua Museum → Taua Culture Center → Mare de Talak Lake → Local Cheese Workshop

Recommended time:9 hours
Core points:In the museum containing local archaeological and folk artifacts, explores the historical roots of Taua, enjoys the traditional music and dance performances of Pulani, Tuareg at the Cultural Center, and enjoys Flamingo and Sunset in Mary de Tarakrak, the mysterious desert lake of Sahel, and a specialty product.ChukuExperience in traditional workshops that make cheese
