🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Around the train station, late at night

  • Supplies Management:Thorough management of bags and valuables in winery and tourist attractions
  • Night going out:Be careful to go alone after 10 pm
  • Emergency situation:Emergency Telephone 112, Police 113, Fire Station 112
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:The timetable is checked in advance because the number of bus operations is small
  • Taxi:Recommendation for use of meters, recommendation of pre -fee negotiations

  • Train connection:It takes about 1 hour and 30 minutes to Liublana
  • Road situation:Caution for sudden curves in the mountainous road section

  • Summer (June-August):Green 15-25 degrees, sometimes showers, light umbrella preparation
