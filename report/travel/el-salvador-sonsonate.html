🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Outside the city, around the central market late at night

  • robbery:Cash and valuables with minimal, fake wallet preparation
  • Gang:Prohibition of access to the outskirts and slums, recommending local guides
  • Night going out:Absolutely prohibited to go alone after sunset, required to use taxi
  • Tourism fraud:Precautions for fake tour guides or excessive charges
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Bus safety:Pickpocket and robbery when using chicken buses (old school buses)
  • Taxi:Only official taxis, checking meters or negotiations with pre -charges
  • Rent -a -car:International driving license required, experience of driving on mountain roads
  • Road situation:Roasting in the rainy season and risk of flooding roads
  • Volcanic area:Volcanic climbing without a guide, weather changeism
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Dry (11-4):Temperature 18-32 degrees, dry and hot, fire danger
  • Woogi (May-October):20-28 degrees of temperature, risk of concentrated heavy rains and landslides
  • hurricane:June-November Hurricane season, occasional weather information check
  • Altitude difference:A rapid change in temperature from the coast (0m) to the volcanic (2,381m)
  • ultraviolet rays:Strong ultraviolet rays, sunscreen and hat required near the equator
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:2-3 times higher price demand for foreigners
