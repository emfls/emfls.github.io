자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main highlights of a 3-day trip to Ulm, Germany?

A 3-day trip to Ulm includes exploring the medieval Ulm Cathedral with its towering spire, visiting Markt Square, City Hall, Fisher Street, and the tilted Schiff House on Day 1. Day 2 focuses on cultural and nature experiences such as the Einstein Monument, Ulm City Museum, a Danube River cruise, and the German Bread Museum.

How long does it take to explore Ulm’s medieval sites on the first day?

It is recommended to spend approximately 8 hours on the first day to thoroughly explore Ulm’s medieval sites including the Ulm Cathedral, Markt Square, City Hall, Fisher Street, and the Schiff House.

What unique experience does climbing the Ulm Cathedral spire offer?

Climbing the 768 stairs of the Ulm Cathedral spire, the tallest cathedral spire in the world at 161.5 meters, offers breathtaking panoramic views of the city of Ulm, the Danube River, and the Alps, along with a close look at the cathedral’s Gothic architecture.
