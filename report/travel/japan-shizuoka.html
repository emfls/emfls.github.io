⚠️Sizuoka travel precautions

🚨 Nature and safetyism

Major areas:Fujisan Mountain Trail, Coastal Cliffs, Waterfall

  • Fuji Mountain Climbing:Climbing from July to September, temperature difference, strong wind, weather rapidism, hiking and waterproofing required
  • Coastal Cliff:Jogasaki coast, Mihono Matsubara, etc.
  • Hot Spring Etiquette:Before entering the hot spring, the ban on towels and head hot springs, tattoos are waterproof tape preparation
  • Beach / Suyeong:Use in summer and waves in summer
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:Use Shinkansen, JR Pass, Shizuketsu Bus, etc.
  • Rent -a -car:Japan is required for left -side traffic, international driver's license, tolls and parking costs.
  • bicycle:It is useful for walking downtown and coast, but maintains a safe distance with vehicles and pedestrians
  • highway:Visitable to Shintomei Expressway, Hyeondo Line 223 (Fuji Mountain Line), etc.
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):Temperature 25 ~ 32 degrees, high humidity, heat wave and ultraviolet light
  • Winter (12-2):Gion 5 ~ 13 degrees, gentle but windy
  • Spring/Autumn:Daily crossing, non -independence, umbrella and outerwear preparation
  • Fujisan:Normal temperature below zero, the wind is very strong, the winter required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination:Fukisan, hot spring, and coastal restaurants and souvenirs and souvenir prices are 2-3 times expensive
  • entrance fee:Sugenji Hot Springs Free/Paid, Omurosan Rift 500 yen, SL Train and Museum
  • Tip Culture:Japan includes unnecessary tips and services in the bill.
