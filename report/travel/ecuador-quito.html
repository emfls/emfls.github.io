자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main historical sites to visit in Quito during a 3-day trip?

The main historical sites include the Quito Historic District, Independence Square, La Compañía de Jesús Church, San Francisco Church, and El Panecillo Hill. These locations showcase Quito's 16th-century Spanish colonial architecture and cultural heritage.

How much time should I allocate to explore Quito's historical district on the first day?

It is recommended to allocate about 9 hours on the first day to thoroughly explore the historical district and visit key landmarks such as Independence Square, La Compañía de Jesús Church, San Francisco Church, and El Panecillo Hill.

What is the significance of La Compañía de Jesús Church in Quito?

La Compañía de Jesús Church is a prominent religious building decorated with gold, representing the Baroque architecture of the Spanish colonial era. It is a key cultural and historical site within Quito's UNESCO World Heritage Historic District.
