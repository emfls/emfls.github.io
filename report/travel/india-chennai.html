What are the main attractions to visit on the first day in Chennai?

On the first day, visitors should explore Marina Beach, one of India's longest beaches known for its Indian Ocean sunrise, Kapaleeshwarar Temple showcasing Hindu architecture, Santome Cathedral reflecting Chennai's Christian heritage, and Port St. George, a historic British colonial fortress and museum.

How long is the recommended travel course for the first day in Chennai?

The recommended travel course for the first day in Chennai is approximately 9 hours, allowing ample time to visit and experience the key cultural and historical sites.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect in Chennai according to the guide?

Travelers can experience a blend of local religious culture at Kapaleeshwarar Temple, Christian heritage at Santome Cathedral, and colonial history at Port St. George, along with enjoying the natural beauty of Marina Beach.
