⚠️Addan travel precautions

🚨 Political safetyism

Current situation:Libya is a political unstable condition, a safety information of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs before traveling required

  • Government permission:Prior permits of local authorities when traveling to Wardan area
  • Guide required:Prohibition of travel alone, accompanying local accredited guides
  • checkpoint:Preparation of passport and permission when passing the military checkpoint
  • Shooting limit:Military facilities, absolute prohibition of shooting government buildings
  • Assembly area:Prohibited absolute approach to political rally or protest areas
🏜️ Desert Environmental Precautions
  • Dewnity risk:At least 4-5L of water per day, supplemented moisture before thirst
  • Solar disease:Avoid outdoor activities at noon and relax in the shade
  • Sand Storm:Indoor evacuation and respiratory protection for sandstorm forecasting
  • Night cold:Desert night temperature dive, warm clothing required
  • Poison:Scorpion, viperism, tent internal confirmation after going to bed
  • Lost:Always carry GPS and compasses, do not fall to the guide
🚗 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • 4WD required:In the desert area, only four -wheel drive vehicles can be moved.
  • Fuel:Gas station intervals are far away and excess fuel preparation required
  • Vehicle inspection:Thorough inspection of tires, engines and coolant before departure
  • Communication equipment:Preparation of satellite telephone or emergency communication equipment
  • Driving time:Driving only from sunrise to sunset
🌡️ Extreme climate precautions
  • Summer (June-September):45-50 degrees of temperature, life risk, travel expenses recommendation
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature 5-25 degrees, fall until minus minus
  • Optimal period:March-May, October-November is the best to travel
  • Daily crossroads:Daily and night temperature difference 30 degrees or more, preparation for clothing is required
  • ultraviolet rays:Desert UV rays very strong, complete blocking required
💰 Local situation precautions
  • Cash -oriented:Nearly impossible use of cards, enough cash preparation
  • Exchange:Pre -exchange in Tripoli, cannot be exchanged in Wadan
  • price:The price of daily necessities is high, low fuel cost
  • Internet:Unstable unstable internet connection
  • electrical energy:Captain, auxiliary battery required
