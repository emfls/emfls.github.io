⚠️ Irai travel precautions

🌊 Marine activity safetyism

Main precautions:Strong bird, toxic marine life, coral reef protection

  • Algaeism:Strong algae occurred in external sea such as Blue Corner, and must be diving with guides.
  • jellyfish:Toxic jellyfish, rash guards are required outside of the jellyfish lake
  • Coral reef protection:Prohibit coral reefs, use of coral reef safety sunscreen
  • Diving Safety:Prohibition of scuba diving without a certificate, compliance with safety suspension
  • Boat safety:Wearing life jackets, compliance with safety rules when moving boats
🏝️ Environmental Protection Regulations
  • Palau Pledge:Duty to prepare environmental protection pledge when entering Korea
  • Prohibition of disposable goods:Prohibition of importing disposable items such as plastic bottles and plastic bags
  • Sunscreen regulation:Prohibition of use of sunscreen with harmful ingredients on coral reefs
  • Garbage processing:All garbage is separated in a designated place
  • Wildlife:Touching marine life, absolutely prohibited to feed
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Woogi (May-October):Schol and typhoon possibilities, weather changeism
  • Dry (11-4):Diving optimal, but the wind can be strong
  • ultraviolet rays:Very strong ultraviolet rays near the equator, enough blocking
  • humidity:Fungalism, waterproofing with high humidity throughout the year
  • typhoon:Preparation for the possibility of changing flights when traveling in a typhoon season
💰 Price related precautions
  • High prices:2-3 times expensive compared to Korea due to dependence on imports of goods
