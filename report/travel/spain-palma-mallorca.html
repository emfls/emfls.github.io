⚠️Palmade Mallorca Travel Note

🚨 Pickpocket and general criminal precautions

Palmad de Mayorca is a relatively safe city, but it is always a good idea to be careful about your belongings.In particular, attention is needed in tourist attractions and beach areas.

  • Main dangerous areas:Especially in tourist attractions such as Palma Cathedral, Borin Street, and Beach
  • Bag management:Always close your bag near your body. On the beach, leave valuables in the hostel
  • Mobile phone management:Recommended for using mobile phones on the beach, waterproof case use
  • Night activity:Avoid late at a time remote beach or mountainous region alone
🚗 Traffic safety precautions
  • Rent -a -car use:Mallorca is the same as Jeju Island, so it is inconvenient to travel without a car rental. International driver's license required
  • Mountain road:Mountain areas such as Valdemosa and Sodar are required for safe driving because there are many winding roads.
  • Public transportation:The bus is good, but you need to check the timetable. Taxi is cheaper
  • parking:It is recommended to use public transportation because it is difficult to park in Palma city.
🏖️ Beach safety precautions
  • Swimming safety:Use of places where safety personnel are swimming only in designated beaches
  • UV protection:Burnism, sunscreen due to the strong sunlight of the Mediterranean,
  • Beach rules:Some beaches are nude beaches, so preliminary check
  • Algaeism:Small beaches such as Karlo Death Moro can be strong in algae
🍽️ Food and restaurant precautions
  • mealtime:Spain is late for lunch at 2-4 pm and dinner at 9-11 pm
  • Restaurant reservation:It is good to book a popular restaurant in advance
  • Tip Culture:5-10% tips are common in the restaurant
  • Seafoodism:There are many fresh seafood, but if you have allergies, be careful
🌤️Chires and costumes
  • Seasonal dress:April-October travel optimal, light clothes in summer, and warm clothes in winter
  • Mountain area:Valdemosa and Sodar are lower than the coast, so prepare the outerwear
  • Beach dress:In addition to swimsuit
  • Daily crossroads:In spring and autumn, the crossover may be large, so the layered look is recommended
