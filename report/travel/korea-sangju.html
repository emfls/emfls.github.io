⚠️ Resident travel precautions

🚨 Safety precautions

Major areas:Gyeongcheon University Cliff section, Nakdong River Bicycle Road Night Section

  • Gyeongcheon University viewing:Don't go beyond the safety railing in the cliff section, but keep the safety distance when taking pictures
  • Bicycle Ride:Must wear helmets, light lamps and back lamps when riding at night
  • Climbing safety:Prohibition of departure from climbing trails when climbing in Cheonghwa Mountain, Hassan for worsening weather
  • Riverside activity:Pay attention to swimming on the Nakdong River, approaching the river
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • Public transportation:The interval between the bus in the city is long, so the timetable is required in advance.
  • Private use:Use of Sangju IC on Gyeongbu Expressway, weekend traffic jams
  • Bicycle rental:Bicycles can be rented in Sangjubo area, recommended pre -booking
  • parking:Tourist attraction parking lot, high -season parking space shortage
  • Taxi:The number of taxis in the city can take a long time.
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-August):25-35 degrees, hot and humid, enough water intake
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature -10-5 degrees, cold, dryness, winter supplies required
  • Spring/Autumn:Big and fine dustism, preparation of masks
  • Riverside area:Strong winds can be low in temperature.
  • Rainy weather:Bicycle path slippery, hiking trails can be controlled
💰 Price related precautions
  • Tourist destination prices:Gyeongcheon University, the price of restaurants around Sangjubo is somewhat higher
  • Museum entrance fee:Resident Museum Free, Bicycle Museum 3,000 won
  • Experience Program:Dried persimmon experience 15,000-25,000 won
  • Bicycle rental:5,000-10,000 won per hour
