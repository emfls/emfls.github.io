🚨 security and safetyism

Major areas:Late at night, outside the Bocca Stardan, an uninhabited island area

  • pickpocket:Pickpocket care in tourist attractions, carry waterproof bags forward and lock the zipper
  • Marine safety:Buddy system compliance with snorkeling, prohibiting swimming alone
  • Boat safety:Essential to be wearing life jackets, canceling tours in case of deterioration of weather
  • Going out late at night:Precautions to go alone after 10 pm, recommend group activities
🚌 Traffic and mobile precautions
  • flight:From Panama City to Bocas Delto -ro for 1 hour domestic flight and weather
  • Boat Taxi:Major transportation, wearing seat belts and life jackets
  • Island between islands:Required of the assistant schedule, it is possible to stop operating at low light
  • Land access:Buses can be used at the Costa Rica border, but it takes time
  • Rainy traffic:Latitude and cancellation of transportation in the rainy season in May-December
