자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What is the recommended travel course for a 2-night, 3-day trip to Gwangyang?

The recommended itinerary includes visiting Gwangyang Maehwa Village to see the plum blossoms, exploring Baekunsan Natural Recreation Forest, touring the historical site Okryongsaji, and enjoying traditional Korean beef bulgogi on Gwangyang Bulgogi Street.

When is the best time to visit Gwangyang Maehwa Village to see the plum trees?

The best time to visit Gwangyang Maehwa Village is in mid-March when approximately 100,000 plum trees are in full bloom, offering spectacular scenic views.

What local cuisine is recommended to try in Gwangyang?

Travelers are encouraged to try Korean beef bulgogi, especially on Gwangyang Bulgogi Street, where it is traditionally grilled over charcoal and seasoned with authentic Korean spices.
