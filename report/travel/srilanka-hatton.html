Major areas:With Adams Peak Climbing, slippery rock around the waterfall

  • Adams Peak Climbing:Departure at 2:00 am, required for normal arrival before sunrise, December-May of Climbing Season
  • Altitude disease:Headaches, nausea, at 2,000m above sea level, immediately descending
  • Weather change:Marking in the alpine zones, changes in weather, raincoats and winter clothes required
  • Waterfall safety:Rocks around the waterfall are very slippery, safe distance

  • Mountain road:Mountain roads, motion sickness preparations required
