자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main attractions to visit in Biel/Bienne during a 3-day trip?

Key attractions include the medieval old town with its 15th-century Gothic Church, Fountain, and Guild Hall, the Swatch and Omega Museums located in the watchmaking district, and the Biel New Museum featuring regional arts, history, and archaeology exhibitions.

How much time should I plan to spend exploring Biel/Bienne on the first day?

It is recommended to spend about 8 hours on the first day to fully experience the old town walk, visit the watch industry museums, and explore the Biel New Museum.

What cultural experiences does Biel/Bienne offer related to the watch industry?

Biel/Bienne is known as the 'City of Time' and offers visitors the chance to learn about the history and technology of global watch brands through visits to the Swatch and Omega Museums, showcasing the region’s significance in watchmaking.
