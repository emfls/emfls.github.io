What are the main attractions to visit in Hyesan City during a 3-day trip?

The main attractions include the Bocheon Conservation Winning Memorial Tower, which commemorates anti-Japanese armed struggle history, Hyesanjinseong fortress, Gunggungjeong observation deck overlooking the Yalu River, and a scenic walk along the Yalu River itself.

How long is the recommended travel course for Day 1 in Hyesan City?

The recommended travel course for Day 1 in Hyesan City is approximately 8 hours, covering key historical and revolutionary sites along with border views.

What historical significance does the Bocheon Conservation Winning Memorial Tower hold?

The Bocheon Conservation Winning Memorial Tower, standing 38.7 meters high, commemorates the anti-Japanese armed struggle led by Kim Il-sung in 1937 and serves as a symbol of revolutionary history in the Liang Lang provinces.
