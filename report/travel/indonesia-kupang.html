자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What are the main attractions included in the Kupang travel itinerary?

The main attractions are Kupang Cathedral, a Dutch colonial-era building; Sasando Museum, where visitors learn about the Sasando instrument and local culture; Passar Cassi Traditional Market, featuring fresh agricultural products, spices, and handicrafts; and Kampung Solor Seafood Night Market, known for freshly caught fish, shrimp, and crab.

How much time should I allocate for the Day 1 tour in Kupang?

The recommended time for the Day 1 itinerary is approximately 9 hours, allowing ample time to visit each site and experience local culture and cuisine.

What cultural experiences can travelers expect in Kupang on this itinerary?

Travelers can experience Dutch colonial architecture at Kupang Cathedral, learn about the history and production of the Sasando musical instrument at the Sasando Museum, explore traditional markets with local agricultural products and handicrafts, and enjoy fresh seafood at the Kampung Solor Seafood Night Market.
