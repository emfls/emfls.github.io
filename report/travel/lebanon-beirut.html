자주 묻는 질문 (FAQ)

What is the recommended itinerary for a 2-night, 3-day trip to Beirut?

The recommended itinerary includes exploring Beirut's historical and religious sites such as the Beirut National Museum, Mohamed Al-Amin Mosque, St. George Greek Orthodox Church, and Al Omari Mosque, with Day 1 focusing on an 8-hour tour of these key locations.

What cultural and historical highlights can travelers expect in Beirut?

Travelers can expect to see Lebanon's Phoenician civilization artifacts at the Beirut National Museum, admire modern Islamic architecture at the Mohamed Al-Amin Mosque, explore Early Byzantine history at the St. George Greek Orthodox Church, and experience multi-religious culture at the 12th-century Al Omari Mosque.

How much time should be allocated to visit the main historical and religious sites in Beirut on Day 1?

It is recommended to allocate approximately 8 hours on Day 1 to thoroughly visit and appreciate the Beirut National Museum, Mohamed Al-Amin Mosque, St. George Greek Orthodox Church, and Al Omari Mosque.
