What is the recommended itinerary for a 3-night, 4-day trip to Virginia Beach?

The recommended itinerary includes spending Day 1 walking along the three-mile Virginia Beach Boardwalk, attending the Neptune Festival, visiting Ocean Front Beach, seeing the King Neptune Statue, and relaxing at 31st Street Park, with an estimated 8 hours to enjoy these highlights.

What are the main attractions to see on the first day in Virginia Beach?

On the first day, visitors should explore the Virginia Beach Boardwalk, enjoy the Neptune Festival if it coincides with their visit, visit the Ocean Front Beach for sunbathing and swimming, take photos at the King Neptune Statue, and unwind at 31st Street Park.

What should travelers prepare for when visiting Virginia Beach?

Travelers should prepare for outdoor beach activities by bringing appropriate swimwear, sun protection, and comfortable walking shoes to enjoy the Boardwalk and beach areas. Planning for about 8 hours on Day 1 ensures a relaxed and comprehensive experience of Virginia Beach’s key attractions.
