⚠️ Precautions on plum travel

🚨 security and safetyism

Main precautions:Unstable security situation after the Libyan Civil War

  • Political anxiety:Since the Libyan Civil War, local security conditions are unstable, so local guides are required.
  • Lack of conflict:Since there is a possibility of conflict between regions,
  • checkpoint:Since there are many military checkpoints, ID cards and travel purposes are always portable.
  • Night travel:Night movement is absolutely prohibited, waiting for the hostel
🏜️ Desert Environmental Precautions
  • Extreme Daily Interior:Temperature difference to 40 degrees daytime and 5 degrees at night, preparation of sufficient clothing
  • Sand Storm:In the event of a sudden sandstorm, moving to the shelter immediately
  • Dewnity risk:At least 4 liters of water per day, water before thirst
  • Loss of direction:Since there is a high risk of getting lost in the desert,
  • Poisonism:Desert biology such as scorpions and vipers, mandatory shoes and sleeping checks
🌡️Checks related to weather
  • Summer (June-September):More than 45 degrees of temperature, extreme hot, travel expenses recommendation
  • Winter (12-2):Temperature 5-20 degrees, night cold, winter supplies required
  • Spring/Autumn:Meeting 15-30 degrees, travel optimal, and preparation for day crossing
  • Strong ultraviolet light:UV rays in the desert area very strong, SPF 50+ sunscreen required
  • Dryness:Extremely dry environment, lip balm and moisturizer required
💰 Price related precautions
  • Cash -oriented:Limited credit card use, enough cash preparation
