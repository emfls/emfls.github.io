    What you could lose to NALI tax

    If NALI applies, the taxable portion can face 45% even in retirement phase. For example, A$30,000 of net rent could incur A$13,500 in tax instead of A$4,500 in accumulation. The sale gain can also be affected. Missing a PCG term requires a separate arm’s-length assessment, and failing the property rule can breach super borrowing law independently of tax.

    How the tax estimate works

    The ordinary scenario assumes a complying fund in accumulation or fully exempt retirement phase. A one-third discount reduces the effective accumulation rate on an eligible sale gain to 10%. The NALI scenario applies 45% to positive net rent and the taxable sale gain, using the same CGT discount eligibility. For a single sale with no other gains or losses, this is effectively 30% of a discount-eligible gain or 45% without the discount.

    The comparison treats rent and the sale separately. It excludes other income, capital losses, market-value adjustments and mixed-phase pension apportionment. Negative entries generate zero tax in the estimate. Ask a registered tax agent to assess the fund’s circumstances before relying on the result.

    Frequently asked questions

    When does 9.35% apply, and can a fixed loan keep 8.95%?

    The real-property variable safe harbour rate is 9.35% for 2026–27. An eligible fixed loan can keep the benchmark for its starting financial year, such as 8.95% for a 2025–26 start, while its initial fixed period remains active. PCG 2016/5 caps the property fixed period at five years and requires conversion to variable at expiry.

    How do I measure LVR and term if I refinance?

    Use the property’s market value when the loan being assessed was entered into and include all acquisition or refinance loans for the asset in the LVR numerator. The limit is 70%. A refinance has up to 15 years less the durations of earlier loans for the same asset.

    Which arrangements can qualify for the August 2026 transition?

    The business real property restriction generally applies to new LRBAs from 10 August 2026. Earlier LRBAs and qualifying refinances are unaffected. A binding purchase contract exchanged before the cutoff can also be exempt even if settlement or loan entry occurs later.

    Does missing a safe harbour term automatically trigger 45% tax?

    A missed term is a reason to review the loan. A fund outside the guideline can support arm’s-length treatment with evidence of comparable commercial terms and actual conduct. Restrictions on which property can be borrowed against are a separate legal test.

    How does 45% NALI tax apply to rental income and a sale?

    The NALI scenario applies 45% to positive net rental income and to the taxable capital gain when NALI applies. A fund that qualifies for the one-third CGT discount can still reflect it in the net gain calculation. NALI can be taxable at 45% even when the fund is in retirement phase.
