Welcome tax questions

How much is the 2026 welcome tax on a $700,000 Montréal property?

A $700,000 taxable base produces $9,349.00 in Montréal transfer duty. The Québec statutory baseline is $8,610.50 on the same base, a difference of $738.50. A local by-law or a different comparative factor can change the comparison.

Which property value is used for Québec welcome tax?

Use the highest of the purchase price before sales taxes, the consideration stated in the deed, and the assessment roll value multiplied by the municipality’s comparison factor in force at transfer. City of Montréal uses 1.00 in 2026.

When is Montréal welcome tax due?

Montréal requires one payment within 30 days of the bill being sent. Reserve the full amount before closing and use the deadline on your municipal bill. Other municipalities may offer different payment arrangements.

Can a parent, child or spouse transfer a property without welcome tax?

Qualifying transfers in the direct line, such as parent to child, or between qualifying spouses may be exempt. Ask your notary to confirm and document the exemption in the deed. A municipality may charge a supplementary duty of up to $200, and registration and notary fees are separate.

Can a first-time buyer get help with the welcome tax?

Montréal stopped taking new applications for its former home purchase assistance program on July 7, 2026. Eligible buyers can check Québec’s refundable tax credit for access to homeownership, which can reimburse up to $5,875 in transfer duty already paid. The credit phases out above a $750,000 tax base and reaches zero by $1 million.
