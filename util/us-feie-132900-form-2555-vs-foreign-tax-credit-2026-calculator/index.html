2026 US Expat Tax Calculator

Compare the $132,900 Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (Form 2555) with the Foreign Tax Credit (Form 1116).

Key takeaways

Form 2555 can lower your U.S. tax when foreign income tax is low. Form 1116 can be more valuable when foreign taxes are high or you qualify for a refundable child credit.

Compare the two with your own wages and foreign tax. The estimate applies the stacking rule and shows remaining Roth IRA contribution room for each option.

What you could lose by switching

Revoking a Form 2555 election can lock you out for five tax years without IRS consent. Filing Form 2555 also gives up the refundable Additional Child Tax Credit and can reduce Roth IRA compensation to zero.

FEIE does not eliminate U.S. self-employment tax. Its usual combined rate is 15.3% subject to Social Security and Medicare rules, and a totalization agreement may exempt covered earnings. Self-employed filers generally use a foreign housing deduction under separate rules.

Frequently asked questions

How do the 330 days and the 2026 FEIE limit work?

The physical presence test requires 330 full days in foreign countries in a consecutive 12-month window. Separately, multiply $132,900 by the qualifying-period days that fall in 2026 divided by 365. A foreign tax home is also required. Married joint filers can reach $265,800 only if both spouses qualify independently and have enough eligible earnings.

How does the housing exclusion change the tax calculation?

Eligible employee housing costs are capped by location and qualifying days. The amount above the base reduces the qualifying wages available for the FEIE. The stacking worksheet then adds the combined exclusion to remaining taxable income, calculates tax, and subtracts the tax on the exclusion alone.

Can I use Form 1116 with Form 2555?

Yes. Foreign income tax on excluded wages is disallowed, but qualifying tax on remaining foreign income can offset U.S. tax up to its Form 1116 limit. Excess creditable tax in this category generally carries back one year and then forward up to ten years, subject to available limits.

Does Form 2555 affect child credits and Roth IRA contributions?

Filing Form 2555 bars the refundable Additional Child Tax Credit. The nonrefundable Child Tax Credit can still offset remaining tax if you qualify. Excluded wages do not count as IRA compensation, while exclusions are added back to Roth modified AGI. Other eligible compensation, including eligible spousal compensation on a joint return, may still support a contribution.

Can I switch tax strategies freely or use FEIE to avoid self-employment tax?

After revoking an exclusion, you generally need IRS consent to claim it again before the sixth tax year after the revocation year. FEIE does not reduce U.S. self-employment tax. The usual combined rate is 15.3% subject to Social Security and Medicare rules, and a totalization agreement may assign coverage to another country.
