Key takeaways

The strategy with more after-tax sale proceeds and side investments builds more wealth on the same cash budget. A fully exempt main residence has no CGT. Rentvesting can generate tax savings when the rental makes a tax loss, while sale costs and CGT reduce what you keep.

2026–27 tax baseline. The rental must qualify as an eligible new build for ongoing negative gearing and the election to retain the 50% CGT discount after 1 July 2027. Confirm eligibility under the ATO’s CGT reform rules before using these results for a property.

Rentvesting and buying FAQs

How are the starting cash and annual budgets matched?

Both strategies start with the higher of the two deposits plus buying costs. The cheaper strategy invests the difference. Each year the home’s mortgage and ownership costs set the starting budget. Rentvesting invests savings or draws on its side portfolio. If that portfolio runs out, both budgets increase by the same amount and the home buyer invests the extra cash.

How does negative gearing work in the rentvesting scenario?

The model subtracts deductible interest, operating expenses and Division 43 capital works from rent received. A tax loss generates an estimated saving at the selected marginal rate when sufficient other taxable income is available. A rental profit produces tax payable. The simulation assumes a qualifying new build for ongoing deductions.

How does Division 43 affect my investment sale?

The annual deduction reduces taxable rental income without using cash. Cumulative deductions reduce the acquisition cost base, which can increase the capital gain. The model subtracts selling costs before applying any eligible discount and taxes the resulting gain at the selected flat rate.

When does the 50% CGT discount apply in the projection?

The model assumes the property qualifies as a new dwelling and the owner elects to retain the 50% discount under the rules starting 1 July 2027. Year 1 is an exact-anniversary sale snapshot and uses the full gain. Later years use the discounted gain. The projection excludes the indexation and minimum-tax calculation for other properties.

How do I enter first-home buyer stamp duty relief?

Enter the combined PPOR stamp duty and buying costs before relief. Turn on the first-home concession and enter the dollar saving from your state or territory assessment. The concession reduces the upfront cash required without changing the mortgage principal.
