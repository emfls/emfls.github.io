Key takeaways

Stay below A$105,048 if single, A$168,076 as a couple, or A$210,096 as a couple separated by illness, respite care or prison. Add A$639.60 per dependent child in your care.

There’s no assets test. The income test adds deemed income from assessable account-based streams to adjusted taxable income. Deeming is 1.75% in the lower tier and 3.75% above it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pass if my income exactly matches the limit?

No. Adjusted taxable income plus deemed income must be strictly below the applicable limit, including the dependent-child allowance. Equality fails the income test.

Is there an assets test for the CSHC?

The CSHC has no assets test. Count taxable bank interest and dividends in taxable income. This calculator applies deeming to assessable account-based pensions and annuities.

When is an older account-based pension grandfathered?

Generally, the stream must have started before 1 January 2015, its owner must have held a CSHC on 31 December 2014, and the owner must have kept the same stream and held the card continuously. Moving to a new product can end that protection.

How does the income test work for couples?

Use both partners’ adjusted taxable incomes and combine the balances of the assessable account-based streams. A partner under 60 has their stream balance excluded. The higher income limit applies to couples separated by illness, respite care or prison.

Does the card guarantee cheaper GP visits?

Your practice decides whether to bulk bill. Count only visits that would become bulk billed because of the card. Use your actual PBS prices where known, since medicine prices may be below the standard co-payment.
