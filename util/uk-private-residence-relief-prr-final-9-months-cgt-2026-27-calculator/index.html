Key takeaways

  • Private Residence Relief (PRR) covers qualifying time as your main home and the final 9 months of ownership. The final period may extend to 36 months if you qualify.
  • Letting Relief is £0 for periods when you rented out the whole property. It may apply if you lived there while letting part of it to a tenant.
  • After reliefs and the £3,000 annual exemption per eligible owner, taxable gains are charged at 18% within the available basic-rate band and 24% above it.

Before you use the estimate

The month-based estimate assumes you moved out before selling and credits up to 9 or 36 additional months from the remaining time away. If you lived in the home or had a qualifying absence during part of the actual final window and had uncovered gaps earlier, calculate the overlap using your dates.

The estimate is for UK residents using the standard Personal Allowance and basic-rate band. It assumes your full CGT exemption is available and excludes other gains, capital losses and adjustments to your income tax band. For unequal ownership shares or different incomes, work out each owner’s gain and available band separately. Non-residents have different reporting duties, including when no tax is due.

Frequently asked questions

Do I get the final 9 months if I moved out before selling?

Yes, if the property was your only or main residence during ownership. The final 9 months can qualify even if the property was empty or let in that period. Count any month that already qualifies only once.

Who qualifies for the extended 36-month final period?

A qualifying disability or long-term care-home stay can extend the final period to 36 months. Conditions apply at disposal, including limits on rights in another home and rules for a spouse or civil partner.

Which absences can count as deemed occupation?

Subject to eligibility and nomination rules, absences can qualify for up to 36 months for any reason, up to 48 months for UK work, or for a period of overseas work without a fixed time limit. You normally need to have lived in the home before and after, unless work prevented your return.

Can I claim Letting Relief if I rented out my entire former home?

Whole-home letting periods qualify for £0 Letting Relief on disposals from 6 April 2020. If you lived in part of your main home while letting another part, the relief is limited to the lowest of £40,000, your Private Residence Relief and the chargeable gain attributable to that letting.

When must I report the sale and pay CGT?

For UK residential property, report and pay any CGT due within 60 days of sale completion using HMRC’s UK property service. Late reporting or payment can lead to interest and penalties. Joint owners report their own shares, and non-residents must generally report even if no tax is due.
