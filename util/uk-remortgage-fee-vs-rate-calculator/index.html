What this estimate includes

  
The calculator assumes fixed rates and equal monthly principal-and-interest repayments. It rounds years to the nearest month, uses a minimum comparison horizon of one month, and stops at mortgage payoff. It excludes early repayment charges, legal fees, and other remortgaging costs.

FAQ

  

    Should you add a UK mortgage product fee to the loan or pay it upfront?
    
Adding the fee increases the balance you pay interest on. Paying upfront leaves the starting loan balance unchanged. Both options include the entire fee in net borrowing cost.

  

  

    What if the product fee takes longer than the fixed deal period to recover?
    
The break-even month is the first month when the higher-fee deal's cumulative interest savings cover its extra fee. If that month falls beyond the comparison horizon, the result shows whether the fee could still be recovered by payoff at the entered rates.

  

  

    How is net borrowing cost calculated?
    
It is the product fee plus interest paid through the comparison month. That equals repayments made plus any upfront fee and the remaining balance, minus your original outstanding mortgage balance.
