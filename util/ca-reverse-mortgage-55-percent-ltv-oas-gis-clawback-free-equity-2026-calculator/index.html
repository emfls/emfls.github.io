Estimate borrowing up to 55% of home value, compare the same cash from an RRSP or RRIF, and see how much home equity could remain.

Key takeaways

  • Canadian homeowners aged 55 or older may qualify. Your youngest borrower’s age, property and location affect the estimate. Existing mortgage payoff and closing costs reduce the cash you receive.
  • A reverse mortgage advance adds $0 to net income (line 23600), with $0 in additional income tax, OAS recovery tax and GIS loss from the proceeds themselves.
  • Extra RRSP or RRIF withdrawals are generally taxable. For 2026 income, OAS recovery starts above $95,323 at 15% of the excess, capped at the OAS received. A withdrawal can also reduce any GIS you receive.

How the borrowing estimate works

The model interpolates between 15% at age 55, 25% at 62, 33% at 68, 42% at 74 and 55% at 80 and older. It deducts 3 percentage points for condos and 2 for regional cities or 7 for rural areas. These adjustments illustrate how a lender could vary an offer. This model assumes a Canadian primary residence. Lenders may impose minimum home values and initial advances, and some properties or locations may be ineligible.

The opening balance includes the cash draw, financed closing costs and any secured debt paid off. Each year’s loan balance is the opening balance multiplied by (1 + nominal rate ÷ 2) raised to twice the number of years. Home value grows once per year at the entered appreciation rate. Equity before selling costs is home value less the balance, floored at $0 under the modeled guarantee.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on a Canadian reverse mortgage at age 55?

The illustrative curve starts at 15% of appraised home value at age 55 for a detached home in a major metro and reaches 55% at age 80. All borrowers must meet the lender’s age requirement. Property type, location and lender underwriting can change an offer. Mortgage payoff and closing costs reduce usable cash.

Does a reverse mortgage reduce OAS or GIS in 2026?

Loan proceeds themselves are tax-free and add $0 to the income used to test OAS or GIS. Other income, including taxable earnings from investing the proceeds, can affect benefits.

How much must I withdraw from an RRSP or RRIF to match the loan cash?

The gross withdrawal must cover the cash you want plus estimated income tax and benefit losses. OAS recovery for 2026 income is 15% of income above $95,323 up to the pension received. The calculator compares incremental recovery and estimates GIS reduction up to the amount you receive.

Why can my equity fall even if my home appreciates?

The full opening balance accrues interest. In this model the nominal annual interest rate is divided into two compounding periods per year. When interest adds more dollars to the balance than appreciation adds to the home’s value, your equity falls.

What happens if my reverse mortgage balance exceeds my home value?

With a qualifying no-negative-equity guarantee, the lender can cover the gap at repayment if you meet the contract terms, including maintenance, property taxes and insurance. Equity can still reach zero, and selling costs can affect the amount you keep.
