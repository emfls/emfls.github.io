Frequently asked questions

Which accounts count for Form 8606?

Combine your own traditional, traditional SEP and traditional SIMPLE IRAs. Add the line 6 amount, other included distributions on line 7 and net conversions on line 8. A spouse calculates their own basis and balances separately.

Can a reverse rollover make the conversion fully nontaxable?

It can if an employer 401(k) accepts the eligible pretax funds, the rollover completes by year-end, and the remaining Form 8606 denominator is fully covered by basis. Outstanding rollovers, taxable amounts remaining, or earlier distributions can leave part of the conversion taxable.

What are the 2026 IRA limits and direct Roth phase-outs?

The combined traditional and Roth IRA contribution cap is $7,500 per person or $8,600 at age 50 or older, subject to compensation and other contribution rules. The direct Roth MAGI phase-out is $153,000–$168,000 for single filers and $242,000–$252,000 for joint filers.
