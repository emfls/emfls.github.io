Australia GST Margin Scheme Calculator 2026–27

Compare sale GST, 7% purchaser withholding and the BAS impact for a residential development.

Key takeaways

  • Eligible margin-scheme sales pay 1/11 of the positive margin. Standard GST is 1/11 of the full GST-inclusive sale price.
  • For sales subject to GST at settlement, purchasers generally withhold 7% under the margin scheme or 1/11 under standard GST.
  • You need a qualifying acquisition and a written agreement with each purchaser by settlement. Eligible construction GST credits can reduce project GST under either method.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate GST under the margin scheme?

Divide the positive difference between the GST-inclusive sale consideration and the eligible acquisition basis by 11. Allocate the original land basis on a fair and reasonable basis across subdivided lots. This estimate assumes equal allocations and sale prices across all lots sold.

What happens if the purchaser withholds more or less than the GST due?

For eligible sales subject to withholding, the purchaser generally remits 7% under the margin scheme or 1/11 under standard GST. The ATO credits the remitted amount against the supplier’s GST liability when it processes the relevant BAS. The difference can contribute to a refund or top-up.

Can the developer still claim construction GST credits?

Yes. Fully creditable, GST-inclusive development invoices can yield credits equal to 1/11 of their value. GST-free costs, wages and other non-creditable amounts are excluded. A purchaser cannot claim a GST credit on property bought under the margin scheme.

What makes a property sale eligible for the margin scheme?

For the taxable sales modelled here, confirm the acquisition history and obtain a written agreement with each purchaser by settlement. An acquisition taxed in full without the scheme generally blocks eligibility. A GST-free going concern can also be ineligible depending on the previous owner’s history.

When can a valuation replace the original land cost?

Use an approved valuation only where Division 75 permits it. For qualifying land held before 1 July 2000, that may be the required valuation date. Other statutory dates or bases can apply, so confirm the correct basis before entering a valuation.
