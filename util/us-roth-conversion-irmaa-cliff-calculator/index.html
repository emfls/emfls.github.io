How the estimate works

Enter a fully taxable conversion and compare the same household with and without it. The calculator adds the increase in 2026 federal income tax to the increase in 12 months of Medicare surcharges, then divides the sum by the entered conversion amount. Conversion room is measured from your income before the conversion.

The estimate applies ordinary income rates and holds deductions fixed. It excludes state tax, credits, preferential capital gains rates, AMT, net investment income tax and changes to deduction phaseouts or taxable Social Security. Income is compared at cent precision. Your filed return and the applicable Medicare schedule determine your actual surcharge. If you have after-tax IRA basis, determine your taxable conversion amount with IRS Form 8606.

After a qualifying life-changing event that reduces your income, you can ask Social Security to use more recent information. A Roth conversion alone does not qualify. Download Form SSA-44 · SSA qualifying-event rules · SSA two-year look-back policy

Frequently asked questions

Which Medicare premium year can a 2026 Roth conversion affect?

Social Security generally uses tax information from two years earlier, so a 2026 conversion can affect 2028 Medicare premiums. This calculator uses the published 2026 CMS schedule to illustrate the possible cost. The 2028 thresholds and premiums may differ.

How much can the first $1 IRMAA cliff cost?

On the 2026 schedule, going from $109,000 to $109,001 as a single filer, or from $218,000 to $218,001 on a joint return, adds $95.70 a month for each person enrolled in both Part B and Part D. That is $1,148.40 over 12 months for one person or $2,296.80 for two.

What income counts toward Medicare IRMAA?

Medicare IRMAA uses adjusted gross income plus tax-exempt interest. The taxable portion of a traditional IRA conversion generally increases adjusted gross income in the conversion year. If you have after-tax IRA basis, IRS Form 8606 helps determine the taxable portion.

What happens if a Roth conversion reaches the next federal tax bracket?

The higher federal rate applies only to taxable dollars above the bracket cutoff. The conversion tax estimate is the difference between regular federal income tax with and without the conversion. Medicare uses a separate tier schedule and can apply a higher surcharge to each covered month.

Can I use SSA-44 to reduce IRMAA after a Roth conversion?

A Roth conversion alone does not qualify as a life-changing event. If a qualifying event such as retirement that reduces your work and income applies, use Form SSA-44 to request that Social Security review your IRMAA using more recent income information.
