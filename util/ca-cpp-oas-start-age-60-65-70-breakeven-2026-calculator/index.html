Key takeaways

  • CPP: The 2026 age-65 maximum is $1,507.65 per month. At that base, starting at 60 pays $964.90 and starting at 70 pays $2,140.86, about 2.22 times the age-60 payment.
  • OAS: The October–December 2026 full rate is $762.50 per month at ages 65–74 and $838.75 after the age-75 increase. Your residence history may reduce the amount.
  • Break-even: With no inflation, discounting or recovery tax, CPP at 65 catches CPP at 60 near age 73.9. OAS at 70 catches OAS at 65 near age 83.1 with the age-75 increase.

How the estimate works

Watch for loss aversion

It can feel risky to give up five years of payments. Compare how you would cover expenses while waiting with how much guaranteed monthly income you would have if you live into your 80s or 90s. If you may qualify for GIS, delaying OAS also delays GIS.

The model takes the 2026 rates as its age-60 baseline and applies your CPI assumption monthly to approximate CPP annual and OAS quarterly indexation. Payments accrue to the selected target age. The model interpolates a crossover within the month where one cumulative total catches the other.

At zero CPI and zero discount, the OAS crossover is about age 83.9 if the age-75 increase is omitted. The calculator includes the increase from the month after age 75, bringing the crossover to about 83.1.

For recovery tax, each 12-month age interval stands in for a tax year. The 2026 threshold and other income stay constant in 2026 dollars. Estimated recovery for the full modeled year is spread over that year's OAS payments. A calendar-year return and actual withholding can differ. Monthly amounts in the cards are gross in 2026 dollars. Lifetime figures exclude regular income tax, GIS, the Allowance and future CPP contribution changes.

Check your own CPP amount in My Service Canada Account. Confirm OAS eligibility with Service Canada, especially if you have fewer than 10 years of residence in Canada, live abroad or may qualify under an agreement.

Frequently asked questions

When does delaying CPP break even?

With flat payments and no discounting, CPP at 65 catches CPP at 60 around age 73.9. CPP at 70 catches CPP at 65 around 81.9 and CPP at 60 around 78.2. Inflation and discounting change those crossover ages.

Can I start OAS at age 60?

OAS starts at 65 at the earliest. CPP can start at 60 if you qualify. You can start the two pensions at different ages.

Does the age-75 OAS increase stack with the deferral bonus?

Yes. The 10% increase applies from the month after you turn 75. At October–December 2026 rates, full OAS deferred to age 70 is $1,037.00 per month before the age increase and $1,140.70 after it.

How does Canadian residence affect OAS?

If you qualify for a partial pension, each completed year of residence in Canada after age 18 generally earns one fortieth of full OAS, up to 40 years. The usual minimum is 10 years when living in Canada and 20 when living abroad. International agreements can affect eligibility.

How is the 2026 OAS clawback estimated?

The recovery tax is generally 15% of net world income above $95,323 for the 2026 income year, capped at the OAS received. The estimate adds CPP and gross OAS to the other income you enter. The 2026 income year informs withholding from July 2027 through June 2028.
