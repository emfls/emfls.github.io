Key takeaways

  • For most 2026 arrivals, file the one-time Article XVIII(7) election by April 30, 2027. File for each Roth IRA and keep a copy.
  • A timely election can defer Canadian tax on eligible earnings. Eligible distributions that would be tax-free in the US can also be exempt in Canada. A Canadian Contribution can expose future earnings to annual Canadian tax.
  • Keeping a traditional 401(k) or IRA invested generally triggers no immediate distribution tax. Cashing out can create US withholding, an early distribution tax and a smaller Canadian credit than you expect.

CRA Roth IRA election letter checklist

Prepare a signed Article XVIII(7) election letter. Include the information below for each Roth IRA. Keep personal identifiers in your offline letter.

  1. Include your legal name, mailing address, Canadian SIN and US Social Security number.
  2. List the Roth IRA trustee or administrator’s name and mailing address.
  3. Record the Roth IRA account number and the date the account was opened.
  4. State the date you became a Canadian tax resident.
  5. Report the balance on the later of December 31, 2008 and the date you became a Canadian resident.
  6. Record the amount and date of the first Canadian Contribution, if any.
  7. Sign a statement electing under Article XVIII(7) to defer Canadian tax on income accrued in the Roth IRA for all tax years.

Frequently asked questions

When is my Roth IRA treaty election due?

File the one-time election for each Roth IRA by the Canadian filing deadline for the year you became a resident. For a 2026 move, that is normally April 30, 2027, or June 15, 2027 if you qualify for the self-employed filing deadline. If late, contact CRA Competent Authority Services.

What happens if I contribute $1 after moving?

A Canadian Contribution can expose all earnings that accrue after the first contribution, even on the previously invested balance. An existing election can still protect the balance immediately before that contribution. CRA excludes certain Roth-to-Roth rollovers, but a traditional-to-Roth conversion can count as a Canadian Contribution.

Does filing make every Roth withdrawal tax-free?

The election addresses Canadian tax on accruing income. Distribution relief under Article XVIII(1) applies to the extent the payment is protected by the treaty and would be excluded from US taxable income for a US resident. For qualified US Roth IRA distributions, a five-tax-year rule and a qualifying condition generally apply.

Can I transfer a US retirement distribution without using regular RRSP room?

An eligible lump sum may qualify for a special deduction under paragraph 60(j). Confirm the eligible amount, Canadian income inclusion and transfer timing. Ordinary RRSP contributions use your available room. US withholding can leave you short of cash to fund the gross transfer.

Will a Canadian foreign tax credit recover all my US tax?

Credit depends on eligible final US tax and Canadian tax attributable to the same income. The 10% US additional tax can qualify in some cases. A Canadian RRSP deduction can reduce the Canadian tax available to offset, even to zero.
