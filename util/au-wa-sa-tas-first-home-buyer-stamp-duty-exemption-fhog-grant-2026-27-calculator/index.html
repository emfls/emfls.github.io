WA, SA & TAS First Home Buyer Stamp Duty & FHOG Calculator

Compare transfer duty and First Home Owner Grants for 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027.

Key takeaways

  • Western Australia: Eligible homes incur $0 transfer duty up to $600,000. A concession applies up to $800,000. Eligible new homes can also receive a $10,000 grant under a separate regional cap.
  • South Australia: Eligible new homes can receive full stamp duty relief and a $15,000 grant at any value. Eligible land to build on can get full duty relief, with the grant tied to the qualifying build.
  • Tasmania: Eligible new homes can receive a $20,000 grant with no price cap for transactions commencing in 2026–27. The $750,000 established-home duty exemption ended on 30 June 2026.

Compare your purchase across three states

Use the dutiable value, generally the higher of contract price and unencumbered market value. For a separate build, enter the land value for duty and the combined land and build value for the WA grant cap. WA land relief is conditional on meeting the building requirements.

Budget for losing a grant or duty exemption

For an otherwise eligible WA home, moving from $800,000 to $800,001 raises duty from $32,300.00 to $32,320.65. South of the 26th parallel, an eligible new home also loses the $10,000 grant when its total value exceeds $800,000.

In Tasmania, an established home at $750,000 settling after 30 June 2026 has $28,935.00 in standard duty. Confirm eligibility and payment timing with the relevant revenue office before relying on the savings at settlement.

Frequently asked questions

How does WA calculate duty just above $600,000?

On an eligible home above $600,000 and up to $800,000, WA charges $16.15 for every $100 or part above $600,000. A value of $600,001 produces $16.15 in duty.

Can a WA home qualify for the grant above the duty concession cap?

Yes. An eligible new home north of the 26th parallel can receive $10,000 with a total value up to $1 million. The home duty concession still ends at $800,000 statewide.

Does South Australia pay the $15,000 grant on vacant land?

Buying eligible vacant land to build your first home can qualify for uncapped duty relief. The $15,000 grant requires a separate eligible new-home or construction transaction and application.

What happened to Tasmania’s established-home exemption?

The exemption for qualifying established homes valued up to $750,000 ended for settlements after 30 June 2026. Standard duty applies under this comparison. The $20,000 grant in 2026–27 is for eligible new-home transactions.

Can I count my grant as cash at settlement?

Grant timing depends on state rules, your transaction and who processes the application. For a separate build, payment can come after a construction milestone. Confirm the payment date with your lender or the revenue office.
