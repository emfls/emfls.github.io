Key takeaways

  • Massachusetts’ $99,600 credit offsets the full tax at a $2 million taxable base. The estimate climbs gradually above that point.
  • In Maryland, a niece or other nonexempt heir generally owes 10%. Inheritance tax paid reduces any Maryland estate tax due, dollar for dollar up to that bill.
  • Pennsylvania’s rates on taxable transfers start at the first dollar. A surviving spouse generally pays 0%, most lineal heirs 4.5%, siblings 12% and other nonexempt heirs 15%. Eligible early payment can save 5% of the tax.

What the estimate assumes

Each state is a separate resident-decedent scenario. The calculator treats your input as both the net taxable estate and the clear value passing to one heir before the modeled taxes. The out-of-state percentage is a proxy for the applicable share of qualifying property and related deductions. For Maryland it also approximates the gross-estate situs ratio on Schedule A. Effective rates use the full entered estate.

The estimate assumes no adjusted taxable gifts, portability, special property exemptions, trust elections, credits for death tax paid to other states, or federal estate tax. A filing obligation can depend on gross estate, gifts, and elections even when this estimate is zero.

Frequently asked questions

Does Massachusetts still have a $2 million estate tax cliff?

For this 2026 resident estimate, the $99,600 credit offsets the entire Table B amount at a $2 million taxable base. Just above that base, the estimate rises gradually. Filing is determined separately using gross estate and adjusted taxable gifts.

Does Maryland add the estate tax and inheritance tax in full?

Maryland inheritance tax paid reduces gross Maryland estate tax up to the estate tax due. A taxable $1 million bequest to a niece can still owe $100,000 of inheritance tax even when Maryland estate tax is zero.

Which Pennsylvania beneficiaries pay inheritance tax?

The general rates are 0% for a surviving spouse, 4.5% for lineal heirs, 12% for siblings and 15% for other nonexempt heirs. Qualifying transfers between a parent and a child age 21 or younger are taxed at 0%.

How much can early payment save in Pennsylvania?

Paying the full eligible tax within three months of death can save 5% of the tax due. On a $150,000 bill the estimated savings are $7,500, leaving $142,500 to pay.

How does out-of-state property affect this estimate?

The calculator treats the percentage as qualifying real or tangible property with situs outside the resident state. It reduces the modeled Massachusetts and inheritance tax bases and prorates Maryland’s Table B credit before the cap. Actual situs and deductions depend on the assets and the return.
