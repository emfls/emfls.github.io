Australia Super Guarantee Charge Calculator 2026–27

Compare Payday Super’s seven-business-day rule with the legacy quarterly charge and see how much a missed payment adds after company tax.

Key takeaways

  • Payday Super: From 1 July 2026, the fund generally has seven business days after payday to receive 12% of qualifying earnings. A missed contribution can attract daily compounding and up to a 60% administrative uplift. Paid core SGC is deductible.
  • Legacy quarterly super: SGC uses salary and wages including overtime, adds 10% simple annual interest and a $20 fee per affected employee per quarter, and provides no tax deduction.

The lost deduction can magnify the cost

At a 25% company tax rate, a $100 deductible contribution costs $75 after tax. A $100 non-deductible legacy charge costs $100, or 33.3% more. At 30%, the same gap is 42.9%. Both comparisons isolate the deduction before overtime, interest and the quarterly fee.

Frequently asked questions

When must a super fund receive a Payday Super payment?

For paydays from 1 July 2026, the fund generally needs to receive the contribution within seven business days after payday. Some cases, including some new employees, have longer deadlines. A clearing-house submission alone does not count as receipt.

How do notional earnings and the administrative uplift work?

Notional earnings compound daily at the GIC rate from the day after the contribution deadline. For a fully missed contribution they accrue through the day before ATO assessment. The uplift starts at 60% of the final shortfall plus notional earnings. Compliance history and voluntary disclosure can reduce the uplift, potentially to 0%.

Why does overtime affect legacy super guarantee charge?

On-time quarterly super generally uses ordinary time earnings. The legacy shortfall uses salary and wages, which include overtime, up to the quarterly maximum contribution base. It also adds 10% simple interest from the start of the quarter to the later of the statement due date and actual lodgment, excluding that end date, plus $20 per affected employee per quarter.

Can a company deduct the super guarantee charge?

The core Payday Super charge is deductible when paid. Separate GIC and penalties on an unpaid assessment are non-deductible. The legacy quarterly charge remains non-deductible, including the June 2026 quarter.

Can a director be personally liable for unpaid SGC?

Yes. The director penalty regime can make directors personally liable for unpaid company SGC. The ATO can issue a Director Penalty Notice, and timing affects the options available to resolve it.
