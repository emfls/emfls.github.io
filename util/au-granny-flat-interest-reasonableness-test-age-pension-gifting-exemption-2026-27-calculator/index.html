Check the Centrelink reasonableness test and see how a lifetime right to reside could affect your Age Pension compared with an outright gift.

Key takeaways

Paying for a recognised lifetime right to live in someone else’s home can keep part or all of the transfer outside the gifting rules. If the reasonableness test applies, the value of the right depends on the arrangement and the published factor for age at next birthday.

The September 2026 Extra Allowable Amount is A$267,000. An assessed contribution above this amount generally means homeowner treatment. At or below it, the contribution counts under the higher non-homeowner assets threshold.

Leaving within five years can change your assessment

Moving out within five years of creating the right can trigger a Services Australia review. A foreseeable move may cause gifting rules to apply. An unexpected event such as sudden illness or a family breakdown may be treated differently.

Before transferring money or title, discuss the arrangement with Services Australia and get independent legal advice about written lifetime occupancy and what happens if you need to leave or the property is sold. Services Australia assessment guide

Frequently asked questions

When does Centrelink apply the reasonableness test?

A lump sum for existing accommodation or an extra transfer on top of a documented property or building cost can trigger the test. A qualifying transfer limited to the home title, purchase price, construction or conversion cost generally uses the actual amount. Services Australia assesses the arrangement.

Which age and pension rate are used?

Use the age at the next birthday when the right is established, taking the younger partner’s age for couples. Multiply the published factor by the combined annual partnered maximum rate even for a single applicant.

What if the entry contribution equals the Extra Allowable Amount?

An assessed entry contribution equal to or below the EAA is included in the assets test under the non-homeowner threshold. Above the EAA, the interest is exempt and the homeowner threshold applies.

How long does an excess gift affect Age Pension?

Any portion above the remaining annual and rolling gifting free areas is generally an assessable asset for five years from the gift date. It is also deemed for the income test. The lower of the two means-tested pension rates applies.

What if I move out within five years?

Services Australia reviews the arrangement. A departure that was reasonably foreseeable when the right was created can trigger gifting rules. Unexpected events such as sudden illness may be treated differently.
