Frequently asked questions

How much do I take home on £12.71 an hour?

At 37.5 hours a week in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, £12.71 an hour gives £24,784.50 gross a year and about £20,622.66 take-home with the pension option on and zero overtime.

How much more will I take home at £13.21 an hour?

At 37.5 hours a week, the adult rate rises by 50p an hour, or 3.93%. This adds £975 gross and an estimated £663 net a year in England, Wales or Northern Ireland with the pension option on and 2026/27 deduction rules held constant.

Who can receive the apprentice minimum wage?

The apprentice rate applies if you are under 19, or if you are 19 or over and in the first year of your apprenticeship. After the first year, apprentices aged 19 or over get the minimum wage for their age band.

How does overtime affect take-home pay?

The calculator adds overtime hours at the selected multiple of the base rate. The extra earnings can increase Income Tax, employee National Insurance and qualifying pension contributions. Base your multiplier on your contract.

How is the total employer cost calculated?

The estimate adds gross pay, employer National Insurance and any employer pension contribution. Standard employer NI is 15% above £5,000 of annual pay. The estimate uses the £50,270 relief threshold for under-21s and qualifying apprentices under 25.
