Key takeaways

  • A contract is personal services income (PSI) when more than 50% rewards your own skills or effort.
  • Meet all three results-test conditions on at least 75% of PSI and you can self-assess as a personal services business (PSB), even with a single client.
  • If the results test fails and 80% or more comes from one client group, the PSI rules apply unless you have a covering ATO determination. Below 80%, passing any one of three other tests is enough.

How the tax comparison works

Each person’s tax is the additional tax after adding their share of this scenario to their existing taxable income. The company scenario adds tax on profit kept after the modeled expenses and worker salary. For the caught scenario, the worker is taxed on net PSI after allowable expenses. Prompt wages and the remainder attributed by the company together make up that taxable amount.

The model covers Australian residents, the resident income tax schedule, the low income tax offset and the selected Medicare levy. It excludes loss offsets or carry-forwards, future dividends and franking, trust distributions, HELP repayments, Medicare levy surcharge and other offsets. Frequently asked questions

How do I know whether a contract produces PSI?

Check each contract separately. When more than half of its payment rewards your own skill or effort, the full contract amount is PSI. At exactly 50%, it is outside PSI. Income primarily from goods, substantial income-producing assets or a business structure generally falls outside PSI.

Can I pass the results test with only one client?

Yes. At least 75% of your annual PSI must meet all three conditions together: payment for a specified result, providing any tools or equipment required, and liability for fixing defects at your own cost. This test can qualify you as a PSB even when one source provides all your PSI.

What happens at exactly 80% from one client and its associates?

If you fail the results test, you cannot use the other three tests to self-assess at exactly 80% or above. The PSI rules apply unless an ATO personal services business determination covers the individual and income year.

What tax costs can the PSI rules add?

Residential occupancy costs and remuneration to an associate for admin or bookkeeping are disallowed against PSI. The associate generally excludes the disallowed payment from assessable income. A company attributes positive net PSI to the worker after deducting eligible expenses and promptly paid worker wages. A sole trader reports PSI directly.

Does passing a PSB test let my company retain profits or split income freely?

Ordinary deductibility and the applicable company tax rate still need to be established. Part IVA can apply to income diversion or company profit retention despite a PSB pass. The company comparison is a current-year illustration and excludes tax on later distributions.
