Official scheme rules and price caps for 2026

The first home buyer stream requires at least a 5% deposit and offers a guarantee of up to 15% of the lender-assessed property value. Eligible, approved scheme loans have no LMI. There is no income cap or annual place limit.

How the estimates work

All three scenarios use the entered interest rate and term with monthly principal-and-interest payments. The buy-now options use the same deposit, and the wait option models a loan for 80% of the projected price. The model uses the purchase price as a proxy for the lender’s valuation.

The LMI estimate applies illustrative GST-inclusive rates in bands above 80% loan-to-value ratio (LVR), up to 4.15% of the base loan at 95% LVR before insurance duty. In jurisdictions modelled with duty, the top effective rate can reach about 4.6%. External territory duty requires confirmation. Premiums, duty treatment and how much LMI you can finance vary by lender and insurer. Helia’s lender mortgage insurance estimator provides an independent estimate.

The wait estimate checks each month for up to 30 years. Prices change at the monthly equivalent of your annual growth assumption. It holds rent and monthly savings flat, counts rent at 52 weeks per year and assumes no interest on savings. Buying costs such as transfer duty and conveyancing, ongoing ownership costs, grants and future rate changes are excluded. Keep a separate buffer for these costs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy with a 5% deposit and pay no lenders mortgage insurance?

If you qualify and a participating lender approves your loan, you can buy with a deposit as low as 5% and pay no LMI. Housing Australia can guarantee up to 15% of the lender-assessed property value. You still repay the full loan and cover upfront buying costs.

Are there income caps or annual place limits in 2026?

There is no scheme income cap or annual place limit. Lenders still assess whether you can afford the loan. The first home buyer stream generally requires applicants to be at least 18, be citizens or permanent residents, meet the 10-year Australian property ownership test and live in the home.

What happens if my price is exactly at the cap?

The cap is inclusive. Both your purchase price and the value assessed by your participating lender must be at or below the cap for the property’s location. Use the official postcode lookup and confirm the result with your lender.

What does it cost to add LMI to a mortgage?

Capitalising LMI adds the premium to your loan balance. You repay the premium and pay interest on it while it is outstanding. The calculator shows the estimated premium and the extra interest separately. Your lender may limit how much you can borrow including LMI.

Does paying rent slow down the 20% deposit projection?

Enter what you can actually save each month after rent and other expenses. The calculator counts your rent separately and updates the 20% target as the assumed property price changes. If your savings miss the target for 30 years, it reports that the target was not reached within the projection.
