Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a lease money factor to an APR equivalent?

Multiply the money factor by 2,400 to get a comparison percentage. A money factor of 0.0025 is approximately equivalent to 6.0%. This convention is separate from the legally disclosed loan APR.

How is the lease payment calculated?

Subtract cash down, trade equity and rebates from negotiated price plus acquisition fee. Spread the difference between that net cap cost and the residual across the term. Add rent, calculated as the sum of net cap cost and residual times the money factor, and any monthly tax.

What happens if the loan is still outstanding at sale?

Sale equity equals expected resale value minus the remaining balance. The calculator subtracts that equity from purchase spending, including when equity is negative. At 0% APR the loan charges no interest. Cent rounding can change individual instalments.
