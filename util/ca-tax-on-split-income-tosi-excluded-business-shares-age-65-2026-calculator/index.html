    Frequently asked questions

    How does the 20-hour excluded business rule work?

    A recipient who turns 18 by year-end meets the current-year bright-line test by averaging at least 20 hours per week while the business operates. Regular, continuous and substantial activity in any five prior tax years can also qualify, and those years can be nonconsecutive.

    When are family-owned CCPC shares excluded?

    The recipient must turn 25 by year-end and directly hold at least 10% of votes and share fair market value. The corporation must be non-professional, have less than 90% gross business income from services and satisfy the separate related-business income test.

    Does a business owner turning 65 exempt a spouse’s dividend?

    The recipient’s spouse or common-law partner must turn 65 by year-end and the same amount must qualify as excluded if received by that owner. Age alone is insufficient.

    How is the TOSI estimate different from regular dividend tax?

    The TOSI scenario applies the top combined dividend rate to the cash amount without the basic personal amount. The regular scenario uses progressive brackets and the applicable non-refundable credits, then subtracts the tax on other income alone.

    Can the business pay a family member a reasonable salary instead?

    Genuine salary for work performed is employment income outside TOSI. Section 67 limits the corporation’s deduction to reasonable compensation. Keep evidence of duties and pay, and account for applicable withholding and payroll contributions.
