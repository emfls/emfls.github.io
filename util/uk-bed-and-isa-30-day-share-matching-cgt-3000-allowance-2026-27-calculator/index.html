Key takeaways

A Bed & ISA sells shares in your general investment account (GIA), subscribes cash and repurchases eligible shares inside an ISA. In 2026/27 the ISA limit is £20,000 and the CGT annual exemption is £3,000. The ISA purchase is matched separately, while the initial sale may still trigger CGT.

30-day matching and Bed & Spouse

For a UK-resident investor, same-day GIA purchases of the same class match first, then GIA purchases in the 30 days after the sale, then the Section 104 pool. A qualifying purchase in your own ISA is held separately under regulation 34. A genuine purchase in your spouse’s own ISA uses their allowance and beneficial ownership. The seller still accounts for the GIA disposal. HMRC matching rules · ISA regulation 34 · HMRC on spouse ownership

Projection assumptions

The comparison covers the slice sold in each scenario and assumes pooled share matching, equivalent holdings, fractional trade sizing, constant prices at execution apart from the spread, and constant tax settings over the projection. The model charges CGT from proceeds at trade time for comparison. It leaves any residual ISA cash uninvested with no interest.

Each projection year uses twelve monthly periods and renews the entered dividend allowance. All taxable dividends use the selected marginal rate. Reinvested net GIA dividends increase the pooled cost basis. Exit CGT uses the assumed exit exemption and basic-rate band entered above, including when checking immediate break-even. Compounded dividend drag includes the lost growth on tax paid. The model excludes GIA loss relief, platform and exit fees, foreign withholding, currency costs and fund-specific reporting adjustments.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I sell with zero CGT?

The calculator allocates your Section 104 pooled cost to the portion sold and deducts the sale commission. It finds the largest sale to the penny whose unrounded gain fits your remaining annual exemption and whose cash proceeds fit your ISA room. Your broker may need to round down to a whole number of shares.

Does the 30-day rule apply to a Bed & ISA?

A qualifying repurchase in your own ISA is held separately for share matching under regulation 34. A personal taxable purchase of the same class can still match the sale on the same day or in the following 30 days, ahead of the Section 104 pool. The original sale is still a CGT disposal.

Can my spouse or civil partner buy the shares in their ISA?

A genuine purchase by a spouse or civil partner for their own beneficial account is a purchase by a different person. They use their own ISA allowance and own the investment. Your GIA disposal still uses your CGT position. A direct transfer of shares between partners is a separate transaction with separate tax rules.

Which purchases attract 0.5% SDRT?

Use 0.5% for chargeable electronic purchases, such as most existing shares in UK-incorporated companies. Use 0% for qualifying exempt ETFs, eligible fund purchases or overseas shares normally outside SDRT, such as most US shares bought overseas. Listing venue alone does not determine liability. Check the instrument and your broker’s quoted charges.

How do you calculate ten-year savings and break-even?

The model reinvests dividends monthly, subtracts GIA dividend tax as the annual allowance is used and adds reinvested dividends to the GIA cost basis. It compares both positions after a hypothetical GIA sale each month. Break-even is the first month-end when the ISA is worth at least as much, including the option of an immediate comparison.
