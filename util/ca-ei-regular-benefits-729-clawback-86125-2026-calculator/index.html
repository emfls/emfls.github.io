How an RRSP deduction affects repayment

The calculator shows the extra deductible amount needed to bring adjusted income down to $86,125 when repayment applies. A contribution must be deductible and claimed for 2026, and the deduction depends on your available room.

A deduction can lower both current income tax and EI repayment. In the income-limited part of the formula, a $1 deduction can save 30¢ of EI repayment. Because the repayment itself is deductible, taxable income then falls by 70¢. At a 35% combined marginal tax rate, that saves another 24.5¢ of income tax, for 54.5¢ in combined current-year savings in this example. When EI paid caps the repayment, the repayment only starts to fall once excess income drops below that cap. RRSP withdrawals are generally taxable.

Frequently asked questions

How is the $729 weekly EI maximum calculated?

Service Canada averages gross insurable earnings over the required 14 to 22 best weeks and generally pays 55%. For 2026, $68,900 of maximum annual insurable earnings equals $1,325 weekly. Multiplying by 55% gives $728.75, which rounds to $729.

How many weeks of regular EI can I get in 2026?

The standard Schedule I table gives 14 to 45 payable weeks based on qualifying hours and the regional unemployment rate at claim start. The hours threshold ranges from 700 at 6% or less to 420 above 13%. An unpaid waiting week delays payment while leaving the payable-week entitlement intact. Temporary measures can waive that wait or extend some claims.

What happens when I work while receiving regular EI?

EI is reduced by 50 cents per dollar of weekly earnings up to 90% of the weekly insurable earnings used to establish the claim. Above that point, an additional dollar of earnings reduces EI by a dollar, until EI is zero. A full work week pays zero EI and does not use a payable week.

Who must repay regular EI above $86,125 in 2026?

Repayment is 30% of the smaller of regular and fishing EI paid in 2026 or adjusted net income above $86,125. The exemption applies if you received less than one week of regular or fishing EI across the preceding 10 tax years, 2016 through 2025. Special EI benefits are excluded from the benefit side of the formula, but taxable special benefits can still count toward income.

How much RRSP deduction would eliminate my EI repayment?

If repayment applies, the extra deduction needed to eliminate it is the difference between your current adjusted net income and $86,125. A smaller eligible deduction can reduce repayment once excess income is below the regular benefits paid. Check your available RRSP deduction limit and the CRA instructions for line 20800 before claiming a deduction.

Sources and calculation scope

The projection uses a standard new 2026 claim, the regular Schedule I entitlement and consistent weekly earnings. It assumes no income during the waiting week and excludes family supplements, violation penalties, seasonal and long-tenured extensions, and income tax withholding. Confirm your entitlement with Service Canada and use the T4E and CRA worksheet to file.
