UK £1,000 Trading & Property Allowance Calculator

Compare the allowances with actual expenses for your side hustle and rental income.

2026/27 tax year · 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027

Key takeaways

  • Up to £1,000 gross can be fully relieved in each category. Eligible trading and property income have separate allowances. Full relief usually means no tax or Self Assessment for that income alone.
  • Above £1,000 gross, compare expenses with the £1,000 deduction. Higher allowable costs generally favour actual expenses. Choose one method per category.
  • Class 4 NIC starts above £12,570 of qualifying trading profits. Rental profit can raise your Income Tax bill while having no effect on Class 4.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to file if I earn exactly £1,000?

Eligible annual gross receipts of exactly £1,000 qualify for full relief in each category. This income usually creates no filing obligation on its own. You may still need to file for another reason or choose to file to claim trading loss relief.

When do actual expenses beat the £1,000 allowance?

For gross receipts above £1,000, actual expenses produce a lower profit when allowable costs exceed £1,000. If costs equal the allowance, the two methods yield the same profit. Actual expenses can also establish a loss for possible relief.

Does selling on Vinted or eBay count as trading?

Buying or making goods to resell for profit can be trading. Selling your own unwanted clothes or household items usually falls outside trading. Include receipts from taxable business activities before deducting fees or costs.

Can I claim the property allowance on a room in my home?

Qualifying furnished accommodation in your home may be covered by Rent a Room relief of up to £7,500, reduced to £3,750 if you share the income. You cannot combine Rent a Room relief and the property allowance on those receipts.

What changes under the proposed £3,000 trading allowance?

HMRC proposes increasing both the trading allowance and its reporting threshold from 6 April 2027. The proposal covers trading and miscellaneous income. The calculator holds other rules constant to isolate the change in Income Tax and Class 4 NIC for your trading receipts.
