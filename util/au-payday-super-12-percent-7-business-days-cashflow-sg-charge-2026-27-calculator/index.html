How the estimates work

Cash flow. Annual SG is 12% of the qualifying payroll entered. The model divides it into equal pay runs and assumes quarterly payment at the former deadline. Earnings early in a 90-day quarter could stay with the employer for about 118 days. The model uses an average of 73 days under quarterly payments and roughly 10 calendar days under Payday Super. The float reduction is annual SG × 63 ÷ 365. The financing estimate assumes you borrow that average amount at the entered rate throughout the year.

Late charge. The estimate compounds the selected annual GIC rate divided by 365 over the entered overdue days. Uplift starts at 60%, with a 20 percentage point reduction for qualifying history and further reductions of 40, 35, 30 or 15 points for valid disclosures on days 1–30, 31–60, 61–120 or later, respectively. STP reports qualifying earnings and SG liability. The ATO determines the actual assessment. The scenario uses a single rate and excludes late payment credits, choice loading and interest or penalties for overdue assessed SGC. SG regulations, sections 13C–13D · ATO guidance on deductibility

Retirement. Both schedules use the same annual gross SG and a constant effective net investment return. Payday deposits arrive 10 calendar days after each modelled pay date. Quarterly deposits arrive 28 days after each quarter-end. The model applies fractional-year growth from receipt to retirement. It assumes steady earnings, a zero starting balance, and no contribution tax, inflation adjustment or future SG rate or cap changes.

Payday Super questions

When is super due under Payday Super?

For qualifying earnings paid from 1 July 2026, the fund usually needs to receive the contribution and the information needed to allocate it within 7 business days after payday. An eligible first contribution for a new employee or to a new complying fund can have 20 business days.

What earnings attract the 12% super guarantee?

Qualifying earnings generally include ordinary time earnings such as base wages and certain ordinary-hours loadings and bonuses. They also include all commissions and qualifying amounts sacrificed to super. For 2026–27, the maximum SG earnings base is AUD 270,830 per employee.

How can voluntary disclosure reduce the late super charge?

A valid disclosure lodged before the ATO assesses the payday can cut administrative uplift by up to 40 percentage points. A separate 20 percentage point reduction may apply based on assessment and estimate history. Paying the outstanding contribution to the fund before assessment can also reduce the principal included in the charge.

Is the new super guarantee charge tax deductible?

The redesigned charge for paydays from 1 July 2026 is generally deductible, including the administrative uplift within the charge. Separate interest on an overdue assessed charge and penalties for failing to pay that assessed charge are non-deductible.

Does receiving super more often always improve my retirement balance?

With a positive return, earlier contributions have more time to earn investment returns. At a zero return there is no timing gain on equal SG. At a negative return, earlier deposits can lose more. Actual outcomes depend on fund receipt dates, investment performance and taxes.
