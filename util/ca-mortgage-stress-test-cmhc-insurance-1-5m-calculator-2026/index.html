Mortgage stress test FAQ

What rate does the Canadian mortgage stress test use?

For this purchase estimate, use the higher of 5.25% or your contract rate plus 2 percentage points. A 4.50% contract rate is tested at 6.50%. The lender uses the qualifying payment to assess debt service. Your estimated monthly mortgage payment uses the contract rate.

How much do I need to put down at $1.5 million?

At exactly $1.5 million, you need at least $300,000 down and high-ratio insurance is unavailable. Below the cap, the minimum is 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% of any amount above $500,000. For example, the minimum on a $650,000 home is $40,000.

Who can get an insured 30-year mortgage?

An eligible first-time buyer or a buyer of an eligible newly built home can use up to 30 years with CMHC Home Start. The premium increases by 0.20 percentage points, so a 3.10% premium becomes 3.30% at 90% loan-to-value. Other high-ratio buyers are limited to 25 years.

Can I add the provincial tax on the CMHC premium to the mortgage?

Ontario (8%), Quebec (9% for premiums paid in 2026) and Saskatchewan (6%) charge tax on the premium. Budget for it in cash at closing. The calculator finances the premium itself. Manitoba exempts mortgage default insurance from its retail sales tax.

Will paying off other debt make me pass?

It can lower TDS, which counts other monthly debt as well as housing. GDS counts housing costs alone. If GDS exceeds 39%, reducing other debt cannot fix it. Higher gross income or lower housing costs can help. The tool checks both the 39% GDS and 44% TDS limits.
