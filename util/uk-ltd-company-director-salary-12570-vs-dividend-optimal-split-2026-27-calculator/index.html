Could a lower salary cost you a State Pension year?

A £5,000 salary is £1,708 below the £6,708 Lower Earnings Limit for a full-year director. If you rely on this job for your NI record, you could lose a qualifying year unless other earnings or NI credits cover it. Dividends do not build your NI record. Check your National Insurance record.

How the comparison works

The model pays the pension contribution first, then funds salary and employer NI. Corporation Tax applies to the remaining profit. Strategies A and B use available post-tax profit for dividends up to your cash target. Strategy C pays as much salary as the budget and target allow.

Estimates assume a full-year director-shareholder with standard category A NI, no other personal income, and a standalone UK trading company with a 12-month accounting period, no associated companies and no adjustments to augmented profit. Scottish PAYE rates, student loans and personal pension tax charges are outside this model. Dividends require available distributable profits and proper declaration.

Director salary and dividend FAQs

Is a £12,570 salary better than £5,000 plus dividends?

It depends on profit, the planned withdrawal and Employment Allowance. A £12,570 salary costs £1,135.50 in employer NI before any allowance. The salary and net employer NI reduce taxable company profit, so compare take-home pay and profit left in the company together.

How much did dividend tax rise in April 2026?

From 6 April 2026 the basic and higher dividend rates rose by two percentage points to 10.75% and 35.75%. The additional rate remains 39.35%. The £500 dividend allowance is a 0% rate and uses space in your income tax bands.

When does Employment Allowance cover my employer NI?

An eligible employer can offset secondary Class 1 NI by up to £10,500 across payroll. A company with one director cannot claim if that director is its only employee liable for secondary Class 1 NI. Use the amount still available after other payroll liabilities.

Can a £5,000 salary cost me a qualifying State Pension year?

Yes. For a full-year director, £5,000 is below the £6,708 annual Lower Earnings Limit. Dividends do not count towards the NI record. Other qualifying earnings or NI credits may still give you a qualifying year.

How does an employer pension contribution affect this comparison?

An eligible contribution paid to a registered pension scheme can reduce company taxable profit and leaves less cash to withdraw today. The calculator treats your input as fully deductible and excludes any personal annual allowance charge. Employer contributions count towards your annual allowance.
