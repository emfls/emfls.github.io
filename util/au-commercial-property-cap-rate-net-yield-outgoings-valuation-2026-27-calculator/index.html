Australian Commercial Property Underwriting Principles

Commercial real estate in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, and regional Australia is appraised primarily through the Income Capitalisation Method rather than comparable residential sales.

1. Capitalisation Rate (Cap Rate) Dynamics

The Cap Rate represents the unleveraged rate of return expected on an investment property:

  • Formula: $ ext{Property Value} = rac{ ext{Net Operating Income (NOI)}}{ ext{Cap Rate}}$
  • Compression: When cap rates compress (fall from 6.5% to 5.5%), commercial property valuations increase sharply.
  • Decompression: Higher interest rates force cap rates upward, lowering asset valuations unless accompanied by strong rental growth.

2. The Bank 1.35x DSCR Covenant Rule

Unlike residential mortgages governed by APRA serviceability buffers, Australian commercial property loans are assessed on the property's ability to service debt independently:

  • Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR): Calculated as $ rac{ ext{NOI}}{ ext{Annual Debt Service}}$.
  • The 1.35x Floor: Commercial lenders (CBA, NAB, Westpac, ANZ, Macquarie) mandate a minimum 1.30x to 1.35x DSCR. If your projected debt service pushes DSCR below 1.35x, banks will reduce the allowable loan amount (forcing higher cash equity) or require personal cross-collateral guarantees.
