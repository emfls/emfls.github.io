UK State Pension & Voluntary NI Top-Up Payback Calculator

Compare the pension you could get with and without buying missing National Insurance years, using 2026/27 rates.

Key takeaways

  • The full new State Pension is £241.30 a week in 2026/27, or £12,547.60 over 52 weeks. It rises 3.9% to £250.70 a week in 2027/28.
  • A full Class 3 year costs £956.80 at the 2026/27 rate. If it adds 1/35 of the full pension, that is about £358.50 a year before tax. Payback is about 2.67 years before tax or 3.34 years at 20%, measured from when you start drawing your pension.
  • In the 35-year model, going from 9 to 10 years can unlock about £3,585 a year before tax. At £956.80, that is about 0.27 years of pension payments before tax.

Before you pay

Paying for a missing year can leave you out of pocket.

If the additional years still leave you below the normal 10-year minimum, or future NI years already get you to the full rate, the selected top-up can cost money and add £0. Check which specific gaps raise your forecast, whether you can claim free NI credits, and what HMRC will charge before you pay.

Check your State Pension forecast and National Insurance record. For a year with a higher or lower historical rate, use HMRC’s quote for the actual cost.

Payback divides the cost by the annual increase after tax. Lifetime net gain multiplies that annual increase by your years drawing pension and subtracts the cost. ROI divides net gain by the cost. The calculation applies your selected tax rate to the entire increase.

Frequently asked questions

Can paying for one missing NI year unlock my State Pension?

It can if the payment completes your tenth qualifying year and you would otherwise finish with nine. Under the simplified 35-year model, ten years are worth £3,585.03 a year at 2026/27 rates. Future contributions or credits could reach that threshold without a top-up.

How long does a full Class 3 year take to pay back?

At the 2026/27 rate, 52 weeks cost £956.80. If one completed year adds £358.50 a year before tax, payback is about 2.67 years before tax or 3.34 years at 20% tax, starting when you draw your pension.

Can I pay Class 2 instead of Class 3 or fill a partial year?

The standard voluntary Class 2 rate is available only if you qualify, such as certain self-employed people with low profits. A partial gap may only require a few weeks of contributions to complete the year. HMRC can confirm the contribution class, missing weeks, deadline and price for each gap.

Does everyone need exactly 35 years for the full rate?

The 35-year calculation applies to a record with no contributions before 6 April 2016. People with earlier records can have a transitional starting amount, affected by factors such as contracting out or Additional State Pension. Check your official forecast to see what extra years would add.

Does Income Tax affect the payback?

Yes. The State Pension is taxable, and the full 2026/27 new State Pension of £12,547.60 over 52 weeks is £22.40 below the standard £12,570 Personal Allowance. Other taxable income and where you live can affect the tax on the increase. The calculator uses one marginal rate for the full uplift.
