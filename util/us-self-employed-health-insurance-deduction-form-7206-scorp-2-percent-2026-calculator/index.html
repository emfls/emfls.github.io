2026 Self-Employed Health Insurance Deduction Calculator

Estimate your Form 7206 deduction, income tax savings, and W-2 premium treatment for S-corp shareholders who own more than 2%.

Key takeaways

Your above-the-line deduction is generally the smaller of eligible premiums and earned income from the business that established the plan. Carry Form 7206 line 14 to Schedule 1, line 17.

  • Skip premiums for every month you could participate in a subsidized employer plan. Use separate eligibility and age limits for qualified long-term care (LTC).
  • For qualifying S-corp shareholders, use Medicare wages from the corporation that established the plan. Include paid or reimbursed premiums in Box 1 and exclude them from Boxes 3 and 5 when the plan meets the FICA exclusion test.

Missing Box 1 reporting can cost you the entire deduction. For a more-than-2% shareholder, the S corporation must pay or reimburse the premiums during the tax year and report them on the W-2. Check payroll records before claiming the adjustment.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my 2026 Form 7206 deduction?

Compare eligible premiums on line 3 with the applicable earned-income limit on line 13. The smaller amount goes on line 14 and Schedule 1, line 17, reducing adjusted gross income.

Does turning down employer coverage preserve the deduction?

Eligibility for subsidized employer coverage blocks premiums for that month even if you decline the plan. Check plans offered through your employer, your spouse’s employer, or the employer of your dependent or child under 27 at year-end.

How does a more-than-2% S-corp shareholder report premiums?

The S corporation must pay or reimburse the premiums and report them as Box 1 wages. A plan meeting the payroll exclusion test keeps the premiums out of Boxes 3 and 5. Box 14a can carry an optional labeled memo in 2026. Missing Box 1 reporting blocks the deduction as entered.

How are qualified long-term care premiums handled?

Exclude amounts for months when you could participate in a subsidized employer LTC plan and apply the annual limit for the covered person’s age at year-end. Apply the limit separately to each covered person.

Can I use excess premiums on Schedule A?

If you itemize, review otherwise eligible unreimbursed premiums left over after the Form 7206 deduction. Only total qualifying medical expenses above 7.5% of AGI are deductible, and the qualified LTC age limit still applies.
