Frequently asked questions

Can both vacancy taxes apply to the same home?

Yes. When both taxes apply in 2026, Vancouver charges 3% and B.C. charges 1% for the lower-rate Canadian citizen or permanent resident category or 3% for foreign owners and untaxed worldwide earners. The gross stack is 4% or 6% of assessed value.

Do short stays count toward the six-month tenancy exemption?

Vancouver counts residential rental periods of at least 30 consecutive days. B.C. applies its own one-month rule and, for the arm’s-length tenancy modeled here, the tenant must make the property their home. Add qualifying periods within the 2026 tax year.

What happens if I miss the City declaration?

Vancouver deems an undeclared property vacant and issues a $250 by-law notice. For 2026 property use, the City declaration is due February 2, 2027. File B.C.’s separate declaration by March 31, 2027.

How much is the B.C. resident credit for 2026?

An eligible B.C. resident can receive up to $4,000 for 2026. The non-refundable credit is limited by provincial tax owed, ownership share and the annual cap across properties. The calculator assumes one eligible owner with a 100% interest and the full credit available.

How does the calculator compare renting with paying the tax?

The annual cash swing adds the vacancy tax avoided to rent after estimated income tax and incremental after-tax rental costs. Break-even monthly rent is the rent required to cover any costs left after the modeled tax savings.
