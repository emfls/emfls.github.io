How Melbourne can cost over A$40,000 more

Consider a resident individual comparing an A$1.15 million Melbourne house with an A$1.2 million house in Brisbane or Perth. Assume 55% land value, 4% annual land growth, A$600,000 of existing taxable land in Victoria, and no existing land in Queensland or WA.

Over 10 years, the model estimates A$120,608 in duty and extra land tax for Melbourne, A$71,092 for Brisbane, and A$72,119 for Perth. Melbourne costs about A$49,516 more than Brisbane and A$48,488 more than Perth despite the A$50,000 lower purchase price. The existing Victorian holdings increase the marginal cost of adding the property.

How the estimate works

Purchase price stands in for dutiable market value. Year one uses the starting land value. From year two, the calculator compounds both the new land and existing holdings. In each state it subtracts the existing portfolio’s annual land tax from the assessment after the purchase. For WA, all entered land is assumed to be in metropolitan Perth for the Metropolitan Region Improvement Tax (MRIT).

NSW uses a three-year average. Queensland uses the lower of the current valuation and its three-year average. The ACT uses a five-year average, assessed separately per property. When prior valuations are unavailable, the model fills the earlier years with the year-one value. The ACT estimate assumes all four quarters are taxable. For a unit with entitlement entered, the calculator infers the residential complex’s land value from the unit’s share.

The first Victorian assessment is 2027. The 2024–2033 schedule incorporates the COVID debt levy and the model switches to the legislated general and trust schedules in 2034. Other future settings are held at the 2026–27 baseline. The estimate is in nominal dollars and excludes exemptions, concessions, foreign surcharges, partial-year settlement adjustments, other levies, registration, council rates and federal tax.

Frequently asked questions

How does existing land affect the new purchase?

For the five states, the calculator subtracts the tax on existing taxable holdings from the tax on existing holdings plus the purchase. Enter holdings in the same assessment group. The ACT assesses the new property by parcel.

When does Victoria’s COVID debt land tax schedule end?

The temporary Victorian land tax rates run through the 2033 assessment year. The model uses the legislated post-2033 schedule from 2034. The published temporary rates already include the levy.

Do discretionary trusts and companies face the same rates?

Queensland generally uses the same $350,000 starting threshold for both. NSW special trusts receive no general threshold, while a standalone domestic company generally receives the $1,075,000 threshold. Victoria and South Australia also have separate trust schedules.

What land value should I use for an apartment?

Start with the 25% apartment preset, then replace it with the land share implied by the valuation notice. In the ACT, enter the unit entitlement percentage so the calculator can apply the rate bands to the whole residential complex and apportion the valuation charge.

How does the calculator project future assessments?

Land values compound after year one, with estimated valuation averaging in NSW, Queensland and the ACT. The model holds 2026–27 rates and thresholds constant apart from Victoria’s scheduled switch in 2034. Each year assumes full assessment, with four taxable quarters in the ACT.
