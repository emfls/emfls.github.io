Key takeaways

For a fully eligible year, A$300,000 of qualifying construction cost gives a A$7,500 Division 43 deduction. It saves A$2,775 at a 37% marginal rate. If the whole cost-base reduction adds to a gain eligible for the 50% discount, it adds A$1,387.50 to CGT at the same rate, leaving A$1,387.50 of net tax savings before reinvestment.

Eligible plant can add to annual savings. Diminishing value starts with larger deductions for the same effective life, while prime cost spreads deductions more evenly.

Skipping a claim can forfeit the tax saving

You can forgo the annual tax saving and still have to reduce the cost base by the Division 43 amount you were entitled to claim. How to read the estimate

Each annual saving equals the eligible deduction multiplied by the holding marginal rate and assumes you can use the whole deduction that year. The model grows each saving from 30 June to the sale contract date at the return you enter. Savings for income years ending on or after sale carry zero growth. The sale-value estimate includes those expected savings at face value.

The property CGT comparison uses the difference between taxable gains before and after the reduction, multiplied by the sale marginal rate. Div 40 balancing adjustments, capital-loss carry-forwards, private use, multiple construction projects, older or special Div 43 regimes, exemptions and rate-band changes require separate calculations. The depreciation schedule assumes continuous business or income-producing use between the dates entered, and the whole adjustment falls within eligible construction costs included in the property basis. Use your tax records to confirm the inputs.

Frequently asked questions

How does the 2.5% capital works deduction apply if I buy an existing rental?

Use the original qualifying construction expenditure and the remaining portion of the 40-year period from completion. An eligible buyer may claim for eligible rental days during the remaining period. Buying the building does not restart the clock.

When can I depreciate previously used plant in a residential rental?

The restriction generally applies to used assets acquired from 7:30 pm AEST on 9 May 2017 by affected investors. Business and entity exceptions can apply. New assets you buy and first use in the rental may still qualify. Restricted assets may produce a separate capital gain or loss at disposal.

Which Division 40 method gives the larger deduction in the first year?

With the same effective life and eligible days, the 200% diminishing value rate usually starts higher and is applied to a shrinking value. Prime cost applies the 100% rate to original cost each year. Both methods stop at the remaining value.

Does skipping Division 43 preserve my CGT cost base?

Generally, eligible capital works amounts you claimed or could have claimed reduce the cost base to the extent it includes that expenditure. Skipping the claim can therefore forfeit a tax saving while leaving the CGT adjustment in place.

Why can the annual tax saving exceed the extra CGT on sale?

An eligible deduction reduces ordinary taxable income dollar for dollar. If the full adjustment increases a gain eligible for the 50% discount, only half the increase is taxable. Matching marginal rates then leave half the deduction’s tax saving after extra CGT. Reinvesting earlier savings may add to that amount. The result depends on eligibility, rates, capital losses and sale timing.
