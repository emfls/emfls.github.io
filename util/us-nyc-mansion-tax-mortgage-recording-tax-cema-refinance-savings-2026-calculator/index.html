Frequently asked questions

How does the NYC mansion tax cliff work?

The combined New York State mansion and NYC supplemental tax rate applies to the entire residential purchase price. At $1 million the tax becomes $10,000. At $2 million it becomes $25,000, a $5,000 jump from one cent below.

Do NYC co-ops pay mansion tax or mortgage recording tax?

Co-op purchases can owe mansion tax at $1 million and above. Individual co-op share loans carry no mortgage recording tax. At the same price and financed amount, the condo-versus-co-op difference in these two taxes is the condo’s borrower MRT.

What borrower MRT rate applies at $500,000?

The estimate uses 1.925% on the whole loan at $500,000 or more for individual residential condos and one- to three-family homes. Below $500,000 it uses 1.80%. The rates omit the 0.25% special additional tax borne by the lender where applicable.

How does a CEMA refinance reduce recording tax?

A qualifying Consolidation, Extension, and Modification Agreement keeps eligible, previously taxed principal in place. This estimate applies MRT to the new-money gap, the positive difference between the new loan and the balance that can be assigned. A qualifying refinance with zero new money has zero gap MRT. Confirm assignment and the taxable amount with your lender and closing attorney.

When do CEMA fees wipe out the savings?

Subtract the gap MRT and incremental bank, assignment and legal fees from the full-loan MRT. A positive amount is the modeled savings. The balance shown is the smallest assignable principal, to the cent, that produces positive savings at your new loan amount.
