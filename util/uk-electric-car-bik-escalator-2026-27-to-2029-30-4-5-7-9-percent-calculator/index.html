Key takeaways

EV BiK rises from 4% to 5%, 7% and 9%. At a fixed list price and tax rate, the 2029/30 bill is 2.25 times the 2026/27 bill.

A £40,000 EV costs a 40% taxpayer £53.33 a month in 2026/27 and £120 a month in 2029/30. Over four years, employee tax totals £4,000 and employer Class 1A NIC totals £1,500 at 15%.

Frequently asked questions

Why does electric company car tax increase by 125%?

The EV BiK percentage rises from 4% in 2026/27 to 9% in 2029/30. The same car and marginal rate therefore produce 2.25 times the first-year tax in year four.

How is the monthly company car tax estimate calculated?

Multiply the P11D list price by the year's BiK percentage and then by your marginal Income Tax rate. Divide the annual tax by 12 for the monthly equivalent. Payroll timing and changes in availability can affect the amount collected.

Who pays Class 1A National Insurance on a company car?

The employer pays Class 1A NIC on the taxable value of the car and any taxable private fuel. The model applies the 2026/27 rate of 15% throughout the comparison.

How much extra tax can employer-paid private fuel add?

For a 30% BiK car in 2026/27, the £29,200 multiplier gives a taxable fuel benefit of £8,760. At 40% Income Tax this adds £3,504 for the employee and £1,314 in employer Class 1A NIC at 15%.

When can a limited company claim the 100% EV first-year allowance?

Qualifying expenditure on a new and unused zero-emission car can be deducted from taxable profits for Corporation Tax purposes when incurred by 31 March 2027. The tax saving depends on the Corporation Tax treatment and the company's ability to use the deduction.
