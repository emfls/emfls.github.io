How the Calculation Works & Mathematical Formula

The Online Card Maker allows you to create personalized cards by selecting a template and entering your details. Each card type (business, invitation, wedding) follows a similar structure defined by the user inputs.

Input Variables:

  • Template: Choose the card style you want to use (e.g., Business Card, Wedding Invitation).
  • Name / Title: This is the primary name or title that will appear prominently on the card.
  • Subtitle: A supportive line of text that can provide additional context or information related to the primary title.
  • Details: Any supplementary information such as dates, locations, or personalized messages.

Mathematical Representation:

While there is no complex mathematical formula for generating card designs, a typical representation of the relationship among these inputs can be seen as:

Card Design = f(Template, Name_Title, Subtitle, Details)

Step-by-Step Example:

For instance, if you want to create a wedding invitation, follow these steps:

  1. Select the Template for a wedding invitation.
  2. Enter your Name / Title: "John & Jane".
  3. Provide a Subtitle: "Together with their families".
  4. Fill in the Details: "November 12, 2023, at 4 PM, Central Park".

After entering these details, click "Download Image" to get your card. Make sure to preview the card by clicking on it before the download.

Decision-Making Tips:

  • Ensure all details are correct before downloading; once downloaded, changes cannot be made.
  • Use clear and concise text for better readability on small card formats.
