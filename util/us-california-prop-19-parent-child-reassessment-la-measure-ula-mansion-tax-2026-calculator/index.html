Budget before missing a deadline or crossing a threshold

A $1.6 million inherited home with a $350,000 prior assessment could see its annual tax rise by $15,000 without the exclusion at a 1.20% rate. A qualifying transfer cuts the modeled increase to $2,464.97.

In July–December 2026, moving from exactly $5,400,000 to $5,400,000.01 adds about $216,000 in ULA alone. At $10,900,000, ULA is $599,500, roughly $163,500 more than just below that threshold.

Frequently asked questions

How is the new Prop 19 taxable value calculated?

For a qualifying 2026 family-home transfer, add $1,044,586 to the prior factored base-year value. Any market value above that ceiling is added to the prior value. A home at or below the ceiling keeps the prior value under the exclusion.

What must a child or grandchild do within one year?

Make the family home your principal residence and file for the homeowners’ or disabled veterans’ exemption within one year for relief from the transfer date. File the separate Prop 19 claim with the county assessor. Grandchildren must also meet the deceased-parent rule. Late exemption filing may allow prospective relief.

Can I inherit a rental or vacation home at the old assessment?

An inherited rental or vacation home generally receives a new assessment at its market value on the transfer date because it does not qualify as the transferor’s principal residence. Qualifying family farms have separate rules.

Does LA charge Measure ULA at exactly $5.4 million?

For July through December 2026 closings, exactly $5.4 million carries zero ULA. The 4% rate starts above that figure and 5.5% starts at $10.9 million. The applicable rate covers the entire gross value. January through June 2026 used $5.3 million and $10.6 million.

How do assumed liens affect LA transfer taxes and seller proceeds?

City and County base taxes use the value after liens remaining on the property, with $500 increments rounded up when the taxable base exceeds $100. ULA uses the full gross value. Seller proceeds here deduct all modeled transfer taxes and assumed liens from gross value, before loan payoffs and other closing costs.
