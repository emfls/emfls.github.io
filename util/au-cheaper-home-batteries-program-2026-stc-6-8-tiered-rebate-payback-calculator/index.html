CER Cheaper Home Batteries Program Guidelines

Who is eligible for the Cheaper Home Batteries Program STC rebate?

Australian residential properties and small businesses installing Clean Energy Council (CEC) approved battery energy storage systems with CEC-accredited installers on or after May 1, 2026 are eligible for point-of-sale STC discounts.

How is the 2026 vs 2027 STC factor difference calculated?

Under statutory CER deeming rules, installations between May 1 and Dec 31, 2026 receive a deeming factor of 6.8. On January 1, 2027, the factor steps down to 5.7, reducing the rebate by 16.2% across all capacity tiers.

Can I combine this federal rebate with state battery rebates?

Yes. Federal STCs are point-of-sale certificates and can be legally stacked with state programs such as NSW Peak Demand Reduction Scheme (PDRS), Victoria Solar Homes, or Queensland Battery Booster where funding remains active.

Assumptions & Statutory Notes

Calculations assume solar self-consumption charging where foregone feed-in tariff is deducted from peak tariff displacement. Actual savings depend on daily weather patterns and dynamic wholesale/retail tariffs.
