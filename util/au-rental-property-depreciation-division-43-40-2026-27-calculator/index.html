Estimate what you can deduct for the building under Division 43 and eligible fixtures and appliances under Division 40.

Key takeaways

  • Building and structural renovations: Eligible works usually deduct at 2.5% a year for 40 years from completion. An established property can still have years left.
  • Plant and equipment: The May 2017 restrictions generally block annual deductions for used assets bought with an established rental. Eligible new assets can still qualify.
  • Tax savings and sale: Your deduction can reduce income tax today. Division 43 can also reduce the property's CGT cost base when you sell.

Account for CGT before spending the tax saving

Keep records of capital works deductions you claim or can claim. They can reduce your property’s cost base and increase the taxable gain. The NPV subtracts the present value of that estimated extra sale tax from the present value of projected year-end tax savings. It assumes a sufficiently large taxable gain and a flat sale-year marginal rate. The cost-base reduction shown reflects only the modeled years. Earlier claims and Division 40 disposal adjustments may also affect your tax.

Frequently asked questions

Can I claim Division 43 on an established rental?

Yes. Eligible construction expenditure for an ordinary residential building started from 16 September 1987 can generally be deducted at 2.5% per year for 40 years from completion. Buying an established property does not restart the period. Eligible structural building renovations have their own cost and completion date.

Can I depreciate carpets or appliances left by the seller?

Individuals generally cannot deduct the decline in value of second-hand assets acquired at or after 7:30 pm AEST on 9 May 2017 for ordinary residential rentals. New replacement assets and certain eligible developer-supplied assets can qualify. A separate capital gain or loss may arise when a restricted asset is disposed of.

How do I choose a Division 40 method?

Prime cost applies the annual rate to cost. Diminishing value uses the declining balance. For qualifying non-business assets, an immediate deduction can apply to your interest costing A$300 or less, subject to the main-use, set and identical-item tests. Low-cost assets below A$1,000 can generally enter an eligible pool at 18.75% in the first year and 37.5% thereafter.

Will the tax saving increase my refund by the same amount?

The estimate is the reduction in Australian resident income tax, plus the change in the full Medicare levy if selected. A deduction can cross a tax bracket. Your refund also depends on withholding, other income, offsets and your personal circumstances. From 1 July 2027, negative gearing restrictions may affect when you can use deductions.

What happens to my CGT cost base when I sell?

Capital works deductions you claimed or could claim generally reduce the cost base to the extent included in it. The 50% discount scenario assumes an eligible resident individual, a qualifying gain and at least 12 months of ownership under the applicable rules. From 1 July 2027, transitional rules generally split gains accrued before and after that date, with exceptions and elections for some dwellings.
