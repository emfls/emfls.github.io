Key takeaways

  • Rent for up to 14 days in total and qualifying rent from your residence is excluded from federal gross income. At 15 days, the exclusion ends for the entire year’s rent.
  • A separate business entity may deduct ordinary and necessary rent at a supportable market rate. A sole proprietor or disregarded single-member LLC cannot pay deductible rent to its owner for this strategy.
  • When every dollar of rent reduces a full 20% QBI deduction, the modeled federal savings on the business side use 80% of your marginal rate.

Augusta Rule FAQ

Can my LLC rent my home for a business meeting?

Use the LLC’s federal tax classification. A partnership or a corporation can rent from an owner when there is a real business purpose and supportable rent. A disregarded single-member LLC and its individual owner are the same federal income taxpayer.

What happens when my home reaches 15 rental days?

The federal exclusion ends for the year’s entire rent, including payments from other renters. Generally report the rental activity on Schedule E, subject to the normal rental-income and expense rules. The calculator estimates tax on gross business rent before any owner-side expenses.

How does the QBI deduction change the estimate?

If every dollar of rent reduces an otherwise available QBI deduction by 20 cents, only 80 cents of that dollar reduces federal taxable income. This tool applies that adjustment to the federal marginal rate and adds the entered state rate separately.

Does receiving Form 1099-MISC make the rent taxable?

A business generally reports qualifying Box 1 payments totaling at least $2,000 to one payee in 2026. The information return does not determine whether you qualify for the federal residence-rental exclusion. Keep the rental-day log and payment records for reconciliation.

What did Sinopoli say about rent for home meetings?

The Tax Court limited the business rent deduction based on available evidence about comparable space and whether meetings actually happened. Keep dated quotes for equivalent space and records of actual meetings and payments. The calculator’s rate bands are a screening aid.
