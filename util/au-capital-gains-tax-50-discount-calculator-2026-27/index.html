Before you wait to sell

A lower tax bill can be outweighed by a fall in sale price or extra holding costs. A capital loss generally offsets capital gains and can carry forward. It cannot reduce your salary, and an exempt former home generally produces no usable capital loss.

1 July 2027 CGT reform planning alert

Eligible 2026–27 disposals can still qualify for the full 50% discount. The legislated transition generally introduces inflation indexation and a 30% minimum tax for affected gains accruing from 1 July 2027. A sale after the transition may need separate calculations for gains accrued before and after that date. Special rules can apply, including for eligible new builds. ATO reform updates

How the estimate works

Subtract the purchase price, eligible transaction costs and eligible capital improvements from sale proceeds. Apply any main residence exemption before using capital losses. Deduct the applicable discount, then calculate the difference between tax with and without the net gain.

The former-home examples assume the choice covers the whole available absence and the home otherwise qualifies for a full exemption, including any limits on treating another home as your main residence. Frequently asked questions

Are capital losses applied before the 50% CGT discount?

Yes. Deduct available current-year capital losses first, then eligible carried-forward losses. Apply the discount to the remaining gain. Excess losses generally carry forward and cannot reduce salary or other ordinary income.

When do I reach 12 months for the CGT discount?

Exclude both the acquisition day and the CGT event day. For example, a purchase on 20 August 2025 can meet the holding test for a sale contract signed on or after 21 August 2026. A property sale usually uses the contract date for the CGT event.

Does all my capital gain get taxed at the 15% rate?

The net capital gain is added to your other taxable income. Only the portion that falls above A$18,200 and up to A$45,000 uses the new 15% resident rate. Higher portions use the higher marginal rates, and any additional Medicare levy is calculated separately.

How does the six-year absence rule affect a former home?

An eligible former main home can remain exempt for up to six income-producing years during an absence, or indefinitely while it earns no income if the choice applies. For the continuous rental scenario shown here, the taxable share after six years uses days beyond the limit divided by total days from first income-producing use to settlement, where the market-value reset applies.

What changes on 1 July 2027?

The legislated transition introduces cost-base indexation and a 30% minimum tax for affected gains accruing from 1 July 2027, with exceptions. Eligible gains accrued before that date retain access to the 50% discount under the transition. This calculator applies only to disposals in 2026–27.
