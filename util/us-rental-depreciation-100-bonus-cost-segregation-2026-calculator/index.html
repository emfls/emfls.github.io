Calculation assumptions

Depreciable basis is (purchase price + capitalizable closing costs) × (1 − land share). Structural depreciation in year 1 uses (12.5 − service month) ÷ 12, with January as month 1. The standard comparison treats all depreciable basis as building basis.

The savings estimate uses one marginal federal rate and assumes you have enough taxable income to absorb losses shown as usable. It excludes basis and at-risk limits, personal use, alternative short-stay exceptions, prior losses, alternative depreciation systems, state taxes and future tax effects. Actual refunds also depend on withholding, credits and the rest of your return.

Frequently asked questions

Which rental property qualifies for 100% bonus depreciation in 2026?

The OBBBA restored 100% bonus for qualifying assets acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025. Eligible 5-, 7-, and 15-year property can qualify. Land is excluded and the remaining building uses its regular recovery period. Check binding contract dates, used-property rules, and any elections.

Does a seven-day average stay make the building 39-year property?

The seven-day test applies to passive activity classification under section 469. The building follows separate MACRS rules, including the 80% dwelling-unit income test and the exclusion for units in establishments where more than half the units are used on a transient basis.

Can passive rental losses offset W-2 income at $150,000 MAGI?

At $150,000 MAGI, the standard active-participation allowance reaches $0 for the filing statuses modeled here. Losses may still offset eligible passive income. Remaining passive losses generally carry forward and may be released on a fully taxable sale of the entire interest to an unrelated party.

When can a short-term rental or real estate professional claim a nonpassive loss?

An average guest stay of seven days or less meets the short-stay exception to rental activity treatment. The taxpayer also needs to materially participate for nonpassive treatment. Real estate professionals need more than 750 qualifying hours, more than half of their personal service time in qualifying real property businesses, and material participation in the rental activity. The 2026 excess business loss thresholds are $256,000 for single and $512,000 for joint filers.

What should I count when I compare study fees and tax savings?

Compare the incremental federal tax savings you can use this year against the study fee. Consider future depreciation and sale taxes separately. Unrecaptured section 1250 gain can be taxed at up to 25%, and section 1245 recapture may be ordinary income.
