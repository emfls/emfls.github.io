How the estimate works

The positive spread between fair market value and strike price, multiplied by the exercised shares, is the ISO adjustment. Regular federal income tax uses 2026 progressive brackets and the basic standard deduction ($16,100 single/MFS, $24,150 head of household or $32,200 joint). The model adds the spread and any entered net AMT adjustments to ordinary income before the standard deduction, which is disallowed for AMT.

The 2026 AMT exemptions are $90,100 for single or head of household, $140,200 for joint and $70,100 for married filing separately. They phase out by 50 cents per dollar over $500,000 for single, head of household and MFS or $1,000,000 for joint. They reach zero at $680,200 for single and head of household, $1,280,400 for joint and $640,200 for MFS. MFS includes the additional AMTI adjustment above $640,200 under IRC §55(d).

Tentative minimum tax applies 26% to the first $244,500 of taxable excess ($122,250 for MFS), then 28%. The crossover uses an integer search within the grant and compares tax rounded to cents. Example: $200,000 of ordinary income, a $50 fair market value and $10 strike give a 784-share crossover for a single filer. One more share produces $4 of AMT under these inputs.

This is a federal planning estimate for ordinary income and stock held through year end. Enter other positive net AMT adjustments as one amount if known. The estimate excludes special deductions, preferential capital gains rates, credits, other compensation, tax-table rounding and state taxes. Married separate filers must be eligible for the standard deduction. Check Form 6251 and Form 8801 before exercising or claiming a credit.

Frequently asked questions

Can I exercise any shares AMT-free if I owe AMT before exercising?

The zero-AMT crossover does not exist if baseline AMT is already positive. The result still separates baseline AMT from the additional AMT attributable to the exercise.

Does a same-year sale create this ISO adjustment?

A same-year disposition generally has no separate ISO adjustment for AMT. Enter only shares held through year end, and include any regular ordinary compensation from dispositions in your income projection.

Will I recover ISO AMT next year?

The ISO adjustment is generally a deferral item. Form 8801 determines eligible credit and usage, which depends on future regular tax, tentative minimum tax and other credit limits. The model treats additional ISO AMT as potential credit and carries forward the unused portion in its simplified scenario.
