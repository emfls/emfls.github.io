  • Income Tax uses net profit. Class 4 National Insurance applies only to trading profit, at 6% and 2% above the relevant thresholds.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does a landlord with £52,000 rent and £30,000 expenses need MTD?

    Yes, if the £52,000 was qualifying income on the 2024/25 return and no exemption applies. The 6 April 2026 threshold uses gross rent plus gross trading turnover. The £22,000 rental profit is used for Income Tax.

    Do quarterly updates mean quarterly tax payments?

    No. Keep digital records, send four quarterly updates and file your annual return through compatible software. Self Assessment payments keep their January and July deadlines.

    Do landlords pay Class 4 National Insurance on rent?

    Class 4 applies to self-employed trading profit. Ordinary rental profit can generate Income Tax and count towards MTD while producing no Class 4 National Insurance.

    Why can my first January bill be 150% of the year's tax?

    If you owe payments on account and made no advances for the year, January combines the outstanding bill with the first 50% advance for the next year. July brings the second 50% advance. The advances are credited against the next year's bill.

    When is voluntary Class 2 available in 2026/27?

    Eligible self-employed people below £7,105 in profits can choose to pay £3.65 per week to protect their National Insurance record. At £7,105 or above, Class 2 is treated as paid.
