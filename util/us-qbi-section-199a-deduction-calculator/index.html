2026 QBI Section 199A Deduction Calculator

Key takeaways

The 2026 federal QBI deduction generally starts at 20% of qualified pass-through business income. Higher taxable income can trigger wage and property limits or phase out service-business income. The OBBBA also introduces a $400 minimum for qualifying active businesses.

Calculation assumptions

This estimate uses $201,775 as the Single threshold. The IRS lists $201,750. The estimate also caps the active-business minimum at 20% of taxable income less net capital gain. Section 199A(i) sets a $400 minimum for eligible taxpayers. Use the official references below for filing.

How the estimate works

Above the threshold, the wage and property cap gradually applies. The cap is the larger of 50% of eligible W-2 wages or 25% of wages plus 2.5% of eligible property UBIA. For an SSTB within the range, the eligible portions of income, wages and property all fall as taxable income rises.

The model adds 20% of net eligible REIT dividends and PTP income. The regular combined deduction is capped at 20% of taxable income after subtracting net capital gain. The estimated tax savings multiply the modeled deduction by your marginal rate. If the deduction spans brackets, actual savings can differ.

Use this estimate for one business. Multi-business netting, loss tracking and cooperative adjustments require separate calculations. Blank, invalid and negative numeric entries count as zero in this estimate.

See 26 U.S.C. § 199A and IRS Form 8995 guidance.

Frequently asked questions

How much qualified business income can I deduct in 2026?

The regular calculation starts at 20% of positive qualified business income. Applicable business limits are calculated first, then eligible REIT dividends and publicly traded partnership income are added at 20%. The regular combined deduction is capped at 20% of taxable income before the deduction, reduced by net capital gain.

When do the 2026 income limits apply?

Under IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32, the phase-in starts above $403,500 for joint returns, $201,775 for married separate returns, and $201,750 for all other returns. The ranges are $150,000 for joint returns and $75,000 for other returns. This calculator uses $201,775 for its Single scenario.

How does the SSTB phase-out work?

Above the threshold, the eligible share of QBI, W-2 wages and qualified property declines as taxable income rises. The wage and property limitation also phases in on those reduced amounts. The business component reaches zero at the upper endpoint.

Who qualifies for the $400 active-business minimum?

Section 199A(i) sets a $400 minimum for a taxpayer with at least $1,000 of aggregate QBI from qualified businesses in which the taxpayer materially participates. This calculator uses a single business and limits the modeled minimum to the regular taxable-income cap.

How do S corporation wages affect the deduction?

When the wage and property limit applies, the calculator shows how much additional eligible W-2 payroll would eliminate that business-level reduction if other inputs stayed constant. An actual salary increase can reduce pass-through QBI, change payroll taxes and affect reasonable compensation planning.
