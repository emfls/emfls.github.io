What changes on 6 April 2027?

Under the Budget 2026 proposal, the Business Asset Disposal Relief (BADR) lifetime limit falls from £1 million to £500,000. Qualifying gains are taxed at 18% in both years. The individual Capital Gains Tax (CGT) annual exempt amount stays at £3,000. GOV.UK BADR measure

Budget 2026 also proposes property income rates of 24%, 44% and 49% from 2027/28. Most unused pension funds and pension death benefits enter the Inheritance Tax estate from the same date. Budget 2026 rates and allowances · HMRC pension technical note

Frequently asked questions

What changes for BADR on 6 April 2027?

Budget 2026 proposes reducing the lifetime limit on qualifying gains from £1 million to £500,000. The BADR rate is 18% in both years and the individual CGT annual exemption remains £3,000. Previous qualifying gains count towards the reduced lifetime limit.

How does Section 24 differ from ownership through an SPV?

An individual residential landlord generally gets an Income Tax reduction based on eligible finance costs, subject to HMRC limits. A limited company special purpose vehicle (SPV) generally accounts for eligible interest under Corporation Tax rules. Compare company cash retained with personal cash and allow for tax on dividends and any costs of transferring an existing property.

What happens to pensions and the residence nil-rate band in April 2027?

Most unused pension funds and pension death benefits enter the estate for Inheritance Tax from 6 April 2027. This can increase the estate value above the £2 million taper threshold. A qualifying residence left to direct descendants can attract a residence nil-rate band, which is reduced by £1 for each £2 above that threshold.

How can VAT affect SDLT on a commercial property purchase?

VAT payable on the purchase generally forms part of the consideration used to calculate SDLT. Where the sale qualifies as a transfer of a going concern, VAT is generally outside the scope of the transfer. Check the option to tax and buyer notification conditions before relying on that treatment.
