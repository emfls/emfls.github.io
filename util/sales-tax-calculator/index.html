How the Calculation Works & Mathematical Formula

The Sales Tax Calculator allows you to add or extract tax from a given total. It uses simple arithmetic to compute the sales tax based on the amount and the specified tax rate.

Here are the input variables:

  • Amount: The total amount of money that may include sales tax.
  • Tax rate (%): The rate of sales tax applied to the amount, expressed as a percentage.
  • Mode: Choose between adding sales tax to the stated amount or extracting tax that is included in the total.

The formula for calculating sales tax is:

Tax Amount = Amount × (Tax Rate / 100)

If you want to add tax, the total becomes:

Total with Tax = Amount + Tax Amount

If you want to extract tax from an inclusive total, the formula is:

Original Amount = Total ÷ (1 + (Tax Rate / 100))

For example, if you have an amount of $100 and a tax rate of 8%

  1. If adding tax: Tax Amount = $100 × (8 / 100) = $8
  2. Total with Tax = $100 + $8 = $108

And if extracting tax from a total of $108:

  1. Original Amount = $108 ÷ (1 + (8 / 100)) = $108 ÷ 1.08 ≈ $100

Common misconceptions include believing that tax included in the total is directly the percentage of that total, which is not accurate. Also, remember that this calculator provides arithmetic results and does not consider specific jurisdictional tax rules or exemptions. Adjust accordingly based on location.
