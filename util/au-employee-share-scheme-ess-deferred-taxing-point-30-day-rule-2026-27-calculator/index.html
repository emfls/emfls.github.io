ESS tax questions

What happens if I sell within 30 days of my deferred taxing point?

An arm’s-length sale through day 30 moves the deferred taxing point to the disposal date. In this model the sale price determines the ESS discount and the disposal produces no separate capital gain or loss. The new taxing point can fall in the next financial year.

When does the CGT clock restart for deferred ESS shares?

For a sale after day 30, the cost base resets to market value at the deferred taxing point. The CGT holding period restarts from that point. The ATO excludes both the acquisition and disposal dates when counting 12 months.

How does the $1,000 taxed-upfront reduction work?

Eligible taxed-upfront plans can reduce the ESS discount by up to $1,000 if income for the ESS test is $180,000 or less before the reduction. The cap applies to eligible discounts across the year. Upfront shares use grant-date market value as the starting cost base.

Do qualifying start-up options get the grant-date CGT clock?

Yes. For shares acquired by exercising qualifying start-up options, the CGT discount holding period begins when you acquired the options. The ESS discount is excluded from assessable income. Include any amount paid to acquire the rights and to exercise them in the amount paid.

Can an ESS capital loss reduce my salary or ESS discount?

A capital loss can offset eligible capital gains. It cannot reduce salary or ordinary ESS income. An unused loss can generally be carried forward to offset eligible future capital gains.
