Key takeaways

Loss aversion and the 61-day wash-sale window

Here is a way to test whether loss aversion is driving your decision. If you held the proceeds in cash today, would you buy the same investment at today’s price? Weigh the estimated tax benefit against transaction costs, portfolio exposure and the potential cost of missing a rebound.

Review all purchases of substantially identical securities from 30 days before through 30 days after a loss sale, including the sale day. Automatic reinvestments, purchases by a spouse, and IRA purchases can trigger the rule.

Capital gains tax FAQ

How do the 2026 long-term capital gains rates work?

Taxable ordinary income fills the brackets first. Eligible net gains on assets held more than one year and qualified dividends are stacked above it and generally taxed at 0%, 15%, or 20%. Net short-term gains generally use ordinary tax rates. The cutoffs depend on filing status.

When does the 3.8% net investment income tax apply?

NIIT is 3.8% of the smaller of positive net investment income and the amount by which modified adjusted gross income exceeds the filing threshold. The threshold is $200,000 for single and head of household, $250,000 for joint filers, and $125,000 for separate filers. Where both rates apply to the same dollar of gain, the 20% rate and NIIT combine to 23.8%.

How does the $3,000 capital loss deduction work?

Net gains and losses within each holding period first, then offset opposite short- and long-term balances. An overall loss can produce a deduction of up to $3,000 ($1,500 if married filing separately). Unused individual losses carry forward until used. The Schedule D worksheet can preserve additional losses when deductions exceed income, and it applies the loss used to short-term losses first.

How much long-term gain can I harvest at 0%?

The additional gain estimate accounts for unused deductions, qualified dividends, and losses that can absorb gains. A sale can still increase ordinary tax by reducing your capital loss deduction or freeing short-term gains that losses previously offset. NIIT, state taxes, credits, and income-based benefits can also change. The separate federal tax estimate checks the modeled ordinary tax and NIIT before and after harvesting.

What is the wash-sale window when harvesting a loss?

Buying substantially identical stock or securities within 30 days before or after selling at a loss can disallow the loss. The sale date is also within the 61-day window. Review purchases across accounts, including IRA transactions, a spouse’s purchases, and automatic dividend reinvestments.
