90-day RRSP contribution alert

Before using newly contributed RRSP funds for the HBP, allow 90 days where possible. CRA compares contributions made in the preceding 89 days with the RRSP’s fair market value immediately after withdrawal. The excess over that value is generally nondeductible.

The $60,000 limit applies to withdrawals after April 16, 2024. For earlier 2024 withdrawals, use the $35,000 per-person limit. FHSAs became available April 1, 2023. Someone who opened an FHSA in 2023 could have built up to $32,000 in regular contributions and transfers by 2026, plus any growth.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the HBP and FHSA for the same home?

Yes, if you qualify under both programs at the time of each withdrawal. At the 2026 HBP limit, $60,000 plus $40,000 of FHSA lifetime funding gives a $100,000 benchmark for one buyer or $200,000 for two. Qualifying FHSA withdrawals can include investment growth, and you do not repay them.

When do I start repaying a 2026 HBP withdrawal?

The repayment period starts in 2031 and runs through 2045. There are no mandatory repayments in 2027, 2028, 2029 or 2030. For a $60,000 withdrawal with no early payments, the initial annual minimum is $4,000.

What happens if I skip all or part of an HBP repayment?

Include the difference between your annual minimum and designated repayment as RRSP income for that year. A $4,000 shortfall at a 30% combined marginal rate adds an estimated $1,200 in income tax. The shortfall reduces your HBP balance without returning money to your retirement account.

How do extra HBP repayments change the schedule?

Designated payments made before the repayment period starts reduce the first year's requirement. In later years, the minimum is based on the remaining HBP balance divided by the years left in the repayment period. You can clear the balance early. Designate your repayment on Schedule 7.

What is the 90-day RRSP rule?

CRA compares RRSP contributions made in the 89 days before an HBP withdrawal with the value remaining in that RRSP immediately afterward. If recent contributions exceed the remaining value, the excess generally cannot be deducted. Waiting 90 days puts a contribution outside that lookback.
