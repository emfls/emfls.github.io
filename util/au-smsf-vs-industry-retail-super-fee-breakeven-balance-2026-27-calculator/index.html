How the estimate works

The model applies the same gross return each year, calculates fees on each option’s own opening balance, then deducts the fees from the grown balance. It charges the SMSF setup cost once in year one and floors balances at zero. The benchmark treats members as having equal balances and applies the percentage admin cap to each account.

Fees stay constant at the entered values. The estimate excludes contributions, withdrawals, tax, inflation, insurance, investment transaction charges, product-specific fee rules and wind-up costs. Fee presets are illustrative. This calculator provides general information for comparing costs.

SMSF fee questions

What balance makes an SMSF cheaper?

Without an admin cap, divide SMSF fixed fees minus total benchmark fixed fees by the benchmark percentage fee minus the SMSF investment fee rate. This gives the usual crossover when the SMSF has higher fixed fees and a lower percentage rate. Equal rates, reversed fee structures or caps can change the result.

Why is the new SMSF levy A$518 in year one?

A continuing newly registered SMSF pays A$259 for its first financial year and A$259 in advance for the next. A continuing established SMSF pays A$259 annually in advance. The model assumes the fund stays open through the projection.

How does adding members affect the crossover?

Members in one SMSF share the modelled fund-level costs. In the benchmark, each member pays their own fixed admin fee and has a separate percentage admin cap, if any. The sensitivity table holds the total balance constant and assumes equal member balances.

Can a per-member admin cap create two crossovers?

Yes. Once each member reaches the cap, only the uncapped benchmark investment charge continues rising. If the SMSF investment rate is then higher than the benchmark's, an SMSF that was cheaper at intermediate balances can become more expensive again.

How are setup costs and gross returns treated?

The projection charges setup once in year one and applies the same gross return to both options. The annualised comparison spreads setup over 20 years for an illustrative yearly fee and crossover. It leaves the projection and year-one cash outlay unchanged.
