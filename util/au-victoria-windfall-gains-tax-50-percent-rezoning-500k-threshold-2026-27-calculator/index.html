Frequently asked questions

How is Victoria’s windfall gains tax calculated?

Compare the Valuer-General’s capital improved values before and after rezoning, then subtract any prescribed deductions. After exemptions, uplift up to $100,000 carries no tax. Above $100,000 and below $500,000, tax is 62.5% of the excess over $100,000. At $500,000 and above, tax is 50% of the full taxable uplift.

What happens at exactly $500,000 of taxable uplift?

The 50% rate applies to the full $500,000, giving $250,000 of WGT. The 62.5% formula reaches the same amount at that boundary, so the tax does not jump when you cross it.

Does residential land qualify for the two-hectare exemption?

Up to two hectares of eligible residential land across the same owner or group in the same rezoning can be exempt. An existing lawful residence can qualify. Construction or renovation needs a building permit, qualifying prior residential circumstances and lawful residential use after completion. A permit alone on vacant land is insufficient. For qualifying land above two hectares, the remaining area’s proportion of uplift is taxable.

When does deferral end and how is interest charged?

A qualifying dutiable transaction, relevant landholder acquisition or the 30-year limit can end deferral. Subdivision apportions the deferred tax and accrued interest across the new lots and the deferral continues. Interest accrues daily on deferred principal at the applicable 10-year TCV bond rate, using the previous financial year’s May average of daily yields.

Can WGT and deferral interest increase my federal CGT cost base?

If the land was acquired after 20 August 1991, eligible non-deductible WGT and deferral interest can generally be included as ownership costs. Compare the adjusted cost bases when you sell. The calculator applies an illustrative effective CGT rate you choose.
