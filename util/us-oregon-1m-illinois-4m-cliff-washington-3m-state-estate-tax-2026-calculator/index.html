2026 Estate Tax Calculator for Oregon, Illinois and Washington

Compare state tax on the same estate, check how close you are to Illinois’s $4 million threshold, and estimate what a credit-shelter trust could save.

Key takeaways

  • Oregon taxes taxable value above $1 million at graduated rates of 10%–16%.
  • Illinois tax starts above $4 million including adjusted taxable gifts. The interrelated limit makes the initial marginal rate about 28.57% before apportionment.
  • Washington uses a $3.076 million exclusion and 10%–35% rates through June 30. From July 1, it uses $3 million and 10%–20% rates.
  • Federal basic exclusion is $15 million per decedent. An estate can owe state estate tax well below that amount.

How the Illinois estimate works

Form 700 line 3 feeds the former federal state death tax credit table after subtracting $60,000 and the credit itself. Within each bracket the model solves the deduction algebraically. It then takes the lower of that result and 2/7 of the positive difference between line 5 (estate plus adjusted taxable gifts) and $4 million. It applies the Illinois asset percentage last.

The estimate displays cents. Official examples are reported in whole dollars. Confirm return amounts with the Illinois Attorney General calculator and see the state death tax credit table.

Estate tax questions

Does moving one dollar above $4 million trigger a large Illinois tax bill?

With no adjusted taxable gifts and full Illinois allocation, the interrelated estimate is $0.29 at $4,000,001. The exclusion limit starts at about 28.57% of each additional dollar. At higher values the former federal credit table can yield the lower amount.

Does a lifetime gift lower the estate below the Illinois threshold?

Illinois adds adjusted taxable gifts to the tentative taxable estate for the threshold calculation. Moving an amount from your estate to adjusted taxable gifts can leave the combined total unchanged. An allowable charitable deduction can reduce the tentative estate without adding an adjusted taxable gift.

Which Washington exclusion and Table W apply in 2026?

For deaths January 1 through June 30, use $3,076,000 and the 10%–35% schedule. For deaths July 1 through December 31, use $3,000,000 and the 10%–20% schedule. The rates apply to Washington taxable value after the applicable exclusion.

How can a credit-shelter trust affect the surviving spouse’s estate tax?

A properly structured trust can use part of the first spouse’s state exclusion and hold assets outside the surviving spouse’s taxable estate. The result depends on ownership, trust terms and the rules in effect at each death.

Can an estate owe state estate tax below the federal basic exclusion?

Yes. The federal basic exclusion is $15 million for 2026. Each state applies its own rules at lower amounts. Adjusted taxable gifts can use part of the federal exclusion, and an estate can have filing obligations even when the estimated tax is zero.
