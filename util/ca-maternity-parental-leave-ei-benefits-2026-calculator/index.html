Frequently asked questions

How is my 2026 weekly EI benefit calculated?

Service Canada uses your best weeks of insurable earnings. The calculator estimates that average by dividing a steady annual insurable salary, capped at $68,900, by 52. It applies the 55% or 33% rate, the weekly cap and whole-dollar rounding.

How do the extra partner sharing weeks work?

The standard limit is 40 shared parental weeks with a 35-week individual maximum. The extended limit is 69 shared weeks with a 61-week individual maximum. An eligible second parent must claim at least 5 standard or 8 extended weeks to reach the respective household maximum. Each parent applies separately and both must use the same option.

Can I switch to standard EI if I return to work early?

Once either parent receives a parental benefit payment for the child, the option is locked. Returning to work does not convert remaining extended weeks to standard payments. The loss shown assumes Parent 1 resumes full-time work and does not claim the remaining weeks later.

Will an employer top-up reduce my EI?

A qualifying supplement leaves EI intact when the combined weekly payment stays within normal weekly earnings and the employer does not draw down accumulated benefits such as vacation, sick leave or severance. Check the plan's payment schedule and terms with your employer.

Does this calculator apply in Québec?

Eligible Québec residents use the Québec Parental Insurance Plan (QPIP) for maternity, parental and adoption benefits. QPIP has its own eligibility rules, rates and schedules. Families with both EI and QPIP claims should confirm coordination with both programs.
