Key takeaways

  • Report and pay any UK residential property CGT due within 60 calendar days of completion.
  • For individuals in 2026/27, the annual exemption is up to £3,000. The remaining basic-rate band is taxed at 18%, and the rest of the gain at 24%.
  • A late property return can start at £100, even with £0 CGT if you are non-UK resident. Unpaid CGT can accrue interest at Bank Rate plus 4 percentage points.

Frequently asked questions

When is my 60-day UK property CGT deadline?

Count 60 calendar days from completion. The disposal date, usually the date of unconditional exchange, determines the tax year. A late payment can accrue interest once the property payment deadline passes.

Do I need to report a UK property disposal if I am non-UK resident and owe £0?

Yes. Non-UK residents must report UK property disposals by the deadline even when there is a loss, full relief or no CGT to pay. Late filing can still incur penalties.

How is my 2026/27 residential property CGT calculated?

Deduct your available annual exemption, up to £3,000 for eligible individuals, from the net gain. Apply 18% to the portion within the unused £37,700 basic-rate band and 24% to the rest.

When do late payment penalties start for property CGT?

The property payment remains due within 60 days of completion. Schedule 56 penalty timing generally follows the annual January basis. The 60-day stress test illustrates exposure if you apply the same percentages earlier.

Can I file the property return with a reasonable estimate?

HMRC allows reasonable estimates when relevant figures are unavailable. Indicate an estimate where required, pay the amount due, and update the return as needed once figures become available. Interest continues on unpaid tax.
