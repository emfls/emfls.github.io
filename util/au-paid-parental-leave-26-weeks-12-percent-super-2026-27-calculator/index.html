How the estimate works

When both parents can claim, the default gives Parent A the maximum and Parent B the reserved days. The optimizer compares every permitted whole-day split that uses the same total number of days. The household package adds cash after estimated tax to super after estimated contributions tax.

Tax is the increase in annual resident income tax plus the standard 2% Medicare levy on added pay. The estimate applies one financial year to all days, holds both incomes fixed across splits, and excludes offsets, Medicare reductions and surcharge, HELP repayments, super interest and additional super taxes, and changes to wages or benefits. Take-home cash estimates the final tax cost. Payslip withholding can differ. The income test is one part of eligibility. Work, residence, claim timing, and sharing rules also apply.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the full 26 weeks of Parental Leave Pay?

If all 130 days are taken in 2026–27, gross payments total $26,122.20 at $200.94 a day. Birth or adoption sets the number of days and the financial year of each leave day sets its rate.

What happens if my partner cannot use their reserved days?

For births and adoptions from 1 July 2026, the standard partnered limit for one parent is 110 days. If the other parent cannot claim or leaves their 20 reserved days unused, the household can lose up to $4,018.80 in gross pay at the 2026–27 rate. A single parent can claim all 130 days if eligible.

Can I qualify if my adjusted taxable income exceeds $186,487?

For a 2025–26 ATI assessment, a claimant above the individual limit can pass if their family ATI is $386,525 or less. Services Australia uses the financial year before the earlier of the claim and birth or adoption dates. A single applicant uses their own ATI for the fallback.

How and when is the 12% super contribution paid?

The ATO pays each recipient’s contribution into their super fund after the relevant financial year. On $26,122.20 gross pay, the base contribution is $3,134.66, or about $2,664.46 after the usual 15% contributions tax. The ATO also includes an interest component.

Can both parents take Parental Leave Pay at the same time?

For births or adoptions from 1 July 2025, eligible parents can generally take up to 20 paid days at the same time. Each parent uses a day from their own allocation for each day of overlap. The calculator compares allocations and leaves scheduling to the parents.
