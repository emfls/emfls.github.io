How the estimate works

Annual exclusions. Enter all gifts to the modeled recipients for the year. Under gift splitting, the calculator assigns half of each third-party gift to each spouse and applies each donor’s own annual exclusion. The noncitizen spouse gift uses its own limit and stays with the donor making that gift. Eligible split-gift consent generally covers all third-party gifts made by either spouse during the year while married. See the IRS Form 709 instructions.

529 contributions. A donor can elect five-year treatment when the donor’s share of the 529 gift to a beneficiary exceeds the annual exclusion. The calculator spreads up to five times that exclusion and adds any excess immediately. It applies the same year’s exclusion to the first fifth, other gifts and any 529 excess for that recipient. It assumes no installments from older 529 elections. If a donor dies within the five-year period, amounts allocable to periods after the donor’s death generally return to the donor’s estate. See IRC §529(c)(2)(B) and (c)(4)(C).

Federal tax. Prior taxable gifts are treated as having used the basic exclusion, up to the year’s limit. The calculator applies remaining exemption separately to each donor’s new taxable gifts. Any new taxable gifts above that exemption are in the 40% federal bracket. The model covers U.S. citizen or resident donors making present-interest gifts and omits portability, tax paid on earlier gifts, other credits, generation-skipping transfer tax and state taxes. Those items can change the amount on an actual return. See Form 709 tax computation instructions.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I give each person in 2027 before using my lifetime exemption?

You can generally give $20,000 in present-interest gifts per recipient in 2027. Two spouses have $40,000 of combined exclusions per recipient. Each can make gifts from their own funds, or eligible spouses can agree to treat their gifts to third parties as made half by each.

Can I owe $0 and still have to file Form 709?

Yes. A single donor who gives a child $25,000 in cash in 2027 generally reports the gift on Form 709. The first $20,000 is excluded, and the remaining $5,000 uses lifetime exemption if available. Gift splitting and a new five-year 529 election also require filing even when the gift tax is zero.

Can I combine a $100,000 529 contribution with a separate $20,000 cash gift?

A $100,000 contribution with the five-year election counts as $20,000 in each year from 2027 through 2031 for that donor and beneficiary. The 2027 share uses the full annual exclusion, so an extra $20,000 cash gift to the same person is generally taxable. With gift splitting and an election by each spouse, the combined 529 amount can reach $200,000.

Which direct tuition and medical payments have a separate exclusion?

Qualifying tuition paid straight to an eligible school and qualifying unreimbursed medical costs paid straight to the provider can be excluded without a dollar cap under Section 2503(e). Room and board, books, 529 contributions and money handed to the recipient to pay bills fall outside this direct-payment exclusion.

What is the 2027 exclusion for a gift to a noncitizen spouse?

The special exclusion is $201,000 for qualifying present-interest gifts in 2027, up from $194,000 in 2026. The gift must otherwise qualify for the marital deduction. A qualifying $210,000 cash gift generally leaves $9,000 of taxable gifts in 2027.
