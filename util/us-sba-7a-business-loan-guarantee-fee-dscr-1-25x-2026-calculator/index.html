Key takeaways

DSCR Underwriting Benchmark: SBA SOP 50 10 enforces a strict 1.25x Debt Service Coverage Ratio. At least $1.25 in Net Operating Income is required for every $1.00 of total principal and interest debt service.

Tiered Statutory Guaranty Fee: Fees apply only to the guaranteed balance (75% or 85%). For a $1,000,000 loan, the fee is $26,250 on the $750,000 guaranteed portion (3.5%), which can be financed into the loan.

DSCR compression risk under variable rate resets

Most SBA 7(a) loans feature variable interest rates adjusting quarterly with the WSJ Prime Rate. A 100 bps rate hike increases annual debt service by roughly $6,500 on a $1M loan, potentially dropping a borderline 1.25x DSCR into default territory. Always stress-test your cash flows against a +2.0% rate spike.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum DSCR required for an SBA 7(a) loan?

Under SBA SOP 50 10 7.1, lenders enforce a standard 1.25x Debt Service Coverage Ratio benchmark based on historical or normalized operating cash flows.

How is the upfront SBA guarantee fee calculated?

The fee is tiered and applies strictly to the guaranteed portion of the loan (75% or 85%). For loans over $1,000,000 guaranteed, the rate is 3.5% on the first $1M and 3.75% on the excess.

What are the maximum lender interest rate margins?

For loans of $350,000 or greater, SBA regulations cap the variable lender spread at WSJ Prime + 3.00%.

Can the guarantee fee be financed into the loan?

Yes, borrowers can roll the statutory upfront fee into the loan balance as long as total financing does not exceed the $5,000,000 cap.

What is the small manufacturer fee waiver?

Eligible small manufacturing enterprises (NAICS 31-33) receive an upfront guarantee fee waiver on loan amounts up to $950,000.
