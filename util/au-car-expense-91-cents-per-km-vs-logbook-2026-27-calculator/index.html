Key takeaways

91¢/km gives you up to $4,550 per car at 5,000 work kilometres. That’s $150 more in deductions than the 88¢ rate in 2024–25 and 2025–26. A representative logbook can support a bigger claim when the work-related share of actual car costs exceeds your kilometre-based deduction.

Protect your claim by checking your trips and records

Counting normal home-to-work trips could overstate both deductions. Genuine itinerant work or essential transport of bulky equipment with no secure storage at work can qualify in limited circumstances. Keep a representative logbook and evidence of your costs before relying on the logbook result. If you are eligible for the $1,000 standard work-expense deduction, compare it with your combined eligible itemised work expenses before choosing how to claim.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I claim using 91¢ per kilometre?

Claim 91¢ for each eligible work kilometre, up to 5,000 km per car for 2026–27. The most you can claim under this method is $4,550. The rate covers car expenses including fuel, registration, insurance and decline in value. Keep records showing how you worked out your eligible kilometres.

What do I need to claim using the logbook method?

Keep a representative logbook for at least 12 continuous weeks, annual odometer readings and evidence of your car costs. Record each work trip’s dates, purpose and distance. A representative logbook can generally be reused for up to five income years. Start a new one if your work use changes materially.

Can I count my regular drive from home to work?

Ordinary commuting is generally private. Limited exceptions may apply if your work is genuinely itinerant or your duties require you to transport bulky equipment that cannot be stored securely at work. Keep ordinary commuting kilometres in total annual distance and exclude them from work kilometres.

How does the $69,883 car limit affect depreciation?

For a car first used in 2026–27, the calculator caps the purchase price used in its full-year estimate at $69,883. With an eight-year effective life, it estimates 25% of that basis for first-year diminishing value or 12.5% for prime cost. Enter your calculated annual decline in value for earlier purchases, part-years or later years.

Does the extra deduction equal my refund?

The calculator applies the progressive resident tax bands to income after each car deduction, plus the selected Medicare levy assumption. The difference is an estimate of tax saved between the methods. Your refund also depends on withholding, offsets and other rules. Eligible employees using the standard work-expense deduction generally cannot add these car expenses to it.
