Australia Foreign Buyer Stamp Duty & FIRB Fee Calculator 2026–27

Compare standard stamp duty, the foreign buyer surcharge and the federal application fee for the same residential purchase across five states.

Key takeaways

On an A$1 million new dwelling, the foreign surcharge plus the A$15,600 FIRB fee adds A$105,600 in NSW, A$95,600 in VIC or QLD, and A$85,600 in WA or SA above standard stamp duty.

The federal fee depends on the purchase-price tier. Where a limited exception permits an established-dwelling purchase, the standard established-dwelling fee is three times the new-dwelling fee.

Budget before you make an offer

A small price increase can push the application into a higher fee tier. A bid just over A$1 million raises the new-dwelling FIRB fee by A$15,700, before any increase in state duties.

Confirm approval requirements and any contract conditions with your conveyancer before signing. A deposit may be at risk if finance or approval falls through and the contract offers no protection.

What the estimate covers

The comparison assumes one fully foreign-owned residential purchase, the full applicable surcharge, and one federal application. The domestic baseline uses ordinary duty with no concessions. NSW includes premium duty on qualifying residential property over A$3.87 million and assumes the property is entirely residential and no larger than two hectares.

First-home, owner-occupier and off-the-plan concessions, exemptions, shared ownership, legal fees, annual land taxes and vacancy fees are outside the estimate. Confirm your state and federal status separately because their definitions can differ.

Standard duty schedules are published by Revenue NSW, SRO Victoria, Queensland Revenue Office, RevenueWA and RevenueSA.

Frequently asked questions

How do foreign buyer surcharges affect stamp duty?

The state surcharge is added to standard transfer duty. For a fully foreign-owned purchase, the model applies 9% in NSW, 8% in VIC and QLD, and 7% in WA and SA to the dutiable value. The federal application fee is charged separately.

Which value determines state duty and the FIRB fee?

This calculator uses the greater of purchase price and market value for state duty. It uses the purchase price for the federal fee schedule, assuming a direct purchase for consideration. The total burden percentage uses purchase price.

Can foreign buyers purchase an established dwelling?

Foreign persons generally cannot purchase established dwellings from 1 April 2025 through 30 June 2029. Limited exceptions apply. When an eligible exception uses the standard established-dwelling schedule, the application fee is three times the standard new-dwelling fee.

When does the 2026–27 FIRB application fee increase?

The fee rises at exactly A$75,000 and immediately above each A$1 million threshold through A$40 million. For example, an A$1 million new dwelling attracts A$15,600, and an A$1,000,000.01 purchase moves to the A$31,300 tier.

What does the extra cost over a citizen or permanent resident include?

The comparison uses a citizen or permanent resident who pays the same standard duty with no foreign surcharge or FIRB fee. The extra cost is the surcharge plus the application fee. Individual exemptions, residency tests and concessions can change the outcome.
