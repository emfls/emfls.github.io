How the estimate works

Divide the December 31 balance by the IRS factor for your age during the distribution year. Calculate separate RMDs for each eligible IRA. This tool models one IRA and rounds dollar results to cents. If your spouse is your sole beneficiary and more than 10 years younger, use Table II.

  • IRS Publication 590-B
  • Treasury RMD final and proposed rules in IRB 2024-33

Frequently asked questions

At what age do RMDs start?

The applicable age is 73 for 1951–1958 births and 75 for 1960 and later births. Treasury proposed age 73 for 1959 births, which this estimate uses. Earlier birth cohorts already started before 2026.

When does the 10% excise tax apply?

The regular excise tax is 25% of the amount you were required to take but did not. The rate can drop to 10% if you withdraw the shortfall and file a return reflecting the tax within the correction window. The window ends at the earliest of a deficiency notice, assessment, or the end of the second following tax year. A reasonable-error waiver may be available through Form 5329.

When is the first RMD due?

For a traditional IRA owner who turns 73 in 2026, the first 2026 RMD can be taken by April 1, 2027. The 2027 RMD is still due December 31, 2027. Any first RMD for 2025 due April 1, 2026 is separate.
