APRA & Australian Tax Office Guidelines

How does negative gearing interact with Interest-Only loans?

Because no principal is paid off during the IO term, 100% of your mortgage payments consist of tax-deductible interest. This maximizes tax write-offs against your salary income at your marginal tax bracket.

What should you do before the 5-year cliff arrives?

Approximately 6 months before your IO period expires, consult a mortgage broker to either request an IO extension from your current lender, refinance to a new 30-year term with another bank, or prepare your cash flow budget for the higher P&I payment.

Why does the loan amortize over 25 years instead of 30?

A standard mortgage contract has a total duration of 30 years. If the first 5 years are spent paying only interest, you must repay 100% of the loan principal within the remaining 25 years.
