⚠️ Critical Deadline: The 60-Day Unlocking Window

In Ontario, you must submit FSRA Form 5.2 within exactly 60 days of transferring funds from a LIRA to an Ontario Schedule 1.1 LIF. If you miss this 60-day window, your entire account remains locked until age 71 with annual withdrawal caps. Always obtain spousal waiver (Form 1.1) in advance.
