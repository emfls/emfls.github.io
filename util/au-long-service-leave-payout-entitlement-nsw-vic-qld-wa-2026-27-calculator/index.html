Which pay and service figures to use

Enter your actual part-time salary or weekly pay. With an hourly rate, the calculator multiplies that rate by your weekly LSL hours. Use your payroll-confirmed ordinary rate and hours where averaging applies. NSW can compare current pay with look-back averages, Victoria can compare 52-week, 260-week and whole-service averages, Queensland uses ordinary hours across mixed employment and WA can average hours by accrual period. This estimate applies one effective rate and one set of hours to the whole balance.

The estimate uses general state laws. Check your applicable award, agreement, portable scheme, industry rules and breaks in service. Queensland qualifying fixed-term expiry requires a reasonable expectation of reaching 10 years and willingness to continue. For other ambiguous reasons, ask payroll which state rule applies.

The PAYG estimate assumes 2026–27 payment, post-17 August 1993 accrual, an Australian resident with a TFN who claims the tax-free threshold, standard Scale 2 settings and an LSL-only lump sum. A combined unused annual leave payment, historic accrual, Medicare or withholding variation, or a different tax scale can change the result. The super comparison assumes all paid leave falls in 2026–27. PAYG is an estimate of tax withheld from this payment. Your annual assessment uses your full tax return.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get long service leave if I resign after seven years?

Yes in Victoria and generally in Western Australia. In NSW, a resignation between five and ten years requires illness, incapacity or a domestic or other pressing necessity. In Queensland, a resignation between seven and ten years requires illness or a domestic or other pressing necessity.

How do I calculate my part-time long service leave payout?

Multiply unused payable weeks by ordinary weekly pay. If paid hourly, use your ordinary hourly rate times your applicable weekly hours. If your roster changed or your pay varies, use the statutory average or ordinary pay figure confirmed by payroll.

How much tax is withheld from unused long service leave?

For normal termination with post-17 August 1993 accrual, Schedule 7 spreads the payment across annual pay periods to estimate the additional withholding. Genuine redundancy, ATO-qualifying invalidity and approved early retirement payments on that accrual use 32% withholding. Unused leave paid after death has zero PAYG withholding.

Do I get super and accrue other leave while taking long service leave?

Employers generally pay 12% super on qualifying paid LSL in 2026–27, within the annual contribution base. Eligible employees continue to accrue annual and sick or carer’s leave. Unused LSL paid at termination attracts no statutory super guarantee.

Why is paid leave available smaller than my termination balance?

NSW and WA can pay out qualifying pro-rata service before the next milestone for taking leave. Queensland can also do so before 15 years of service. The super estimate uses the remaining leave available to take while employed, after deducting leave already used.
