2026 Rental Property Depreciation Recapture Tax Calculator

Estimate federal and state taxes, cash after mortgage payoff, and potential 1031 deferral on a U.S. residential rental or commercial property sale.

Key takeaways

  • Building depreciation can become unrecaptured Section 1250 gain at sale, with a maximum federal rate of 25%.
  • Section 1245 recapture on qualifying cost-segregated assets is ordinary income and can reach 37%. Remaining long-term gain uses 0%, 15%, or 20% federal bands.
  • Budget for 3.8% NIIT where applicable and for state tax before estimating what you will take home.
Missed depreciation can still increase your tax bill

Your basis generally falls by the depreciation you were allowed to take, even if you skipped it on Schedule E. Review your depreciation schedule before closing. A qualifying Form 3115 correction may recover missed deductions through a Section 481(a) adjustment. IRS Publication 946

How the estimate works

Purchase price plus capital improvements, less allowed or allowable depreciation, gives adjusted basis. Net sale proceeds are the sale price less selling costs. The model allocates positive aggregate gain to recapture and appreciation, then applies federal rate bands, incremental NIIT, and your flat state rate. The mortgage payoff comes out of cash after the sale.

Estimated depreciation treats improvements as placed in service with the building and uses years held as depreciable time. Use your records for the IRS mid-month convention, improvements added later, and exact service dates. The model assumes all assets share the holding period. Enter a fraction above one year for a sale held longer than a year.

For filing, allocate basis and net proceeds among individual assets, including land and cost-segregated components. Some 15-year improvements have other classifications. Individual-asset gains can trigger recapture even when the overall sale has a loss. The planning estimate excludes loss benefits, other preferential gains, Section 1231 lookback, passive loss releases, AMT, and state-specific adjustments.

Frequently asked questions

Is all rental depreciation recapture taxed at 25%?

Unrecaptured Section 1250 gain from a straight-line building held more than one year has a 25% maximum federal rate. Section 1245 recapture on qualifying components is ordinary income and can reach 37% in 2026.

What if I never claimed depreciation on Schedule E?

Allowable depreciation generally still reduces your basis. Eligible method corrections may involve Form 3115 and a Section 481(a) catch-up adjustment. Confirm the correction and timing before filing.

Can I multiply all of my gain by 3.8% for NIIT?

NIIT is 3.8% of the lesser of net investment income or MAGI above the threshold. The threshold is $200,000 for single filers and $250,000 for married couples filing jointly.

What can still be taxable in a 1031 exchange?

A qualifying exchange can defer gain on investment or business real property. Cash, net debt relief, nonqualifying assets and special recapture rules can leave some gain taxable now.

Does paying off the mortgage reduce the taxable gain?

Mortgage payoff reduces cash you take home. In a conventional sale, gain is generally determined by net sale proceeds and adjusted tax basis, regardless of the loan payoff.
