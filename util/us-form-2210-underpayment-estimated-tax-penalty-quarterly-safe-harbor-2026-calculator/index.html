Form 2210 questions

How do I calculate my 2026 safe harbor?

Generally, take the lower of 90% of your 2026 tax or 100% of your eligible 2025 tax. Use 110% of prior-year tax if 2025 AGI exceeded $150,000, or $75,000 if filing separately in 2026. Under the regular method, one-fourth of the target is due on each installment deadline.

What counts toward the $1,000 exception?

Subtract federal withholding from your current-year tax after the applicable refundable credits. The remainder must be under $1,000. Estimated payments are excluded from this test.

How can December withholding reduce earlier penalties?

Under the default method, 25% of annual federal income tax withholding is credited on each installment deadline. Federal income tax actually withheld from a December paycheck or eligible IRA distribution is included in that annual total.

Can a January estimated payment erase an earlier shortfall?

A January estimated payment can pay down earlier shortfalls, starting with the oldest. The penalty for the days those installments were late still accrues through the payment date.

What if most of my income came late in 2026?

Form 2210 Schedule AI can reduce earlier required installments when income is uneven. This calculator uses equal required installments under the regular method. Use the IRS instructions to work through Schedule AI separately.
