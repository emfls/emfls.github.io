Estimate the tax on canceled student debt, the Form 982 insolvency exclusion, and how much to set aside each month.

Key takeaways

  • Income-driven repayment (IDR) forgiveness is generally taxable again in 2026. The temporary ARPA exclusion ended on December 31, 2025.
  • Qualifying PSLF, Teacher Loan Forgiveness, and death or disability discharges can yield $0 in federal tax on the canceled balance.
  • Form 982 can reduce taxable debt. Subtract the fair market value of all assets from total liabilities immediately before discharge. The exclusion is limited to the forgiven balance.

A forgiven balance can still leave a tax bill

Using the example below, a $75,000 IDR discharge adds $20,464 in estimated federal and state tax if you are solvent. A $30,000 insolvency exclusion lowers that bill to $12,000. With five years left, setting aside $200 a month would cover the lower estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Is IDR student loan forgiveness taxable in 2026?

Generally, yes. The temporary ARPA exclusion ended December 31, 2025. Qualifying IBR, PAYE or ICR balances canceled in 2026 generally produce ordinary cancellation-of-debt income unless another exclusion applies. If you receive Form 1099-C, review Box 2 and verify the effective discharge date.

Which student loan discharges remain federally tax-free?

Qualifying Public Service Loan Forgiveness and Teacher Loan Forgiveness remain federally excluded. The post-2025 death or total and permanent disability exclusion requires a Social Security number valid for employment, issued before the return due date and included on the return.

How do I measure insolvency for Form 982?

Use your financial position immediately before the discharge. Include the full forgiven loan in total liabilities. Include the fair market value of everything you own, including your home, vehicles, cash and retirement accounts. For this single discharge, the exclusion is the smaller of your forgiven balance and the amount by which liabilities exceed assets.

How do I report the insolvency exclusion?

Attach Form 982 to your federal return, check line 1b, and enter the excluded amount on line 2. Review Part II for any required reduction of tax attributes. Report any remaining taxable cancellation-of-debt income on your return and retain records supporting your assets and liabilities.

How are state tax and the monthly savings target estimated?

The state estimate applies your entered rate to the remaining federal taxable cancellation income. It assumes your state follows the same exclusions. Divide the combined tax by the number of full months left to set a savings target with no interest or existing savings. Future-year targets use 2026 rules as a baseline.
