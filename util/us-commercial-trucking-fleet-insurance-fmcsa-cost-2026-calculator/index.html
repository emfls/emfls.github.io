FMCSA Regulations & Commercial Fleet Insurance Dynamics

Commercial motor carriers operating across state lines are subject to strict federal financial responsibility requirements administered by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) under 49 CFR Part 387.

1. Primary Auto Liability & Form BMC-91X

  • Statutory Minimums: Non-hazardous property haulers exceeding 10,001 pounds Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) must file BMC-91X proof of at least $750,000 public liability. Hazardous commodities require either $1,000,000 or $5,000,000 depending on placarding.
  • Broker Market Reality: Virtually all major freight brokers, shippers, and 3PLs require a minimum of $1,000,000 Combined Single Limit (CSL) regardless of the $750,000 federal minimum. Carrying only $750,000 severely restricts load board access.

2. Essential Operational Extensions

A compliant carrier requires multi-layer protection beyond primary liability:

  1. Motor Truck Cargo (MTC): Protects against loss, damage, or theft of transported freight. The universal industry contract requirement is $100,000 per trailer.
  2. Physical Damage: Protects financed or owned tractors and trailers against collision, turnover, fire, and storm loss based on stated value.
  3. Bobtail / Non-Trucking Liability (NTL): Essential for owner-operators operating their tractor without a trailer or off-dispatch.
