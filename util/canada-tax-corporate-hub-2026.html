Frequently asked questions

How is new RRSP room estimated for 2026?

Start with 18% of your 2025 earned income, up to the 2026 annual dollar limit of $33,810. Your personal deduction limit can also reflect unused room and pension adjustments. Check your latest CRA notice of assessment or reassessment for your personal limit and available contribution room.

When is the RRSP contribution deadline for the 2026 tax year?

March 1, 2027 is the last day to make an RRSP contribution that can qualify for a deduction on your 2026 return. Report first-60-days contributions on your 2026 return even if you carry the deduction forward.

What are the standard CRA payroll late-remittance penalty rates?

The standard rates are 3% for 1–3 days late, 5% for 4–5 days late, 7% for 6–7 days late and 10% for more than 7 days late or no remittance. A payment received on time through the wrong method can also attract a 3% penalty.

When can the $500 threshold or a 20% payroll penalty apply?

For ordinary late or deficient payroll remittances, the $500 threshold limits the amount subject to penalty. The threshold can be lost if the failure was knowing or grossly negligent. A further failure in the same calendar year can attract a 20% penalty when it was knowing or grossly negligent and a penalty already applied earlier that year. CRA interest may also apply.
