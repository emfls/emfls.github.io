Business margin and break-even planner

Use GST-exclusive prices and costs. Enter fixed costs and expected sales for the same period.

Example: A$50 per sale minus A$30 in variable cost gives a 40% contribution margin. At A$1,000 in fixed costs, break-even is 50 units and projected operating profit at 80 units is A$600.

Operating profit before income tax equals unit contribution × expected units sold − fixed costs. If employer super is a fixed payroll cost for your scenario, include it in fixed costs.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Superannuation Guarantee rate for FY2026–27?

The general employer Superannuation Guarantee rate is 12% from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027. The transitional rate for Norfolk Island is 11% for that financial year.

Which earnings count towards Superannuation Guarantee?

Under Payday Super, use qualifying earnings. These include ordinary time earnings (OTE) and can also include amounts such as salary-sacrificed super contributions. The FY2026–27 annual maximum contribution base is A$270,830 per employee.

How do I calculate contribution margin?

Subtract variable cost per unit from selling price per unit to get the unit contribution. Divide the unit contribution by the selling price and multiply by 100 to get the contribution margin percentage.

How many units do I need to sell to break even?

When the unit contribution is positive, divide fixed costs by the unit contribution and round up to the next whole unit if needed. If the unit contribution is zero or negative, selling more units cannot cover positive fixed costs.
