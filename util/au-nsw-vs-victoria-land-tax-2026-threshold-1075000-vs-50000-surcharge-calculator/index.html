Key takeaways

At $900,000 of taxable land in each state, an individual under the general schedules pays $0 in NSW and $4,050 in Victoria before surcharges. The annual gap is $4,050.

NSW general land tax starts above $1,075,000. Victoria charges from $50,000 under its general schedule and $25,000 under the trust schedule. NSW special trusts have no general threshold.

Frequently asked questions

Which land values should I enter?

Enter the NSW assessed average unimproved value and the Victorian assessed site value of taxable holdings. Use the Victorian capital improved value, which includes buildings and improvements, for the separate vacancy tax.

Why can Victoria charge tax when NSW charges zero?

Under the general schedules, NSW starts charging above $1,075,000 and Victoria begins at $50,000. A taxable holding of $500,000 produces $0 general land tax in NSW and $1,950 in Victoria.

Do trusts receive a tax-free threshold?

NSW special trusts generally pay 1.6% from the first dollar of taxable land value, with 2% applying above the premium threshold. An eligible fixed trust can use the NSW general schedule. Victorian trusts assessed at trust rates start at $25,000 and use the general rate from $3 million.

Can a surcharge apply when general land tax is zero?

NSW can charge the 5% foreign surcharge on taxable residential land below its general threshold. Victoria's 4% absentee uplift starts at the relevant $50,000 general or $25,000 trust threshold. An existing home can also incur VRLT independently of ordinary land tax.

Is the 10-year difference a forecast?

The 10-year difference is the 2026 annual gap multiplied by ten. It holds tax schedules, valuations, ownership and vacancy tier constant. A property that stays liable for vacancy tax can move to a higher tier in later years.
