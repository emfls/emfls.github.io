How the comparison works

  • The lease finances the drive-away price less the estimated vehicle GST credit, with a GST-exclusive financed residual and a GST-inclusive buyout. The upfront credit models a taxable purchase and full pass-through from the lessor. GST on actual lease invoices follows separate rules. The running-cost credit is 10% of eligible GST-exclusive costs, or 1/11 of their GST-inclusive amount.
  • The model assumes an eligible employer arrangement, full-year private availability, monthly payments, and a single Australian resident with no dependants or other income-test adjustments. For taxable cars it models type 1 employer FBT passed through in pre-tax deductions without employee contributions. Check an actual quote for employer-specific limits or an employee contribution method.
  • The term estimate repeats the full-year 2026–27 results and includes the balloon. Actual FBT years end on 31 March, and first-year RFBA may be prorated. The model holds the quoted car base value, HELP balance cap and rates constant. It excludes the possible one-third FBT base reduction after four years, resale value and the opportunity cost of cash.
  • The taper scenario holds the quote constant and compares a qualifying commitment by 31 March 2027 with a new one from 1 April 2027 under the bill. The applicable LCT limit can change from July. Under the proposal, the 15% rate would extend to eligible cars at or below A$75,000 from 1 April 2029.

Frequently asked questions

Does the $91,661 threshold use the drive-away price?

Use the LCT value, including GST and dealer delivery. Take out quoted registration, stamp duty, compulsory third-party insurance and any LCT included in the drive-away price. Check that LCT has never been payable and that the car was first held and used on or after 1 July 2022.

Can a PHEV qualify after 1 April 2025?

Only a continuing arrangement can qualify. The PHEV's use must have been exempt before 1 April 2025, and a financially binding commitment already in place must cover continued private use. A renewal or change that creates a new commitment can end the exemption.

How can an FBT-exempt EV increase HELP or the Medicare levy surcharge?

The statutory estimate uses 20% of the car's FBT base value, grossed up by 1.8868, when the reportable value exceeds $2,000. Add the RFBA to taxable income after salary sacrifice for the income tests. Without qualifying hospital cover the single surcharge starts above $105,000 in 2026–27.

What changes under the proposed April 2027 EV taper?

Under the proposal, eligible new commitments from 1 April 2027 to 31 March 2029 would use a 15% statutory rate above $75,000 and within the applicable fuel-efficient LCT limit. Eligible cars at or below $75,000 would keep full relief until the next phase. Coverage for an existing commitment would continue under its previous treatment.

How much is the end-of-lease balloon?

The ATO minimum residual guidelines for a car with an eight-year effective life are 46.88% at three years, 37.50% at four years and 28.13% at five years. The calculator includes an estimated GST-inclusive buyout from after-tax pay so the lease and purchase comparisons both end with ownership.
