Key takeaways

Division 293 can apply when adjusted income plus low-tax super contributions exceeds A$250,000. The ATO charges 15% on the smaller of the amount above A$250,000 and your low-tax contributions.

The standard concessional cap rose from A$30,000 to A$32,500 on 1 July 2026. Eligible unused caps can raise your personal cap. Each dollar fully subject to Division 293 generally faces 30% in combined tax, 17 percentage points below the 47% top resident benchmark including the standard Medicare levy.

Frequently asked questions

How much Division 293 tax will I pay above A$250,000?

Compare the amount above A$250,000 with your low-tax contributions and multiply the smaller figure by 15%. For example, A$225,000 in Division 293 income and A$32,500 in low-tax contributions produce A$7,500 above the threshold and A$1,125 in Division 293 tax.

What counts as income for Division 293?

Start with taxable income and add reportable fringe benefits, net financial investment loss, net rental property loss and any net amount on which family trust distribution tax was paid. Subtract any assessable First Home Super Saver released amount and the qualifying zero-rated taxed element of a super lump sum.

Can unused cap from previous years trigger Division 293 tax?

Yes. If your total super balance was below A$500,000 on 30 June 2026, the ATO can automatically apply available unused cap from the previous five financial years once you exceed A$32,500. At the same adjusted income, the additional low-tax contributions can bring the combined amount above A$250,000.

What happens if my concessional contributions exceed my personal cap?

The ATO generally adds the excess to assessable income and applies a 15% tax offset for contributions tax already paid. The excess is excluded from the low-tax contribution base. Any assessed increase in taxable income can still affect your Division 293 calculation.

Can I pay Division 293 tax from my super?

Yes, where your fund can release the money. After receiving your notice, you can elect to release an amount from an eligible super account. The ATO issues a release authority to the nominated fund. Paying this way preserves cash today and reduces the amount held in super. Check your assessment for the payment due date.
