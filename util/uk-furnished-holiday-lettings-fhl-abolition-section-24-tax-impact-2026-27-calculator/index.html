Key takeaways

  • Abolition took effect on 6 April 2025 for Income Tax and CGT and 1 April 2025 for Corporation Tax. The 2026/27 tax year runs entirely under the new rules.
  • Individuals now calculate residential property profit before mortgage interest and claim a limited 20% finance cost tax reduction. The higher taxable profit can take you into the 40% band or reduce your Personal Allowance above £100,000.
  • Former FHL profits no longer count as relevant UK earnings for personal pension relief. A typical residential sale uses 18% and 24% CGT rates.

How the estimate works

Both income tax scenarios use 2026/27 bands, so the comparison isolates the change in mortgage interest treatment. Each scenario takes the tax on other income plus property profit and subtracts the tax on other income alone. The Section 24 credit then reduces the incremental income tax.

The calculation covers one individually owned UK residential property business with no brought-forward losses or finance costs. It excludes other income types, Gift Aid, pension tax relief from the income tax total, and the Child Benefit charge from cash results. The company estimate assumes a full accounting year, no associated companies and no dividend income in the company. It excludes dividend extraction, incorporation and transaction taxes, and company gains on sale.

Frequently asked questions

When did the furnished holiday lettings regime end?

It ended on 6 April 2025 for Income Tax and Capital Gains Tax and on 1 April 2025 for Corporation Tax. A former qualifying FHL normally joins the owner's existing UK or overseas property business.

Why can I owe more income tax even if my mortgage and rent stay unchanged?

Section 24 calculates residential rental profit before mortgage interest. The finance cost credit is limited to 20% of the eligible amount, which can leave more tax due at higher rates and reduce your Personal Allowance if adjusted net income exceeds £100,000.

Can I still get tax relief on pension contributions funded by holiday let profits?

Former FHL profit no longer adds to relevant UK earnings for personal pension tax relief. Other qualifying earnings may support contributions, and eligible individuals may still qualify for basic-rate relief on up to £3,600 gross under the low-earnings rule.

Does selling my former FHL still qualify for Business Asset Disposal Relief?

A sale of an ongoing former FHL generally falls under standard residential CGT at 18% and 24%. Transitional relief can still apply where a qualifying FHL business actually ceased before 6 April 2025 and the disposal meets the remaining conditions.

How should I interpret the limited company SPV comparison?

The company benchmark deducts qualifying mortgage interest in full and applies Corporation Tax to the resulting profit. Retained company cash is before shareholder extraction taxes and ignores the cost of transferring an existing property into a company.
