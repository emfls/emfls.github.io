How the estimate works

  1. Multiply the fuel multiplier by the company car’s BiK percentage and the qualifying months divided by 12. Apply your marginal tax rate and the employer’s Class 1A rate separately to that benefit value.
  2. Divide 4.546 litres by imperial MPG and multiply by the pump price in pounds per litre. Multiply that cost per mile by annual private mileage to get the annual pump bill.
  3. Assume private miles are spread evenly across the year. Subtract employee tax from fuel costs avoided during coverage. Divide employee tax by pump cost per mile to find break-even miles during coverage, then annualise using the same coverage fraction.

If your driving is seasonal, compare private miles actually driven during coverage with the covered-period threshold. This estimate uses one marginal tax rate. The actual tax can differ if the benefit crosses bands, affects allowances, or is provided through salary sacrifice.

HMRC uses qualifying days for part-year reductions. Starting a fuel benefit late by itself does not qualify. A withdrawal reduction requires that private fuel stays withdrawn for that car for the remainder of the tax year.

Frequently asked questions

How is company car fuel benefit calculated in 2026/27?

Multiply £29,200 by the company car’s appropriate BiK percentage. At 30%, the full-year fuel benefit is £8,760. A 40% taxpayer would pay £3,504 if the whole benefit falls in that band, and the employer would owe £1,314 in Class 1A National Insurance.

How do I avoid a fuel benefit charge when repaying private fuel?

You must be required to repay the full cost and do so by 6 July following the tax year. Record all private mileage, including ordinary commuting. The applicable HMRC Advisory Fuel Rate or higher can be used with accurate records. A lower rate can qualify if it demonstrably covers the full cost.

Does charging a pure electric company car trigger car fuel benefit?

Electricity is outside the car fuel benefit rules. Charging a pure electric company car therefore produces £0 car fuel benefit. Separate company car BiK can still apply. Private petrol or diesel supplied for a plug-in hybrid can trigger a fuel benefit charge.

Does taking free fuel for fewer months reduce the charge?

HMRC uses qualifying days. A reduction can apply when private fuel is withdrawn and stays withdrawn for the rest of the tax year, or when the car is unavailable for a qualifying period. Starting private fuel late by itself does not qualify, and restarting it for the same car in the same tax year can remove the withdrawal reduction.

How many private miles make employer-paid fuel worthwhile?

Divide the employee income tax on the benefit by the pump cost per mile to find the miles needed while fuel is covered. At £3,504 tax, 40 UK MPG and 145p per litre, full-year break-even is about 21,263 miles. Employer Class 1A National Insurance is a separate cost.
