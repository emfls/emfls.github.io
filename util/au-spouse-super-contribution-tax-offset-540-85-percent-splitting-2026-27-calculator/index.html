Australia Spouse Super Tax Offset & Splitting Calculator

Estimate the $540 tax offset and 85% contribution split for 2026–27. Compare both partners’ super balances with and without splitting.

Key takeaways

  • An eligible $3,000 after-tax spouse contribution can give the payer a $540 tax offset when the recipient’s income for the offset is $37,000 or less. The maximum falls to $270 at $38,500 and reaches $0 at $40,000.
  • With fund approval, up to 85% of eligible taxed concessional contributions can be transferred to a spouse, limited by the sender’s applicable concessional cap. At the $32,500 standard cap, the split can be $27,625.
  • The 2026–27 general transfer balance cap is $2.1 million. The first Division 296 threshold is $3 million of total super balance per person.

Check what you could lose before contributing

Exceeding the recipient’s after-tax cap can remove the entire offset and lead to extra tax. The offset is non-refundable and only reduces available income tax liability. Money paid into super is generally preserved until a release condition is met.

      Frequently asked questions

      How is the $540 spouse super tax offset calculated?

      Multiply the smaller of your eligible after-tax contribution and the income-adjusted contribution base by 18%. At $38,500 of spouse income, the base is $1,500 and the maximum offset is $270. The offset can only reduce available income tax liability.

      Which income and balance figures count for the offset?

      Use the receiving spouse’s assessable income excluding assessable First Home Super Saver releases, plus their reportable fringe benefits and reportable employer super adjusted for excess concessional contributions. Use total super across all funds on 30 June 2026. The calculator covers contributions before age 75. Ask the fund about the short acceptance window after a 75th birthday.

      Does splitting use the receiving spouse’s contribution cap?

      No. The split is a rollover. The original contributions count toward the sender’s concessional cap. The maximum taxed split is the lesser of 85% of eligible contributions and the sender’s applicable cap. Funds can choose whether to offer splitting.

      When can I split my 2026–27 contributions?

      Usually apply to your fund in 2027–28. An application in the contribution year is possible if the entire benefit will be withdrawn, rolled over or transferred before that year ends. At application the recipient must be under 60, or aged 60 to 64 and unretired.

      How do the $2.1 million and $3 million thresholds differ?

      The $2.1 million general transfer balance cap limits transfers into the retirement phase. Your personal cap may be lower. For 2026–27 the first Division 296 threshold looks at year-end total super balance and can apply an additional 15% tax to the relevant share of earnings when balance exceeds $3 million.
