Canada EI Clawback Calculator 2026

Estimate your Employment Insurance repayment and see how claiming an RRSP deduction could change what you owe.

Key takeaways

  • The 2026 threshold is $86,125. If you owe a repayment, it is 30% of the smaller of your regular and fishing EI paid in 2026 and your income above that threshold.
  • Special benefits are exempt. The 10-year exemption also applies if you were paid less than one week of regular or fishing EI in 2016–2025.
  • An RRSP deduction can reduce the repayment. The amount needed to reach the threshold is the excess income, provided you can deduct that much for 2026.

How the RRSP savings estimate works

The model subtracts the tested RRSP deduction from repayment income and calculates EI repayment again. A small deduction can leave the EI bill unchanged when regular benefits already cap the repayment. Your unused room may carry forward even though this scenario uses the annual $33,810 limit as its cap.

Combined savings equal the EI repayment avoided plus the estimated income tax savings. When repayment falls, its line 23500 deduction falls too. The model therefore applies your marginal rate to the RRSP deduction minus the repayment avoided. With the pre-filled figures, a $9,875 deduction saves $2,962.50 of EI repayment and $2,350.25 of income tax, for $5,312.75 in estimated current-year savings.

Report any applicable income adjustments using your T4E chart. If you also have OAS repayment, use the Federal Worksheet. Actual tax savings can vary with tax brackets, credits and benefits. The scenario excludes future tax on RRSP withdrawals.

Frequently asked questions

How much EI do I repay in 2026?

If the repayment rule applies, you repay 30% of the smaller of your regular and fishing EI paid in 2026 and the part of your repayment income above $86,125. At exactly $86,125, the repayment is zero.

How does the 10-year first-time claimant exemption work?

You are exempt for 2026 if you were paid less than one week of regular or fishing EI over tax years 2016 through 2025. A claim spanning two years can qualify in the first year and lose the exemption in the second if at least one week was paid in the earlier year.

Are maternity, parental, sickness and caregiver benefits clawed back?

Special EI benefits are exempt, including maternity, parental, sickness, compassionate care and family caregiver benefits. If you received both regular and special EI, only the regular or fishing portion may be subject to repayment. Taxable special benefits still count toward income.

Where does EI repayment go on my tax return?

T4E box 7 shows the repayment rate. Use the T4E repayment chart or the Federal Worksheet as applicable. The repayment is deductible on line 23500 and is included in tax payable through line 42200.

Can an RRSP contribution eliminate my EI repayment?

A deductible RRSP contribution claimed for 2026 can eliminate the repayment if it reduces repayment income to $86,125 or below. Check your CRA assessment for available room. The $33,810 annual dollar limit applies to newly earned 2026 room, and unused room may carry forward. Income tax savings are smaller when the RRSP deduction replaces part of the EI repayment deduction.
