Texas homestead FAQ

How much is the Texas school homestead exemption in 2026?

A qualifying residence homestead gets a $140,000 school district exemption. A qualifying homeowner age 65 or older or with a disability receives $60,000 more, for a total of $200,000 before any optional school exemption.

When does the 10% appraisal cap start?

It starts on January 1 of the tax year after the owner first qualifies for a homestead exemption. Once active, the appraised value is limited to the lower of market value or last year's appraised value plus 10% and qualifying new improvements. Repairs and ordinary maintenance generally do not count.

Can my school tax change after I qualify for a ceiling?

The school ceiling limits annual school tax dollars while you qualify. The amount you owe can fall below the ceiling. Qualifying improvements or statutory adjustments can change the ceiling, so use the current amount from your tax office. Local taxing units can have separate rules.

How do local optional exemptions affect my tax bill?

Each taxing unit can adopt its own percentage exemption up to 20%, with a $5,000 minimum when adopted. A unit with no percentage exemption uses 0%. Enter the rates and exemptions for the individual units on your tax statement.

Who qualifies for the total disabled veteran homestead exemption?

Texas Tax Code Section 11.131 provides a full residence homestead exemption for a veteran receiving VA compensation at the 100% rate for a service-connected disability, with a 100% disability or individual unemployability rating. Confirm eligibility with your appraisal district.
