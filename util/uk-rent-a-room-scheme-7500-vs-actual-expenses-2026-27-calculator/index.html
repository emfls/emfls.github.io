Rent a Room questions

What counts towards the Rent a Room limit?

Add your share of the rent and payments you receive for bills or provided services, such as meals, cleaning and laundry. HMRC also counts relevant balancing charges. The annual limit is £7,500 if you are the sole recipient or £3,750 per recipient when the income is shared.

Do I need Self Assessment at or below the limit?

Qualifying room income at or below your limit is automatically exempt and does not by itself require a return. You may still need to file for another reason. If you want to opt out to record a loss, contact HMRC or make the election through the appropriate return entries.

When do actual expenses beat the flat allowance?

Above the receipts limit, actual expenses reduce taxable profit more when they exceed your allowance. Equal expenses and allowance give equal taxable profit. HMRC defaults to actual profit (Method A) above the limit unless a flat allowance election (Method B) applies.

Which costs are allowable under Method A?

Include the attributable letting share of eligible utilities, Council Tax, insurance, repairs and letting fees. Domestic item replacements may qualify when you use actual expenses and meet HMRC conditions, including exclusive use by the lessee. Initial purchases, personal costs and capital improvements are excluded.

What happens if I make a loss?

Actual expenses can create a property loss that can generally reduce future profits of the same property business, including future taxable excess under Method B. Within the exemption limit, elect out for that tax year to record an eligible loss. A carried loss gives no immediate cash refund in this estimate.
