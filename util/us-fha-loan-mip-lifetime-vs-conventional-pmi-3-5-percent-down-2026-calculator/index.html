🎯 The "FHA Refinance-Out" Strategic Window

Because FHA MIP lasts for the entire 30 years when putting down < 10%, most savvy homeowners use FHA for its lower interest rate and flexible credit underwriting, then refinance into a Conventional loan once equity hits 20% (80% LTV). ⚠️ Warning: The FHA 10% Down Payment Cliff

If you put down 10.0% or more on an FHA loan, annual MIP automatically cancels after 11 years. However, if you put down 9.9% or less (such as the standard 3.5%), MIP is locked for the entire 30-year duration. Never stay in an FHA loan for 30 years without evaluating refinancing once your equity reaches 20%.
