2026 NIIT & Additional Medicare Tax Calculator

Estimate your 3.8% Net Investment Income Tax and 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax using the income each tax covers.

Key takeaways

  • 3.8% NIIT applies to the smaller of net investment income and modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) above the filing threshold.
  • 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax applies to Medicare wages plus net self-employment earnings above the filing threshold.
  • For joint filers both thresholds are $250,000. Qualifying surviving spouses use $250,000 for NIIT and $200,000 for Additional Medicare Tax.

Avoid a surprise at filing

Two spouses who each earn $150,000 in Medicare wages can owe $450 of Additional Medicare Tax on a joint return. Their combined wages exceed the $250,000 threshold by $50,000, while each employer withholds the surtax only after paying its employee more than $200,000.

A taxable traditional IRA withdrawal can also raise MAGI above the NIIT threshold. For example, $10,000 of additional MAGI above the threshold can expose up to $10,000 of existing investment income to NIIT, adding up to $380.

What counts as investment income?

Include taxable interest and dividends, eligible net capital gains, rents, royalties, passive business income and other eligible investment income. Subtract only allowable deductions properly allocable to investment income. Exclude federally tax-exempt municipal bond interest, the Section 121 excluded portion of home-sale gains, Social Security benefits, and 401(k), IRA and Roth IRA distributions. Taxable distributions and taxable Social Security benefits can still affect MAGI.

Frequently asked questions

How is the 3.8% NIIT calculated?

Multiply 3.8% by the smaller of your net investment income and the amount of MAGI above your filing threshold. Either amount can bring the tax base to zero.

Can a traditional IRA withdrawal trigger NIIT?

The taxable portion of a traditional IRA withdrawal generally raises MAGI. This can make more of your separate investment income subject to NIIT even though the IRA withdrawal itself is excluded from net investment income.

Does NIIT apply to municipal bonds or the sale of a home?

Federally tax-exempt municipal bond interest and home-sale gains excluded under Section 121 are outside net investment income. Include otherwise eligible taxable gains, including taxable gains from selling investments or the taxable portion of a home-sale gain.

Why can a two-earner couple owe Additional Medicare Tax with no surtax withheld?

Each employer withholds the extra 0.9% on wages it pays an employee above $200,000. Two spouses earning $150,000 each have $300,000 in combined Medicare wages. On a joint return they owe $450 of Additional Medicare Tax on the $50,000 above their $250,000 threshold.

How can a 401(k) or HSA contribution reduce NIIT?

Eligible pre-tax 401(k) deferrals and eligible HSA contributions can lower NIIT MAGI. A lower MAGI saves NIIT when it lowers the smaller of net investment income and MAGI above the threshold. At or below the threshold, NIIT is zero.
