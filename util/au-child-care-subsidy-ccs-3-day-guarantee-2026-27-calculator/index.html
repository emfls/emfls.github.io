Australia Child Care Subsidy Calculator 2026–27

Estimate what you’ll pay after CCS and compare the cost of a fourth or fifth day of care under the 3 Day Guarantee.

Key takeaways

  • The 3 Day Guarantee gives eligible families at least 72 subsidised hours per child per fortnight. Six 12-hour sessions use the full allowance.
  • Standard CCS is 90% up to A$88,520 of family adjusted taxable income and falls to 0% at A$538,520. Eligible younger siblings can receive up to 95%.
  • CCS applies to the lower of your hourly fee and the cap. The default 5% withholding increases the bill you pay during the year.

Child Care Subsidy FAQs

Does the 3 Day Guarantee make child care free?

No. Eligible families can use at least 72 subsidised hours each fortnight. That covers six 12-hour bookings, with a gap fee usually still payable. Families arrange a place directly with their provider.

Who qualifies for 100 subsidised hours?

A single parent, or both parents in a couple, can qualify by each doing more than 48 hours of recognised participation per fortnight. Valid exemptions and other eligibility pathways also apply, including 100 hours for an Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander child.

Does my second or third child receive 95% CCS?

An eligible younger child aged 5 or younger can receive 95% when the family has at least two eligible children in that age group and family ATI is at most $146,437. A separate income schedule applies above that threshold. At $370,727 or more, the standard rate applies to every child. In Home Care uses the standard rate per family.

Why compare my provider’s hourly fee with the rate cap?

CCS applies to the lower of the provider’s hourly fee and the cap for that care category. To find the hourly fee, divide the daily charge by the booked session length. Any amount above the cap adds to your gap.

Will I get the withheld 5% back?

Services Australia generally withholds 5% to reduce the chance of an overpayment. At balancing it recalculates your entitlement using your actual income. The withheld amount may offset an overpayment or be paid to you if money is owed.
