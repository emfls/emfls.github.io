Avoid an excess transfer and a missed minimum

Check your personal cap and available space in ATO online services before moving money. If you go over your cap, the ATO can require you to commute the excess and notional earnings. Excess transfer balance tax is 15% the first time and 30% for repeat excesses, applied to the notional earnings.

Check the annual minimum with your fund before 30 June. A shortfall can cost the fund its tax exemption for the affected pension earnings, subject to ATO exceptions. Retirement-phase investment earnings are generally tax-free at fund level. Accumulation earnings are generally taxed at 15%.

Frequently asked questions

Who gets the full A$2.1 million cap in 2026–27?

If your first retirement-phase income stream starts between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027, your personal cap starts at A$2.1 million. An earlier retirement-phase income stream means your own cap history determines how much indexation you receive.

How does the ATO calculate proportional indexation?

Divide your highest ever end-of-day transfer balance by the personal cap on the first day you reached that balance. Round the percentage used down to a whole number and subtract it from 100. Apply the unused percentage to the A$100,000 increase. If you ever reached or exceeded the cap that applied at the time, you receive zero indexation.

Can my existing pension balance be higher than my personal cap?

Yes. Investment growth can take a pension balance above the cap without creating an excess. The ATO uses the credits and debits in your transfer balance account to check the cap. The fund's actual pension balance determines the minimum drawdown.

How is my minimum rounded and what if I start after 1 July?

For a full year, multiply your 1 July balance by the rate for your age on that date. Round to the nearest A$10, rounding A$5 ties up. A later start normally uses your age and balance at commencement and prorates for the days remaining, including the start date. A pension started on or after 1 June generally has no minimum for that year. This calculator models a full year.

When are pension payments tax-free?

Account-based pension payments are generally tax-free from age 60 if paid from a taxed super fund. An untaxed fund element may be taxable, and eligibility to access super has separate rules. The monthly and fortnightly amounts are averages of the annual minimum, and your fund sets the actual payment schedule.
