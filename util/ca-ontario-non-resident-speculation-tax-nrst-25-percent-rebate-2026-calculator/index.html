    Frequently asked questions

    Does a 1% foreign ownership share trigger the full Ontario NRST?

    Yes, when the transfer is taxable. Ontario applies the 25% rate to the entire applicable value of consideration, even if a foreign national takes just 1% of title with a Canadian sibling or friend.

    When can I claim the Ontario spousal exemption at closing?

    Both spouses must be named on title at registration. The qualifying spouse can be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident, eligible Ontario nominee or protected person. All purchasers must meet the principal-residence certification rule and any additional purchasers must meet Ontario’s eligibility rules. Ontario’s definition of spouse also includes certain common-law partners.

    What are the four-year Ontario permanent resident rebate requirements?

    The foreign national must pay the tax, receive permanent resident status within four years of registration and be on title alone or exclusively with their spouse. The purchasers must move in within 60 days and maintain the property as their principal residence through filing or until the rebate conditions are met, whichever is later. The Ministry generally must receive the application within 180 days of PR status, subject to an outside limit of four years and 180 days from when the tax became payable.

    Which transfer taxes stack in Toronto in 2026?

    Toronto adds its municipal land transfer tax to Ontario land transfer tax. For eligible foreign-buyer transactions, the separate 10% Toronto Municipal Non-Resident Speculation Tax also stacks with Ontario’s 25% NRST. Toronto changed the municipal land transfer tax rates above $3 million on April 1, 2026 for land with one or two single-family residences.

    Does a full provincial rebate cover financing interest and Toronto tax?

    A successful Ontario claim returns the provincial NRST paid. Borrowing costs remain payable to the lender. Toronto administers its own MNRST rebate with a separate application and a 90-day filing deadline after permanent residency.
