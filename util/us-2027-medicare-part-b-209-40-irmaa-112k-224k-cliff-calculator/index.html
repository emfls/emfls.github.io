Why one extra dollar can cost $2,378.40

On a joint return, $224,000 of 2025 MAGI leaves both spouses in the standard 2027 tier. At $224,001, each spouse with both Parts B and D pays $99.10 more per month. Over 12 months, that adds up to $2,378.40 for the couple. The first individual cliff works the same way at $112,000.

Check the income year on your SSA notice and include tax-exempt interest when totaling MAGI. For a tax year you can still plan, compare the one-tier premium savings with the value and tax impact of shifting discretionary income, such as a Roth conversion or taxable gain. The top tier starts at exactly $500,000 for individual schedules and $750,000 for joint filers.

Request a lower IRMAA with Form SSA-44

If you received an IRMAA notice and a qualifying life-changing event changed your income or filing status, gather proof of the event and the tax return or estimate for the more recent year. Work stoppage, fewer work hours, marriage and the death of a spouse are qualifying event categories. If both spouses received notices, each can contact SSA about their own determination.

The estimates assume 12 months of the selected Part B and Part D coverage. Deductibles, late-enrollment penalties, premium reductions and other plan costs are separate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the standard Medicare Part B premium in 2027?

The standard 2027 Part B premium is $209.40 per month per beneficiary. The Part B deductible is $292 per year, and the Part A inpatient deductible is $1,776 per benefit period.

Which income determines my 2027 IRMAA?

Social Security generally uses your 2025 tax return for 2027 premiums, or 2024 information if 2025 information is unavailable. Medicare MAGI is adjusted gross income plus tax-exempt interest, including tax-exempt municipal bond interest.

What does $1 over the first 2027 IRMAA threshold cost?

Moving from $112,000 to $112,001 on an individual return, or $224,000 to $224,001 on a joint return, adds $83.70 per month for Part B and $15.40 for Part D if enrolled. Over 12 months that is $1,189.20 per person with both, or $2,378.40 for two people with both.

Can I use Form SSA-44 after retiring or losing a spouse?

If work stoppage, work reduction, marriage, death of a spouse, or another qualifying event lowered your income or changed your filing status, ask Social Security to use more recent tax information with Form SSA-44. Provide evidence of the event and your updated income. Social Security decides whether to revise your premium.

Is the Part D IRMAA amount my drug plan premium?

Part D IRMAA is a separate monthly charge added to the premium set by your drug plan. In 2027 the add-on ranges from $0 to $96.40 per enrolled beneficiary. Enter your plan premium to include it in your estimate.
