Frequently asked questions

How is Northern Territory stamp duty calculated?

Use the higher of the purchase price and market value. Up to A$525,000, divide that value by 1,000 to get V and apply D = 0.06571441 × V² + 15 × V. Above A$525,000, the applicable flat rate multiplies the full dutiable value.

What happens at exactly A$3 million and A$5 million?

At A$3 million, the rate rises to 5.75% and duty reaches A$172,500. At A$5 million, the rate rises to 5.95% and duty reaches A$297,500. Each rate applies to the full dutiable value.

Does the A$50,000 HomeGrown Territory Grant have a property price cap?

There is no purchase or build price cap. Eligible first-home buyers can buy off the plan, purchase a new home or build one. The qualifying contract or owner-builder foundation start must fall within 1 October 2024 to 30 September 2027 inclusive. A continuous 12 months of principal residence is required.

Who can apply for the A$30,000 FreshStart New Home Grant?

Eligible current or previous homeowners can apply when buying or building a new NT home. The same transaction window and 12-month principal residence condition apply. FreshStart applications are due by 31 December 2027. The two grants cannot be combined.

Can I count the grant as cash at settlement?

Confirm timing with your lender or the Territory Revenue Office. Direct applications are generally paid after settlement for a purchase or after foundations are laid for a build. The calculator subtracts duty from the grant and excludes the deposit, legal fees and other costs.
