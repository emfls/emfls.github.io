    Payroll tax FAQs

    How does single-state comparison work?

    Each row assumes all of the same Australia-wide wages are taxable in that state. The relocation gap is the highest estimated annual tax less the lowest across those scenarios.

    How are deductions apportioned for an interstate group?

    Use group-wide taxable wages to set the rate and available deduction. The calculator multiplies that deduction by the state's share of the group's national payroll. A designated group employer may claim it on the group's behalf.

    What taxable wages should I enter?

    Enter annual wages taxable under each state's rules, including taxable employer super contributions. The 12% super guarantee applies to qualifying earnings subject to ATO rules. Include the actual taxable contributions in your input.

    How do the Victorian surcharges work?

    Mental health and COVID debt each add 0.5% on the Victorian share of Australian wages above $10 million, plus another 0.5% on the Victorian share above $100 million. The regional rate applies to base payroll tax.

    Can I use the monthly estimate on my return?

    The monthly figure is one twelfth of the annual estimate and assumes even payroll for budgeting. Calculate each return using actual wages, the applicable period thresholds and your revenue office's lodgement rules.
