Key takeaways

  • Deduct up to $10,000 per tax return, per year in qualified interest for 2025–2028. You can take the standard deduction.
  • To qualify, buy a new, U.S.-assembled vehicle for primarily personal use with a first-lien purchase loan originated after 2024.
  • The IRS reduces capped interest by $200 for every $1,000 or fraction above $100,000 MAGI ($200,000 on a joint return).

How the estimate works

Interest is modeled on a fixed-rate, fully amortizing loan with equal monthly periods, starting one month after origination. The model uses APR as the interest rate and excludes fees, daily interest, extra payments, lender rounding and refinancing. Use your lender’s reported interest when filing.

All four years use 2026 ordinary federal income tax brackets and base standard deductions. The model treats MAGI as AGI for the tax estimate and calculates savings from the tax difference before and after the deduction, including any bracket crossings. Credits, state tax, preferential income rates and other deductions are outside the estimate.

Effective after-tax APR is 12 times the monthly discount rate that equates the amount financed to the present value of the loan payments less estimated tax savings. The model credits each year’s tax savings in December. Actual savings timing depends on withholding and filing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I deduct auto loan interest with the standard deduction?

Yes. Claim qualified interest in Part IV of Schedule 1-A (Form 1040) for tax years 2025 through 2028. You can use Schedule 1-A whether you take the standard deduction or itemize.

Do used cars, leases or vehicles assembled abroad qualify?

Used-car purchases, leases and vehicles finally assembled outside the United States are ineligible. A qualifying new car, minivan, SUV, pickup or motorcycle must be primarily designed for public roads, have GVWR under 14,000 pounds and be financed for predominantly personal use. Verify the final assembly location using the VIN or vehicle label.

How does the MAGI phaseout affect a deduction smaller than $10,000?

First limit qualified interest to $10,000. Then reduce that amount by $200 per $1,000 or part of $1,000 above $100,000 MAGI, or $200,000 for joint filers. For example, $7,000 in qualifying interest at $124,200 single MAGI yields a $2,000 deduction.

What documents do I need to claim the deduction?

Report the vehicle identification number (VIN) on Schedule 1-A and keep your loan and interest records. From 2026, lenders generally report $600 or more of interest on a specified loan using Form 1098-VLI. The $600 amount is a lender reporting threshold.

How much does a $10,000 deduction save in federal tax?

If all $10,000 offsets ordinary taxable income in the 22% bracket, the federal tax reduction is $2,200. If the deduction crosses into a lower bracket, the portion in that bracket saves tax at the lower rate.
