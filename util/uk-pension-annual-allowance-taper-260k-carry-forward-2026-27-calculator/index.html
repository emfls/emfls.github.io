Key takeaways

The £60,000 allowance tapers only when threshold income exceeds £200,000 and adjusted income exceeds £260,000. It falls to £10,000 at £360,000 of adjusted income if both tests are met. Use this year’s allowance first, then the oldest eligible balance. The 2023/24 balance expires after 5 April 2027.

Pension annual allowance FAQ

When does the tapered annual allowance apply?

Both tests must be met. Threshold income must exceed £200,000 and adjusted income must exceed £260,000. The £60,000 allowance drops by £1 per full £2 above the adjusted-income threshold, reaching £10,000 at £360,000.

How do I calculate threshold income and adjusted income?

For the common arrangements shown here, threshold income is HMRC net income less gross relief-at-source payments plus relevant post-8 July 2015 salary sacrifice or flexible remuneration. Adjusted income starts from HMRC net income separately and adds back net-pay contributions and employer pension saving, including salary sacrifice already counted in that saving. A defined-benefit pension input is generally included after subtracting any member contributions already included in that input.

Which unused pension allowance expires on 5 April 2027?

Any remaining 2023/24 allowance expires at the end of 5 April 2027. Use the 2026/27 allowance first, then eligible balances from 2023/24, 2024/25 and 2025/26 in that order. Each prior year requires membership in a UK registered or qualifying overseas scheme, and balances must account for any carry-forward already used.

Does carry-forward increase my personal tax-relief limit?

Personal contribution relief generally depends on 100% of relevant UK earnings in 2026/27. Eligible members under 75 with lower earnings can receive relief on up to £3,600 gross through relief at source. Employer contributions have no personal earnings cap and count towards annual allowance. The theoretical £240,000 annual allowance total assumes four untapered years and all three prior balances intact.

Can I use carry-forward after flexibly accessing my pension?

Unused allowance cannot increase the £10,000 money purchase annual allowance for post-access defined-contribution savings. Eligible unused allowance can still cover other pension inputs under the alternative annual allowance test, which matters when you have defined-benefit accrual or made contributions before first access. The charge uses the larger excess from the applicable tests.
