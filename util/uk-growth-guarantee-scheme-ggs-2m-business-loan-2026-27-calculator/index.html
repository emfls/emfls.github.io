UK Growth Guarantee Scheme (GGS) Underwriting Guide

The Growth Guarantee Scheme (GGS) launched by the British Business Bank in July 2024 (and continuing through 2026) is designed to help small and medium-sized UK businesses access debt finance for working capital, capital investment, and expansion.

1. Key Scheme Mechanics

  • 70% Guarantee to the Lender: The government covers 70% of the lender's outstanding balance if the business fails. This unlocks credit for businesses that may lack sufficient collateral.
  • Facility Caps: Maximum £2,000,000 per business group (or £1,000,000 for businesses operating under the Northern Ireland Protocol).
  • Turnover Threshold: Maximum annual group turnover of £45,000,000.
  • Max Terms: Up to 6 years for term loans and asset finance; up to 3 years for revolving credit facilities and invoice discounting.
