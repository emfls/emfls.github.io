What you could give up by refinancing

Replacing a low-rate first mortgage gives up that rate on the full balance. A longer term can lower the monthly payment while raising total interest and leaving more debt when you sell. A variable-rate HELOC can get more expensive, and its payment can rise when the draw period ends. Both loans put your home at risk if you miss payments.

How the estimates work

The opening blended note rate weights each note rate by principal. Modeled APR annualizes the monthly rate that equates scheduled payments to starting principal less closing costs. A lender’s disclosed APR can differ, especially for a variable-rate HELOC. Payments exclude property taxes and insurance.

The Schedule A estimate assumes one home and a fully qualifying first-mortgage balance. It applies the cap to monthly opening balances, giving the first-mortgage portion priority, and assumes the existing remaining term is the original remaining term for older refinanced debt. Publication 936 uses tax-year average balances and its worksheet can give a different result.

Estimated savings multiply deductible interest by your entered marginal rate. Actual savings depend on the standard deduction, other itemized deductions and applicable limits. This estimate excludes points, mortgage insurance and separate business or investment deductions.

Lender LTV and CLTV limits are underwriting assumptions. For the HELOC, the requested cash also represents the credit limit used in the comparison.

Frequently asked questions

Is HELOC interest deductible in 2026?

You may deduct qualifying interest as home mortgage interest on Schedule A if you itemize and use the proceeds to buy, build, or substantially improve the qualified home that secures the loan. Interest on proceeds used for credit card debt, tuition, a car, or investments does not qualify as a home mortgage interest deduction.

Does the $1 million limit apply to my new HELOC or cash-out proceeds?

Generally, no. Eligible acquisition debt from on or before December 15, 2017 can retain the $1 million limit ($500,000 for married filing separately), subject to the refinancing rules. New acquisition debt falls under the $750,000 limit ($375,000 for married filing separately), and older qualifying debt reduces room under that limit.

How does a cash-out refinance affect deductible interest?

Replacement principal can retain the acquisition purpose of the balance it pays off. The additional cash must separately satisfy the use-of-funds rule. The earlier loan date can carry over for qualifying replacement principal through the original loan term, subject to special rules.

Why can a higher-rate HELOC cost less than a lower-rate refinance?

With a HELOC, the new rate applies to the amount you draw. Refinancing applies a new rate to the whole replacement loan, including your existing balance, and can restart the repayment term. Compare cumulative interest and fees, and check how much principal remains at each horizon.

When are the extra closing costs recovered?

At the first month when cumulative after-tax interest savings cover the higher upfront fees. The comparison uses interest plus upfront fees minus modeled federal tax savings. It also displays each balance so you can see how much principal is still due.
