Frequently asked questions

What are the Social Security benefit percentages at 62, 67 and 70?

For this birth cohort they are 70%, 100% and 124% of the full retirement age amount. SSA reduces the first 36 early months by 5/9 of 1% per month and any additional early months by 5/12 of 1%. Delayed credits add 2/3 of 1% per month through 70.

When do the two comparisons break even with no COLA or discounting?

With a $2,000 full retirement age benefit, 62 vs 67 breaks even at 78 years 8 months, and 67 vs 70 at 82 years 6 months. The model compares monthly payments rounded to cents.

How do COLA and the discount rate work?

Starting amounts use a common age-62 basis. The same assumed COLA applies to all options each year from age 63. Present-value mode discounts each year’s monthly payments back to age 62 in annual steps.
