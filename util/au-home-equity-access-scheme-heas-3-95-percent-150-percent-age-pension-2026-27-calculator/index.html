How the projection works

Each loan charges interest after that fortnight’s draws, using its annual rate divided by 26. Pension and interest rates stay at the values shown. The model estimates a new security value and age-based limit once a year and stops new HEAS draws when the balance reaches the limit. It assumes a new loan, no repayments, no fees or sale costs, no prior advances and constant other secured debt.

The couple scenario assumes both partners qualify, participate and split pension amounts, home equity, nominated equity and borrowing equally. The model rounds each security share down to a $10,000 multiple and uses the younger partner’s age. Services Australia assesses each applicant’s circumstances.

The commercial rate is editable and represents an illustrative price for the same draws. Actual offers can differ in fees, rates, cash draw options and borrowing limits. These projections are estimates for comparing scenarios. Services Australia determines eligibility and loan amounts.

Frequently asked questions

Can a self-funded retiree use HEAS?

Yes, if they meet the qualifying pension and other scheme rules. At least one partner, or a single applicant, must be Age Pension age. Services Australia also checks suitable Australian property security, insurance and insolvency status. An eligible person receiving $0 pension may borrow up to 150% of their maximum qualifying pension rate, subject to the security limit.

How much can full and part pensioners borrow fortnightly?

Subtract your actual fortnightly pension from 150% of the maximum qualifying pension rate. With the single benchmark of $1,237.70, a full pensioner has up to $618.85 available and someone receiving $300 has up to $1,556.55. Advances and the age-based loan limit can reduce those amounts.

How does a lump-sum advance change my fortnightly loan?

Each advance lowers the fortnightly ceiling by the advance divided by 26 for the next 26 fortnights. Two advances share a combined limit of half the maximum annual pension per participant. A full-rate pensioner who takes the maximum advance generally has no fortnightly loan payment for that window.

How is the age-based maximum loan worked out?

Subtract other secured debt and the amount of equity you choose to exclude, then round the resulting eligible property share down to a multiple of $10,000. Multiply the number of $10,000 blocks by the statutory age factor. A couple’s assessment uses the younger partner’s age.

What does the no negative equity guarantee cover?

At HEAS settlement the guarantee generally caps the amount recoverable at the secured property’s market value less other secured debt. Fraud, misrepresentation or later secured borrowing that interferes with recovery can disqualify a claim. Australian commercial reverse mortgages entered into from 18 September 2012 generally also have negative equity protection.
