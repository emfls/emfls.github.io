UK remortgage arrangement fees FAQ

When is a UK mortgage with a product fee cheaper than a fee-free deal?

A product fee deal (£999 or £1,499) is cheaper when your outstanding mortgage balance is above the breakeven threshold (typically £150,000 to £180,000 on a 2-year fix). On larger balances, the monthly interest savings from the lower rate outweigh the upfront fee.

What happens if I add the £999 or £1,499 fee to my mortgage balance?

Adding the arrangement fee to the mortgage balance avoids paying upfront cash, but you pay interest on that fee over your entire remaining mortgage term (e.g. 25 years), increasing both your monthly payments and total lifetime interest.

How is the remortgage breakeven balance calculated?

The breakeven balance is the exact loan amount where the total cost (all monthly payments plus remaining capital balance at the end of the 2-year or 5-year fixed period) is identical between the fee-paying deal and the fee-free deal.

Do fee-free remortgage deals include free valuation and legal fees?

Most UK lenders offer standard remortgage packages that include fee-free standard legal conveyancing and basic property valuation, or provide cashback (e.g. £250 to £500) if you choose your own solicitor.

Should I choose a 2-year or 5-year fixed rate deal?

A 5-year fix spreads the arrangement fee over 60 months instead of 24 months, cutting the annualized fee impact significantly and making fee-paying deals viable at lower mortgage balances, while locking in payment certainty.

Calculation assumptions and methodology

Calculations assume standard capital and interest repayment amortisation with monthly interest calculation. Early repayment charges (ERCs) from your existing lender are not included and should be verified before switching before term end.
