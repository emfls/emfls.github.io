Australia Land Tax Calculator 2026

Compare annual investment property land tax in NSW, Victoria and Queensland. See how trust ownership and foreign or absentee surcharges affect your rental yield.

Key takeaways

On A$900,000 of taxable land held personally without a surcharge, annual land tax is A$0 in NSW, A$4,050 in Victoria and A$3,500 in Queensland.

  • NSW: The general threshold is frozen at A$1,075,000. Special trusts have no general threshold.
  • Victoria: Under the COVID Debt Repayment Plan, general land tax starts at A$50,000. The trust surcharge schedule starts at A$25,000.
  • Queensland: The threshold is A$600,000 for non-absentee individuals and A$350,000 for companies, ordinary trustees and absentee individuals.

Frequently asked questions

Which value goes into the land tax calculation?

Use total taxable land value from the applicable state assessments after exemptions. NSW generally uses a three-year average. Victoria generally uses site value. Queensland generally uses the lower of statutory and averaged value for each parcel. Use market value including buildings in the rental yield field.

How does NSW tax a discretionary family trust?

A discretionary family trust assessed as a NSW special trust has no general threshold. Tax is 1.6% up to $6,571,000 and 2% on the excess. At $900,000 of taxable land the special trust pays $14,400 before any foreign surcharge. A trust that qualifies for the general threshold uses a different schedule.

When does the Victorian absentee owner surcharge apply?

For an eligible absentee assessment, Victoria adds 4% of the full taxable land value once the applicable threshold is reached. The general threshold is $50,000 and the trust surcharge threshold is $25,000. An absentee owner on the general schedule with $50,000 of taxable land pays $2,500 in total.

How does Queensland tax absentee individuals and foreign entities?

Queensland uses a $350,000 base-tax threshold for absentee individuals, companies and ordinary trustees. Liable absentees and foreign entities pay an extra 3% on the value above $350,000. Absentee individuals have their own base rates from $5 million, and non-absentee individuals have a $600,000 threshold.

How does land tax reduce net rental yield?

Divide annual land tax by property market value and multiply by 100 to get the drop in percentage points. At $1,250,000 of market value, a $4,050 annual bill reduces yield by 0.324 percentage points. The calculator subtracts operating expenses and then land tax from rent to show before and after yields, before financing and income tax.
