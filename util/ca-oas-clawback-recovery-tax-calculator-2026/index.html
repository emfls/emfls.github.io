Canada 2026 OAS Clawback & Deferral Calculator

Estimate how much Old Age Security you keep after recovery tax and see what changes if you defer OAS or use your TFSA for part of your retirement spending.

Key takeaways

  • The clawback is 15% of income above the threshold up to the value of your OAS. It starts above $93,454 for 2025 income and above $95,323 for 2026 income.
  • OAS rises 10% after your 75th birthday. Deferring after age 65 adds 0.6% per month to your payments once they start, up to 36% at age 70.
  • A TFSA withdrawal can help preserve OAS. Compare an equal withdrawal from your TFSA and the portion of a taxable RRIF or RRSP withdrawal you can actually skip.

Pension income splitting for couples

Eligible spouses or common-law partners can jointly elect to split up to 50% of qualifying pension income, including eligible RRIF income. OAS itself cannot be split. Compare the recovery tax for both partners. CRA pension income splitting rules.

Frequently asked questions

When does the OAS clawback start?

The recovery is 15% of income above $93,454 for 2025 or $95,323 for 2026, capped at the OAS payable. These income years correspond to the July 2026–June 2027 and July 2027–June 2028 payment periods, respectively.

How does turning 75 affect OAS?

Your OAS pension increases by 10% starting the month after your 75th birthday. The calculator annualizes a single age band, so a year that includes the change can have a different total.

How much does deferring OAS increase my pension?

Each month of deferral after age 65 adds 0.6%, up to 36% at age 70. The higher payment starts when you take OAS. You give up the payments during the deferral period and may also delay GIS or a partner's Allowance.

Can a TFSA withdrawal reduce the OAS clawback?

A TFSA withdrawal does not raise net income for OAS purposes. Using a TFSA for funds you could otherwise withdraw from a taxable RRIF or RRSP can reduce the clawback if your income moves through the recovery range. Required RRIF withdrawals still count as income.

Can couples use pension income splitting to reduce the clawback?

Eligible couples may elect to allocate up to 50% of qualifying pension income, which can include eligible RRIF income. OAS itself cannot be split. Check both partners' incomes because the election can lower one person's recovery tax and raise the other's.
