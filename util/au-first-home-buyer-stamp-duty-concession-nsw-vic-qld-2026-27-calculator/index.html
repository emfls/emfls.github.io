Key takeaways

  • NSW: Eligible homes pay $0 duty up to A$800,000. The discount tapers to zero at A$1 million.
  • Victoria: Eligible first homes and building land pay $0 duty up to A$600,000. The discount tapers to zero at A$750,000.
  • Queensland: Eligible established homes pay $0 duty up to A$700,000. Eligible new homes and residential land for a first build have uncapped duty relief. The A$30,000 new-home grant requires a total value below A$750,000.

How the estimate works

The estimate assumes all buyers qualify, the whole property is residential and the contract falls between 1 July 2026 and 30 June 2027. It excludes foreign purchaser surcharges, ownership splits, mixed-use property, large-lot apportionment and separate off-the-plan concessions. Frequently asked questions

Which first homes qualify for zero stamp duty?

Eligible buyers pay zero duty on NSW homes up to A$800,000, Victorian homes up to A$600,000 and Queensland established homes up to A$700,000. Queensland fully exempts eligible new homes and residential land for a first build without a price cap.

What happens when I buy above the exemption threshold?

NSW and Victoria reduce the discount as the dutiable value rises. Queensland reduces its established-home discount in A$10,000 bands. At A$800,000 the Queensland first-home discount ends and the ordinary home concession may still apply.

Can I get the Queensland A$30,000 grant at exactly A$750,000?

The total value of the new home, including land and variations, must be below A$750,000. The Queensland new-home duty concession can still apply above that grant limit.

How do I enter land and a separate building contract?

Select vacant residential land and enter its dutiable value. For the grant estimate, enter the combined value of the land and completed home, including relevant building variations. The combined value is used only for the grant.

What happens if I miss the move-in deadline?

The revenue office can reassess duty or require you to repay a grant. The move-in deadline and minimum stay depend on the state, property type and benefit. Check them before arranging a lease or setting a build schedule.
