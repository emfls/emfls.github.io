How the election is calculated

The calculator reduces eligible current-year costs by your risk buffer, down to $0. It then adds only the post-year-end costs your plan can cover. For carryover, that amount is capped by the employer and IRS limits. For a grace period, enter only costs you expect to incur within your actual grace window.

Net FSA savings equal the election multiplied by your combined marginal tax rate, minus any elected funds with no modeled reimbursement. With a positive tax rate below 100%, savings rise until you cover all modeled costs or hit your plan limit. Above that point, each additional dollar forfeited costs more than its tax savings. With a 0% tax rate, the calculator suggests a $0 election.

The estimate assumes no incoming FSA carryover or employer FSA funding. Subtract eligible costs you expect existing FSA funds to cover before entering expenses. HSA figures show base annual contribution room before employer or employee deposits and exclude part-year adjustments, age-55 catch-ups, and HSA tax savings.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 2027 health FSA and LPFSA limit?

The 2027 employee salary-reduction limit is $3,500 for either a general-purpose health FSA or a limited-purpose health FSA, up from $3,400 in 2026. An employer may set a lower limit.

Can I carry over $700 and also use a grace period?

A health FSA can offer a carryover, a grace period of up to 2½ months, or neither. Eligible unused 2027 funds can carry into 2028 up to $700 if the employer allows it. A grace period instead lets you use leftover funds for eligible expenses incurred within that window. A claims submission deadline for expenses already incurred is a separate plan feature.

Can my general-purpose FSA affect my spouse’s HSA?

Yes. General-purpose FSA coverage generally blocks HSA contributions for you and for a spouse whose expenses the FSA can reimburse, even if neither of you files an FSA claim. Check both employers’ plan terms, including any general-purpose carryover or grace-period coverage, before contributing to an HSA.

How much can I put into an HSA with an LPFSA in 2027?

With full-year HSA eligibility, the 2027 base HSA limit is $4,500 for self-only coverage or $9,000 for family coverage. Employer HSA deposits count toward that limit, and eligible spouses subject to the family limit share it. Use the LPFSA for eligible dental and vision expenses and the HSA for other qualified costs or future expenses. Each expense can be reimbursed only once.

How does the calculator decide how much I should elect?

It subtracts your risk buffer from eligible current-year spending, then adds separately forecast spending that the employer’s carryover or grace period can cover. It picks the election that gives the largest estimated tax savings after modeled forfeiture, subject to the payroll election limit.
