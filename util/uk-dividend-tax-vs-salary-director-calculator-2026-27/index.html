What the estimate assumes

Enter figures for 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027. The model uses England, Wales and Northern Ireland Income Tax rates, full-year director NI under standard category A, and salary and dividends from this company as your only income. It assumes a 12-month accounting period, no associated companies, and taxable profits equal to augmented profits. Mixed strategies distribute all available post-tax profit and assume sufficient distributable reserves.

A direct company pension contribution is assumed deductible in full in the period paid. Check that it qualifies and fits your annual allowance across all schemes. Scottish PAYE rates, student loans, other income or reliefs, pension allowance charges, and future withdrawal tax are outside this estimate.

Frequently asked questions

What dividend tax rates changed on 6 April 2026?

The basic rate rose from 8.75% to 10.75% and the higher rate from 33.75% to 35.75%. The additional rate stays at 39.35%. The £500 dividend allowance taxes the first £500 of otherwise taxable dividends at 0%. That amount still uses space in your tax bands.

Can a £12,570 salary leave more cash than a £5,000 salary?

Yes. At £12,570, a company with no Employment Allowance pays £1,135.50 of employer NI. The company generally deducts both salary and employer NI, lowering taxable profit by £13,705.50 before any pension contribution. The trade-off depends on company profit and the personal tax bands your dividends reach.

Does a £5,000 director salary count towards the State Pension?

A £5,000 salary is below the £6,708 annual Lower Earnings Limit for 2026/27. This directorship alone would fall short of the earnings threshold. Other employment or National Insurance credits may still give you a qualifying year.

Can my company claim the £10,500 Employment Allowance?

A company cannot claim if its sole director is its only employee liable for employer Class 1 National Insurance. Eligible employers can offset up to £10,500 of qualifying employer NI. After other staff payroll, enter only the balance available to offset this director’s NI.

How does a direct company pension contribution affect the comparison?

The estimate deducts a qualifying contribution to a registered pension scheme before calculating available salary or dividends. It shows the amount paid to the pension separately from cash take-home. Annual allowance charges and tax on eventual pension withdrawals are outside the estimate.
