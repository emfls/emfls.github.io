UK Shared Ownership Rules & Guidance

Can I staircase up to 100% full ownership?

Most shared ownership leases allow full 100% staircasing, turning you into the sole freeholder or leaseholder. Once you reach 100%, you no longer pay any rent to the housing association.

How does the 80% SDLT threshold work on staged payments?

Under HMRC rules, staircasing transactions that take your total ownership up to 80% or less are completely exempt from SDLT. However, as soon as a staircasing tranche takes total ownership above 80%, linked-transaction SDLT applies to the full cumulative consideration.

Who determines the property price when staircasing?

The price you pay for additional shares is always based on the open market value at the time of staircasing, assessed by an independent RICS (Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors) qualified surveyor.
