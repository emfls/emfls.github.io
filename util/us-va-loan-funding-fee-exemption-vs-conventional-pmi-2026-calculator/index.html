VA Loan Regulations & Guidelines Explained

Can the VA funding fee be financed into the loan?

Yes. The vast majority of VA borrowers choose to roll the one-time funding fee into their total mortgage balance, meaning you do not need to pay it out of pocket at closing.

Does a VA loan require monthly private mortgage insurance (PMI)?

No. VA loans are backed by the federal government and strictly prohibit lenders from charging monthly mortgage insurance premiums, regardless of whether you put 0% or 10% down.

What is the interest rate difference between VA and conventional loans?

Because VA loans carry a federal guaranty and historically low default rates among veterans, lenders typically price VA 30-year fixed mortgages 0.25% to 0.50% lower than standard conforming conventional loans.

Assumptions & Statutory Notes

VA funding fee rates reflect statutory schedules under 38 U.S.C. § 3729. Conventional PMI estimates assume credit score 720+ with automatic Homeowners Protection Act cancellation upon reaching 78% original LTV.
