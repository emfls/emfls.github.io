Key takeaways

  • £33,493 is the crossover. Without pension contributions, Scotland charges less Income Tax above £12,570 and below £33,493. Above the crossover, it charges more.
  • 69.5% Scottish marginal rate in the taper. On salary from £100,000 to £125,140 with no pension contribution, Scotland’s 67.5% marginal Income Tax plus 2% employee NI compares with 62% combined elsewhere in the UK, before student loans.
  • Pension contributions can restore your Personal Allowance. In a salary-only scenario, £110,000 of gross salary needs £10,000 of gross pension contributions to bring adjusted net income to £100,000.

How much of a £1,000 raise would you keep?

If the whole raise falls inside the Personal Allowance taper and there are no pension or loan deductions, you keep about £305 in Scotland or £380 in England, Wales or Northern Ireland. Each £1 of adjusted net income above £100,000 removes 50p of tax-free allowance until it reaches zero at £125,140.

Frequently asked questions

When does Scottish Income Tax become higher than in England?

For salary alone with the standard Personal Allowance, Scottish and rest-of-UK Income Tax are equal at £33,493. The Scottish starter band saves £39.67, which the extra one percentage point above £29,526 gradually offsets. Both bills are zero up to £12,570.

How does the £100,000 Personal Allowance taper produce a 69.5% marginal rate?

From £100,000 to £125,140 of salary with no pension contribution, each extra £1 removes 50p of Personal Allowance. Scotland taxes £1.50 at 45%, for 67.5% Income Tax plus 2% employee NI. England, Wales and Northern Ireland tax £1.50 at 40%, for 60% plus 2% NI. Student loan deductions can raise those totals.

How do salary sacrifice and relief at source differ?

Salary sacrifice reduces pay used for Income Tax, employee NI and student loan repayments. With relief at source, you generally pay £80 for each £100 of gross pension contribution and the provider adds £20. The gross amount reduces adjusted net income and extends eligible tax bands. The calculator includes eligible additional tax relief in estimated take-home after it is claimed.

How much pension contribution restores my Personal Allowance?

In this salary-only model, the gross contribution target is salary minus £100,000, with a minimum of zero. On £110,000 of salary, the total target is £10,000. The calculator subtracts your current gross contribution to show the additional amount needed.

Which student loan thresholds apply in 2026/27?

Plan 2 is 9% above £29,385, Plan 4 is 9% above £33,795, and postgraduate loans are 6% above £21,000. Use your actual repayment plan for both sides of the comparison. This calculator models one plan at a time using annual thresholds.
