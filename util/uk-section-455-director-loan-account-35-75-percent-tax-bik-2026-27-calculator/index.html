Key takeaways

  • 35.75% Section 455 tax applies to chargeable loans made from 6 April 2026. The 6 April 2022 to 5 April 2026 rate is 33.75%. Qualifying repayments within nine months of the company’s accounting period end can reduce the charge before it is due.

  • Section 455 ties up company cash. After a qualifying late repayment, Section 458 relief is due nine months and one day after the end of the accounting period in which repayment occurs.

A temporary repayment can leave a tax bill

If £6,000 of an original loan appears cleared but a qualifying £6,000 redraw is matched to that repayment, £6,000 of the original loan stays chargeable. At 35.75%, the company still needs £2,145 for Section 455. A separate £15,000 arrangements rule can also apply outside the 30-day window.

When the company pays and reclaims Section 455

For a loan made in the accounting period ending 31 March 2027, the usual payment date is 1 January 2028. A qualifying repayment by 31 December 2027 can reduce the amount due. If the loan is repaid in February 2028 and that accounting period ends 31 March 2028, relief is due from 1 January 2029.

Claim within four years from the end of the financial year of repayment. Section 458 relief can refund the eligible Section 455 tax. Any late-payment interest on that tax remains a cost. HMRC payment and reclaim guidance · CTA 2010 Section 458

Frequently asked questions

Which Section 455 rate applies if the deadline falls after 6 April 2026?

Use the date the company made the original loan. The rate is 33.75% for loans made from 6 April 2022 through 5 April 2026 and 35.75% for loans made from 6 April 2026. Split loans made under different rates into separate calculations.

What happens if my balance exceeds £10,000 for only one day?

The small-loan exemption is lost for that tax year. Calculate the interest benefit using the full loan balance under the applicable valuation method and deduct interest actually paid for the year. The director pays Income Tax on the benefit and the company generally pays 15% Class 1A National Insurance.

Can I repay the loan and borrow again within 30 days?

Where qualifying repayments and qualifying advances each total at least £5,000 in a 30-day window and the new loan is in a later accounting period than the old one, the matched portion of the repayment is applied to the new loan. For example, matching £6,000 of an £8,000 repayment leaves only £2,000 available to reduce the old loan.

When can the company reclaim Section 455 tax it has already paid?

After a qualifying late repayment, relief becomes due nine months and one day after the end of the company’s accounting period in which it occurs. The company must claim within four years of the end of the financial year of repayment. Section 455 late-payment interest stays payable.

Can I clear the balance with a dividend?

A lawfully declared dividend credited to the account can repay the loan. The company needs sufficient distributable profits and must follow the proper process. The shareholder’s dividend tax depends on their remaining allowance and the bands the dividend falls into. Clearing the loan stops further borrowing benefit on the cleared balance, while any benefit already accrued can still be taxable.
