Key takeaways

The annual non-concessional cap rose from $120,000 to $130,000 on 1 July 2026. If you can start a bring-forward arrangement, you may be able to put in up to $390,000 over three financial years.

Your 30 June 2026 total super balance (TSB) sets the tier. At $2.10 million or more, your 2026–27 cap is nil. An active prior-year arrangement keeps its original limits.

Check your cap before transferring money

Leaving a $20,000 excess in super can cost $9,400 in excess contributions tax. Check your 30 June balance, fund payment deadlines and any active arrangement in ATO online services first.

A prior window that started in 2024–25 or 2025–26 keeps its original cap, typically $360,000 over three years or $240,000 over two. Check that it still covers 2026–27 before entering the unused balance. Even with an unused balance, the cap for 2026–27 is nil if your 30 June 2026 TSB reached $2.10 million.

Official 2026–27 ATO thresholds

From 1 July 2026, the annual non-concessional cap is four times the $32,500 concessional cap. The general transfer balance cap is $2.10 million.

Frequently asked questions

When does the bring-forward period start?

If eligible, you generally trigger it automatically when your total non-concessional contributions exceed $130,000 in 2026–27. The $390,000 and $260,000 limits cover the full three-year or two-year period, starting with 2026–27.

Can an active prior-year arrangement use the new $390,000 cap?

The original cap applies until that arrangement ends. If your 30 June 2026 total super balance is at least $2.10 million, your cap for 2026–27 is nil even if the original arrangement has an unused amount.

Can I bring contributions forward if I turn 75 this year?

You can meet the age test if you are under 75 at any time during 2026–27. Your fund generally needs to receive voluntary contributions by the 28th day after the end of the month you turn 75. Check your fund’s acceptance rules and payment timing.

How does the ATO release option change the tax?

Leaving the excess in super attracts 47% tax on the excess. Under the release option, the ATO can release the excess plus 85% of associated earnings. All associated earnings are included in assessable income, with a 15% non-refundable tax offset on those earnings. Use the amounts and response deadline on your ATO determination.

Does a downsizer contribution use up my after-tax cap?

Eligible people aged 55 or older may contribute up to $300,000 each from a qualifying home sale without using their NCC cap. The contribution counts in their next 30 June total super balance and can affect future cap eligibility. Amounts moved into retirement phase count towards the transfer balance cap.

See ATO age and fund acceptance rules and ATO downsizer rules.
