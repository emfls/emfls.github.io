Key takeaways

On A$45,000 in wages, standard working holiday maker (WHM) income tax is A$6,750. Qualifying full-year residents can compare A$4,020 in base resident income tax, a difference of A$2,730 before offsets and levies.

A WHM departure payment leaves you with 35% of its taxed super component. The ordinary departing Australia superannuation payment (DASP) leaves 65% of that component.

Budget for withheld pay and a smaller super payout

On A$45,000, an unregistered employer with a TFN can withhold about A$13,500, leaving A$6,750 less cash in your pay than the standard WHM tax estimate. At 45% no-TFN withholding, that cash gap grows to A$13,500. Any refund depends on your assessment.

Every A$1,000 in the taxed super component yields about A$350 under WHM DASP, A$300 less than the ordinary payout.

How the estimate works

For income tax, the tool treats your gross wages as your only taxable income. It excludes deductions, offsets, Medicare levy and surcharge. A Medicare levy can apply depending on residency, income and exemptions. Part-year residency can change the tax-free threshold.

Payroll estimates assume one employer, even weekly pay, no withholding variation and no pay-period rounding. At a registered employer the higher rate starts on the pay after cumulative wages cross a band, so payroll withholding can differ from annual tax. The unregistered estimate applies the foreign-resident brackets to annualised weekly pay and scales withholding back to the weeks worked. The no-TFN scenario assumes 45% withholding applies throughout the period.

For super, all wages are treated as qualifying earnings, such as ordinary time earnings. The estimate caps mandatory contributions at the annual base, deducts standard contributions tax from new super and adds the existing taxed balance. Fund fees, investment returns, other super components and additional contributions taxes are outside this estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Do subclass 417 and 462 workers get a tax-free threshold?

The standard working holiday maker scale taxes the first A$45,000 at 15% from the first dollar. A qualifying Australian tax resident from an eligible treaty country can receive resident treatment.

When can my employer withhold 45%?

The 45% no-TFN rate can apply when you have not provided a tax file number. With a TFN, an unregistered employer uses the foreign-resident withholding scale, starting at 30%. PAYG withholding is credited at assessment.

Who can use the Addy resident comparison?

Australian tax residents who are nationals of Chile, Finland, Germany, Israel, Japan, Norway, Turkey or the United Kingdom may qualify. Treaty residency rules can affect eligibility. This calculator assumes full-year residency and a full A$18,200 threshold.

What does the 65% DASP rate apply to?

When a fund payment includes super attributable to contributions made while you held a working holiday maker visa, the 65% rate applies to the taxable component of that payment. Any tax-free component attracts no DASP tax.

When can I claim my departing Australia superannuation payment?

You can generally apply after leaving Australia once your eligible temporary visa has ceased and you hold no other active Australian visa. Australian or New Zealand citizens and Australian permanent residents generally cannot claim DASP.
