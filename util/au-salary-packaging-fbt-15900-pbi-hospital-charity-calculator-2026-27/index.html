How the estimate works

FBT uses the statutory grossed-up limits, so rounding a packaging guide up to the nearest dollar can produce a small modeled excess. The model assumes no other fringe benefits use the caps and treats entered benefits as entirely reportable. It includes the low income tax offset and up to A$1,000 for the standard work-related deduction. It assumes that the benefits for the FBT year match the full-year salary sacrifice. If packaging starts or stops between April and June, check which income statement will report those benefits.

Use this comparison for a single person with no dependants and either full-year cover or no cover. It excludes other income, add-backs, other deductions, senior offsets and Medicare exemptions. Individual assessments and PAYG withholding may differ.

Salary packaging FAQs

Who can use the $15,900 or $9,010 salary packaging amount?

ATO-endorsed public benevolent institutions and health promotion charities generally have a $30,000 grossed-up exemption cap. Eligible public and not-for-profit hospitals and public ambulance services have a $17,000 cap. The $15,900 and $9,010 figures are rounded Type 2 equivalents. Ask payroll which category applies to your employer.

How does the $2,650 meal entertainment cap work?

Eligible employers have a separate $5,000 grossed-up cap for salary-packaged meal entertainment and entertainment facility leasing. This is roughly $2,650 for Type 2 benefits. Any grossed-up excess uses available general cap space first. Type 1 benefits consume cap space faster because they use 2.0802.

Why can HELP repayments rise when taxable pay falls?

HELP includes the full reportable fringe benefits amount in repayment income. When the reportable benefit value exceeds $2,000, multiplying by 1.8868 usually puts more back into the income test than was taken out of salary. The 2026–27 marginal schedule starts above $69,528.

Can packaging trigger the Medicare levy surcharge or affect Child Care Subsidy?

Yes. For a single person without suitable hospital cover, the full reportable amount can push income above the $105,000 surcharge threshold. Child Care Subsidy uses family adjusted taxable income and generally counts 53% of reportable fringe benefits from FBT-exempt employers and 100% from other employers.

What if my employer pays full FBT or gets an FBT rebate?

Standard employers generally pay 47% on the grossed-up taxable value in this scenario. Eligible rebatable employers can reduce their gross FBT by 47% within the applicable caps. The employee comparison includes employer FBT as a separate after-tax cost when the pass-through option is selected.
