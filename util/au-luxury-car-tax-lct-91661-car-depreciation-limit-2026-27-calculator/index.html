Australia Luxury Car Tax & Car Limit Calculator 2026–27

Compare EV and petrol luxury car tax (LCT), estimate your business GST credit and see how the depreciation limit affects your after-tax purchase cost.

Key takeaways

  • LCT starts above $91,661 for fuel-efficient cars and $80,809 for other cars. The tax is 30% of the GST-inclusive excess.
  • At the same price of $91,661 or more, a qualifying fuel-efficient car saves $3,255.60 in LCT.
  • The passenger-car depreciation limit is $69,883. The usual GST credit cap is $6,353 at 100% eligible business use.

How the net cost is estimated

The calculator assumes a fully taxable dealer sale with no LCT previously paid and the same percentage for business and creditable use. The passenger-car scenarios assume a vehicle designed mainly to carry passengers, with a payload under one tonne and room for fewer than nine passengers. The calculator deducts the estimated GST credit from the vehicle price including LCT before applying the car limit and the business-use percentage.

Net cost equals the price including LCT, less the GST credit and the estimated income tax benefit. The tax benefit assumes you ultimately claim the full eligible depreciation base at your selected rate. Your cash due at purchase and yearly deductions are separate figures. The estimate excludes on-road charges, financing, the timing of deductions, FBT and resale or disposal adjustments.

This is a planning estimate. Check your invoice and vehicle classification before relying on it for a purchase or a claim. Your calculator inputs are processed in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How is luxury car tax calculated for 2026–27?

Subtract the applicable threshold from the GST-inclusive vehicle value before LCT. If the difference is positive, multiply it by 10/11 and then by 33%. This equals 30% of the GST-inclusive excess. LCT is zero at or below the threshold.

How much LCT can an EV or fuel-efficient car save?

At the same price of $91,661 or more, a qualifying fuel-efficient car saves $3,255.60 in LCT compared with a standard car. Savings are smaller between the two thresholds and zero at or below $80,809. The higher threshold applies to qualifying zero-emission EVs and cars rated at 3.5 L/100 km or less on the combined cycle.

Can my business claim GST on luxury car tax?

LCT carries no GST, so it generates no GST credit. On an eligible passenger car above the car limit, the ordinary GST credit cap is $6,353 at 100% creditable use for 2026–27. At 50% creditable use, the cap is $3,176.50. GST registration and credit eligibility are required.

How does the $69,883 depreciation limit work?

For a passenger car subject to the car limit, deduct the GST credit you can claim from the vehicle cost, then apply the $69,883 limit. Multiply the result by the business-use percentage to estimate the business share of the cost you can depreciate. The deduction follows the applicable depreciation rules over time.

Does a one-tonne payload exempt a vehicle from LCT?

A payload of at least one tonne removes the passenger-car depreciation limit. For LCT, a commercial vehicle designed mainly to carry goods qualifies for the commercial exemption. A passenger-focused vehicle with a payload of at least one tonne but under two tonnes may still attract LCT if designed to carry fewer than nine passengers.
