Australian General Insurance Duty & Compounding Tax Rules

Commercial business insurance policies in Australia are subject to a dual-tier taxation regime consisting of Commonwealth Goods and Services Tax (GST) and state-administered Insurance Duty.

1. The Tax-on-Tax Compounding Phenomenon

  • Calculation Mechanism: State Revenue Offices mandate that state stamp duty is assessed on the gross amount payable including GST.
  • Mathematical Example: A $1,000 base premium incurs $100 GST ($1,100 subtotal). In South Australia (11%), the duty is $121 ($1,100 × 11%), yielding a total of $1,221. The effective tax rate is 22.1% rather than a simple 21.0%.

2. GST Recovery vs Non-Recoverable Duty

Businesses registered for GST can claim back the 10% GST on their quarterly Business Activity Statement (BAS). However, state insurance duty is strictly non-recoverable on BAS, remaining an outright overhead expenditure (though deductible against corporate income tax).
