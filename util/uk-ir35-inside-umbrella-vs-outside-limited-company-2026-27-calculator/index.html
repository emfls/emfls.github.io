Get the agency’s Key Information Document and check the margin, employer costs, holiday pay and pension treatment against the rate you were quoted. How the estimate works

Umbrella gross pay is solved so it adds up to the assignment pot after the margin and salary sacrifice, together with employer NI and the levy. NI uses annual thresholds. Actual payroll uses weekly or monthly thresholds and can differ with uneven pay or pay-period rounding.

The company calculation applies to one standalone company, a 12-month accounting period, one director-shareholder and current-year trading profit available for distribution. It assumes allowable expenses and qualifying direct employer pension contributions. The £12,570 salary is an editable scenario. Associated companies or short accounting periods can lower the Corporation Tax thresholds.

The estimate assumes a full tax year, standard NI, and no other income, student loans, benefits, separate holiday-pay accrual or other pension deductions. Check the pension annual allowance and your employer’s scheme rules. Under HMRC’s salary sacrifice rules, cash earnings must remain at or above the National Minimum Wage. Confirm contract status separately with HMRC’s off-payroll working guidance.

IR35 take-home FAQs

Why is an umbrella assignment rate higher than gross pay?

The assignment rate funds the umbrella margin, employer National Insurance, the Apprenticeship Levy where applicable and any agreed employer pension costs. Income Tax and employee National Insurance then come out of gross taxable pay.

How does the £100,000 Personal Allowance taper affect contractors?

The £12,570 Personal Allowance falls by £1 for each £2 of adjusted net income over £100,000 and reaches zero at £125,140. For employment income in this range the effective marginal Income Tax rate is 60% before employee National Insurance. Dividends can also trigger the taper.

What changes if I cap dividends at £50,270 of total income?

The calculator draws only the post-Corporation-Tax profit that fits below £50,270 after salary. The £500 dividend allowance sits within that band. Remaining cash stays in the company and future withdrawals can create a personal tax bill.

Can a single-director company claim Employment Allowance?

A company generally cannot claim where a director is its only employee liable for employer Class 1 National Insurance. A company with two directors may qualify if both earn above the Secondary Threshold and the company meets the other eligibility rules. Use the option only if sufficient allowance is available.

How is the matching inside IR35 day rate calculated?

The calculator holds billable days, umbrella margin, pension and levy setting constant. It finds the lowest day rate in whole pennies that produces at least the limited company cash take-home. A negative rate change means the original umbrella rate already exceeds that target.
