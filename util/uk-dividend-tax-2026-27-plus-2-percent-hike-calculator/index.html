Watch what the taper can cost you

Above £100,000 in adjusted net income, another £2 of outside-ISA dividends can remove £1 of Personal Allowance until it reaches zero. You may lose take-home pay from salary or other income as well as paying tax on the dividends. Before selling holdings for Bed and ISA, weigh the projected dividend tax saving against any Capital Gains Tax, fees and dealing spreads.

How the comparison works

The estimate uses default HMRC income ordering. Salary, pensions and other non-savings income come first, followed by savings interest and then dividends. Qualifying gross pension contributions and Gift Aid can restore the Personal Allowance and extend both the basic and higher dividend band limits. Each band’s tax is rounded down to the penny.

The rate-rise comparison uses the same income in both tax years. Existing ISA savings compare your estimate with all entered ISA dividends treated as outside-ISA income. The Bed and ISA projection limits the transfer to your unused allowance and caps the projected sheltered dividends at the outside-ISA dividends you entered. It assumes the same income and yield for a full year.

This is a UK dividend tax estimate. It excludes tax on other income, alternative Personal Allowance allocations, overseas withholding, pension charges, Capital Gains Tax and transaction costs. Dividend tax questions

How much did UK dividend tax rise in April 2026?

From 6 April 2026, the basic rate rose from 8.75% to 10.75% and the higher rate rose from 33.75% to 35.75%. The additional rate stays at 39.35%. For £10,000 of dividends charged at either increased rate after allowances, the rise adds £200.

Does the £500 Dividend Allowance use up my tax band?

Yes. Up to £500 of dividends above the Personal Allowance are charged at 0%, but still fill the tax bands they fall into. For example, if £270 of basic-rate space remains, the first £270 uses that space and the next £230 uses higher-rate space.

How do dividends affect the £100,000 Personal Allowance taper?

Outside-ISA dividends count towards adjusted net income, including dividends charged at 0% by the Dividend Allowance. The Personal Allowance falls by £1 per £2 above £100,000 and reaches zero at £125,140. Qualifying gross Gift Aid and relief-at-source pension contributions can reduce adjusted net income.

Do Scottish taxpayers pay the same dividend tax rates?

Yes. Dividend income uses UK-wide rates and a £37,700 basic-rate limit on taxable income before qualifying extensions. Scottish earnings still sit below savings and dividends when filling the UK dividend tax bands.

How does Bed and ISA affect future dividend tax?

Bed and ISA generally involves selling investments you hold outside an ISA and buying them inside a Stocks & Shares ISA, using your remaining ISA subscription allowance. Future dividends inside the ISA are free of UK dividend tax. A sale may trigger Capital Gains Tax, and dealing fees or spreads can reduce the benefit.
