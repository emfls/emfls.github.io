Victoria CIPT vs Stamp Duty Calculator 2026–27

Compare the final transfer duty on a qualifying entry purchase with an exempt resale. See the 10-year transition loan and 25 years of commercial and industrial property tax in Australian dollars.

Key takeaways

  • Entry purchase. Pay general transfer duty at settlement or finance it with an eligible 10-year TCV loan. Annual CIPT starts in the calendar year after the 10-year transition ends.
  • Qualifying exempt resale. A standard transfer that meets the exemption rules can save A$142,500 upfront on a A$2.5 million dutiable value. The original transition clock continues.
  • Annual bill. CIPT is usually 1% of unimproved site value each year, or 0.5% for eligible build-to-rent property. Regular Victorian land tax can also apply.

Comparison assumptions

Year 0 is settlement. Each later row represents the next calendar assessment year, assuming ownership at midnight on the preceding 31 December. The model grows site value each year from settlement and places the annual tax and repayment cash flows at the end of each model year for discounting.

Buyer A makes the qualifying entry purchase and pays cash duty or finances the full amount with TCV. Buyer B purchases a comparable property that already entered the reform and qualifies for a full exemption. The traditional benchmark pays the same general duty in cash at settlement and incurs no CIPT. Nominal and present-value breakeven compare buyer B with that benchmark, using the first annual charge that takes cumulative after-tax CIPT above the duty saved.

The estimate assumes constant rates, uninterrupted qualifying use and a deduction benefit available in the same year as the CIPT payment. It excludes regular land tax, regional duty concessions, surcharges, GST, CGT, loan-interest deductions, fees and early loan payout or break adjustments. TCV eligibility also depends on the buyer, approved lender and property, including a sale price of up to A$30 million and duty of up to A$1.93 million.

Frequently asked questions

When does the first CIPT bill apply?

The first assessment year is the calendar year after the 10-year transition ends. A property entering in 2026 can first be assessed in 2037. Liability generally falls on the owner at midnight on the preceding 31 December.

Is a second sale always exempt from stamp duty?

The exemption depends on the transaction and qualifying use. This calculator assumes an exempt standard resale. Check the SRO property clearance certificate for the entry date and interest, and confirm that the transaction meets the exemption rules.

Does CIPT replace Victorian land tax?

Regular Victorian land tax can still apply alongside CIPT. CIPT generally uses 1% of unimproved site value, or 0.5% when the property qualifies for the build-to-rent land tax benefit.

How does the TCV transition loan work?

Eligible entry buyers may finance transfer duty through the Treasury Corporation of Victoria. The loan has a fixed rate set at settlement and 10 equal annual payments of principal and interest. Use your quoted rate for the estimate.

How is the hold-period breakeven calculated?

Breakeven is the first modelled year when cumulative after-tax CIPT for the exempt resale exceeds the duty saved at settlement. The present-value calculation discounts each annual charge using your opportunity cost. Use a 0% deduction benefit if you cannot claim or use it.
