How the estimate works

The 2026 employee health FSA salary-reduction limit is $3,400. Enter your plan’s carryover limit, or $0 for none. The comparison uses one spending estimate for both accounts and excludes separate grace-period claims. The calculation excludes amounts already carried into 2026. The HSA forecast applies a constant return to this year’s remaining balance with no later contributions, withdrawals or fees. Your plan’s eligibility depends on more than the standard HDHP deductible and out-of-pocket limits. Confirm eligibility with your plan, especially for Medicare, partial-year coverage or other insurance.

Frequently asked questions

What are the 2026 HSA limits and FSA carryover?

The HSA limit is $4,400 for self-only coverage or $8,750 for family coverage, including employer contributions, plus $1,000 for an eligible account holder age 55 or older. A health FSA can allow up to $680 of unused funds to carry forward. The actual plan cap can be lower or zero.

When can I save 7.65% in employee payroll taxes?

Eligible payroll elections can save 6.2% Social Security below the $184,500 wage base and 1.45% standard Medicare. The model accounts for crossing that wage base, excludes Additional Medicare Tax and gives direct HSA deposits no payroll savings. Enter a state rate only if your state allows that account’s tax benefit. Use zero if the two accounts receive different treatment.

What makes an HSA triple tax advantaged?

Eligible contributions may be deductible or excluded from federal income, earnings can compound free of federal tax and withdrawals for qualified medical expenses can be federal-tax-free. Unused money stays in the HSA. State tax treatment can differ.
