2026 Québec Parental Insurance Plan Calculator

Compare Basic and Special benefits under QPIP (RQAP), including how sharing leave changes your payout and how an earlier return to work affects first-year income.

Key takeaways

Québec caps insurable earnings at $103,000 in 2026. Basic can pay up to 59 household benefit weeks with the sharing bonus. Special can pay up to 46 weeks at 75%, with a higher weekly rate and a shorter benefit schedule. Federal EI uses a $68,900 cap.

Before choosing a plan

A higher weekly cheque can cost you paid weeks. A longer leave can cost you wages. Compare both verdicts before the first parent applies, because that choice applies to both parents and is generally binding.

How the estimate works

The calculator assumes two eligible wage earners, steady earnings, and one child born in 2026. It estimates each parent’s insurable weekly earnings by dividing capped annual salary by 52. Actual QPIP and EI earnings calculations can differ for irregular pay. The model uses the full exclusive and base QPIP allotments and assigns any earned sharing bonus to the parent you select.

For QPIP first-year income, the model assumes both parents take all modeled benefit weeks within the same 52-week window, earn their full regular salaries during their remaining weeks, and receive no pay from work during benefit weeks. The schedules can overlap. Actual deposit dates may differ from benefit weeks.

EI is a hypothetical comparison for the same salaries outside Québec. Its figures cover the full claim, so extended benefits may run beyond one year. The estimate excludes tax, employer top-ups, childcare costs, low-income increases or supplements, and work-while-on-claim adjustments.

Frequently asked questions

What is the QPIP earnings cap in 2026?

Québec caps annual insurable earnings at $103,000 in 2026, equivalent to about $1,980.77 per week. At that cap, this estimate gives $1,386.54 at 70%, $1,089.42 at 55%, or $1,485.58 at 75%.

How do we unlock the sharing bonus?

Both parents must each receive at least eight shareable parental weeks under Basic or six under Special. That unlocks four weeks at 55% or three weeks at 75%, respectively. Exclusive maternity and paternity weeks do not count.

Who receives the seven Basic parental weeks at 70%?

The first seven shareable parental benefit weeks across the household use the 70% rate. Use your planned leave calendar to enter how many of those weeks the other parent will receive.

Can parents choose different QPIP plans or switch later?

The first parent's choice applies to both parents. Québec generally does not allow a plan change once it receives the application, except in exceptional circumstances.

How does federal EI compare with QPIP?

Federal EI outside Québec uses a $68,900 earnings cap in 2026. Maternity and standard parental benefits pay 55% up to $729 weekly. Extended parental benefits pay 33% up to $437 weekly, while maternity stays at 55%.
