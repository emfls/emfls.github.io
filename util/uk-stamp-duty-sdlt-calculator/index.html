What this estimate includes

  
Each rate applies to the portion of the price in its band. The total is rounded down to the nearest pound after adding all bands. The effective rate divides the total SDLT payable by the purchase price.

  
This calculator covers the purchase price or lease premium for individual residential purchases in England and Northern Ireland. Scotland uses Land and Buildings Transaction Tax (LBTT). Wales uses Land Transaction Tax (LTT). For new leases with rent or other special cases, see the HMRC residential SDLT guidance.

FAQ

  
Who qualifies for first-time buyer relief?
Every buyer must have never owned a home anywhere in the world and intend to live in this property as their only or main home. For purchases up to £500,000, the first £300,000 is taxed at 0% and the rest at 5% before any non-UK resident surcharge. Above £500,000, standard bands apply across the full purchase price.

  
Can I get a refund when I replace my main home?
If you pay the additional property surcharge when buying your new main home, you can usually claim back the higher-rate portion if you dispose of your former main home within 36 months of the purchase and otherwise qualify. See HMRC’s refund guidance for eligibility and claim deadlines.

  
How do the two surcharges interact?
Additional property rates add 5 percentage points to each standard band when the higher rates apply. The non-UK resident surcharge adds another 2 percentage points to each applicable band, including any 0% band and first-time buyer bands. For a straightforward purchase, the surcharges generally apply from £40,000.
