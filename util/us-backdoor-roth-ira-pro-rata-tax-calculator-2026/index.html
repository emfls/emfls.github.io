  • The 2026 IRA contribution cap is $7,500, or $8,600 if you’re 50 or older by year-end. Eligible compensation and other IRA contributions can reduce your available room.

How Form 8606 counts your IRAs

Form 8606 adds the total December 31 traditional IRA value on line 6 to eligible distributions on line 7 and conversions on line 8. With no other distributions, the pool equals the modeled pre-tax dollars plus total after-tax basis. The calculator divides basis by this pool, applies that share to the conversion, and carries unused basis forward. It shows the ratio to 12 decimal places and estimates dollar amounts to cents.

The model covers one IRA owner and assumes no other reportable distributions, outstanding rollovers, recharacterizations or other line 6 adjustments. For a 2026 contribution deposited in 2027, line 4 generally removes it from the basis used for a 2026 conversion. The conversion estimate uses flat marginal rates and omits bracket changes, credits, penalties and state-specific basis rules.

Backdoor Roth IRA FAQ

Can I make a backdoor Roth IRA contribution if my income is too high?

A backdoor Roth starts with a nondeductible traditional IRA contribution, followed by a Roth conversion. There is no MAGI ceiling on conversions, but new contributions need eligible compensation and available IRA contribution room. The 2026 combined traditional and Roth cap is $7,500, or $8,600 at age 50 or older.

Which balances count for the December 31 pro-rata rule?

Form 8606 combines the year-end values of traditional, traditional SEP and traditional SIMPLE IRAs you own, even if different custodians hold them. Roth IRAs, employer 401(k) accounts and your spouse’s own IRAs are outside your pool. The form also adds the year’s reportable conversions and applicable distributions.

Why is my conversion taxable if I already paid tax on the contribution?

Part of each modeled conversion comes from after-tax basis in proportion to its share of the combined IRA pool. The remainder is generally taxable as ordinary income. Any basis you did not recover carries forward on Form 8606 line 14.

How can a 401(k) reverse rollover reduce pro-rata tax?

If your employer plan accepts the rollover, eligible pre-tax IRA dollars can move into the plan and after-tax IRA basis stays behind. A basis-only conversion can have zero conversion tax if no pre-tax IRA dollars remain in the year’s pool. Check the plan’s rules and eligibility, including the two-year restriction on rolling a traditional SIMPLE IRA to a non-SIMPLE plan.

What if I make my 2026 IRA contribution in early 2027?

An eligible nondeductible contribution designated for 2026 can still be reported for 2026. Form 8606 generally excludes an early-2027 deposit from the basis used for a 2026 conversion. Report the conversion for the calendar year in which it actually occurs.
