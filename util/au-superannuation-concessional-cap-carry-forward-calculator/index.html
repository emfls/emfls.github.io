How the estimate works

Planning estimate for an Australian resident with one modeled employer and taxed accumulation super. Savings compare in-cap voluntary contributions with taking the same amount as taxable income. Enter actual employer totals across funds if they differ from the estimate. Tax offsets, Medicare levy reductions or surcharge, defined benefits and tax on excess contributions are outside this estimate.

The 2026–27 general concessional cap is A$32,500. Employer Super Guarantee, salary sacrifice and deductible personal contributions all count. The SG estimate is 12% of annual qualifying earnings up to the A$270,830 annual base. Use amounts paid into super in the relevant year for a cap check.

Carry-forward room is available when your total super balance at 30 June 2026 was below A$500,000. The current-year cap is used first, then the oldest remaining amount from 2021–22 through 2025–26. Any remaining 2021–22 amount expires after 30 June 2027.

The 2026–27 resident schedule is 0% through A$18,200, 15% through A$45,000, 30% through A$135,000, 37% through A$190,000 and 45% above. The estimate measures the tax difference across each band, optionally adds a full 2% Medicare levy reduction and subtracts the usual 15% fund tax on the voluntary contribution.

Division 293 adds 15% on the lesser of in-cap concessional contributions or the excess of Division 293 income plus those contributions over A$250,000. The savings estimate subtracts the increase in Division 293 tax compared with the employer-only contribution scenario.

Frequently asked questions

Does a total super balance of exactly A$500,000 qualify for carry-forward?

The balance must be below A$500,000 at 30 June 2026 to use unused amounts in 2026–27. At exactly A$500,000, only the general cap applies in this model.

Which unused cap does the ATO apply first?

Once you exceed the current-year cap, the ATO applies remaining eligible amounts from the oldest year first. Earlier unused amounts expire after five financial years.

Is Division 293 a flat extra 15% on every contribution?

The extra tax is 15% of the smaller of eligible contributions and the amount by which Division 293 income plus those contributions exceeds A$250,000. Excess concessional contributions are generally excluded from this calculation.
