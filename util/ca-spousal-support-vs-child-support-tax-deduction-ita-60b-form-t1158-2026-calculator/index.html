Watch for a lost deduction

A one-time settlement replacing future spousal support generally gives up the payer’s deduction, and the recipient generally keeps the settlement free of income tax. Unpaid child support can also reduce or eliminate the periodic deduction. Before signing a settlement or filing a return, check the payment history and whether a catch-up payment or court-ordered retroactive amount qualifies under the CRA lump-sum rules.

How the estimate works

The model assumes one payer and one recipient living apart, with separately stated support under a post-April 1997 order or written agreement. Monthly obligations are multiplied by 12. The model applies the payments to child support due first, then caps eligible spousal support at the scheduled amount. It uses that same allocation for both parties.

The tax comparison applies progressive federal and provincial brackets to each person’s change in taxable income, with the federal abatement for Quebec. It excludes tax credits, benefit changes, Ontario surtax and health premium, and payroll contributions. Tax saved is an estimated reduction in income tax liability. Any refund depends on the full return and amounts already paid.

Separate recipients or orders, prior-year spousal balances, different recipient allocations, and retroactive payments need their own calculation. Frequently asked questions

Is child support deductible in Canada?

For orders and written agreements made after April 1997, child support is generally neither deductible for the payer nor taxable for the recipient. Older arrangements can have different rules.

How does child support priority affect the spousal deduction?

The model allocates payments to child support due for the current year plus any opening tax-allocation shortfall. For $12,000 in child support and $6,000 in scheduled spousal support, a total payment of $12,000 produces a $0 spousal deduction. Prior-year allocation and recipient reporting can require a separate review.

Can I deduct spousal support paid as a single lump sum?

A one-time settlement that replaces future periodic support is generally non-deductible and non-taxable. A single payment that catches up qualifying periodic arrears or pays court-ordered retroactive support for a specified period can receive different treatment.

What does Form T1158 register?

Form T1158 registers a court order or written agreement that includes spousal support with the CRA. File a separate form for each arrangement being registered. A child-only arrangement generally requires no T1158 registration.

Which CRA lines show spousal and child support?

When support reporting applies, the payer reports total support on line 21999 and the allowable deduction on line 22000. The recipient reports total support received on line 12799 and the taxable portion on line 12800.
