What this estimate includes

Both scenarios use the same fixed rate and scheduled principal-and-interest repayment. The term is rounded to the nearest month. The lump sum applies immediately. Each extra deposit applies after that month’s interest and alongside the scheduled payment. Most Australian lenders calculate interest daily, so actual results will vary.

Redraw extra repayments reduce principal immediately. An offset reduces the balance charged interest. The offset payoff estimate assumes you use the offset funds to close the loan as soon as they cover the outstanding balance. The annual offset fee is prorated over the months until payoff and paid separately from repayments.

FAQ

Does a 100% offset save the same interest as extra repayments?

With the same rate, scheduled repayment and deposit timing, both strategies save the same gross interest. The package fee reduces net savings on the offset.

Tax reminder if you later rent out the property

Interest attributable to borrowing for the property may be deductible while it is rented or genuinely available for rent. Withdrawing your own savings from a separate offset account does not change the purpose of the original borrowing. The ATO treats a redraw as a new borrowing and looks at how you spend the redrawn funds. A redraw for private spending can make the loan mixed-purpose and require you to apportion the interest. This is general information only. Ask a registered tax agent about your circumstances.
