What this estimate includes

Both scenarios use the same fixed rate and scheduled principal-and-interest repayment. The term is rounded to the nearest month. The lump sum applies immediately. Each extra deposit applies after that month’s interest and alongside the scheduled payment. Most Australian lenders calculate interest daily, so actual results will vary.

Redraw extra repayments reduce principal immediately. An offset reduces the balance charged interest. The offset payoff estimate assumes you use the offset funds to close the loan as soon as they cover the outstanding balance. FAQ

Does a 100% offset save the same interest as extra repayments?

With the same rate, scheduled repayment and deposit timing, both strategies save the same gross interest. The package fee reduces net savings on the offset.

Tax reminder if you later rent out the property

Interest attributable to borrowing for the property may be deductible while it is rented or genuinely available for rent. Withdrawing your own savings from a separate offset account does not change the purpose of the original borrowing. The ATO treats a redraw as a new borrowing and looks at how you spend the redrawn funds. A redraw for private spending can make the loan mixed-purpose and require you to apportion the interest. This is general information only. Ask a registered tax agent about your circumstances.

How the Calculation Works & Mathematical Formula

The Australia Home Loan Offset vs. Redraw Calculator allows users to estimate the interest saved and the reduction in loan term by utilizing lump-sum savings and ongoing monthly deposits. Let's break down each input variable:

  • Loan balance (A$): The total amount of the mortgage still owed.
  • Annual interest rate (%): The yearly interest charged on the mortgage expressed as a percentage.
  • Remaining term (years): The time left until the loan is fully repaid.
  • Initial lump-sum savings (A$): A one-time additional payment towards the loan principal.
  • Monthly extra deposit (A$): Regular added payments that reduce the principal each month.
  • Annual offset package fee (A$): The yearly cost of maintaining an offset account.
  • Account setup: Choose between a 100% Offset Account or Redraw option.

The formula to calculate total interest saved and time reduced can be expressed as:

Total Interest Saved = (Original Loan Payment) - (New Loan Payment)

Months Taken Off = (Original Loan Term in Months) - (New Loan Term in Months)

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does an offset account differ from a redraw facility?

An offset account reduces the interest charged by lowering the effective principal, while a redraw facility allows you to withdraw extra payments made on your loan. Both can save you money, but they operate differently.

Can I switch from a redraw facility to an offset account?

Yes, many lenders allow you to switch between loan features. However, it may involve fees or changes in terms, so it's important to discuss with your lender before making the switch.

Are there any tax implications when using an offset account?

If the property is rented out, the interest paid on the mortgage may be tax-deductible. However, the offset funds must be related to the property's acquisition for tax benefits to apply.
