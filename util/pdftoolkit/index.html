              FAQ – PDF Toolkit

              Are my PDF files uploaded or stored on a server?
              No. All processing is done 100% in your browser. Files are never uploaded or saved online.
              Is the PDF Toolkit free to use?
              Yes! All tools are free, with no watermarks or registration required.
              Is there a file size or page limit?
              Limits depend on your device/browser memory, but there is no artificial size/page limit set by the tool.
              Will my file quality be reduced?
              For merging, splitting, and rotating: quality is preserved. For compression, only minimal compression is done in-browser.
              Can I merge, split, compress, and encrypt confidential documents safely?
              Yes. Since everything is processed locally in your browser, your confidential files remain private and secure.
