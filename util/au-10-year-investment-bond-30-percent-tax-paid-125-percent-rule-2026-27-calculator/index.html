Australia 10-Year Investment Bond Calculator 2026–27

Compare a tax-paid investment bond with direct investing and see how a contribution increase can restart the 10-year clock.

Key takeaways

  • After 10 full policy years, an eligible withdrawal has no additional personal tax under section 26AH.
  • Tax-paid bonds can favour income-heavy portfolios at a 47% marginal rate. Low-yield growth assets can favour direct holding when the 50% CGT discount applies.
  • As little as one cent over the payable 125% ceiling can restart the clock. A skipped assurance year leaves a zero ceiling for the next year.

How the estimate works

The model assumes a tax-offset-eligible Australian investment bond and a full surrender. It estimates the bonus as positive bond growth above total premiums. The bond’s effective rate approximates annual internal tax on income and positive capital growth. The direct portfolio reinvests after-tax cash income, accounts for entered franking credits and defers CGT until sale. The last deposit and the preceding year's reinvested income get no CGT discount because they have been held for just under 12 months.

Actual returns, fees, asset turnover, tax credits, capital loss carryforwards and the withdrawal bonus reported by your provider can affect the outcome. The estimate assumes a constant marginal rate and excludes the Medicare levy surcharge. Use the policy start date and your provider’s premium records to confirm the exact tax-free date.

Frequently asked questions

When is an investment bond withdrawal free of additional personal tax?

For an eligible bond, section 26AH no longer includes a withdrawal bonus in assessable income after the full 10-year eligible period has elapsed. A premium increase above the 125% limit starts a new period at the beginning of that assurance year.

How much of an early withdrawal bonus is taxable?

The assessable share is 100% in policy years 1–8, two-thirds in year 9 and one-third in year 10. Where eligible, the 30% non-refundable offset applies to that assessable share. The calculator estimates the bonus as positive growth above total premiums on full surrender.

Can I contribute again after skipping a policy year?

Yes, but a positive premium after a zero-premium assurance year on an existing policy triggers a reset. Check premium totals by policy anniversary and ask your provider about a separate policy before paying any excess.

Can a low-yield growth portfolio do better when held directly?

Yes. A 47% marginal rate produces a 23.5% effective rate on gains eligible for the 50% CGT discount under the 2026–27 comparison rules. Deferring CGT until sale can also favour direct holding. Reforms for gains accruing from 1 July 2027 can change that result.

Why can the bond’s effective tax rate be below 30%?

The life insurer’s ordinary taxable income rate is 30%. Franking credits can reduce its effective tax cost. Use the provider’s effective rate if available and account for eligible direct franking credits when comparing the same portfolio.
