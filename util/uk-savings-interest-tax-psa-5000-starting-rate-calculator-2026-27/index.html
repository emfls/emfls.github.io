How the estimate works

Gross interest is balance × AER ÷ 100. The model tapers the Personal Allowance by £1 for each £2 of total income above £100,000, reaching £0 at £125,140. It applies the allowance to non-savings income first, then uses any left over against savings interest, followed by the starting rate and PSA. The 0% savings bands still occupy space in the tax bands. Taxable interest is split across the applicable rates.

The tax-free balance is the largest ordinary savings balance that keeps estimated tax on all savings interest at £0. It accounts for other interest and both PSA cliffs, and rounds the balance down to the penny. If your other interest already incurs tax, the limit is £0.

Net AER uses the extra savings tax compared with leaving this balance out. The Cash ISA comparison shelters as much as your remaining room allows and keeps the rest in ordinary savings at the same AER. The estimate covers savings tax only under the stated allowance allocation. Any effect of the taper on tax due on wages, pensions or rent is outside the estimate, as are dividends, reliefs that alter allowances or bands, fees and alternative allocations of the Personal Allowance.

Frequently asked questions

How much savings interest can I earn tax-free in 2026/27?

The Personal Savings Allowance is £1,000 at the basic rate, £500 at the higher rate and £0 at the additional rate. Unused Personal Allowance and the starting rate for savings can cover more. Someone with no other income can earn up to £18,570 in savings interest tax-free.

How does the £5,000 starting rate for savings work?

Each £1 of non-savings income above your Personal Allowance reduces your £5,000 starting-rate band by £1. With the standard £12,570 Personal Allowance, £15,000 of non-savings income leaves £2,570 at the starting rate. The band reaches £0 at £17,570.

Can bank interest move me into a higher tax band?

Yes. Taxable-account interest counts when determining your band, including interest covered by the Personal Savings Allowance. In this calculator's standard bands, crossing £50,270 cuts the allowance to £500 and crossing £125,140 removes it. Cash ISA interest stays outside that calculation.

What rates does Budget 2026 propose from 6 April 2027?

Budget 2026 proposes savings rates of 24%, 44% and 49%, four percentage points above the 2026/27 rates of 20%, 40% and 45%. The starting rate and Personal Savings Allowance are unchanged in the proposal. The new rates are in Finance Bill 2026–27.

How does a Cash ISA change my after-tax AER?

Cash ISA interest is tax-free. At the same AER, the portion held inside an ISA earns the advertised rate while the rest may incur tax. The annual ISA subscription limit is £20,000 across your ISAs. Enter your remaining room to compare the return on your full balance.
