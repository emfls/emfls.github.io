Frequently asked questions

When does New York’s 2026 estate tax credit reach zero?

The credit begins to phase out above $7,350,000 and reaches zero at $7,717,500. At that point, the full tentative tax of $734,780 applies. One more dollar adds about 14 cents of tax.

Can a lifetime gift push an estate over the cliff?

Yes. Certain taxable gifts made in the three years ending on the date of death increase the New York estate tax base. Exclude amounts already in the federal gross estate and gifts that qualify for a statutory exception.

How does a Santa Clause bequest increase what heirs receive?

A fully deductible charitable bequest equal to the excess over the basic exclusion can bring the taxable estate back to the exclusion. Heirs gain when the tax saved exceeds the bequest cost. The modeled 2026 break-even is approximately $8,142,990.65 of New York taxable estate.

How is the applicable credit calculated during the phase-out?

Subtract the taxable estate from the 105% threshold and multiply the difference by 20. Apply the statutory tax table to that reduced tax base to get the credit. The ET-706 worksheet carries its phase-out ratio to four decimal places for filing.

Does the $15 million federal exclusion eliminate New York estate tax?

The 2026 federal basic exclusion is $15 million and New York’s is $7.35 million. A New York resident estate can owe state estate tax even when its federal filing base is below the federal threshold. Check New York and federal filing rules separately.
