Avoid a surprise bill at lodgement

A reportable fringe benefits amount from an EV novated lease, reportable salary-sacrificed super, or a negative-gearing add-back can lift your HELP repayment above the amount your employer withheld. Check your income statement for the reportable amounts and budget for any shortfall. A voluntary payment reduces debt, while the compulsory repayment still applies to any eligible balance and repayment income above the threshold.

How the payoff estimate works

Each cycle applies the voluntary lump sum in the first year only, then the 1 June indexation, then the compulsory repayment at assessment, capped at the remaining balance. The estimate assumes the entire balance is eligible for indexation, which generally applies to debt unpaid for more than 11 months. The ATO uses the lower of CPI and WPI for the actual rate.

The projection holds the 2026–27 brackets, add-backs and indexation assumption constant for up to 50 years. Wage growth applies to taxable income only. Estimated years count annual repayment cycles. The model assumes one HELP balance and excludes new borrowing, overseas levies and low-family-income Medicare levy exceptions.

HECS-HELP repayment FAQ

When do compulsory HELP repayments start in 2026–27?

Repayment starts when your repayment income exceeds $69,528. At $80,000 of repayment income, the published formula gives $1,570.80 before any cap for the debt outstanding.

What gets added back to taxable income for HELP?

Add total net investment losses, reportable fringe benefits, reportable super contributions and exempt foreign employment income. For example, $68,000 of taxable income and $5,000 of reportable fringe benefits give $73,000 of repayment income and a formula repayment of $520.80.

Why can my payslip withholding differ from my HELP repayment?

Your employer applies ATO withholding rules to the pay they issue. The ATO uses your full-year repayment income at assessment, including relevant amounts from outside payroll. Extra withholding is a tax credit and does not reduce your HELP loan each payday.

ATO employer guidance

How does paying before 1 June reduce indexation?

A voluntary payment credited before 1 June can reduce the balance exposed to indexation. At an assumed 3.2%, a $2,000 payment against eligible debt avoids $64 of indexation that year. The ATO advises allowing up to four business days for electronic payments to appear.

ATO voluntary repayment timing

How did the old HELP repayment cliff work?

In 2024–25 the applicable rate was charged on all repayment income. Crossing from $54,434 to $54,435 raised the calculated repayment from zero to $544.35. The 2026–27 formula begins at 15 cents per dollar above its threshold.
