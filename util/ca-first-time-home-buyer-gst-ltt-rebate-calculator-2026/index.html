Canada First-Time Home Buyer Rebate Calculator 2026

Estimate up to $50,000 in federal GST relief, Ontario and Toronto land transfer tax refunds, BC property transfer tax exemptions, and the federal home buyers’ credit.

Key takeaways

Eligible new homes at or below $1 million can recover the full 5% federal GST. Ontario and Toronto refunds can save up to $8,475 combined. In BC, a qualifying new build can save up to $20,000 in property transfer tax. The $1,400 federal credit is claimed on your 2026 return.

Before you count on the rebate at closing

For an eligible new home, moving from $1 million to $1.25 million cuts federal relief from $50,000 to $25,000. At $1.5 million it falls to zero. Check whether the builder credits the rebate, whether your price already includes it, and when transfer tax relief is applied. Budget for any amounts you must pay before reimbursement and claim the federal income tax credit when you file.

Frequently asked questions

How much federal GST can a first-time buyer recover?

For an eligible builder purchase, federal relief can cover the full 5% at or below $1 million. It falls from $50,000 to zero between $1 million and $1.5 million. A $1.25 million purchase can qualify for $25,000. The original builder agreement must be signed from March 20, 2025 through December 31, 2030, and other CRA conditions apply. Ordinary resales receive zero under this program.

Can I claim both Ontario and Toronto land transfer tax refunds?

Eligible buyers of homes in the City of Toronto can receive up to $4,000 from Ontario and up to $4,475 from Toronto, limited by the corresponding taxes. The refunds can apply to new or resale homes. Toronto’s higher luxury rates apply from April 1, 2026, to the portions above $3 million for properties with one or two single-family residences.

Which BC property transfer tax exemption applies to a new home?

The general first-time buyer program can save up to $8,000 and phases out between $835,000 and $860,000. The newly built home exemption can eliminate the tax through $1.1 million and phases out to zero at $1.15 million. The calculator uses the larger eligible exemption. Each program has its own eligibility and occupancy conditions.

How much is the 2026 Home Buyers’ Tax Credit on line 31270?

The maximum federal credit is $1,400, calculated as the $10,000 Home Buyers’ Amount times the 14% federal rate. Eligible co-buyers can share the $10,000 claim. The credit reduces federal income tax when you file and its value depends on how much federal tax you owe.

Can two buyers combine their FHSA and Home Buyers’ Plan withdrawals?

Two eligible buyers with sufficient funds can plan around $200,000 using $40,000 in lifetime FHSA contributions and up to $60,000 in RRSP Home Buyers’ Plan withdrawals per person. Qualifying FHSA withdrawals can include investment growth. Home Buyers’ Plan withdrawals generally need to be repaid to the RRSP.
