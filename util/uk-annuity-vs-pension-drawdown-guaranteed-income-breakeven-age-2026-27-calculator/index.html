⚠️ Longevity Risk vs Inheritance Trade-Off

Pension drawdown gives you complete flexibility and lets you pass remaining funds to your children. However, withdrawing above 5% per year significantly increases the probability of running completely out of money in your 80s. A lifetime annuity eliminates market crash anxiety and outliving your capital.
