Canada Part XIII Withholding Tax Calculator 2026

Compare NR301 treaty rates for Canadian dividends, pensions, RRIF and RRSP withdrawals. Check how much a Section 217 election could refund.

Key takeaways

  • Canada generally withholds 25% on taxable Part XIII payments. Portfolio dividends commonly drop to 15% for eligible treaty residents. Ordinary non-participating arm’s-length interest is generally exempt.
  • Qualifying periodic pensions and RRIF payments can be withheld at 15% for U.S. residents and 0% for U.K. residents. A lump-sum RRSP withdrawal generally stays at 25% for both.
  • Section 217 may refund part of the withholding on eligible retirement income. The calculation uses worldwide income and compares graduated tax with the Part XIII tax due.

Frequently asked questions

Who receives Form NR301?

Give Form NR301 or acceptable equivalent evidence to the Canadian payer. It establishes beneficial ownership, treaty residence and eligibility for the particular income. The payer keeps the documentation. Some relief, such as Ireland’s pension exemption, requires a separate CRA authorization.

How does the RRIF periodic-payment limit work?

For a standard RRIF, the limit is the greater of twice the applicable annual minimum or 10% of the January 1 fund value. The payment that takes the year-to-date total over the limit, and any subsequent payment that year, is classified as non-periodic. The treaty determines which rate applies.

Does the U.S. treaty reduce a lump-sum RRSP withdrawal?

A lump-sum withdrawal before RRSP maturity generally remains subject to 25% Canadian withholding for a U.S. treaty resident. Qualifying periodic pensions and RRIF payments can be withheld at 15%. Periodic CPP and OAS benefits paid to U.S. treaty residents are exempt from Canadian Part XIII withholding.

How does Ireland’s pension exemption affect withholding?

Article 18 limits Canadian tax on qualifying periodic pensions to the lesser of 15% of the annual amount above C$12,000 and the treaty’s Canadian-resident tax comparison. Request CRA authorization through NR5 so eligible payers can apply the exemption at source.

How does a Section 217 election generate a refund?

A timely election lets eligible non-residents compare the Canadian Part XIII tax due on qualifying retirement income with graduated federal tax, the non-resident surtax and applicable credits and adjustments. The 90% test governs access to full credits. The refund depends on eligible tax actually withheld and the tax payable on the return.
