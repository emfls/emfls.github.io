UK 4-Year FIG Regime Break-Even Calculator 2026/27

Compare estimated UK Income Tax and Capital Gains Tax before giving up your allowances to claim Foreign Income and Gains relief.

Key takeaways

  • A FIG claim saves UK tax when the relief is worth more than the allowances you give up, after any foreign tax credits.
  • You need 10 consecutive non-UK-resident tax years before your four-year window begins. You must be UK tax resident in the claim year.
  • The regime replaced the non-dom remittance basis on 6 April 2025. Amounts relieved by a FIG claim can be brought to the UK without a further UK tax charge.

Claiming FIG can increase your UK tax

Even a claim on one small source removes both the Personal Allowance and CGT exemption for the whole tax year. If UK income alone reaches £125,140, the standard Personal Allowance is already £0. The remaining modelled allowance cost is then up to £720 of CGT on UK gains.

What the estimate covers

The credits approximate one foreign income source and one foreign gains source. The estimate uses entered income as adjusted net income before the Personal Allowance. It excludes savings interest, dividends, capital losses, special CGT reliefs, pension or Gift Aid adjustments, National Insurance, legacy remittances, split-year allocation and Overseas Workday Relief. Marriage and Blind Person’s allowances may also be lost. The estimate assigns them no value.

HMRC requires a Self Assessment election for the amounts claimed. Confirm each source’s eligibility and foreign tax treatment for your return. See HMRC helpsheet HS266.

Frequently asked questions

Who qualifies for the 4-year FIG regime?

You must be UK tax resident in the claim year and within the four consecutive tax years starting with your first UK-resident year after at least 10 consecutive non-UK-resident tax years. A temporary non-resident year uses up one year of the window. A split year counts as UK resident for this check.

Can I bring FIG-relieved income and gains to the UK?

Yes. Qualifying amounts relieved by a valid FIG claim can be brought to the UK without an additional UK tax charge. Historic amounts from the old remittance basis follow separate rules.

Which allowances do I lose if I claim only foreign gains?

A claim for income, gains or both removes both the Income Tax Personal Allowance and the CGT Annual Exempt Amount for that tax year. Marriage Allowance, Married Couple’s Allowance and Blind Person’s Allowance are also lost where applicable.

How does foreign tax credit affect break-even?

The arising-basis estimate limits each credit to the eligible foreign tax entered or the UK tax attributable to that source, whichever is lower. A fully offset foreign liability can leave little UK tax to save. A FIG-relieved amount cannot also receive foreign tax credit relief.

When is the Personal Allowance already worth £0?

The standard Personal Allowance falls by £1 per £2 of adjusted net income over £100,000 and is fully withdrawn at £125,140. If UK income alone reaches that level in this model, the remaining modelled allowance cost is up to £720 of CGT on UK gains.
