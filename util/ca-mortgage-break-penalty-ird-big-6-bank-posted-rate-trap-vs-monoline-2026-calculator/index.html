Key takeaways

  • A closed fixed mortgage can cost more than three months’ interest to break when the interest rate differential (IRD) is larger.
  • Subtracting your original discount from a comparable posted rate can widen the IRD and increase your penalty.
  • An eligible lump sum can shrink the penalty. Refinancing pays before renewal only when interest savings cover the penalty and switching fees.

Refinance now or wait for renewal?

Each refinance scenario compares waiting on the same starting balance. The lump-sum cases assume you’d make the same payment if you waited. The model holds the balance and rates constant through renewal, with fees paid upfront. It excludes scheduled principal payments and the cost of giving up access to your cash.

A lower rate can still cost you money. Pay particular attention to the net loss and the cash needed upfront. Get a dated payout quote and confirm the timing of any lump sum before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

How is a closed fixed mortgage penalty estimated?

This calculator takes the higher of three months’ interest or the interest rate differential (IRD). For IRD, it multiplies the positive rate difference by the balance and the fraction of a year left. Your lender’s quote may use a different term match, day count or present-value calculation.

Why can a posted-rate discount raise the IRD?

In the posted-rate model, the lender subtracts the original discount from today’s posted rate for a comparable term. That lowers the comparison rate and can widen the gap versus your contract rate. A contract-rate model compares directly with the lender’s current comparable rate.

What rate should I enter for a closed variable mortgage?

Enter your current mortgage rate in the contract-rate field. The calculator uses three months’ interest and skips both IRD comparisons. Check which rate and day count your lender uses for the actual charge.

Can I use my lump-sum privilege before breaking the mortgage?

Ask your lender to confirm your unused allowance and when you can apply it before payout. Some allowances use the original principal. Convert the eligible dollar amount to a percentage of your current balance for this calculator.

What refinance rate breaks even before renewal?

Subtract total switching costs divided by the balance and years remaining from your current annual rate, using rates as decimals. A lower offered rate gives a modeled gain. The estimate holds the balance and rates constant until renewal and assumes switching costs are paid upfront.
