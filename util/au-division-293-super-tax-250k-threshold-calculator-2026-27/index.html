Australia Division 293 Tax Calculator 2026–27

Estimate the extra tax on concessional super contributions and compare paying from cash with releasing money from super.

Key takeaways

Division 293 adds 15% to the smaller of your eligible concessional contributions and the amount your combined Division 293 income and contributions exceed A$250,000.

The 2026–27 concessional cap is A$32,500 before eligible carry-forward. Payday Super is 12% on up to A$270,830 in annual qualifying earnings per employer.

Check what you could give up by skipping salary sacrifice

If a voluntary contribution fits within your concessional cap and is fully subject to Division 293, the typical 15% fund contributions tax + 15% Division 293 tax = 30%.

For a dollar otherwise taxed above A$190,000 at the 45% Australian resident marginal rate plus the full 2% Medicare levy, 47% − 30% = 17 percentage points. On A$1,000 entirely within those assumptions, that is A$170 less tax. Check cap headroom, your effective marginal rate and when you can access the money before deciding.

Frequently asked questions

Can I owe Division 293 tax if my taxable income is below $250,000?

Yes. The threshold applies to your Division 293 income plus your eligible concessional contributions. If you have $240,000 of Division 293 income and $15,000 in eligible contributions, the combined total is $255,000 and the tax is 15% of $5,000, or $750.

Do rental and share investment losses reduce Division 293 income?

Net rental property and net financial investment losses are added back to taxable income for Division 293. Enter them as positive amounts and use net losses, rather than gross expenses or investment losses you could not deduct against income.

Do contributions above $32,500 count for Division 293?

Contributions within your applicable cap count, including any eligible carry-forward cap used. Excess concessional contributions are generally excluded from the contribution side of Division 293 and taxed at your marginal rate with a 15% offset for fund contributions tax. ATO determinations can change their treatment.

Can salary sacrifice still save tax with Division 293?

Yes. A dollar that would otherwise face the 45% top resident marginal rate and 2% Medicare levy can face 30% in total tax if it is contributed within the concessional cap and fully subject to Division 293. That is a 17-percentage-point difference under those assumptions. Super access restrictions still apply.

How long do I have to elect to pay from super?

You generally have 60 days from the date of your Division 293 assessment to elect to release money from super. The election window does not extend the payment due date on your notice. The ATO sends a release authority to your nominated fund after you lodge a valid election.
