ATO Vehicle Cost Limit & GST Rules (2026–27 Financial Year)

Under section 40-230 of the Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 (ITAA 1997), the Australian Taxation Office applies strict limits on depreciation deductions and GST input tax credits for motor vehicles designed principally for carrying passengers:

1. The Statutory $69,883 Car Cost Limit

  • Indexed Limit: For the financial year beginning 1 July 2026, the ATO indexed car limit is set at $69,883. If you acquire a passenger car for more than $69,883, the excess cost cannot be depreciated, claimed under capital allowances, or added to a depreciation pool.
  • Permanent Loss: Unlike Luxury Car Tax (LCT) which is payable at purchase, the depreciation cap creates a permanent loss of tax deductions for the vehicle's entire ownership life.

2. The $6,353 Maximum GST Input Tax Credit

Regardless of whether you paid $80,000 or $150,000 for a passenger car, the maximum GST credit you can claim on your BAS is strictly capped at one-eleventh of the car limit: $6,353 ($69,883 / 11), multiplied by your business use percentage.

3. The 1-Tonne Payload Commercial Exemption

Commercial vehicles—including dual-cab utes, vans, and flatbeds designed to carry goods with a payload capacity of 1,000 kg (1 tonne) or greater—are legally classified as commercial vehicles under Taxation Ruling TR 2018/3. They are 100% exempt from the $69,883 car limit and eligible for uncapped GST credits and accelerated depreciation.
