Key takeaways

A qualifying 1031 exchange can defer gain on investment or business real estate. Cash kept and net debt relief can trigger tax up to your realized gain. Cash you add can offset debt relief, while a larger new mortgage leaves cash boot taxable.

Protect your tax deferral before closing

Receiving sale proceeds directly or missing a deadline can put the deferral at risk. Arrange a qualified intermediary before transferring the old property in a typical deferred exchange. Count calendar days. A weekend or holiday gives no automatic extension, and your federal return due date can shorten the 180-day window.

Frequently asked questions

Can a larger replacement mortgage offset cash boot?

No. A larger loan cannot erase cash you keep. Additional cash paid into the exchange can offset a reduction in debt, down to zero. In this model, recognized gain is the lesser of realized gain and total boot.

Is all depreciation taxed at 25%?

For a long-held rental building with straight-line depreciation, this estimate allocates recognized gain to unrecaptured Section 1250 gain first, up to accumulated depreciation, and uses its 25% maximum federal rate. Actual tax can be lower. Ordinary-income recapture for additional depreciation or Section 1245 property requires a separate calculation.

How does deferred gain affect replacement basis?

In the gain-only model, subtract deferred gain from the replacement price plus eligible capitalizable closing costs. A lower basis can reduce future depreciation and increase the gain on a later taxable sale. Reconcile replacement equity and exchange funds before using the estimate.

When are the 45-day and 180-day deadlines?

Count both periods in calendar days from transfer of the relinquished property. Identify replacement property in writing within 45 days and receive it by day 180 or the federal return due date including valid extensions, whichever is earlier. Weekends and holidays do not automatically extend the day counts.

What does tax deferred today mean?

It is the estimated tax on a taxable sale minus estimated tax on the recognized exchange gain using the same rate assumptions. Deferred gain generally carries into the replacement property through a lower basis. The estimate applies optional NIIT and state tax only to regular long-term gain, and actual liability can differ.
