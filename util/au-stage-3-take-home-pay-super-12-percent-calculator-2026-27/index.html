Frequently asked questions

How much does the 15% bracket save me?

At $45,000 or more in taxable income, the rate cut reduces annual income tax by $268 after LITO. Below $45,000, savings depend on the amount in the affected bracket and whether LITO already covers some or all of your income tax.

How do I work out my cash salary from a package that includes super?

With minimum employer super and all cash pay qualifying, divide the package by 1.12 up to a package of $303,329.60. Above that, subtract the maximum compulsory contribution of $32,499.60. Check your contract for contributions above the legal minimum.

Does the 15% HELP rate apply to all my income?

Between $69,529 and $129,717 of repayment income, you repay 15 cents per dollar above $69,528. The next tier uses a $9,028 base plus 17 cents per dollar above $129,717. From $186,051, the rate is 10% of total repayment income.

Does private hospital cover remove the Medicare levy?

Eligible private hospital cover can remove the surcharge for the days you are covered. The ordinary Medicare levy generally still applies, subject to its own reductions and exemptions. This calculator models the surcharge for singles with no eligible cover all year.

Why might my payslip differ from the calculator?

The weekly, fortnightly and monthly figures spread estimated annual liabilities evenly. Payroll uses ATO withholding schedules and may reflect bonuses, pay dates, your tax declarations and super eligibility. The ATO reconciles withholding against your annual liability after you lodge.
