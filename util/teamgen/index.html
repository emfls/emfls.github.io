❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What happens to the names I enter?
The generator uses the names in this tab without sending the list to a server or saving it. Google Analytics and Google AdSense may process device and usage data for analytics and advertising.
Can I create more than 20 teams?
Currently, the maximum is 20 to keep things readable. You can split again if needed.
Can I copy or export the team results?
Yes! You can select and copy the results manually, or screenshot for quick sharing.
Can I control team balance or size?
No, the shuffle is random. Teams may vary in size when names don’t divide evenly.
Will the shuffle change each time?
Yes. Every click produces a fresh, randomized team distribution.
