What the projection includes

One owner, one residential property and the same constant interest-only balance on both routes. The SPV is a UK company eligible for Marginal Relief, letting commercially to unconnected tenants, with a full 12-month accounting period, no associates or other profits, and fully deductible borrowing costs. The calculation assumes ordinary other income only, an unused dividend allowance and no carried-in reliefs or losses.

Each year repeats the 2026/27 cash and tax calculation. Cash is added at year-end, with growth on positive opening balances and no borrowing charge on accumulated shortfalls. Later-year rule changes, loss and unused relief carryforwards, purchase or transfer taxes, property value changes, principal repayments, sale taxes and final SPV withdrawals are outside the estimate. Company balances are before any later personal extraction tax. Future property tax rates differ from this baseline. GOV.UK rates and allowances

Frequently asked questions

How does Section 24 reduce mortgage interest relief?

The individual calculates rental profit before mortgage interest. The 20% tax reduction uses the lowest of eligible finance costs, property profit and eligible income above the Personal Allowance. Unused finance costs may carry forward.

Why can rental income face a 60% marginal Income Tax rate?

Between £100,000 and £125,140 of adjusted net income, an additional £2 of ordinary income removes £1 of Personal Allowance. At the 40% higher rate, that adds 20 percentage points to the marginal charge before finance cost relief.

How are profits taxed when a letting company pays dividends?

An eligible letting company deducts qualifying mortgage interest before Corporation Tax. Dividends come from post-tax profits and stack above other income. The £500 dividend allowance uses band space and a dividend can also reduce the Personal Allowance.

What does the 10-year retained SPV balance measure?

It accumulates post-Corporation-Tax cash left in the company, using the entered net return on positive balances. Shareholders may owe tax when they later take money out. The same net return applies to saved personal cash for comparison.

What mortgage spread makes the SPV break even?

It is the company mortgage rate at which the selected strategy ties personal ownership, minus the personal mortgage rate. Below that spread the company route has the higher modelled wealth, holding the other inputs fixed.
