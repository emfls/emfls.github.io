Frequently asked questions

What is the 2026 maximum for self-employed CPP and CPP2?

With a full CPP contribution year and no T4 employment, the maximum is $9,292.90 at $85,000 or more in net pensionable self-employment income. That includes $8,460.90 of CPP and $832.00 of CPP2.

Which CPP amounts go on lines 22200 and 31000?

Line 22200 deducts the employer-equivalent half of base CPP and both halves of the first enhancement and CPP2. Line 31000 claims the employee-equivalent half of base CPP as a federal non-refundable credit base. The corresponding provincial credit is generally claimed on line 58280.

How does a T4 side job affect self-employed CPP?

Employment uses the shared $3,500 exemption and available earnings bands first under standard payroll withholding. Even a small T4 can use some exemption and raise CPP on business income. For filing, reconcile your actual T4 box 16 and 16A contributions using Schedule 8.

Can I stop making CPP contributions at age 65?

Working beneficiaries aged 65–69 who already receive a CPP or QPP retirement pension can elect to stop. Self-employed-only filers generally make the election on Schedule 8. Those with a CPP-covered employer generally use Form CPT30. An election taking effect midyear requires Schedule 8 proration.

What does the quarterly CPP reserve cover?

The reserve spreads gross estimated CPP payable across four quarters, rounded up to the cent. Required CRA instalments depend on your net tax owing and the instalment method you use.
