How the estimate works

The model subtracts prior LCGE use and applies the remaining room to each claimant’s share. Regular gain tax is the excess gain × 50% × the combined marginal rate. Federal adjusted taxable income is other taxable income plus 30% of the sheltered gain plus all excess gain. The calculator applies 20.5% above the federal exemption and subtracts the regular federal comparator, flooring additional tax at zero.

Percentage-based provincial scenarios apply their factor to the federal excess and use that factor for projected provincial recovery. The Québec scenario applies its own threshold and rate to the modeled adjusted income, compares that result with regular Québec tax, and projects recovery separately.

Planning estimate. The model excludes AMT credits and other return adjustments, including the Québec federal abatement, capital losses, cumulative net investment loss restrictions and older AMT balances. Confirm eligibility, actual AMT and carryover on your tax forms.

Frequently asked questions

Which gains qualify for the lifetime capital gains exemption?

Qualifying gains can come from qualified small business corporation shares or qualified farm or fishing property. The 2026 lifetime limit is $1,275,000 in gross gain per eligible individual. Prior claims and other limits can reduce the deduction available.

Can a fully sheltered gain trigger federal AMT?

Yes. The federal AMT calculation generally includes 30% of the gain sheltered by the LCGE and 100% of excess gain. Federal AMT uses a 20.5% rate above the $181,440 exemption in 2026, with applicable adjustments and credits.

How much tax do I need to pay to recover AMT?

The available federal carryover credit depends on how much ordinary federal tax exceeds minimum tax in each of the following seven years. Unused credit generally expires after that window. The calculator assumes the same tax amounts each year.

Can my spouse or family trust beneficiaries each claim the LCGE?

Multiple eligible people can each use their own available LCGE if they receive qualifying gains through ownership or a valid trust allocation and designation. Simply being related does not grant a claim. Check ownership, attribution and each person's deduction limit before relying on the group estimate.

Does Québec apply 19% to the federal AMT amount?

Québec calculates its own minimum tax using a 19% rate and a $183,680 exemption in 2026. It compares the result with Québec tax under its own rules. Federal T691 and Québec TP-776.42-V are separate calculations.
