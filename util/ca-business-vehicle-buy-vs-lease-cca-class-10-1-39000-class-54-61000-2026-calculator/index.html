CRA Automobile Deduction Rules & Tax Traps

What is the difference between Class 10.1 and Class 54 ZEV?

Class 10.1 applies to luxury and standard passenger vehicles with a hard cost ceiling of $39,000 for 2026. Any purchase price paid above $39,000 cannot be depreciated. Class 54 applies to zero-emission electric and plug-in hybrid passenger vehicles with an elevated $61,000 ceiling before tax.

How does the CRA personal taxable standby charge work?

If an employee or shareholder drives a corporate-owned vehicle for personal reasons, they must pay personal income tax on a standby charge of 2% per month (24% per year) of the original vehicle cost, plus an operating expense benefit of $0.33 per personal kilometre. This personal tax penalty often wipes out the corporate tax write-off!

Why does the $0.73/$0.67 per-km reimbursement avoid all tax traps?

When the vehicle is owned personally, the corporation pays the shareholder/employee a statutory reasonable allowance of $0.73/km (first 5,000 km) and $0.67/km. The corporation deducts 100% of this payment from its taxable profits, and the recipient receives it 100% tax-free with zero standby charges.
