Frequently asked questions

How fast does compound interest double lifetime mortgage debt?

At 6.10% AER, lifetime mortgage roll-up debt doubles approximately every 11.7 years.

What is the No Negative Equity Guarantee?

An ERC-mandated rule guaranteeing that neither you nor your beneficiaries will ever have to repay more than the gross proceeds from selling the home.

Can I make voluntary interest payments?

Yes, all ERC-compliant mortgages allow borrowers to make flexible repayments to keep debt balances flat.

Are voluntary interest payments compulsory?

No, they are optional. You can cease payments at any time without penalty or risk of foreclosure.

How does equity release affect Means-Tested State Benefits?

Taking a cash lump sum may impact eligibility for Pension Credit and Universal Credit. Professional financial advice is strongly advised.

Calculation assumptions and related tools

Projections use standard annual compounding (AER). Property values assume continuous annual inflation. Early repayment charges (ERCs) and solicitor fees are excluded.
