B.C. Speculation and Vacancy Tax Calculator 2026

Estimate tax for your share of a B.C. home, compare the 1% and 3% rates, and see what a qualifying tenancy could save.

Key takeaways

  • 2026 rates are 1% and 3%. The lower rate doubled from 0.5%. The higher rate rose from 2% and is scheduled to reach 4% in 2027.
  • Up to $4,000 in B.C. resident credit. When fully available, the ownership-adjusted credit offsets the tax on the first $400,000 of taxable assessed property value.
  • A qualifying principal residence or six qualifying tenancy months can reduce 2026 tax to $0. Required declarations are still due March 31, 2027.

Calculate your 2026 tax

Enter the taxable residential value from BC Assessment and the interest for one owner. Run the estimate separately for each co-owner.

Enter the eligible balance under the SVT Act after any income transfers. It can include unused amounts from the prior two years. The estimate assumes no credit dollars have been claimed on another property this year.

Choose an exemption only after confirming the statutory conditions.

An arm’s-length tenant needs a written agreement and must make the unit their home. A non-arm’s-length tenant needs permission and must live there longer than anywhere else in each qualifying month. Owners and their spouses cannot count as their own tenants.

Count distinct months from eligible occupancy periods of at least one month each. For mixed tenancies, count only qualifying months.

If any related tenant fails this test, select arm’s-length tenancy and count only those eligible months.

Use your share of added rental costs, such as management, insurance and preparing the home.

Frequently asked questions

Which tax rate applies to my ownership share?

For 2026, the lower rate is 1% for eligible B.C. residents and specified Canadian citizens or permanent residents. The higher rate is 3% for foreign owners and untaxed worldwide earners. Each owner applies their own rate to their share of the taxable assessed value.

Can the B.C. resident credit eliminate the tax on a $400,000 property?

Yes, if the full share-adjusted credit is available. At 1%, the $4,000 maximum credit covers the first $400,000 of taxable property value. A 50% owner of a $400,000 home has $2,000 of gross tax and a maximum $2,000 credit for that property.

Do six months of renting qualify?

The tenancy can qualify if occupancy totals at least six months in the calendar year and each qualifying period lasts at least a month. An arm’s-length tenant generally needs a written tenancy agreement and must make the unit their home. A related tenant must meet separate tests, including a B.C. residency and income test for higher-rate owners.

When do I declare if I owe $0?

Owners required to declare in designated taxable areas must file for the 2026 tax year by March 31, 2027, even if they qualify for an exemption. Each co-owner files a separate declaration. Failing to file can trigger an assessment at the 2026 default rate of 3%.

What changes in 2027?

The higher rate is scheduled to rise to 4% for the 2027 tax year. The 2027 preview holds your assessed value and ownership share constant. Check your 2027 assessment and eligibility before relying on the estimate.
