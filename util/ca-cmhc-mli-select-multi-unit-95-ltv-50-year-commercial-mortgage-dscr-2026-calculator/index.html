Frequently asked questions

What is CMHC MLI Select?

CMHC MLI Select is an insurance program offering multi-family real estate investors up to 95% LTV, 50-year amortizations, and reduced debt coverage ratios (1.10x DCR) based on affordability, energy efficiency, and accessibility points.

How does Canadian semi-annual interest compounding work?

Under the Canadian Interest Act, mortgages compound semi-annually not in advance, using the effective monthly conversion factor rm = (1 + r/2)^(2/12) - 1.

How much down payment does CMHC MLI Select save?

On a $5M multi-family acquisition, conventional 75% commercial debt requires over $2,041,000 down due to 1.25x DCR limits. CMHC MLI Select requires only $250,000 down, saving over $1,791,000 in equity.

How do you achieve 100 points?

Points are earned by combining affordability commitments (20%+ units below 30% median income), energy efficiency improvements (25%+ to 40%+ reductions over NECB), and accessibility upgrades.

Can you refinance existing properties with MLI Select?

Yes, MLI Select is available for both new construction and refinancing existing multi-family rental properties.
