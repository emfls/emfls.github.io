How the estimate works

Both plans charge 9% on earnings above their threshold. Plan 2 starts at £29,385 a year (£2,448.75 monthly or £565.09 weekly). Plan 5 repayments began in April 2026 at £25,000 a year (£2,083.33 monthly or £480.76 weekly). Monthly results are the annual estimate divided by twelve.

Plan 2’s £29,385 and £52,885 thresholds are held through 2029/30. Plan 5 uses the announced £26,025 threshold for 2027/28. The growth assumption applies to each plan after those years. Plan 2’s first-year cap can be toggled off for an uncapped comparison.

For someone above the loan threshold, the marginal deduction on an extra pound is normally 37% in the basic band (20% tax, 8% NI, 9% loan) or 51% in the higher band before the Personal Allowance taper (40%, 2%, 9%).

Scope and assumptions

The lifetime model annualises each period and applies interest to the opening balance before deducting payments. It uses the 2026/27 academic-year rate for the first model row and does not prorate the September change against April payroll. Actual SLC interest accrues daily and is applied monthly. PAYE rounding, multiple plans, overseas rules, future caps and future policy changes are outside the estimate. Lifetime totals are nominal. Marginal rates use standard England/Wales income tax and category A employee National Insurance assumptions.

Frequently asked questions

Why is Plan 5 £394.65 more per year at £40,000 or £60,000?

Both plans charge 9% above their thresholds. At or above £29,385, the scheduled annual difference is 9% of £4,385, or £394.65. Actual repayments stop when the loan is cleared or written off.

Which interest thresholds and cap apply to Plan 2?

For 2026/27 the surcharge rises from zero at £29,385 to 3 percentage points at £52,885. A 6.0% cap applies from 1 September 2026 to 31 August 2027. Plan 5 normally charges RPI only.

When can each plan be written off?

Plan 2 is written off 30 years after the April you were first due to repay. Plan 5 is written off after 40 years on the same basis. Use your remaining years if you are already part-way through repayment.
