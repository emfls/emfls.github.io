Converting unsecured debt to secured mortgage debt

Credit card debts and personal loans are unsecured: if you default, creditors cannot seize your home without a lengthy court process. When you roll debt into your mortgage, it becomes secured against your property. A subsequent job loss or rate hike puts your family home at direct risk of bank foreclosure.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 30-year interest trap?

The trap occurs when borrowers refinance short-term debt into a 30-year mortgage to lower monthly payments, inadvertently paying five to ten times more in total lifetime interest.

What is a shadow repayment?

A shadow repayment involves rolling debt into a low-rate mortgage but voluntarily making higher payments to extinguish the debt in 3 years instead of 30.

Does consolidating credit cards hurt my credit score?

Opening a new loan creates a hard credit inquiry, but clearing maxed-out credit card balances rapidly lowers credit utilization, improving your comprehensive credit score.

Can I use a mortgage offset account instead?

Yes, holding funds in a 100% mortgage offset account saves interest at your home loan rate while maintaining liquidity.

Should I cancel my credit cards after consolidating?

Yes. ASIC strongly advises closing consolidated credit card accounts immediately to prevent re-accumulating revolving debt.
