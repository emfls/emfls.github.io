⚡ Key Takeaways: 2026/27 Annual Leave Loading Rules

  • Standard 17.5% Modern Award Bonus: Full-time and part-time permanent employees covered by Australian Modern Awards receive an extra 17.5% on top of their base ordinary weekly pay when taking annual leave.
  • ATO 12.0% Super Guarantee on Loading: Under ATO Ruling SGR 2009/2, leave loading is legally classified as Ordinary Time Earnings (OTE) subject to 12.0% Super Guarantee unless the employer can prove in writing that it compensates for lost overtime.
  • FY 2026–27 Stage 3 Tax Cuts: Your holiday paycheck withholding reflects the Stage 3 individual income tax scale (16% up to $45k, 30% up to $135k, 37% up to $190k, and 45% above) plus the 2% Medicare levy.

⚠️ Warning: Don't Forfeit Super Guarantee on Your Leave Loading

Many Australian payroll systems incorrectly mark the 17.5% leave loading as "super-exempt". Unless your award or employment contract explicitly states the loading is paid to compensate for lost overtime hours (with historical payroll records proving overtime was worked), the ATO mandates that your employer pay the 12.0% Super Guarantee on your loading amount!

Frequently Asked Questions: Australian Annual Leave Loading

What is the standard Australian annual leave loading rate?

The standard annual leave loading rate under most Australian Modern Awards and enterprise agreements is 17.5% of the employee's ordinary weekly rate of pay. It was originally introduced in the 1970s to compensate workers for lost overtime and shift penalty opportunities while taking annual leave.

Is 12.0% Superannuation Guarantee (SG) payable on annual leave loading?

Yes, under ATO Superannuation Guarantee Ruling SGR 2009/2, annual leave loading is classified as Ordinary Time Earnings (OTE) and subject to the 12.0% Super Guarantee unless the employer can produce written evidence showing the loading is demonstrably referable to a lost opportunity to work overtime.

How is tax withheld on annual leave loading in Australia?

Under ATO PAYE guidelines, leave loading is taxed at your marginal income tax rate (including the 2% Medicare levy and any applicable HECS-HELP repayments) for the financial year. If your employer withholds too much tax during your leave period, the excess is reconciled and refunded when you lodge your annual tax return via myGov.

Are shiftworkers entitled to higher than 17.5% leave loading?

Yes. Many Modern Awards specify that continuous shiftworkers receive either 20.0% annual leave loading or the actual shift penalties and weekend loadings they would have received had they worked, whichever is greater.

Is annual leave loading paid out on termination of employment?

Under Section 90(2) of the Fair Work Act 2009, if an employee has untaken accrued annual leave at the time of termination, the employer must pay the amount that would have been payable had the employee taken the leave, which includes the 17.5% leave loading if their applicable Award or enterprise agreement provides for it.
