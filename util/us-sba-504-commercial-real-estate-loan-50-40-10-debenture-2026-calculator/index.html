US SBA 504 Commercial Real Estate Loan Program Rules

The SBA 504 Loan Program provides small businesses with long-term, fixed-rate financing to acquire owner-occupied commercial real estate (must occupy at least 51% for existing buildings or 60% for new construction) and heavy machinery.

1. The 50-40-10 Structure & Down Payment Tiers

  • Bank 1st Mortgage (50%): Provided by a conventional commercial bank (such as JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo, or community lenders) with a senior lien on the property.
  • SBA CDC 2nd Debenture (Up to 40%): Funded 100% by a US government-guaranteed debenture issued through a nonprofit Certified Development Company (CDC). Capped at $5,000,000 standard ($5,500,000 for manufacturers).
  • Borrower Equity Injection (10% to 20%):
    • 10% Standard: Existing business purchasing standard commercial property.
    • 15% Tier: Applies if business is a startup (<2 years) OR property is special-purpose (hotels, gas stations, funeral homes, car washes).
    • 20% Tier: Applies if project involves BOTH a startup AND special-purpose real estate.
