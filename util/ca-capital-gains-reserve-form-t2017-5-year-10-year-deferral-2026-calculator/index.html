Canada Capital Gains Reserve Calculator 2026

Compare five-year and qualifying ten-year deferral on an installment sale or vendor take-back (VTB) note. See what goes on the reserve schedule and how much tax shifts out of 2026.

Key takeaways

Eligible sellers can spread a gain over up to five tax years, or up to ten for qualifying transfers. Your reserve is the smaller of the amount supported by principal due after year-end and the statutory cap.

  • The standard cap starts at 80% of the gain. The qualifying ten-year cap starts at 90%.
  • At the 2026 inclusion rate, half of the capital gain recognized each year enters taxable income in this estimate.
  • First-year tax deferral can be substantial even when total tax savings are zero.

Frequently asked questions

How does Form T2017 calculate the maximum capital gains reserve?

Multiply the gain by the fraction of sale proceeds payable after year-end. Compare that amount with the gain multiplied by the statutory percentage for that year and use the smaller amount. Each following year, add back last year’s reserve and subtract the new reserve.

Who qualifies for the 10-year capital gains reserve?

The longer schedule can apply to qualifying family farm or fishing property or qualified small business corporation shares transferred to a child. An eligible transfer to a child-controlled corporation can require a joint Form T2066 election. Confirm which CRA transfer rule applies before claiming the longer reserve.

Can a non-resident seller claim a reserve?

The reserve is generally unavailable for a year if you are non-resident at the end of that tax year or at any time in the following year. The calculator disables the reserve when you flag this condition and leaves non-resident tax outside the estimate.

What if the buyer pays late or the vendor take-back note lasts longer?

Use principal contractually payable after year-end. An overdue installment was already payable and does not increase the reserve. In the final recognition year the cap falls to zero, so a balance can still be outstanding when all gain has been reported.

When does spreading the gain reduce total tax?

With constant other income and tax rates, annual recognition may keep part of the taxable gain in lower marginal brackets. If the entire gain stays in the same brackets either way, the reserve changes when tax is paid and total bracket tax is the same.
