Australia Paid Parental Leave & 12% Super Calculator 2026–27

Plan up to 130 paid days, check each parent’s income and work tests, and estimate what you’ll receive in cash and super.

Key takeaways

  • 130 days can pay $26,122.20 before tax for children born or adopted from 1 July 2026, assuming all days are taken in 2026–27 at $200.94 per day.
  • Couples have 20 days reserved for each parent to use. A single parent can use all 130 days. Eligible parents can overlap up to 20 days under the standard rule.
  • 12% ATO super is paid separately from take-home cash. On 130 days, the base contribution is about $3,134.66 before interest and any fund tax.

How the estimate works

For each parent, the calculator estimates tax on their other taxable income, then adds employer leave and government leave in that order. The increase in income tax and a flat 2% Medicare levy is deducted from each payment to estimate its take-home value. It assumes full-year Australian tax residency and the full tax-free threshold. Medicare levy reductions or exemptions, the surcharge, offsets, HELP repayments and payroll withholding are outside this estimate.

Government super is calculated on gross government pay and rounded per recipient, so a split can move the family total by one cent. Cash plus super adds take-home cash to the base ATO contribution before interest and fund tax. Employer super and changes to income-tested benefits are outside the estimate.

If your leave crosses 30 June, budget each year separately using the rate in force when you take the days. Services Australia may allow eligible parents to take single days, including weekends when the care and work rules are met.

Frequently asked questions

Who can get all 130 Paid Parental Leave days?

An eligible single parent can claim all 130 days. A partnered family typically needs two eligible parents to use the full pool. Either parent can generally use up to 110 days, and they can take up to 20 days at the same time.

What happens if I earn more than the individual ATI limit?

Services Australia checks family ATI if your individual ATI exceeds $186,487 for the 2025–26 assessment year. The fallback limit is $386,525. For a single parent, Services Australia uses that parent’s own ATI for the fallback.

How do I check the Paid Parental Leave work test?

Check for at least 330 qualifying hours in a 295-day period within the 392 days before birth or adoption. The gap between qualifying workdays can be up to 12 weeks. Paid leave may count and Services Australia can assess exceptions.

When does the ATO pay the 12% super contribution?

The ATO generally pays each recipient’s fund after the financial year in which they received government leave pay. The actual payment includes an interest component. Contributions generally count toward the concessional cap and are taxed at 15% in the fund.

Can I receive government leave and employer paid leave together?

Yes. You can generally take government Paid Parental Leave before, during or after employer paid leave. Check whether your employer pays its full benefit or adjusts it when government pay overlaps, then enter the employer amount you expect to receive.
