Frequently asked questions

How should I compare an HDHP with an HSA, a PPO, and a health FSA?

Compare annual premiums, expected cost sharing, and the in-network out-of-pocket maximum for each health plan. Then account for employer HSA contributions and any tax savings. A health FSA is a spending account, and general-purpose health FSA coverage usually prevents HSA contributions. Check whether your employer offers an HSA-compatible limited-purpose FSA.

When does IRS Form 6252 apply to an installment sale?

An eligible installment sale generally has at least one payment after the tax year of the sale. Form 6252 allocates gain to eligible payments using the gross profit percentage. Interest is handled separately, and ordinary depreciation recapture generally belongs in the year of sale.

Can I withdraw retirement money before age 59½ without the 10% additional tax?

It depends on the account, timing, and exception. The age-55 separation rule can apply to qualifying employer plans. Section 72(t) substantially equal periodic payments have their own schedule, and eligible governmental 457(b) distributions generally avoid the 10% additional tax except for certain rolled-in amounts. Ordinary income tax may still apply.

Is the marginal rate the same rate I should use to estimate HSA savings?

The marginal rate applies to the next increment of ordinary taxable income. The effective rate here is estimated federal income tax divided by taxable income. An eligible HSA contribution can span several brackets, so this estimator subtracts the tax after the contribution from the tax before it.
