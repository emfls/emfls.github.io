Frequently asked questions

Can I write off several assets if the invoice exceeds A$20,000?

Yes, if they are separate eligible assets and each costs less than A$20,000 after eligible GST credits. Eligible businesses deduct the business-use share of each asset first used or installed ready for use in the income year.

Does a A$21,998 GST-inclusive asset qualify?

With a full GST credit, its threshold cost is A$19,998.18, so it passes the cost test. With a partial credit, subtract only the eligible credit before testing the full asset cost. Without a credit, the cost is A$21,998 and exceeds the limit.

What if my asset costs exactly A$20,000 or I use it partly for business?

At A$20,000 after GST credits, the asset enters the pool. The threshold applies before multiplying by business use. The pool may still be written off in full when its year-end balance before depreciation is below A$20,000.

How does a deduction affect a sole trader's tax bill?

The calculator compares estimated income tax before and after the deduction across resident marginal brackets. When the deduction spans two brackets, the saving uses both rates. The optional full Medicare levy scenario adds 2% to positive taxable income.

Will I get the full deduction back in cash?

A deduction reduces taxable income. Cash savings depend on the tax payable at your rates and any eligible GST credit. A company taxed at 25% with enough taxable income saves A$2,500 in income tax on a A$10,000 deduction.
