Frequently asked questions

Can spouses elect $6,800 combined in 2026?

Yes, if both spouses can elect $3,400 through their own employers. Each employee has a separate election and balance. Assign each expense to one account to avoid double reimbursement.

Can a plan offer carryover and a grace period?

A health FSA can offer one of these features or neither. A carryover plan can preserve up to $680 of the unused 2026 balance for the following plan year. A grace period can allow new eligible expenses for up to 2½ months after the plan year ends. A claims filing deadline is separate.

How much can I forfeit before the FSA costs me money?

Your estimated tax savings are your break-even forfeiture. With $831.60 in tax savings, losing $400 leaves you $431.60 ahead of paying after tax. Losing $831.60 breaks even, and larger losses put you behind.

When does the full 7.65% FICA saving apply?

The full rate applies when the entire election reduces wages subject to both Social Security and regular Medicare tax. The 2026 Social Security wage base is $184,500. Use 1.45% when all the affected wages are above that base and subject only to regular Medicare. The 2.35% option includes Additional Medicare Tax when it applies to all the affected wages.

Can I contribute to an HSA while covered by a health FSA?

General-purpose health FSA coverage generally blocks HSA contributions for the covered months, including coverage through a spouse. A limited-purpose dental and vision FSA can be compatible if you meet the other HSA eligibility rules. Check how your plan handles carryover and grace-period coverage.
