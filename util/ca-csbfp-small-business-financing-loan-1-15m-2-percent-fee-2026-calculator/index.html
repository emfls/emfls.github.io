Canada Small Business Financing Act (CSBFA) Guide

Administered by Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED), the CSBFP helps Canadian startups and established businesses access commercial credit by sharing loan risk with financial institutions (RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO, CIBC, Desjardins, and Credit Unions).

1. Statutory Loan Caps & Allocation Rules

  • Overall Cap: $1,150,000 per borrower across all Canadian financial institutions.
  • Real Property: Up to $1,000,000 for purchasing or improving land/buildings (maximum 15-year term).
  • Equipment & Leaseholds: Up to $500,000 for equipment purchases and commercial renovations (maximum 10-year term).
  • Working Capital: Up to $150,000 for intangible assets, software, and operational cash flow.
