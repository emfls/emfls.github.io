Key takeaways

  • Under FRA all year: SSA deducts $1 for every $2 in earnings above $24,480.
  • Reach FRA in 2026: SSA deducts $1 for every $3 above $65,160 in earnings before your FRA month.
  • Starting in your FRA month: Your earnings no longer trigger withholding. Qualifying deductions before FRA can permanently increase your monthly benefit.

Budget for the checks you may miss now

SSA can skip an entire check even when only part of it is owed. It generally pays back excess withholding after reconciling your earnings the following year. The separate FRA credit raises future monthly benefits. Reaching break-even depends on how long you collect the higher payment.

How the calculator estimates your FRA credit

The estimate caps the annual deduction at your scheduled pre-FRA benefits. It then rounds the number of checks skipped up to a whole check. If only part of the last check is ultimately deducted, that month can still count toward the FRA adjustment.

Each credited month removes one month of early-claim reduction, up to the number of original reduction months. The reduction is 5/9 of 1% of your PIA per month for the first 36 months and 5/12 of 1% for each additional month. Break-even divides the reconciled deduction by the annual increase from the 2026 credit.

This estimate uses a constant gross check for 2026 cash flow and unrounded PIA-based amounts for the FRA comparison. It models your own retirement benefit and this year’s credit only. SSA payment rounding, cost-of-living adjustments, taxes, other years’ credits, and changes to your earnings record can affect your result.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I earn while collecting Social Security in 2026?

If you are under FRA all year, the annual limit is $24,480 and SSA deducts $1 per $2 above it. If you reach FRA in 2026, the limit is $65,160 for earnings before your FRA month and SSA deducts $1 per $3 above it. Starting in your FRA month, the earnings test ends.

Which income counts toward the earnings test?

Count gross wages and net earnings from self-employment. Dividends, capital gains, pensions, IRA and 401(k) withdrawals, and ordinary passive rental income are excluded. Earnings from a rental business can require different treatment.

Can I get a full check if I retire midyear and already exceeded the annual limit?

If the special monthly rule applies, a whole month can qualify for a full check when gross wages are at or below $2,040 if under FRA all year, or $5,430 before FRA in your FRA year, and you perform no substantial services in self-employment.

Why might SSA skip two checks when I owe less than two checks?

SSA can initially skip whole checks to cover the expected deduction. For example, an $800 deduction on a $600 monthly benefit can mean two checks skipped and $400 paid back after SSA reconciles the year.

What happens to my monthly benefit at full retirement age?

SSA can credit months with full or partial deductions and reduce your early-claim penalty. The resulting monthly increase is permanent. This calculator compares the increase from 2026 credits with your reconciled 2026 deduction to estimate the time to break even.
