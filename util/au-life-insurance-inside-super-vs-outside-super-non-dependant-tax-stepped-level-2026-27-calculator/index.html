The adult child death tax trap: Section 302-10 ITAA 1997

While super is ideal for young families with dependent spouses and minors (0% tax), holding large death cover in super once children become financially independent adults exposes the estate to an unnecessary 17% or 32% death benefit tax. Consider cross-ownership or non-super policies for estate planning.

Frequently asked questions

Why is insurance inside super cheaper upfront?

Super funds claim a statutory 15% tax deduction on group life premiums, passing savings to members.

What is the Australian super death benefits tax?

Super death benefits paid to non-dependants (like adult children 18+) are taxed at 17% for taxed elements and 32% for untaxed elements.

Are retail policies outside super tax-free?

Yes, death benefit proceeds from policies held personally outside super are 100% tax-free to all beneficiaries.

What is the 20-year balance drag on super?

Deducting insurance premiums reduces the compounding retirement pool. A $1,020 annual deduction reduces a 20-year super balance by over $44,700 at 7% return.

Stepped vs level premiums: Which is better?

Stepped premiums start cheaper but rise ~8% annually. Level premiums cross over around Year 8 and save substantial money over 15 to 20 years.
