How to read the estimate

The annual withholding estimate uses one payer and annual totals. HMRC accounts for NRLS by quarter in a year ending 31 March, while the Income Tax year ends 5 April. Use documented tax deductions for the Self Assessment year if the figures differ. The monthly boost is one-twelfth of the estimated deduction avoided by having full-year approval.

Rental tax is the difference between your total modeled Income Tax with and without the rental. Net rental cash subtracts expenses, interest, entered principal and this rental tax from rent. Net yield divides that amount by the property value. The full-interest comparison applies a pre-restriction deduction to current-year interest at the same 2026/27 bands and recalculates the allowance. Brought-forward finance costs affect the Section 24 scenario.

The estimate covers one UK property business and ordinary non-savings income. It excludes savings, dividends, treaty adjustments, property losses brought forward, the property allowance and other individual reliefs. It calculates the final balance for this tax year before any new payments on account for the next year. Inputs stay in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

When does an agent or tenant have to deduct 20%?

A UK letting agent generally applies 20% to rent after eligible expenses paid by or at the agent’s direction, regardless of the rent amount. A direct-paying tenant generally applies 20% to gross rent if their share for the landlord averages more than £100 a week, or if HMRC directs them to operate the scheme.

What changes when HMRC approves Form NRL1?

Once HMRC notifies the payer, they can pay rent without deducting NRLS tax. The landlord still reports the rent through Self Assessment and pays any tax due. The benefit is the timing of cash receipts.

Can I claim the UK Personal Allowance while living abroad?

British and EEA citizens generally qualify. Other non-residents may qualify under a relevant double taxation agreement or another statutory route. In 2026/27 the standard allowance is £12,570 and falls by £1 for each £2 of adjusted net income above £100,000.

How does Section 24 affect residential mortgage interest?

Qualifying interest goes into a 20% tax reduction based on the lowest of available finance costs, qualifying property profit and adjusted income above the Personal Allowance. A profit or income cap can leave unused finance costs to carry forward.

How do I estimate a refund or balancing payment?

Subtract NRLS tax actually deducted and any other tax payments or credits already made for the year from your final Income Tax. A negative balance indicates an estimated refund and a positive balance indicates an estimated payment. Use your payer’s records, including the NRL6 where applicable.
