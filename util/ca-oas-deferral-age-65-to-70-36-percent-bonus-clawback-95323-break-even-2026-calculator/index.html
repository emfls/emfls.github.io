What you give up by waiting

You give up a payment every month you defer. A larger monthly pension pays off only if you live long enough for it to catch up. Avoiding clawback helps only to the extent that recovery tax would have reduced those early payments. If you qualify for GIS, deferring also means missing GIS payments.

How the estimate works

The model uses the Q4 pension rate and the 2026 recovery threshold throughout, in constant 2026 Canadian dollars. It uses the same residence years at both start ages. Your first modeled payment month follows the start age, and the age-75 increase begins in the month after your 75th birthday.

The model divides a capped annual recovery estimate by 12 within each age band. Your actual tax return can differ in partial payment years. Recovery withheld from July 2027 to June 2028 uses 2026 income. Use the CRA rules for your individual income adjustments and withholding.

Lifetime totals sum monthly net payments up to the selected age, excluding ordinary income tax, GIS and the Allowance. Break-even is the first month-end when the deferred pension catches up, searched through age 100 even if you choose a shorter lifetime. Discounted break-even values each payment at age 65 using your real annual rate.

OAS deferral FAQ

How much more OAS do you get by waiting until age 70?

Each month of deferral after 65 adds 0.6%, up to 36% at 70. At Q4 2026 rates, the full monthly pension rises from $762.50 to $1,037.00 before recovery tax. After turning 75, the deferred maximum becomes $1,140.70.

How does the 2026 OAS clawback work?

The recovery tax equals 15% of adjusted net world income above $95,323, up to the OAS you receive. At the same income, a larger pension can leave more OAS after recovery and takes a higher income to eliminate entirely.

When does delaying OAS break even?

Break-even occurs when the delayed pension's cumulative net payments catch up with the age-65 option. With full OAS, zero recovery and a zero real rate, waiting to age 70 catches up at about age 83 and one month in this model.

Should I defer OAS if I qualify for GIS?

Do not defer OAS if you qualify for the Guaranteed Income Supplement. GIS payments require OAS to be in pay, so you miss GIS during the deferral period. Deferring OAS does not increase GIS.

How does Canadian residence affect my OAS?

A partial pension generally pays one-fortieth of the maximum for each completed year of Canadian residence after age 18, up to 40 years. The usual minimum is 10 years for people living in Canada and 20 years for people living abroad. International agreements can affect eligibility.
