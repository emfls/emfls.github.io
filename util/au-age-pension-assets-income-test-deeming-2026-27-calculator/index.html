Key takeaways

The maximum Age Pension is $1,237.70 per fortnight for a single person or $1,866 combined for a couple, before tax. The lower result from the assets and income tests sets the estimated payment.

Your principal home is generally exempt. Financial assets count towards the assets test and may also generate deemed income at 1.75% and 3.75% a year.

The 7.8% implied return taper trap

While the assets test sets a positive part pension, an extra $1,000 of assessable assets can reduce your pension by $3 per fortnight, or $78 annualised. That is 7.8% of the extra assets in foregone pension before tax. The full $78 effect applies when the whole $1,000 falls within that taper range.

Frequently asked questions

Does my home count towards the Age Pension assets test?

Your principal home is generally exempt. Homeowners have a lower assets free area. Include assessable assets such as bank deposits, vehicles and investment property. Special rules may apply to large blocks and proceeds from selling your home.

How does 1.75% and 3.75% deeming work?

Apply 1.75% a year to the first $66,800 of financial assets for a single person or $110,600 combined for a pensioner couple. Apply 3.75% to the balance, then divide by 26. Check how Services Australia assesses your super or income stream before entering it.

How does the $300 Work Bonus work for couples?

Each eligible pensioner can offset up to $300 per fortnight against their own wages or eligible income from active self-employment. A partner cannot use the other person’s allowance. Unused amounts can build up in a Work Bonus balance, which this estimate excludes.

Which means test sets my estimated payment?

The calculator subtracts each test’s reduction from the same maximum rate and takes the lower result. If both tests allow the full rate, there is no reduction. Annualised amounts use 26 fortnights at the selected assumptions.

Does a positive estimate qualify me for the Pensioner Concession Card?

Services Australia generally issues the card when you receive Age Pension. A positive estimate indicates you may pass the modelled means tests. Services Australia also assesses age, residence and other eligibility requirements.
