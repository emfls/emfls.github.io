Skipping depreciation can cost you twice

You may miss a deduction while you own the rental and still owe tax at sale on gain attributable to the depreciation you could have claimed. The allowed-or-allowable rule generally reduces basis even when you skipped the deduction. Reconcile your depreciation schedules before estimating sale proceeds after tax.

How to read the tax estimate

The estimate applies your selected marginal rates to each gain category and values released losses at your ordinary marginal rate. A negative result indicates a modeled reduction in other federal tax. Actual tax can vary with Schedule D and Section 1231 netting, income across brackets, and the amount of other taxable income available to absorb the released loss.

NIIT is estimated for this property's positive sale gain, assuming the released losses qualify as deductions for this included investment income. Enter sale-year NIIT MAGI before the sale gain and before deducting the released losses. Other investment income is outside the NIIT calculation. The 1031 line shows gross regular gain tax deferred in a qualifying exchange without taxable boot, before NIIT and any benefit from released losses.

This model uses one building basis from the initial service date and excludes later separately depreciated assets, ADS, ordinary recapture, same-year dispositions, state taxes, and any tax benefit from a disposition loss. Frequently asked questions

Which costs go into rental building basis?

Start with purchase price and capitalizable closing costs, subtract the portion allocated to land, and add qualifying building improvements. This calculator treats the improvements as placed in service with the building. Later improvements generally have their own depreciation schedules.

How does the mid-month convention affect depreciation?

A property placed in service in July gets 5.5 months of depreciation in its first year. A sale in September gets 8.5 months in the sale year, subject to the remaining depreciable basis. The intervening full years use the full annual deduction until the building basis is recovered.

What happens to passive losses I cannot deduct each year?

Unused losses generally carry forward and can offset eligible passive income. A fully taxable sale of the entire activity to an unrelated buyer generally releases remaining suspended losses, including any eligible current-year loss, which may also reduce other taxable income.

Is unrecaptured Section 1250 gain always taxed at 25%?

No. The 25% federal rate is a ceiling. A lower ordinary rate can apply, and the depreciation-related gain for a straight-line rental sale is limited by recognized gain. NIIT may apply separately. Basis generally reflects depreciation allowed or allowable.

How much federal gain tax can a 1031 exchange defer?

A qualifying fully deferred real-property exchange can postpone federal tax on both the unrecaptured Section 1250 gain and the remaining capital gain. Cash, other non-like-kind property, or net debt relief can trigger recognized gain. A fully deferred exchange generally does not release suspended losses in full.
