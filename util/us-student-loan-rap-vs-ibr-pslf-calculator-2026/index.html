Key takeaways

  • RAP uses your full AGI with a $50 monthly reduction per tax dependent and a $10 scheduled payment floor. Regular forgiveness takes up to 360 qualifying months.
  • Legacy IBR protects 150% of the poverty guideline. Pay 10% or 15% of income above that threshold, subject to the 10-year Standard cap.
  • PSLF can forgive a remaining balance after 120 qualifying months. Compare out-of-pocket costs before choosing a plan.

What the estimate assumes

Projections start with zero qualifying months and keep AGI, household size, interest rate and 2026 guidelines constant. The model uses one unsubsidized Direct Loan balance, monthly interest on principal and scheduled on-time payments. It excludes existing accrued interest, capitalization events, fees, daily accrual differences and payment allocation for spouses who both owe eligible federal loans.

PSLF assumes qualifying Direct Loans and qualifying employment for all 120 months. The Standard benchmark amortizes the starting principal over 120 months without a minimum bill, so it ends with no balance left. Frequently asked questions

How is the 2026 RAP payment calculated?

Apply the rate for your income tier to your entire adjusted gross income (AGI). Divide the annual base by 12, subtract $50 for each dependent claimed on your federal return, then apply the $10 monthly floor. A final payoff may be smaller.

Does RAP always reduce principal by $50 a month?

The target is the lesser of $50 or your on-time monthly payment. The government covers the gap between that target and the principal your payment already covered, up to the remaining principal. Any current-month interest your full on-time payment leaves unpaid is waived.

How do new and old IBR differ?

New IBR charges 10% of discretionary income for up to 240 qualifying months. Old IBR charges 15% for up to 300 qualifying months. Both use the 150% poverty guideline exclusion and the 10-year Standard cap. New IBR generally requires no prior outstanding federal balance when borrowing in the July 2014 through June 2026 window.

Can I get PSLF with RAP, IBR or 10-year Standard?

RAP, eligible IBR and the legacy 10-year Standard plan can count toward PSLF on eligible Direct Loans with qualifying employment. PSLF forgives the remaining balance after 120 qualifying months. Starting on the 10-year Standard schedule generally pays the loan off by month 120. The new Tiered Standard plan does not count toward PSLF.

How much tax might I owe on student loan forgiveness?

PSLF forgiveness is federally tax-free. For regular RAP or IBR forgiveness, multiply the forgiven balance by your assumed combined marginal tax rate for a rough estimate. Federal and state rules at the time of discharge determine the actual bill.
