2026 official LLP parameters

Eligible studentYou or your spouse or common-law partner. Children are excluded. Use an unlocked RRSP of which you are the annuitant. CRA eligibility also covers Canadian residency, age, designated institutions and qualifying enrolment, including certain disability exceptions.
Limits$10,000 per calendar year and $20,000 per participant per cycle. Two independently eligible participants can withdraw up to $40,000 combined. Excess withdrawals are taxable in the year withdrawn.
Withdrawal windowFour full calendar years including the first withdrawal year, plus the following January if repayment has yet to begin and eligibility continues. A new cycle can start in the year after the previous balance reaches zero.
Contribution deductionContributions from the preceding 89 days may be non-deductible to the extent they exceed the account value immediately after the withdrawal.
RepaymentGenerally 10 years. The first minimum is 1/10 of eligible draws, before any early repayments. Later minimums divide the remaining balance by the remaining years.
FilingGive Form RC96 to the issuer for each withdrawal. Report withdrawals and designate eligible repayments on Schedule 7. Repayment contributions can generally be made in the repayment year or in the first 60 days of the next year. Claim no deduction for amounts designated as repayments.
Federal loan0% federal interest. Payment typically begins after a six-month non-repayment period following school. Check provincial or territorial terms separately.

How the estimate works

For tax comparisons, the calculator applies your combined marginal rate to each ordinary withdrawal, taxable excess or repayment shortfall. Actual tax depends on your tax brackets, credits and other income. For repayment details, use your CRA LLP Statement of Account. The projection assumes no repayments before the first required year and excludes special cases such as cancellation, non-residency, death and RRSP maturity at age 71.

The federal loan comparison funds only the eligible LLP portion, drawn in January of each selected year in both scenarios. It assumes one loan with that principal, payable in equal monthly instalments after the last draw. LLP deposits and shortfall tax are timed at year-end. Any monthly cash-flow difference compounds at the outside savings rate, and the difference in RRSP balances is valued at the assumed after-tax rate. Both strategies are compared at the same date, after the full repayment schedules. The estimate excludes provincial loans, grants, fees and any disallowed contribution deductions.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take the full $20,000 in 2026?

One participant can withdraw up to $10,000 per calendar year and $20,000 per participation period. Two eligible partners using their own RRSPs can reach $40,000 combined over their respective cycles.

When do LLP repayments start?

Starting with the years after the first withdrawal, the second consecutive year without at least three months as a qualifying student usually triggers repayment. Repayment starts by the fifth year after the first withdrawal.

What happens if I miss a repayment or pay extra?

Report the shortfall as RRSP income on line 12900. It reduces the LLP balance but does not restore the money to your RRSP or create new contribution room. Designated repayments are non-deductible. Paying extra reduces later minimums.

Does a contribution made 89 days before withdrawal block the LLP?

Recent contributions can lose their deduction. For a single RRSP, subtract its fair market value immediately after the withdrawal from eligible contributions in the preceding 89 days. A positive difference is non-deductible.

How does a 0% federal student loan compare?

An eligible federal loan can cover the same education funding while leaving the RRSP invested. Compare the timing of monthly loan payments with LLP repayments, investment returns and taxes. Provincial balances can have different terms.
