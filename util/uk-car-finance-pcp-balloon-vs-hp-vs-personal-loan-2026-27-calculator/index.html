Why PCP monthly savings evaporate at renewal

Dealerships sell PCP based on affordable monthly cash flow. However, if used car values decline and your vehicle is worth less than the GMFV balloon at 48 months, you will have ZERO equity to roll into your next deposit. Drivers who roll negative equity into new PCPs face compounding debt cycles.

Frequently asked questions

Why does PCP accrue more interest than Hire Purchase?

Because the GMFV balloon payment is deferred until the very end, interest is charged on that entire lump sum throughout the full term.

What are excess mileage charges on PCP?

If you return the vehicle having exceeded the contracted mileage limit, finance companies charge a fixed fee (typically 8p to 15p per mile) for each mile over the cap.

Can I sell a car on PCP privately?

No, you do not legally own the car until the balloon payment and option-to-purchase fee are cleared. You must settle the finance before transferring ownership.

What is Voluntary Termination (VT)?

Under Section 99 of the Consumer Credit Act 1974, you have the statutory right to return the car once you have paid 50% of the total amount payable.

Is Hire Purchase better if I want to keep the car?

Yes. If you intend to own the vehicle long-term, HP or a personal bank loan results in significantly lower total interest charges than PCP.
