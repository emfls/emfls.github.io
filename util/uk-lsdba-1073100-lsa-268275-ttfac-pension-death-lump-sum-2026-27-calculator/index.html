Key takeaways

  • £268,275 LSA caps most tax-free retirement cash. The wider £1,073,100 LSDBA also covers certain serious ill-health and death lump sums.
  • A TTFAC could preserve more allowance if you used the old Lifetime Allowance (LTA) through a defined benefit pension or annuity and took little or no tax-free cash.
  • For qualifying death lump sums, death before 75 usually lets the remaining LSDBA shelter part or all of the payment. Death at 75 or over generally makes the full payment taxable.

Check before you take pension cash

A certificate can cost you tax-free cash

If your certified historic use exceeds the standard deduction, your remaining allowance can fall. If it matches the deduction, that allowance gains nothing. A valid certificate is binding. The administrator must cancel an inaccurate certificate. Compare both allowances and gather complete records before applying. A member’s first relevant lump sum entitlement can close the application window even before the money reaches their account. Finance Act 2024, paragraph 127.

6 April 2027 · Inheritance Tax on pensions

For deaths from 6 April 2027, most unused pension funds and death benefits will count towards the estate for Inheritance Tax. The standard 40% rate can apply above available estate thresholds after exemptions and reliefs. This calculator estimates Income Tax only. HMRC technical note and Inheritance Tax rates.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between LSA and LSDBA?

The LSA limits tax-free pension commencement lump sums and the tax-free portion of uncrystallised funds pension lump sums. These amounts generally use both allowances. The LSDBA also covers certain serious ill-health and death lump sums.

When can a Transitional Tax-Free Amount Certificate help?

It can restore allowance if you took less tax-free cash than the standard transition assumes. For example, 60% LTA use with no protection normally deducts £160,965 from each allowance. If your certificate shows £30,000 against each, it restores £130,965 of headroom for each allowance before other use.

When is the deadline to apply for a TTFAC?

A member must apply with complete evidence and have the certificate issued before entitlement to their first relevant lump sum after 5 April 2024. Eligible personal representatives can apply by 31 October following the tax year of a relevant death lump sum payment, subject to the member’s lifetime entitlement restriction.

How are pension death benefits taxed before and from age 75?

For a qualifying lump sum after death before age 75, the excess over remaining LSDBA is generally taxable. Eligible beneficiary flexi-access drawdown income falls outside the LSDBA lump sum test and can be tax-free, subject to scheme and tax conditions. After death at age 75 or over, lump sums and withdrawals generally count as the individual beneficiary’s taxable income.

Do pensions face Inheritance Tax from 6 April 2027?

For deaths on or after 6 April 2027, most unused pension funds and death benefits enter the estate for Inheritance Tax. The standard 40% rate can apply to the taxable estate after thresholds, exemptions and reliefs. Some payments, including qualifying death-in-service benefits, are excluded.
