Key takeaways

The MLS starts above $105,000 for singles or $210,000 for families before the child adjustment. The rate is 1% to 1.5% for liable days. Full-year qualifying hospital cover breaks even on tax alone when your annual premium equals your full-year surcharge. The 2% standard Medicare levy is calculated separately.

Avoid paying both the premium and the surcharge

Extras-only cover does not exempt you from MLS. Confirm that the policy provides qualifying hospital cover for everyone required. The maximum excess is $750 for a single policy or $1,500 for a couple or family policy.

Buying hospital cover after your Lifetime Health Cover base day (usually 1 July after your 31st birthday) can add 2% to the hospital premium for each year you were over 30, up to 70%.

Frequently asked questions

Who may owe the MLS in 2026–27?

The surcharge can apply when your income for MLS purposes exceeds $105,000 as a single person or $210,000 for a family, and you or a person you must cover lacks qualifying hospital cover. The family thresholds rise by $1,500 for each MLS dependent child after the first.

Which income does the MLS rate apply to?

Income for MLS purposes selects the tier and includes adjustments such as reportable super contributions and net investment losses. The selected rate applies to your full surcharge base: taxable income for MLS, reportable fringe benefits and amounts on which family trust distribution tax has been paid.

What happens if I have hospital cover for only part of the year?

For a stable household, multiply the full-year surcharge by the number of days without qualifying cover when you were liable, then divide by 365. Days when you, your spouse and all relevant dependants had qualifying cover are excluded.

Does extras-only cover remove the surcharge or the 2% levy?

Extras-only policies do not provide the hospital cover required to avoid the MLS. Qualifying hospital cover can remove the surcharge for covered days. The standard Medicare levy is separate, generally 2% of taxable income, with possible reductions or exemptions.

How do I compare the hospital cover quote with the surcharge?

Compare your full-year surcharge with your share of the annual premium for qualifying cover after any applicable rebate and Lifetime Health Cover loading. Their difference is the annual saving or extra cost before claims and out-of-pocket costs.
