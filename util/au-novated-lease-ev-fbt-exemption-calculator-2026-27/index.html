Calculation assumptions and ATO sources

The estimate uses a full novation with eligible GST credits passed through, fixed annual running costs and year-round private availability. GST is 10% of the ex-GST amount, equivalent to 1/11 of a GST-inclusive bill. The purchase-credit cap is separate from GST treatment of lease instalments and running costs. The gross buyout uses the vehicle price and is paid after tax. Enter a quote that already includes any LCT payable.

For taxable leases the model assumes payroll employee contributions with GST remitted, and employer FBT passed on pre-tax at the Type 1 rate when the budget cannot cover the statutory value. It holds the selected treatment, FBT base, salary, 2026–27 tax rates and full standard Medicare levy flat for the term. It excludes offsets, low-income levy reductions, partial years, changes after four years, and early termination. The loan assumes full financing with no upfront fees, deposit or balloon.

Frequently asked questions

Which EVs qualify for the 2026–27 FBT exemption?

An eligible battery EV can qualify through 31 March 2027 if it was first held and used on or after 1 July 2022, no luxury car tax was ever payable, and a current employee or their associate uses it. The 2026–27 fuel-efficient threshold is $91,661 for the relevant sale or import. An eligible salary package can fund lease payments and associated running costs from pre-tax pay.

Can I salary package a plug-in hybrid FBT-free?

New PHEV arrangements from 1 April 2025 generally use standard FBT treatment. A pre-existing exemption may continue under a qualifying financially binding commitment. This calculator models new PHEV arrangements and estimates a post-tax employee contribution using the 20% statutory formula.

What would change from 1 April 2027?

Under the proposed taper, eligible EVs with an FBT base value above $75,000 and up to the applicable fuel-efficient limit would use a 15% statutory rate for new commitments from 1 April 2027. Full relief would continue for eligible new commitments up to $75,000 through 31 March 2029. The proposal includes transitional protection for qualifying existing arrangements.

Why can an FBT-exempt EV still affect HELP or Child Care Subsidy?

For a full year with no employee contribution, the statutory estimate is the FBT base value multiplied by 20% and the 1.8868 reporting factor. The reportable amount can affect HELP or HECS repayments, Medicare Levy Surcharge, Division 293 and Child Care Subsidy. Each program has its own income test.

How are cash, a loan and a novated lease compared?

All three options use the same vehicle and running costs over a matched 3- or 5-year term. The loan fully amortises, and the lease includes a post-tax buyout. The offset-adjusted comparison compounds each payment to the end of the term at the entered offset rate, including the day-one cash purchase.
