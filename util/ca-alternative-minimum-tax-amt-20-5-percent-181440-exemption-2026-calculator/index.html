Canada Alternative Minimum Tax Calculator 2026

Compare regular federal tax with the 20.5% minimum tax, estimate the provincial impact, and see how much you could recover by 2033.

Key takeaways

For individuals, federal AMT starts at 20.5% of adjusted taxable income above $181,440. You pay additional federal tax only if the minimum tax after eligible credits exceeds regular federal tax. A business sale or a large capital gain can put you over that threshold even in a year with modest ordinary income.

Frequently asked questions

When does federal AMT apply in 2026?

For individuals, the federal calculation starts at 20.5% of adjusted taxable income above $181,440. Additional tax applies only when the net minimum tax exceeds regular federal tax. Most taxable trusts have no exemption.

How are capital gains treated for AMT?

Ordinary gains generally count at 100% for federal AMT and 50% for regular federal tax. The model counts 30% of fully LCGE-sheltered gains and 30% of gains on qualifying gifts of publicly listed securities for AMT.

How do charitable donations and deductions affect AMT?

Federal AMT recognizes 80% of the regular charitable donation credit and 50% of the basic personal credit. The calculator also halves the specified restricted deductions. Enter only gifts eligible to claim this year.

Does Quebec charge AMT when federal AMT is zero?

It can. Quebec compares its own regular tax with its own minimum tax, using a 19% rate and a $183,680 individual exemption for 2026. The provincial estimate requires amounts from the Quebec worksheet.

When can I recover federal AMT paid for 2026?

The seven following tax years run from 2027 through 2033. You can generally use the credit when regular federal tax is higher than federal minimum tax, up to the available headroom and remaining credit. Any unused balance expires.
