Understanding the Australian R&D Tax Incentive (Division 355)

Jointly administered by the Australian Taxation Office (ATO) and AusIndustry (Department of Industry, Science and Resources), the R&D Tax Incentive is Australia's flagship innovation program.

1. Refundable Offset for Startups & SMEs (<$20M Turnover)

  • Rate: Company Tax Rate (25% for Base Rate Entities) + 18.5% statutory premium = 43.5%.
  • Cash Refund: Unlike normal tax credits that simply carry forward, refundable tax offsets are paid out as cash refunds to loss-making companies after their company tax return (Company Tax Return Section 21) is processed.
  • 18.5% Net Advantage: A regular tax deduction saves 25¢ per dollar spent. The R&D offset returns 43.5¢ per dollar, delivering an 18.5¢ permanent net cash benefit.

2. Non-Refundable Offset for Large Corporates (>=$20M Turnover)

Companies with turnover of $20M or more receive a non-refundable tax offset based on R&D intensity:

  • Tier 1 (Up to 2.0% Intensity): Tax Rate + 8.5% premium (38.5% at 30% tax rate).
  • Tier 2 (Above 2.0% Intensity): Tax Rate + 16.5% premium (46.5% at 30% tax rate).
