Key takeaways

  • 15% withholding with no minimum sale price. On a standard A$400,000 sale, the buyer withholds A$60,000 at the default rate. The former A$750,000 threshold was removed for contracts signed from 1 January 2025.
  • Australian tax residents can avoid withholding. Each resident owner needs to give the buyer their valid ATO clearance certificate by settlement.
  • Foreign residents can use an approved variation. A lower rate can release cash at settlement. Your tax return still accounts for the gain and credits the amount actually withheld.

How to read the estimate

Settlement cash equals your share of the sale price less withholding, selling costs and the mortgage payout. If the balance is negative, the shortfall shows how much cash you may need to bring. Ask your conveyancer to confirm who pays each cost if it’s split differently from the title shares.

Estimated tax on the gain is the increase in ordinary income tax when your taxable gain is added to other income. The estimate treats all entered selling costs as eligible disposal costs. It excludes separate capital losses, part-year residency, levies, offsets, other tax credits and settlement adjustments such as GST, rates or a deposit already paid to you. The refund or bill shows this sale’s contribution to your tax return.

The foreign-resident estimate assumes a fully taxable gain before income tax. The full 50% discount is generally unavailable for assets acquired after 8 May 2012, and an eligible life event can be an exception to the main residence rules. Frequently asked questions

Does the A$750,000 threshold still apply?

The threshold was removed for contracts signed from 1 January 2025. On a standard A$400,000 sale, the default 15% withholding is A$60,000. A valid clearance certificate or variation given to the buyer by settlement changes the amount withheld for that vendor.

Does every Australian-resident co-owner need a certificate?

Yes. Each Australian tax resident listed on the title needs their own certificate. If a 40% owner on a A$400,000 sale has no certificate, the buyer generally withholds A$24,000 on that owner’s A$160,000 share. Other owners’ documents determine the withholding on their own shares.

How early should I apply for ATO clearance?

Apply as soon as you plan to sell and allow at least 28 days before settlement. A certificate is valid for 12 months from issue. Give the buyer a valid certificate at or before settlement.

When can I claim withholding back?

Claim the withholding as a credit in the tax return for the income year the contract was signed. You can claim once settlement and payment to the ATO have occurred. A cash refund depends on your assessment and other liabilities. An early-year sale can leave the money tied up for around a year or longer.

What can a foreign-resident variation save at settlement?

An approved 5% rate on a A$400,000 vendor share reduces withholding from A$60,000 to A$20,000 and releases A$40,000 at settlement. Give the buyer the valid ATO notice by settlement. The variation reduces the credit by the same amount, while the underlying tax on the gain stays the same.
