Estimate the tax saving for both partners and the refund for up to four earlier tax years.

Key takeaways

Transferring £1,260 can cut the recipient’s Income Tax by up to £252 per year. Four full backdated reductions add up to £1,008. Including 2026/27, the maximum household saving is £1,260 if the donor owes no extra tax.

The recipient’s usual income cap is £50,270 in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, or £43,662 in Scotland. The oldest year, 2022/23, is claimable through 5 April 2027.

Check the £11,310–£12,570 tax trap before claiming

Above £11,310, the donor pays 20% on the excess in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, or 19% in Scotland within this range. At £12,570, that costs £252 or £239.40 respectively. Full recipient relief leaves a £0 net gain in rUK and £12.60 in Scotland. If the recipient has little tax to offset, the transfer can leave you worse off.

Frequently asked questions

Can I transfer less than £1,260?

The transfer is fixed at £1,260. It is 10% of the standard £12,570 Personal Allowance rounded up to the next £10. The recipient can save up to £252, capped at their Income Tax liability.

Can Marriage Allowance leave our household worse off?

Yes. With a £12,570 donor income and £13,000 recipient income in England, the recipient saves £86 while the donor pays £252 more, leaving the couple £166 worse off.

How does Marriage Allowance differ in Scotland?

The recipient can pay Scottish starter, basic or intermediate rate, normally with income up to £43,662. A donor within the £11,310–£12,570 range pays 19% on the amount above £11,310. The recipient still gets up to £252, subject to their actual tax liability.

Which years can I backdate and when does the oldest expire?

In 2026/27 you can claim for eligible years from 2022/23 to 2025/26. The 2022/23 deadline is 5 April 2027. Four full reductions can produce a £1,008 refund for the recipient. The donor may have a separate tax bill.

How do I claim for free and which PAYE codes can change?

The donor can apply directly to HMRC for free. For standard PAYE in England or Northern Ireland, 1383M and 1131N are example codes for the recipient and donor. Welsh and Scottish prefixes and other adjustments can change the codes. HMRC provides the actual codes.
