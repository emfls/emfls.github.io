Roth conversion and IRMAA questions

How much can I convert without entering a higher federal tax bracket?

The conversion-room column gives the largest whole-dollar taxable conversion that leaves taxable income at or below each bracket ceiling. It includes unused deductions and recalculates the enhanced senior deduction as income rises.

Which Medicare year does a 2026 Roth conversion affect?

SSA generally uses tax information from two years earlier, so a taxable 2026 conversion generally affects 2028 premiums. The calculator uses the official 2026 CMS schedule as a benchmark. Actual 2028 thresholds and surcharges can differ.

Can a $1 conversion trigger a surcharge for both spouses?

Yes. If joint Medicare MAGI is exactly $218,000 under the 2026 benchmark, $1 more moves both covered spouses into tier 1. With full-year Part B and D coverage for each spouse, the household pays $2,296.80 more for that premium year.

Does the senior deduction reduce Medicare MAGI?

The senior deduction reduces taxable income. Medicare MAGI is based on AGI plus tax-exempt interest before that deduction. A taxable conversion can therefore increase Medicare MAGI and reduce the enhanced senior deduction at the same time.

When does the five-year clock end for a 2026 Roth IRA conversion?

For calendar-year filers, the conversion clock ends January 1, 2031. Before then, withdrawing taxable converted principal while under age 59½ can trigger a 10% additional tax unless an exception applies. Separate rules govern earnings and distribution ordering.
