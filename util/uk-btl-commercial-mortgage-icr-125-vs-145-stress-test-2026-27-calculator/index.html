Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) Buy-to-Let Underwriting Standards

Following the implementation of Bank of England PRA Supervisory Statement SS13/16, UK mortgage lenders must apply strict affordability stress testing on all buy-to-let mortgage applications:

1. The ICR Formula & Section 24 Disparity

  • Formula: Max Loan = Annual Gross Rent / (Stress Rate × ICR Multiple)
  • Why 125% vs 145%: Prior to Section 24 of the Finance (No. 2) Act 2015, all landlords could deduct mortgage interest as a business expense. Today, individual higher-rate (40%) and additional-rate (45%) landlords receive only a 20% basic tax credit. Lenders enforce an aggressive 145% ICR on personal names to buffer against tax liabilities, whereas Limited Company SPVs pay Corporation Tax on actual net profits and qualify for the standard 125% ICR.

2. The 5-Year Fixed Rate Stress Relief Advantage

Under PRA guidelines, mortgages with a fixed rate term of 5 years or longer qualify for stress test exemption. Lenders are permitted to assess rental affordability against the actual product pay rate (e.g. 4.85%). In contrast, 2-year fixed or variable tracker mortgages must be stressed at the pay rate plus 2.0% (with a statutory floor of 5.50%), severely restricting borrowing capacity.
