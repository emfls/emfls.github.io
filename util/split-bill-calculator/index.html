How the Calculation Works & Mathematical Formula

The Split Bill Calculator allows users to evenly distribute a subtotal amount among a specified number of people, with an option to include tax and tip. This is especially useful when dining out with friends or splitting costs across a group.

Input Variables:

  • Subtotal: The initial amount before tax and tip are added.
  • Tax (%): The percentage of tax that will be applied to the subtotal.
  • Tip (%): The percentage of the tip to be added after the tax has been calculated.
  • People: The total number of individuals among whom the total bill will be split.

Mathematical Formula:

The total amount per person can be calculated using the formula:

Total per person = (Subtotal + (Subtotal × (Tax / 100)) + (Subtotal × (Tax / 100) × (Tip / 100))) / People

Example Calculation:

Suppose the inputs are as follows:

  • Subtotal: $100
  • Tax: 10%
  • Tip: 15%
  • People: 4

Step 1: Calculate the tax amount: $100 × (10/100) = $10

Step 2: Add the tax to the subtotal: $100 + $10 = $110

Step 3: Calculate the tip amount: $110 × (15/100) = $16.50

Step 4: Add the tip to the subtotal + tax: $110 + $16.50 = $126.50

Step 5: Divide by the number of people: $126.50 / 4 = $31.625

Thus, each person owes approximately $31.63.

Practical Tips: Always check if the tip is applied before or after tax, as this can change the total due. A common misconception is that tax and tip are calculated solely on the subtotal, but they often apply after adjustments.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I enter my subtotal for the Split Bill Calculator?

To enter your subtotal, simply input the total amount of your bill excluding tax and tip. This represents the base charge for your meal or service.

What if I want to change the percentage of tax and tip?

You can modify the tax and tip percentages by entering your preferred values in the respective fields. The calculator will automatically adjust the total based on your inputs.

Can I split the bill unevenly?

This tool is designed for equal splitting. For uneven splits, you would need to manually calculate each person’s share based on their individual orders or preferences.
