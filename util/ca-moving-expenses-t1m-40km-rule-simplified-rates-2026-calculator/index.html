Canada Moving Expenses Deduction Calculator 2026

Check the 40-kilometre test and estimate your CRA Form T1-M deduction on Line 21900, unused expenses and potential tax savings.

Key takeaways

  • You need to be at least 40 km closer to the new work location by the shortest public route. If the test fails, the deduction is $0.
  • Your current-year deduction is limited to net eligible income at the new location. You can claim unused eligible expenses against qualifying income there in a future year.
  • The simplified rates below are 2026 planning assumptions pending CRA confirmation. The CRA rate page currently lists the 2025 tax year.

Avoid losing part of your claim

Save the two route measurements, travel and lodging records, receipts, and proof of reimbursements while you have them. If the income cap limits your claim, record the unused balance so you can claim it in a future year with qualifying income at the new location.

This estimate assumes an eligible work or business relocation and paid expenses. For study-related moves, additional residency rules or a later home sale, check CRA guidance. Confirm the simplified rates for your tax year before using the estimate to file.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure the 40-kilometre rule?

Measure the shortest public route from each home to the same new work location. Subtract the new home's distance from the old home's distance. A reduction of at least 40 km passes the distance test.

Which simplified travel rates does the calculator use?

The planning scenario uses $23 per meal, up to $69 per person per day, and the table's cents-per-kilometre rate for where the trip started. Confirm the CRA schedule for your tax year before filing.

How do I enter more than 15 days of temporary living?

Enter the length of your stay near the old or new home. Include only eligible lodging charges and meals from the period you claim, up to 15 days in total. Keep your lodging receipts and records of the stay.

Which home sale and purchase costs can I enter?

Eligible selling costs include commissions, legal or notary fees, advertising and a mortgage prepayment penalty. If you sold the old home because of the move, eligible purchase costs can include legal or notary fees and title transfer or registration charges, excluding GST/HST.

What happens if eligible expenses exceed new-location income?

The current-year deduction is limited to net eligible employment or self-employment income from the new work location. Keep a record of the unused balance and claim it against eligible income from that location in a future year.
