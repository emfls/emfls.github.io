California SDI questions

Does California SDI have a wage cap in 2026?

No. California withholds 1.30% of covered wages in 2026. SB 951 removed the wage limit on January 1, 2024. In 2023, the last capped year, 0.90% applied to the first $153,164 of covered wages, with a maximum of $1,378.48.

Do RSU vests, cash bonuses and NSO exercise spreads count for California SDI?

Include the amount payroll treats as California SDI-covered wages. Typically this includes cash bonuses, the taxable value when RSUs vest and settle, and the taxable spread on a nonstatutory option exercise. The spread is the fair market value at exercise minus the exercise price, multiplied by the shares exercised. Use payroll records when vesting and settlement differ or services span multiple states.

When does the 14.6% California marginal wage rate apply?

It applies when the next dollar of covered wages is also in the 12.3% California income tax bracket and subject to the 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax, formerly the Mental Health Services Tax. The surcharge starts above $1 million of California taxable income. The joint 12.3% bracket starts above $1,536,426 of taxable income. When the 37% federal bracket and 2.35% combined Medicare rate also apply, the modeled marginal rate is 53.95%.

Can I deduct California SDI on my federal return?

Mandatory CA SDI contributions generally count toward the state and local tax deduction if you itemize federally. The combined 2026 SALT cap is $40,400 for single or joint filers and starts to phase down above $505,000 of modified adjusted gross income, with a $10,000 floor. Once you reach your applicable cap, additional SDI does not increase that deduction.

Why does Washington still show a payroll cost with zero CA SDI?

The comparison assumes you earn all wages in the state shown. Washington has no state tax on wages in 2026, but covered employees can owe capped Paid Family and Medical Leave premiums and uncapped WA Cares premiums. Texas and Florida show zero for the state wage taxes and employee payroll charges modeled here.

See EDD stock option treatment, IRS guidance on deductible taxes, and 26 U.S.C. § 164 for the 2026 SALT limits.

How this estimate works

The model treats the amounts entered as wages subject to each modeled tax, applies 2026 standard deductions to estimate taxable income, and uses progressive brackets for income tax. The calculation excludes tax credits, AMT, itemizing, pretax benefit adjustments and any California voluntary disability plan.

If you worked across state lines, received equity tied to service in more than one state or changed employers, check your payroll records for covered wages, sourcing and withholding. This estimate uses annual totals, so paycheck-by-paycheck rounding can differ. Calculation inputs are processed in your browser. Analytics and advertising may receive ordinary usage and device data.
