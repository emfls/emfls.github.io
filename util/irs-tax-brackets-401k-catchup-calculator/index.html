FAQ

What is the SECURE 2.0 401(k) super catch-up limit for ages 60 to 63?

For 2025 and 2026, employees turning ages 60, 61, 62, or 63 can contribute an enhanced $11,250 catch-up above the base elective deferral limit ($23,500 in 2025; $24,500 in 2026), replacing the standard age-50 catch-up.

How does pre-tax 401(k) contribution reduce federal income tax?

Pre-tax elective deferrals reduce taxable ordinary income dollar for dollar after subtracting your filing-status standard deduction.

When does the Roth catch-up requirement apply?

For 2026, if your 2025 FICA wages from the employer sponsoring your plan exceeded $150,000, your catch-up contributions must be Roth. The calculator counts those dollars toward your deferral and excludes them from current-year tax savings. IRS catch-up guidance

What does the defined contribution limit cover?

The annual additions limit is the lesser of 100% of eligible compensation or $70,000 in 2025 and $72,000 in 2026. It includes regular employee deferrals, employer contributions and employee after-tax contributions. Eligible catch-up contributions are separate. Your plan may impose lower limits.
