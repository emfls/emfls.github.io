Understanding FCA Policy Statement PS26/24 & Its Two Mandatory Implementation Dates

UK consumer-credit lenders, credit brokers, motor dealerships, buy-now-pay-later providers, and comparison platforms face a two-stage compliance transition under FCA Policy Statement PS26/24 amending the Consumer Credit sourcebook (CONC 3). Rather than forcing every financial promotion update on a single midnight cut-off, the FCA split implementation into two legally distinct calendar milestones that marketing and compliance teams must track separately across paid search, landing pages, print collateral, and point-of-sale banners.

The first milestone—23 November 2026—brings the core PS26/24 financial promotions framework into force, modernising how clear, fair, and not misleading credit ads present rate triggers, representative examples, and uniform-rate offers under the Consumer Duty. The second milestone—23 February 2027—activates Annex B Part 2 (CONC 3.5.11R), establishing stricter prominence and mandatory disclosure rules whenever a promoted credit agreement requires security over property or assets, a lien, or a third-party personal guarantee. Publishing a secured loan or guarantor finance advert on or after 23 February 2027 without the updated CONC 3.5.11R risk statement creates an immediate regulatory breach.

The Strict 100% Rule for Representative APR Omission

A widespread compliance misconception among digital acquisition teams is assuming that if 80% or 90% of approved customers receive the headline APR, the ad can drop the word "Representative" or omit the full CONC 3.5 Representative Example. That assumption fails FCA scrutiny. Under CONC 3.5 and PS26/24, a firm may only omit Representative APR qualification when the advertised APR applies to strictly 100% of resulting credit agreements entered into as a consequence of the promotion.

If even 1% of accepted borrowers receive a higher risk-based tier rate (for example, 99% receive 7.9% APR and 1% receive 11.9% APR), the offer is NOT uniform. The financial promotion must display a compliant Representative Example—including the rate of interest (fixed or variable), the Representative APR with equal or greater prominence than any rate/incentive trigger, the amount of credit, the duration, the monthly instalment, and the total amount payable—and the firm must hold auditable conversion evidence proving that at least 51% of resulting agreements will actually be granted at or below that Representative APR.

How UK Statutory APR Is Solved Under CONC App 1.2

UK statutory Annual Percentage Rate (APR) is not a simple nominal interest rate multiplied by 12, nor is it a flat percentage add-on. Under CONC App 1.2, the statutory APR is the unique effective annual compounding rate X that equates the present value of all credit drawdowns (net of any mandatory upfront fees deducted from the advance) to the present value of all future repayments and mandatory charges over the exact repayment schedule.

Notice two subtle arithmetic traps that routinely trip up marketing spreadsheets before FCA compliance sign-off:

  • Effective Annual vs. Nominal Monthly Compounding: A 7.90% nominal fixed interest rate compounded monthly produces an effective annual rate of (1 + 0.079/12)^12 - 1 = 8.19% APR even with £0 arrangement fees. Printing "7.9% APR" when the nominal monthly rate is 7.9% understates statutory APR by 0.29 percentage points.
  • Arrangement Fees Added to Loan vs. Deducted Upfront: When a £195 mandatory arrangement fee is added to a £10,000 loan over 48 months, the borrower pays interest on £10,195 while receiving a £10,000 net cash advance—pushing the statutory APR from 8.19% up to 9.25% APR. If the same £195 fee is deducted upfront (net advance £9,805), the effective APR rises even slightly higher to 9.28% because less cash is received on day one.

Pre-Publication Financial Promotion Sign-Off Checklist (2026/27)

  1. Confirm Effective Regime Date: Verify whether the campaign remains live on or after 23 November 2026 (Core PS26/24) and whether any evergreen ad creative will still run on or after 23 February 2027 (Annex B Part 2 CONC 3.5.11R).
  2. Audit the 51% vs. 100% Conversion Cohort: Document the exact percentage of accepted borrowers expected to receive the headline rate. Below 51% = stop publication immediately; 51%–99.9% = mandatory full Representative Example; 100% flat rate across all accepted applicants = eligible for simplified uniform-rate presentation.
  3. Check Trigger Words & Visual Prominence: Any mention of a rate, "0% finance," "low monthly payments," or comparative savings acts as a trigger requiring the Representative APR to appear with greater prominence than the trigger across both desktop and 375px mobile viewports.
  4. Insert CONC 3.5.11R Security/Guarantee Statements: For any loan involving a charge over a home, vehicle bill of sale, business asset lien, or personal guarantor, embed clear, unobstructed security/guarantee risk language ahead of the 23 February 2027 deadline.
  5. Preserve Sign-Off Records: Archive the exact APR calculation sheet, landing page screenshot, and Compliance Officer approval timestamp in your FCA financial promotions register.

Frequently Asked Questions (2026/27)

What are the exact implementation dates for UK FCA PS26/24?

Core PS26/24 consumer-credit financial promotions changes take effect on 23 November 2026, while Annex B Part 2 governing CONC 3.5.11R security and guarantee disclosures takes effect three months later on 23 February 2027.

When can a lender omit the Representative APR wording under PS26/24?

Representative APR omission is ONLY eligible when the advertised APR applies to 100% of resulting credit agreements. If even 1% of approved applicants receive a higher risk-tiered rate, a full Representative Example remains mandatory.

What happens if fewer than 51% of borrowers receive our advertised rate?

The promotion fails the statutory CONC 3.5 Representative APR threshold. You must raise the advertised Representative APR until you reasonably expect at least 51% of resulting agreements to be granted at or below that rate.

Why is UK statutory APR higher than the fixed nominal interest rate?

Statutory APR under CONC App 1.2 uses effective annual compounding (monthly interest compounded 12 times per year) and incorporates mandatory arrangement or broker fees into the total charge for credit.

Does this calculator provide formal FCA Section 21 financial promotion approval?

No. It is strictly a regulatory checklist and APR verification calculator—not legal advice or Section 21 approval. Firms must obtain sign-off from an FCA-authorised compliance officer or qualified solicitor before publishing ads.
