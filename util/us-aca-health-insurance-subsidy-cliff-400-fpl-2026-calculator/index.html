Key takeaways

The federal subsidy cliff is back for 2026. In the contiguous states and D.C., the 400% cutoff is $62,600 for one person and $84,600 for two. At the cutoff, the expected benchmark contribution is 9.96% of household MAGI. Above the cutoff, the federal premium tax credit is $0.

A qualifying HSA contribution, deductible traditional IRA contribution or pre-tax 401(k) deferral may bring your Marketplace income back within range.

Estimate your 2026 benchmark premium

One extra dollar can erase a year of federal credits. For a two-person household with a $24,000 annual benchmark, going from $84,600 to $84,601 can cost $15,573.84 in federal credits. If you receive more advance credit than you qualify for in 2026, the excess generally has to be repaid in full.

How the estimate works

Within the modeled income range, the calculator multiplies MAGI by the IRS contribution rate, interpolating between each band’s endpoints, and subtracts that amount from the annual benchmark premium. The estimated credit bottoms out at zero. The benchmark minus that credit, divided by 12, gives the estimated monthly premium.

This planning estimate uses unrounded ratios to interpolate percentages. Reconcile actual premiums and advance credits using Form 1095-A and the IRS Form 8962 instructions, which apply their own rounding and monthly rules. The calculator assumes that everyone included in the benchmark premium qualifies for the credit. Eligibility can change with Medicaid, Medicare, affordable employer coverage or tax filing status. An exception may apply below 100% FPL. State subsidies are outside this federal estimate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 2026 ACA subsidy cliff?

The enhanced federal Marketplace credits expired after 2025. For 2026, household income above 400% of the federal poverty level generally eliminates the federal premium tax credit. Exactly 400% is within the income limit. For a two-person tax household in the contiguous states or D.C., the cutoff is $84,600.

What counts as ACA household MAGI?

Start with adjusted gross income and add tax-exempt interest, excluded foreign income and nontaxable Social Security benefits. Combine the applicable MAGI for you, your spouse if filing jointly, and dependents who are required to file a federal return. Use your projected 2026 income, including income such as taxable capital gains and taxable retirement withdrawals.

Which premium should I enter?

Use the annual premium for the second-lowest-cost silver plan (SLCSP) for eligible household members in your area. The estimate assumes that same benchmark is your enrolled plan for all 12 months. Your actual federal credit can be lower if your enrolled plan costs less, your coverage varies by month or you qualify for other coverage.

Which contributions can lower ACA MAGI?

Eligible HSA contributions, deductible traditional IRA contributions, and traditional pre-tax 401(k) salary deferrals can reduce MAGI. Check account eligibility, remaining contribution and deduction limits, and deadlines. Roth or nondeductible contributions do not reduce MAGI. Exclude any payroll deferrals or deductions already included in your projection.

What does the effective marginal return measure?

It adds the estimated federal credit restored and the estimated federal income tax saved, then divides that sum by the contribution needed for the one-dollar buffer. Federal income tax saved is the contribution multiplied by the rate you enter. The estimate assumes that rate applies across the whole contribution and excludes other taxes and future withdrawals.
