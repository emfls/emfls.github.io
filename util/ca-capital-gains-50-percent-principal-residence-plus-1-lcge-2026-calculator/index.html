How the estimate works

Start with proceeds minus the adjusted cost base and selling expenses. Apply any principal residence exemption before subtracting other eligible 2026 losses. A positive net capital gain then enters taxable income at 50%. On qualified property the model shelters the smaller of the net gain and the unused LCGE, measured in gross gain dollars. It applies the selected marginal rate to the amount left after inclusion and the LCGE deduction.

The effective rate divides estimated tax by the positive net gain after PRE and losses, before LCGE. The model uses one rate for the whole taxable amount. It excludes progressive bracket changes, credits, prior-year losses, recapture, corporate or trust tax, and AMT. Annual and cumulative gains limits and cumulative net investment losses can reduce the actual LCGE claim. Use CRA Form T657 to determine your claim.

Keep enough sale proceeds in reserve for tax.

Before spending or reinvesting the proceeds, confirm the cost base, permitted losses, and eligibility for each exemption. A loss on a personal-use home generally cannot offset other gains. A short holding period can also cause a home sale to be taxed as business income under CRA’s residential property flipping rules.

Frequently asked questions

What is Canada’s capital gains inclusion rate in 2026?

The standard inclusion rate is 50%. On a $100,000 net gain with no exemption, $50,000 enters taxable income. At a 40% combined marginal rate, that produces $20,000 of estimated regular tax.

How does the principal residence +1 year work?

For an eligible designation, divide designated years while resident in Canada plus one by total ownership tax years and cap the result at 100%. If you were a non-resident during the year you acquired the home, omit the extra year. The +1 rule helps cover a calendar year in which you move between homes. A family generally designates one home per year, and you must report the sale and designation even when the gain is fully exempt.

What qualifies for the $1.275 million LCGE?

Eligible individuals can claim the LCGE on qualifying small business corporation shares or qualified farm or fishing property. The $1,275,000 2026 cap is shared across those assets over your lifetime. Actual eligibility and the amount you can deduct depend on CRA rules, prior claims, and your gains limits.

Why can I owe Alternative Minimum Tax if the LCGE shelters my gain?

AMT is a parallel calculation with different treatment of exempt gains and deductions. A large LCGE claim can generate AMT even if the regular income tax on the sale is zero. Use CRA Form T691 to check the federal amount.

What does the comparison with the cancelled proposal show?

It estimates how much more regular tax an individual would have paid if the cancelled proposal had applied. The calculator uses one-half inclusion for the first $250,000 of gains remaining after exemptions and losses and two-thirds inclusion on the excess, assuming the full annual threshold is available.
