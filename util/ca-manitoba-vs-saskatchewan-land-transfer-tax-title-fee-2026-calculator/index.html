Compare 2026 land transfer and mortgage registration costs for the same property in Winnipeg, Saskatoon or Regina, and Alberta.

Key takeaways

On a $500,000 home with a $400,000 mortgage, estimated tax and registration costs are $7,924 in Manitoba, $2,275 in Saskatchewan and $1,000 in Alberta. Saskatchewan costs $5,649 less than Manitoba in this scenario.

Manitoba taxes the purchase in tiers and offers no provincial land transfer tax rebate solely for first-time buyers. Saskatchewan charges no provincial land transfer tax and applies its standard 0.40% title fee to the full title value above $6,300.

Frequently asked questions

Does Manitoba waive land transfer tax for first-time home buyers?

First-time buyer status alone does not qualify for a Manitoba land transfer tax rebate. For a standard $500,000 transfer, the progressive tax is $7,650 before registration fees. Statutory exemptions may apply to certain other transactions.

Does Saskatchewan charge 0.40% on the amount above $6,300?

For a standard transfer above $6,300, Saskatchewan charges 0.40% of the entire title value. A $500,000 title costs $2,000 to transfer. Any mortgage registration is charged separately.

When does Saskatchewan’s reduced 0.10% rate apply?

Examples include adding or removing a joint tenant when another joint tenant remains, certain parcel consolidations or subdivisions, and the first raising of a condominium title. ISC requires an affidavit for eligible transactions. A simple change of name is listed separately as free.

What changes if I buy without a mortgage?

Enter zero for the registered principal. The calculator removes mortgage registration costs in all three provinces and retains applicable title charges and Manitoba transfer tax.

How does Alberta charge for a partial $5,000 increment?

Alberta counts each started $5,000 increment. A $500,001 transfer therefore has 101 increments and costs $555, including the $50 base levy. A mortgage registration uses its own principal and base levy.
