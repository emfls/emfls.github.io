Key takeaways

Logbook can lower employer FBT when documented business use makes the private-use share of costs smaller than the statutory amount. The statutory method uses 20% of the applicable car base value, prorated for days available for private use.

Eligible after-tax employee contributions reduce taxable value dollar for dollar. Employer FBT is 47% of the grossed-up value. Employee reporting always uses the lower gross-up factor of 1.8868.

Avoid paying more FBT than necessary

Skipping a valid logbook can leave you paying the higher statutory amount. Keep cost and odometer records so you can substantiate the operating cost method. Before increasing a contribution to eliminate employer FBT, weigh the employee’s after-tax payment against the employer’s FBT saving.

RFBA can affect HECS–HELP repayments, the Medicare levy surcharge, Child Care Subsidy and Division 293 tax. Check the employee’s other reportable benefits before relying on this car-only estimate. ATO guidance on RFBA consequences

Frequently asked questions

How is car FBT calculated under the 20% statutory formula?

Multiply the car’s FBT base value by 20% and by the share of the FBT year it was available for private use. Subtract eligible after-tax employee contributions, with a minimum taxable value of zero. For 2026–27 the year has 365 days.

When does the one-third base-value reduction apply?

Apply it from 1 April after the fourth anniversary of when the employer or its associate first held the car, whether owned or leased. The reduction applies once and carries forward. Non-business accessories added after acquisition are excluded from the reduction.

When does the logbook method give a lower result?

It gives a lower result when operating costs multiplied by the private-use share fall below the statutory value before contributions and statutory FBT is still positive. To claim business use, keep adequate cost and odometer records and a valid logbook covering at least 12 continuous weeks in a logbook year.

How much does an employee need to contribute to reduce car FBT to zero?

An eligible after-tax contribution equal to the method’s taxable value before contributions reduces that car’s employer FBT to zero. Use the GST-inclusive contribution where applicable. The calculator shows both the total needed and what remains after contributions already entered.

How does car FBT affect the employee’s income statement?

If the employee’s total reportable taxable benefits exceed AUD 2,000 for the FBT year, the reportable amount uses the Type 2 factor, 1.8868, even if the employer uses Type 1. RFBA can affect HECS–HELP repayments, the Medicare levy surcharge, Child Care Subsidy and Division 293 tax.
