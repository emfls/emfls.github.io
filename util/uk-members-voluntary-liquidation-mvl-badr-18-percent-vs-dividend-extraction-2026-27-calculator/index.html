What could erase the MVL saving?

HMRC’s two-year anti-phoenixing rule. Continuing or resuming the same or a similar trade or activity, directly or indirectly, within two years from a winding-up distribution can cause it to be taxed as dividend income if the other TAAR conditions also apply. Those cover a minimum 5% interest, the company’s close-company status and a main purpose of avoiding or reducing Income Tax.

Closing the company is a formal process for a solvent business and requires a licensed liquidator. Confirm BADR eligibility, the relevant distribution dates and the full liquidation cost before relying on the projected saving. HMRC TAAR guidance · Insolvency Service guidance

Frequently asked questions

When does a liquidation qualify for 18% BADR?

Each shareholder must qualify and claim personally. For typical trading-company shares, the tests usually include being an employee or officer and meeting the personal company shareholding, voting and economic interest tests for two years. Where the trade has ceased, the shares generally must be disposed of within three years.

How does the announced £500,000 BADR limit change my tax?

For qualifying disposals from 6 April 2027, the announced limit would replace the £1 million lifetime limit. Previous claims still count. A shareholder who loses relief on £500,000 of gains that would otherwise qualify at 18% could pay £30,000 more if all those gains move to 24%.

Why can my lump-sum dividend increase the tax on my salary?

Dividends count towards adjusted net income. Above £100,000 the Personal Allowance falls by £1 for every £2 of extra income until it reaches zero. The calculator includes the resulting increase in tax on other income as well as the tax on the dividend itself.

How many years will staged basic-rate dividends take?

The calculator pays up to the available basic-rate headroom each tax year, then pays any remaining balance in the final year. For a 50/50 split, the smaller headroom limits both payments. The projection holds the same income, rates and allowances throughout.

What happens if I restart a similar business within two years?

A similar activity within two years of the distribution can satisfy one condition of the winding-up TAAR. If the ownership, close-company and tax-purpose conditions also apply, the relevant proceeds can be taxed as dividend income.
