Estimate, not a promise

The calculator divides the detected word count by your selected speed. Technical material, unfamiliar vocabulary, accessibility needs, and non-space-delimited languages can change actual reading time.

FAQ

What reading speed should I choose?

225 WPM is a useful starting estimate for general English prose; adjust it for your audience and content.

How are words counted?

The tool uses Unicode-aware sequences of letters, numbers, and internal apostrophes or hyphens.
