Australia CGT 6-Year Absence Rule & Main Residence Calculator

Compare estimated tax on a 2026–27 home sale with and without the absence choice under s118-145.

Key takeaways

A former home can remain fully CGT-exempt for up to six years of income use in each absence.

  • Time genuinely vacant can be covered indefinitely. Moving back in as your main residence can start a new allowance when you move out again.
  • If the sale is only partly exempt and s118-192 applies, both the cost base and the ownership period restart at first income use.

A missed valuation can cost you. Keep evidence of first income use, market value at that date and eligible costs. Claiming this home for overlapping dates can also increase the CGT on a second home.

How the estimate works

The sale contract sets the income year. Apportionment counts settlement days inclusively and starts at purchase settlement or the deemed first-income-use date. A continuous absence uses the six-year calendar anniversary. For interrupted rentals, the calculator pauses the first rental's day allowance during each vacancy. Check borderline interrupted periods against your exact dates.

The calculator applies the non-exempt share to the gain, then applies the discount if eligible. It adds the remaining gain to other taxable income and takes the difference in resident income tax plus the standard levy. The second column assumes no absence choice, so all days away are non-exempt.

For an Australian-resident individual selling a whole dwelling. The estimate excludes capital losses, levy reductions or exemptions, the Medicare levy surcharge, offsets, indexation, mixed home and income use, and partial elections that end the absence choice early.

Frequently asked questions

When does the six-year absence rule start?

First establish the dwelling as your genuine main residence. After you move out, the six-year allowance counts time the dwelling is used to earn income. Non-income-producing vacancy can be covered indefinitely under the absence choice.

Do vacancies or a move back in restart the six years?

A vacancy pauses the rental allowance within the same absence. A fresh allowance is available when you genuinely re-establish the dwelling as your main residence and later move out again.

When must I use the first-rental market value?

Section 118-192 applies if you first used the home to earn income after 20 August 1996, would have qualified for full exemption immediately before that use and receive only a partial exemption on disposal. The market value replaces the earlier cost base and starts the deemed ownership period.

What if I rented the property before moving in?

Rental days before you establish the property as your main residence stay non-exempt. The first-use market value reset is unavailable because the dwelling would not have qualified for a full exemption immediately before its first income use.

Can I claim a different main residence for the same dates?

Generally, choosing the former home under section 118-145 precludes another main residence for the same days. Section 118-140 can allow up to six months of overlap when changing homes if its conditions are met. This calculator models one dwelling only.
