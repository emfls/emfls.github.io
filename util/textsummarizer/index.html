❓ FAQ – Text Summarizer
Is this tool using AI or ChatGPT?
No. It uses a simple word frequency algorithm. Fast and private, but not as smart as AI-based summaries.
Is my text uploaded or stored?
Never. All processing happens in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server.
What languages are supported?
It works best with English. Other languages may work, but results could vary.
How accurate are the summaries?
It picks the most "important" sentences based on word repetition. It’s basic but helpful for quick overviews.
What can I use this for?
Summarizing news, blog posts, essays, research papers, or any long text without needing login or account.

Related Calculators & Quick Guide

The Text Summarizer ranks sentences from pasted text and returns a selection of those sentences at the length you choose.

Does it write new sentences?
It selects sentences from your input, so the output uses the original wording.
What can I change if the result leaves out important context?
Increase the sentence count and check the selected sentences against the original.
  • Measure the length of the original and summary
  • Compare how long the original and summary take to read
  • Clean copied text before summarizing

Scope and limitations

Reviewed on August 11, 2026. This browser tool may vary by device, browser, input data, local processing limits, or pseudorandom behavior. Verify the result independently before important use.
