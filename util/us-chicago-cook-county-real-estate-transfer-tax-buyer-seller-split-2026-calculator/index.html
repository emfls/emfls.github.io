Frequently asked questions

Who pays real estate transfer tax in Chicago?

The buyer typically pays the $3.75 City portion per $500. The seller estimate includes the $1.50 CTA portion, $0.25 Cook County portion and $0.50 Illinois portion per $500. Check the contract and closing statement for the final allocation.

How does $500 stamp rounding work?

Divide taxable sale price by $500 and round up to a whole stamp. In Chicago, $500,000 uses 1,000 stamps and costs $6,000. At $500,000.01 the count is 1,001 and the combined tax is $6,006.

How does Evanston select its municipal rate?

Evanston lists $5 per $1,000 through $1.5 million, $7 over $1.5 million through $5 million, and $9 above $5 million. The calculator selects one rate by sale price for its municipal estimate, which is typically paid by the seller. County and state stamps are added separately.

What does a sale cost where there is no municipal transfer tax?

With $0.25 county tax and $0.50 Illinois tax per $500, a $500,000 sale costs $750 under the default seller allocation. That is $5,250 less in combined tax than Chicago at the same price. Confirm the municipality for the parcel before using this scenario.

Which closing costs does the calculator cover?

It estimates transfer taxes for a standard taxable sale using the same entered amount for every layer. Exemptions, deductions, refunds, recording fees, title costs and property tax prorations require separate figures from your closing agent.
