Key takeaways

One Class 3 year costs £956.80 at the 2026/27 rate and can add about £358.50 a year. If it adds one full qualifying year's worth of pension, the cost is recovered in 2.67 years before tax or 3.34 years at a 20% marginal rate, with pension growth set to 0%.

  • The full new State Pension is £241.30 a week (£12,547.60 over 52 weeks), up 4.8% under the triple lock.
  • In the standard model, a purchase that takes you from 9 to 10 qualifying years unlocks pension based on all 10 years. If work or credits already take you to 10, the purchase has a smaller effect.
  • Class 2 costs £189.80 for a full year at the 2026/27 voluntary rate. Eligibility depends on your circumstances.

How to read your estimate

Your current record gives one estimate. The model then adds future qualifying years to both pension-age scenarios, and adds purchased years only to the top-up scenario. The difference between those two pension-age estimates is the gross gain from buying. All three start at 2026/27 rates.

Break-even starts when you claim. It credits each year's extra pension at the assumed rate for that year and prorates the final year needed to recover the cost. Net profit is the cumulative after-tax gain less the top-up cost. ROI divides net profit by that cost. These are nominal figures and exclude the cost of waiting to claim, opportunity cost, means-tested benefit changes and individual tax allowances.

Pre-2016 records, contracting out, protected payments and overseas contributions can change your entitlement. The exact price of a gap can vary with the tax year and how many weeks you already paid. Use your official forecast and gap quote before paying.

Frequently asked questions

Does buying one year when I have nine unlock the State Pension?

Usually, 10 qualifying years are needed. In the standard model, going from 9 to 10 unlocks 10/35 of the full rate, about £68.94 a week at the 2026/27 rate. If future work or credits already take you to 10, compare your top-up with that higher baseline. Overseas contribution agreements can also affect the minimum.

Does everyone need exactly 35 qualifying years for a full pension?

The 35-year full-rate rule applies to National Insurance records that started from April 2016. Earlier records have a transitional starting amount and contracting out can change how many years you need. Check your State Pension forecast to see what another year will add.

How far back can I fill National Insurance gaps?

The usual window covers the previous six tax years, with a deadline of 5 April each year. The 2025/26 gap, for instance, can generally be filled by 5 April 2032. Some recent years retain their original rates and partly paid years may cost less than a full year.

Can I pay Class 2 instead of Class 3?

Eligible self-employed people can pay the voluntary Class 2 rate, £3.65 a week in 2026/27. Confirm your eligibility for the gap with HMRC. Voluntary Class 2 is unavailable for periods abroad from the 2026/27 tax year onwards.

Will the 2027/28 State Pension automatically mean a tax bill?

The proposed full rate exceeds the standard Personal Allowance on a 52-week basis. Your tax depends on total taxable income and reliefs. The government announced a Pension Tax Allowance from 2027/28 for eligible pensioners with total income up to the full new State Pension.
