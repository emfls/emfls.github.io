How the estimate works

Eligible sellers aged 55 or over can contribute up to A$300,000 each, subject to the combined eligible sale proceeds. The model allocates limited proceeds to member 1 first. Each person must give their fund the approved form at or before contribution. ATO extensions to the usual 90-day deadline require a separate check. The downsizer has no work test, upper age limit or total super balance restriction. It is outside the 2026–27 concessional cap of A$32,500 and non-concessional cap of A$130,000.

First-time retirement-phase entrants from July 2026 generally use the A$2.1 million cap. Enter your ATO personal cap and transfer balance account, including credits and debits, to estimate available room. Where eligible, the retirement-phase amount may earn investment income tax-free inside a complying fund. Accumulation earnings are generally taxed at up to 15%.

The assets test estimates the normal payment reduction at 20 September 2026 rates. It compares the same homeowner status in both scenarios. Enter the assessable part of the proceeds being transferred for the before case and include all other assessable assets in both cases. Eligible proceeds set aside for a replacement principal home can have a temporary exemption, which needs a separate assessment. The taper uses complete A$250 steps above the full-rate threshold. Annual figures use 26 fortnights.

Scope and assumptions

Planning estimate for a standard account-based retirement income stream. The model uses age to count potentially eligible recipients. Actual payments depend on residence, the income test including deeming, rent assistance and other eligibility rules. Illness-separated couples, transitional rates and other income streams have different treatment.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I contribute after selling my home?

Up to A$300,000 per eligible person and A$600,000 for two, capped in total at the eligible proceeds. The special contribution is available for one qualifying disposal per individual.

What is the 2026–27 general transfer balance cap?

A$2.1 million from 1 July 2026. A prior retirement-phase starter should use their ATO personal cap and current transfer balance account. Contributions beyond available retirement-phase room can remain in accumulation.

Do downsizer contributions count under the Age Pension assets test?

Accumulation super is generally exempt below Age Pension age 67. An account-based pension is assessable, and at Age Pension age accumulation generally counts too. Above the full-rate assets limit, the regular reduction is A$3 per fortnight per A$1,000 for a single or a couple combined.
