A price drop can leave you with a tax bill

AMT on a typical exercise and hold uses the share value at exercise. If the shares fall after year-end or remain illiquid, you may still owe tax based on the earlier value. A future credit may take years to use.

Check both holding periods before selling. A qualifying sale occurs after both the one-year anniversary of share transfer at exercise and the two-year anniversary of grant. Its gain is generally long-term capital gain for regular tax. An earlier sale may create ordinary income plus a capital gain or loss. A sale in the exercise year generally removes the AMT exercise adjustment for the shares sold.

What the estimate covers

The estimate uses the 2026 ordinary-income rate schedules before credits. It adds back either the basic standard deduction or the entered Schedule A taxes. It assumes the entered options qualify as ISOs, the shares are vested at exercise and held through year-end, and the ISO spread is the only AMT deferral item.

Actual return calculations can differ because of IRS tax-table and return rounding, preferential income rates, other deductions or AMT adjustments, and credits. The estimate excludes state taxes, payroll taxes, sale proceeds, and prior-year minimum tax credit balances. The share limit is a tax calculation and may exceed your available grants.

Frequently asked questions

How does an ISO exercise create AMT?

For a typical vested ISO exercise with shares held past year-end, multiply the exercise-date fair market value minus the strike price by the shares exercised, with a floor of zero. Add that bargain element to alternative minimum taxable income. Additional AMT is the positive difference between tentative minimum tax and the regular tax comparison amount.

What changed in the 2026 AMT exemption phase-out?

The exemption falls by 50 cents per dollar of AMTI above $500,000 for single, head of household, and married filing separately, or $1 million for married filing jointly. When the exemption phases out, another dollar can increase taxable AMTI by $1.50. That produces a 39% or 42% tentative marginal rate at 26% or 28%, respectively. In the 2026 phase-out bands shown here, the 28% bracket already applies.

How is the zero-AMT share limit calculated?

The calculator finds the largest spread in whole cents that keeps tentative minimum tax at or below regular tax. It divides that spread by the positive spread per share and rounds down to a whole share. Existing AMT makes a zero-AMT exercise unavailable. If the per-share spread is zero and baseline AMT is zero, AMT sets no share limit.

What makes an ISO sale qualifying or disqualifying?

To qualify, sell after both the one-year anniversary of share transfer at exercise and the two-year anniversary of grant. A qualifying gain is generally long-term capital gain for regular tax. An earlier sale can create ordinary income, and gain beyond the ordinary income portion may be capital gain. A same-year sale generally eliminates the ISO exercise adjustment for those shares.

Can I recover ISO-related AMT through Form 8801?

AMT attributable to the ISO deferral may generate a credit for future years. The estimate subtracts AMT on exclusion items such as the standard deduction or SALT from total modeled AMT. Future credit use generally depends on how much regular tax exceeds tentative minimum tax in that year. Unused eligible credit may carry forward.
