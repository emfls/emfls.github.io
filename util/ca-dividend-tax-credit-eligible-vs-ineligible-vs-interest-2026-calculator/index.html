Compare eligible dividends, non-eligible dividends, capital gains and interest in Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta. See how each affects your Old Age Security (OAS).

Key takeaways

Eligible dividends receive the larger tax credit and add more reportable income. Each $100 adds $138 for eligible dividends, $115 for non-eligible dividends, $50 for realized capital gains and $100 for interest or foreign dividends.

Compare the change in your total tax and OAS repayment for the same cash amount. A Canadian dividend can lower your income tax even while raising the income used for OAS recovery.

Net tax owed is the change in combined federal tax, provincial tax and OAS recovery from the baseline. A negative amount reflects lower total liability. The dividend credits themselves are non-refundable.

The 38% gross-up can cost you OAS

$70,000 in eligible dividends adds $96,600 to income before recovery. That dividend amount alone exceeds the 2026 threshold of $95,323. At $96,600 of total recovery income, the gross repayment is $191.55 if you received at least that much OAS. Your actual recovery calculation also includes your gross OAS and other applicable income.

What the estimate covers

The model assumes a Canadian resident with a taxable, non-registered account. It uses the entered baseline for both taxable and OAS recovery income before the line 23500 deduction. It includes basic personal credits, Ontario and B.C. basic reductions, and the federal and provincial dividend credits.

The estimate excludes age and pension income credits, employment and dependant credits, foreign withholding and foreign tax credits, special OAS income adjustments, other benefit clawbacks, deductions after line 23600 and alternative minimum tax. Frequently asked questions

Which Canadian dividends qualify for the eligible credit?

Canadian corporations can designate eligible dividends, typically from profits taxed at the general corporate rate. Non-eligible dividends commonly come from small-business-rate profits. Use the designation on your T5 or T3 slip.

How can the effective tax rate on a dividend be negative?

The new dividend credits can reduce tax already owed on your other income by more than the grossed-up dividend adds. The calculator measures the change from your baseline. Unused Canadian dividend credits do not create a standalone refund.

How do eligible dividends affect the OAS clawback?

Each $1 of eligible dividends adds $1.38 to income before recovery. The 2026 repayment is 15% of adjusted income over $95,323, capped at the OAS received. The tax return then deducts the recovery on line 23500 and adds it to total payable.

Is the 50% capital gains inclusion applied to my sale proceeds?

It applies to the realized gain after cost basis and selling expenses. For example, a $10,000 realized gain adds $5,000 of income. The model assumes a taxable gain without losses or exemptions.

Do foreign dividends receive the Canadian dividend tax credit?

Foreign dividends are generally included in full in Canadian income and do not qualify for a Canadian dividend tax credit. Foreign tax paid may be eligible for a separate credit, which this comparison excludes.
