The IRA rollover trap

A later IRA withdrawal never qualifies for the QDRO exception. If you withdraw before age 59½ and no separate IRA exception applies, each $10,000 of fully taxable cash withdrawn adds $1,000 in federal tax.

QDRO withdrawal FAQ

Who can use the direct QDRO cash exception?

This calculator covers spouse and former-spouse alternate payees who receive a fully pre-tax, rollover-eligible award directly from a 401(k) or 403(b) under a plan-qualified domestic relations order. Ask the plan administrator which payment options the order and plan allow.

Why is my check smaller than the QDRO cash amount?

A taxable eligible rollover distribution paid to you generally has 20% federal withholding, plus any applicable state withholding. Withholding is a payment toward tax. The 2026 estimate uses the increase in federal tax across brackets and your entered state rate to estimate the payout’s effect at filing.

Does the QDRO exception follow the money into a traditional IRA?

IRC §72(t)(3)(A) excludes IRA withdrawals from the QDRO exception. Before age 59½, a later withdrawal generally adds 10% federal tax on the taxable portion unless you qualify for a separate IRA exception. The direct rollover itself does not incur the additional tax.

Can I take cash and roll over the rest of the award?

If your order and plan permit the split, request the cash portion from the plan under the QDRO and have the rest paid directly to a traditional IRA. The plan withholds federal tax on the taxable eligible cash portion. The direct rollover stays tax-deferred.

What if the divorce award already sits in an IRA?

An IRA interest transferred to a spouse or former spouse under a qualifying divorce or separation instrument can qualify for tax-free transfer treatment under IRC §408(d)(6). A later withdrawal is subject to IRA distribution rules. Use this calculator for a QDRO payment originating in an employer plan.
