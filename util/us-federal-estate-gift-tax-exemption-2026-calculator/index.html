A $0 federal estimate can still leave a state tax bill

A missed portability election can cost the surviving spouse unused exclusion. State thresholds can also expose an estate to tax well below the federal threshold.

  • Oregon $1 million, Massachusetts $2 million, and Illinois $4 million.
  • Washington $3.076 million for deaths January through June 2026 and $3 million starting July 1, 2026.
  • New York $7.35 million. The credit phases out above the exclusion and reaches zero at 105% ($7,717,500), exposing the full taxable estate to the state schedule. See New York Tax Law § 952.

State filing tests and deductions vary. State estate tax and related federal deductions are outside this estimate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 2026 federal estate and gift tax exemption?

The basic exclusion is $15 million per person in 2026. OBBBA removed the scheduled sunset and provides for inflation adjustments after 2026. Federal estate tax generally increases by 40 cents for every dollar above the available threshold.

How can a surviving spouse reach $30 million in federal exclusion?

A survivor with the full $15 million of eligible deceased spousal unused exclusion (DSUE) can combine it with the survivor’s own $15 million basic exclusion in 2026. The first spouse’s executor generally must elect portability on Form 706, even for an estate below the filing threshold. Prior use can reduce what remains available.

Does the $19,000 annual gift exclusion reduce the lifetime exclusion?

A qualifying present-interest gift within the $19,000 annual exclusion per donor per recipient does not use lifetime exclusion. Eligible spouses who consent to split gifts can cover $38,000 per recipient. Taxable gifts above those amounts can use lifetime exclusion, and gift splitting generally requires Form 709 reporting.

Are prior taxable gifts counted twice on Form 706?

Form 706 adds adjusted taxable gifts to the taxable estate, applies the unified rate schedule, then subtracts the applicable credit and any gift tax payable computed for line 7. Adjusted taxable gifts exclude gifts included in the gross estate. The line 7 amount uses the IRS worksheet, which may differ from historical gift taxes paid.

How much federal estate tax can ten years of annual gifts save?

At constant 2026 limits, gifts to three recipients over ten years total $570,000 for one donor or $1,140,000 for eligible spouses. A fully taxable estate could save $228,000 or $456,000, respectively, if the full transfer reduces the taxable estate. Savings fall when the transfers cross the federal threshold.
