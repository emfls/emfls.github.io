QLD vs WA vs SA Land Tax Calculator 2026–27

Compare annual land tax, ownership surcharges and ten years of holding costs at the same taxable land value.

Key takeaways

  • At $800,000 of taxable land, an individual would pay $0 in SA, $1,250 in WA and $2,500 in QLD annually.
  • QLD’s individual scale starts at $600,000. Its company, trustee and absentee scales start at $350,000. The 3% absentee or foreign surcharge applies to the excess above $350,000.
  • WA charges $300 just above its $300,000 threshold. SA’s general threshold is $936,000, while its trust scale starts just above $25,000.

Land tax questions

What land value should I enter?

Enter the combined taxable land value for one ownership as at midnight on 30 June 2026. Use the state assessment’s taxable value after exemptions and apply the same number across states for a like-for-like comparison.

How does Queensland’s 3% surcharge work?

QLD adds 3% of taxable land value above $350,000 for eligible absentee individuals, foreign companies and foreign trustees. Above $5 million the absentee individual base scale differs from the company and trustee scale.

How does a discretionary trust affect land tax?

QLD generally uses its company and trustee scale from $350,000. WA uses its standard rate scale. In SA the trust scale generally starts above $25,000, although certain trust holdings can qualify for the general scale.

What is included in Western Australia’s estimate?

The calculator uses RevenueWA’s statewide land tax scale. The separate Metropolitan Region Improvement Tax may apply to taxable land in the Perth metropolitan area and needs its own calculation.

How is the 10-year holding cost calculated?

The estimate multiplies the selected annual amount by ten. It holds taxable values, thresholds, rates and ownership constant, so future assessment notices may differ.
