Australia Age Pension Work Bonus Calculator 2026–27

Estimate how much Age Pension you could keep with the $300 fortnightly Work Bonus and an income bank of up to $11,800.

Key takeaways

  • Services Australia automatically offsets up to $300 of eligible work income each fortnight before applying the Age Pension income test.
  • Unused credit builds an individual balance up to $11,800. A first claim for an eligible payment generally starts with $4,000.
  • At the standard taper, every $1 the bonus keeps out of the income-test taper can preserve up to 50¢ of pension for a single person or a couple receiving two pensions, and up to 25¢ for one partnered recipient.

Frequently asked questions

How does the $300 Work Bonus apply to my pay?

Services Australia uses up to $300 of fortnightly credit first, then draws from your Work Bonus balance. For example, $650 of eligible pay with a $200 balance leaves $150 of assessable work income. The pension income test then adds any other assessable income.

How does the $11,800 bank build up, and who starts with $4,000?

If you earn $200 in eligible work income in a fortnight, the unused $100 can increase your balance until it reaches $11,800. First-time claimants of an eligible payment generally start with $4,000. Returning recipients should use their actual Centrelink balance.

Can partners share a Work Bonus balance?

Each eligible partner has their own credit and balance. A partner’s spare credit cannot offset your wages. Once each eligible person’s Work Bonus is applied, the remaining income counts toward the combined income test.

Does Work Bonus reduce deemed income, rent or super pension income?

Work Bonus applies to eligible wages and qualifying active self-employment income earned through personal exertion. Include the assessed amount from deeming, rent or super pensions in other income where applicable. These amounts cannot use the Work Bonus balance.

Why can seasonal and steady work produce different pension totals?

Fortnights with little or no eligible work can build the balance. A contract can spend that balance in a few pay cycles. Even with the same annual pay, the timing of income, the fortnightly free area and the pension floor can change the total pension.
