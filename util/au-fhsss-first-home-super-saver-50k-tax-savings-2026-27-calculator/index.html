How the comparison works

Both scenarios start in July 2026 with monthly deposits on the first of each month. The projection holds salary, the selected interest rates and 2026–27 tax and cap settings constant through the final June. FHSS deemed earnings use the number of days in each calendar year. Bank interest compounds daily and the model deducts tax on that interest each financial year using progressive income tax.

The bank-only path saves the after-tax value of your pre-tax budget plus any additional after-tax budget. In the FHSS path, eligible contributions go into super and the after-tax value of budget above the limits goes into the bank. If both contribution types arrive together, after-tax contributions count first.

The release estimate uses your final-year taxable salary after modelled sacrifice and applies one marginal rate to the assessable release. Actual tax can differ if the release changes your tax bracket. This projection assumes no prior FHSS contributions, enough after-tax contribution cap space and that all salary is qualifying earnings for SG. It excludes carry-forward caps, Division 293 tax, tax offsets other than FHSS, partial Medicare reductions, the Medicare levy surcharge, student debt and fees.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I put towards my first home through FHSS?

Up to A$15,000 of eligible voluntary contributions per financial year and A$50,000 per person in total can count. Both limits use the original contribution before the 85% release factor. Mandatory employer super guarantee uses concessional cap space but does not count towards FHSS.

Can two buyers use FHSS for the same home?

Yes. Two eligible buyers may each use their own A$50,000 contribution limit towards the same Australian home, for up to A$100,000 in combined eligible contributions. Each person requests their own determination and release, and tax is calculated individually.

How does the ATO calculate associated earnings?

The ATO compounds deemed earnings daily on 85% of eligible concessional contributions and 100% of eligible after-tax contributions. For current contributions, earnings start on the first day of the contribution month and run through the determination date. The SIC can change each quarter.

What tax applies to an FHSS release?

The assessable part is 85% of eligible concessional contributions plus all associated earnings. After-tax contribution principal is excluded. The calculator uses a single marginal rate plus the selected Medicare levy, less a 30 percentage point offset, with a zero floor. At 30% income tax and 2% Medicare, the estimate is 2%. The release can move part of your income into another bracket at assessment.

What happens if I release money and cannot buy a home?

If you miss the applicable contract or notification requirements, you may need to recontribute the required amount and notify the ATO. Keeping the money without meeting the requirements can trigger a separate 20% FHSS tax on the assessable release.
