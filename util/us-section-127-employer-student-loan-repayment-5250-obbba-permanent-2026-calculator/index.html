How the estimates work

The model applies qualified tuition assistance to the shared $5,250 cap first. The cash comparison replaces only the federally excluded loan benefit. Both scenarios include assistance above the cap in wages and assume it is taxable. If your state taxes the benefit, the taxable raise matches its value after state tax.

Within a single rate band, the equivalent raise equals the after-tax loan benefit divided by one minus the combined marginal tax rate. The calculation applies Social Security and Additional Medicare only to the portion of cash pay within their respective taxable ranges. It holds your federal and state marginal rates constant and uses this employer’s wages for Social Security. It excludes credits, local taxes, and self-employment income.

The payoff estimate adds one-twelfth of the 2026 tax-free loan benefit to your own payment every month until payoff. It keeps the annual benefit and loan rate constant, accrues interest monthly at the annual rate divided by 12, and caps the last payment at the amount due. Actual plans may end sooner, and servicers may accrue interest daily or apply payments differently. Taxable employer payments, fees and forgiveness are outside the projection.

FAQ

Is employer student loan repayment permanently tax-free under Section 127?

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act removed the sunset for qualified student loan payments under Section 127. The combined exclusion is $5,250 per employee in 2026. Inflation indexing starts in 2027.

How does tuition assistance affect my $5,250 limit?

Qualified tuition assistance and employer payments of qualified student loan principal or interest share the same federal limit. If your employer provides $2,000 for tuition in 2026, up to $3,250 remains for loan payments. Amounts above the limit generally count as taxable W-2 wages unless another exclusion applies.

Who can use the benefit and who receives the payment?

Your employer needs a qualifying written educational assistance plan that covers student loans. The loan must qualify and have been taken out for your own education. Depending on the plan, your employer can pay you or your loan servicer.

Do state tax rules and the Social Security wage cap affect savings?

Yes. Check state conformity separately. In 2026, a cash raise incurs 6.2% Social Security tax only on wages up to $184,500. Regular Medicare applies to all covered wages, and only the employee may owe the 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax.

Can I deduct interest paid through a tax-free Section 127 benefit?

Leave interest covered by the tax-free benefit out of the student loan interest deduction. Eligible interest you pay yourself may qualify for a deduction of up to $2,500, subject to income and filing rules.
