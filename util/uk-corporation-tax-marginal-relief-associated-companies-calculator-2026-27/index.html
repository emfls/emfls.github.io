Frequently asked questions

How is Corporation Tax Marginal Relief calculated?

Inside the adjusted band, multiply the upper limit minus augmented profits by taxable profits divided by augmented profits, then multiply by 3/200. Subtract that relief from 25% of taxable profits.

How do associated companies and short periods change the limits?

Divide both limits by one plus the number of other associated companies. With three others, the full-year limits become £12,500 and £62,500. For a short period in this model, multiply both by accounting days divided by 365.

Which dividends count towards augmented profits?

Include qualifying exempt ABGH distributions that are not excluded under the group rules. These amounts can move the company into a higher band or reduce Marginal Relief. The rates apply to taxable profits.

Can the same equipment qualify for both full expensing and AIA?

Use either allowance for the same expenditure. Full expensing deducts 100% of qualifying new and unused main-rate plant and machinery with no annual cap. AIA also covers some second-hand equipment and is generally limited to £1 million per year, subject to period and sharing rules.

How much Corporation Tax can a director's employer pension contribution save?

An allowable £5,000 employer contribution saves £1,325 if it stays entirely within the Marginal Relief band and there are no qualifying exempt distributions. The employer generally claims the deduction in the period it pays a registered scheme. The payment must meet the wholly and exclusively test. Timing and personal pension allowances can affect the outcome.
