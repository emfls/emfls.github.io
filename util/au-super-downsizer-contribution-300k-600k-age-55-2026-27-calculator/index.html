Australia Super Downsizer Contribution Calculator 2026–27

Check whether you can put up to A$300,000 each into super from a home sale and how that could affect your Age Pension.

Key takeaways

  • Up to A$300,000 each or A$600,000 per couple. Both members must qualify, and the total is limited to the sale proceeds. One spouse can be the sole owner.
  • Age 55+, 10 years of ownership and usually 90 days after settlement. Give your fund form NAT 75073 before or with each payment.
  • Separate contribution and retirement rules. Downsizer contributions sit outside ordinary contribution caps. Transfers into retirement phase use each member’s personal cap.

Check what you could lose in Age Pension

Your principal home is generally exempt. Super generally becomes assessable at age 67, and an income stream can count earlier. A$100,000 newly above the full-rate threshold can reduce the combined payment for a two-recipient couple by up to A$7,800 over 26 fortnights. That can exceed the tax benefit on investment earnings.

The estimate isolates the new assessable amount against the same asset baseline. On a sale, cash outside super may already be assessable. Qualifying proceeds set aside for a replacement principal home can be exempt for up to 24 months, with a possible extension. Frequently asked questions

How much can we contribute if only one spouse was on the title?

Each eligible spouse can contribute up to A$300,000, even if only one held the property title. Together you can contribute up to A$600,000, limited to the sale proceeds available after any earlier downsizer contributions from that sale. Each spouse must qualify separately.

Does the ATO apply a maximum age or a work test?

You must be at least 55 when the contribution reaches your super fund. There is no maximum age or work test for a qualifying downsizer contribution, including at age 75 or older. Access to the resulting super balance follows separate release rules.

When is the deadline and can I pay in instalments?

The standard deadline is 90 calendar days after the ownership change, normally settlement when you receive the proceeds. Give your super fund NAT 75073 before or with each payment. You can make multiple payments from the same sale within the allowed period and caps. For a later payment, confirm any extension with the ATO. You cannot use the scheme again on another home sale.

What caps apply when the money goes into super and retirement phase?

An eligible downsizer contribution enters the tax-free component and does not use your concessional or non-concessional cap. Your total super balance does not block the contribution, but the amount is included in your balance at the next measurement. Retirement-phase transfers use your personal transfer balance cap. The 2026–27 general cap is A$2.1 million.

Can selling my home and adding money to super reduce my Age Pension?

Your principal home is generally exempt. Once you reach Age Pension age, super generally counts in the assets test and under income-test deeming. A younger partner’s accumulation super is generally exempt while it is not paying an income stream. The standard assets taper is A$3 per fortnight per A$1,000 of excess assets for a single recipient or a couple combined. Qualifying proceeds set aside for a replacement home can have a temporary assets-test exemption.
