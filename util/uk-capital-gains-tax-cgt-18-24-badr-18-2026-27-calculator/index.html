Key takeaways

Most individuals can take £3,000 of gains tax-free. Above that, the unused basic-rate band is taxed at 18% and any excess at 24%. Qualifying Business Asset Disposal Relief (BADR) gains are taxed at 18% within the £1 million lifetime cap.

April 2027 BADR cap cut could add up to £30,000

The Finance Bill proposes cutting the lifetime cap from £1 million to £500,000 for qualifying disposals from 6 April 2027. If the full £500,000 difference would move from 18% to 24%, the extra CGT is 6% × £500,000 = £30,000. The current tax year ends 5 April 2027. Check disposal timing, eligibility and the transitional rules before relying on the comparison. Finance Bill, clause 8 and Schedule 1

UK residential property has a 60-day reporting deadline

Most UK residents who owe CGT on UK residential property must report and pay within 60 days of completion. Non-residents generally must report UK property or land disposals even when there is no tax to pay. HMRC property reporting rules

Bed and ISA can shelter future gains

If you have £3,000 of unused CGT allowance, selling eligible shares with an embedded £3,000 gain can use it with £0 CGT. You can then repurchase eligible investments in a stocks and shares ISA if you have room for the subscription. A later £3,000 gain inside the ISA is free of UK CGT. Outside the ISA, the same gain could cost £540 at 18% or £720 at 24% if it were fully taxable. HMRC guidance on Bed and ISA

Frequently asked questions

Which CGT rate applies to shares, crypto and UK residential property?

For an individual in 2026/27, taxable gains in the unused UK basic-rate band are charged at 18% and the rest at 24%. The £37,700 band applies to taxable income after the Personal Allowance, including for Scottish taxpayers. Most trustees pay a flat 24% on standard gains.

How do brought-forward capital losses interact with the allowance?

Use eligible losses from previous years only to bring gains down to the annual exempt amount. Any unused balance carries forward. Losses arising in the same tax year are dealt with first.

What changes for Business Asset Disposal Relief in April 2027?

Qualifying gains are taxed at 18% in 2026/27 within the £1 million lifetime cap. The Finance Bill proposes a £500,000 cap for disposals from 6 April 2027, including prior claims. Up to £500,000 could move from 18% to 24%, adding up to £30,000. Transitional rules may affect the disposal date used.

Can a spouse transfer their CGT allowance or basic-rate band?

Each spouse or civil partner uses their own allowance and band. A qualifying no-gain/no-loss transfer carries over the original cost. If the receiving partner becomes the beneficial owner before the sale, they account for their share of the gain using their own available allowance and band.

Does Bed and ISA avoid tax on the original sale?

Selling investments held outside an ISA is a CGT disposal. Eligible investments repurchased inside a stocks and shares ISA can grow free of UK CGT. ISA subscription limits, fees and price movements can affect the benefit.
