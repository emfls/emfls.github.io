Compare the 20% merged R&D expenditure credit with ERIS cash support. See the PAYE cap and estimate Patent Box savings separately.

Key takeaways

  • Merged RDEC: 20% gross. A credit fully taxed at 25%, 19% or the 26.5% marginal rate leaves 15%, 16.2% or 14.7% respectively before payment restrictions. An eligible loss-maker can get up to 16.2% in cash before the PAYE cap and other offsets.
  • ERIS: A loss-making SME that meets the 30% intensity test can deduct an extra 86% and surrender eligible losses for a 14.5% credit. The maximum before the payroll cap is 26.97% of qualifying R&D spend.
  • Patent Box: The 10% rate saves £15,000 per £100,000 of qualifying IP profit in the 25% main-rate comparison.

Check what you give up before surrendering a loss

An ERIS cash payment uses up trading losses that could otherwise shelter profits. Before surrendering them, compare the cash now with any tax savings from keeping the loss. Frequently asked questions

How much is the 20% merged RDEC worth after tax?

A credit wholly within the 25%, 19% or 26.5% incremental tax band leaves 15%, 16.2% or 14.7% of qualifying R&D spend respectively, before any payable-credit restrictions. If the credit crosses a band, the calculator uses the change in Corporation Tax across the bands.

Who qualifies for ERIS at the 30% threshold?

The company must be an SME with a tax trading loss before the 86% enhancement. At least 30% of relevant expenditure must qualify as R&D under the intensity rules, including connected-company expenditure where applicable. An eligible prior claim may allow one year of grace.

What limits the maximum 26.97% ERIS cash return?

The 14.5% cash rate applies to the lower of the available enhanced trading loss and 186% of qualifying R&D spend. A smaller loss or the payroll cap can reduce the cash received. The loss surrendered can no longer be used for other relief.

How does the PAYE and NIC cap affect each scheme?

For a 12-month period the cap is £20,000 plus three times relevant PAYE and Class 1 NIC liabilities unless exempt. Excess merged RDEC at this payment step carries forward. ERIS cash claims must stay within the cap.

What profit should I enter for Patent Box?

Use the qualifying IP profit after applying HMRC streaming and the R&D fraction. On the 25% main-rate benchmark, £100,000 of qualifying profit produces £15,000 of tax savings at the 10% Patent Box rate. Patent Box requires an election.
