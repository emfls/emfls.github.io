How the estimate works

The 2026–27 fuel-efficient luxury car threshold is A$91,661. An otherwise eligible electric car can meet the price test at exactly A$91,661 if no LCT has ever been payable. Check earlier acquisition or import history for used cars. A brand-new PHEV commitment is outside the exemption from 1 April 2025. Some existing, financially binding exempt arrangements may continue.

The 2026–27 resident marginal bands are 0% through A$18,200, 15% through A$45,000, 30% through A$135,000, 37% through A$190,000 and 45% above. Gross resident tax at A$45,000 is A$4,020. The salary comparison calculates tax before and after package deductions, includes the low income tax offset and uses the selected full Medicare levy or levy exclusion scenario. Reduced levies and other offsets need a separate estimate.

The indicative purchase GST credit is one-eleventh of the GST-inclusive vehicle value, capped at A$6,353 (A$69,883 car limit). This assumes the value entered is the GST-inclusive creditable purchase price before the cap. Use the provider’s net quote so the purchase credit is not deducted twice. Eligible running expenses entered as fully GST-creditable are reduced by one-eleventh. Put GST-free and partly creditable expenses into other costs after any applicable credit.

Even with A$0 employer FBT, the notional reportable fringe benefits amount (RFBA) can be substantial. This full-year statutory estimate uses base value × 20% × 1.8868. A base value of A$65,000 gives A$24,528.40. The reporting threshold is more than A$2,000 in aggregate notional taxable value for the FBT year. Employer reporting can depend on car availability, employee contributions and the valuation method.

This is a full-year cash-flow scenario, with full-exemption estimates limited to confirmed eligible commitments by 31 March 2027. The ATO lists proposed changes for new commitments from 1 April 2027. Verify the status and terms of an April–June commitment. Include comparable loan repayments or annualised cash purchase costs and operating costs. The annual difference excludes separate residual payments, resale value, timing of cash flows, HELP, MLS, Child Care Subsidy and Division 293 effects.

Frequently asked questions

Can an EV at exactly A$91,661 qualify?

Yes, it can pass the 2026–27 price test at that value. The other exemption conditions, including whether luxury car tax was ever payable, still apply.

Does a zero FBT bill mean zero impact on HELP or the Medicare levy surcharge?

The reportable amount can affect HECS-HELP repayments, Medicare levy surcharge (2026–27 base thresholds A$105,000 single and A$210,000 family, with applicable child adjustments), Child Care Subsidy, and Division 293 (A$250,000, including relevant super contributions). Each test has its own income rules.

Can I deduct the purchase GST credit from the net lease quote again?

Enter annual lease finance payments after any credits already passed on by the provider. The purchase credit is shown separately for checking the quote.
