Key takeaways

  • At an 8.51% MPIR, a $550,000 room costs $128.23 per day or $46,805 per 365 days as full DAP, before indexation.
  • Under the new rules, retention accrues daily at 2% p.a. on the RAD balance for up to five years. With retention as the only deduction, this model refunds at least 90% of the deposit.
  • A RAD can improve your Age Pension under the assets test. The benefit depends on your eligibility, income test and where your assets sit within the taper band.

Projected accommodation costs

Net estate cost is the loss of end-of-stay wealth relative to keeping the full room price invested with no accommodation charges. It adds retention, foregone after-tax growth on the RAD and future-valued DAP, then subtracts future-valued extra pension. A negative cost represents a projected net gain relative to that benchmark.

Consider cash access as well as the fee

The 2% retention is a visible loss. DAP payments, lost investment growth and the benefit of keeping cash available also matter. A larger RAD ties up more cash until refund. Check that you can cover ongoing care costs and unexpected bills under each option before relying on the lowest projected cost.

How the estimate works

  • DAP. Starting daily DAP is unpaid room price × agreed MPIR ÷ 365. Starting annual DAP uses 365 days. Base DAP is carried at full precision for this estimate, and indexed DAP uses the published rounding method. The projection counts actual calendar days.
  • RAD. Retention is calculated on the balance and posted monthly in this model. The estimate stops at the fifth anniversary and limits retention to 10% of the original deposit. Your provider’s posting dates and any separately authorised deductions affect the refund.
  • Indexation. Published DAP indices apply through September 2026. Future indices use the annual CPI assumption spread over two changes each year. The reference index is the latest one on or before the care start date.
  • Estate and pension. The model keeps the after-tax return and estimated pension increase constant. It assumes fixed payment shares and DAP paid from liquid funds, and excludes care fees, other RAD deductions, refund timing and changes to pension eligibility. RAD can still count in the separate aged care means assessment.

Frequently asked questions

How much of a RAD can the aged care provider retain?

Under the new accommodation rules, retention accrues daily at 2% a year on the remaining balance and stops five years after the first RAD payment. Regular deductions reduce the balance used for future accrual. The refund can also be affected by other authorised deductions.

Which MPIR should I enter for my DAP?

Use the MPIR in effect when you agreed to the room price. The published rate for 1 October to 31 December 2026 is 8.51%. The agreement rate sets the base DAP, and eligible daily payments can then rise under DAP indexation.

How does DAP indexation work with a combination payment?

The provider applies the index on 20 March and 20 September under eligible arrangements. For a fixed split, you pay only the unpaid share of the indexed maximum DAP. Retention deductions from the RAD do not increase that unpaid share.

What happens if I have grandfathered accommodation arrangements?

Under the grandfathered accommodation rules modelled here, RAD retention is zero and the DAP stays at its starting rate. With no other deductions, the entire RAD is refundable. Confirm your arrangement after any move or break in care.

Will paying a RAD automatically increase my Age Pension?

A RAD is exempt from the social security assets test. An actual increase depends on your starting assets, pension eligibility, income and the applicable rate limits. It can still count as an asset for the separate residential aged care means assessment.
