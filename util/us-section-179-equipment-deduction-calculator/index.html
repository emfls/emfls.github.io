FAQ

  

    What is the IRS Section 179 spending cap and business-use requirement?
    
The annual Section 179 limits above apply to the business-use share of qualifying property. Section 179 requires business use above 50%. For listed property such as a heavy SUV, this threshold also applies to bonus depreciation. This calculator uses the above-50% threshold for both asset choices.

  

  

    What is the Section 179 limit for heavy SUVs over 6,000 lbs GVWR?
    
For SUVs subject to the cap, the Section 179 limit is $32,000 for 2026 ($31,300 for the 2025 comparison). Eligible remaining business basis can qualify for bonus depreciation.

  

  

    What happens if taxable income limits my Section 179 deduction?
    
The elected amount exceeding the income limit carries forward to a later tax year, subject to that year's limits. The full election reduces the basis available for bonus depreciation in Year 1.
