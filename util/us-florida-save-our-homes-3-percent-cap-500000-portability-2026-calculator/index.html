Estimate the assessment you can transfer to your new Florida homestead and compare your property taxes over 10 years.

Key takeaways

  • An eligible move can transfer up to $500,000 of assessed-value reduction. An equal- or higher-value home keeps the dollar benefit up to the cap. A lower-value home keeps the old percentage discount up to the cap.
  • Florida’s annual SOH ceiling is the lesser of 3% or CPI. The 2026 cap is 2.7% for an existing homestead. A newly established homestead starts at just value minus any approved transfer.
  • For a new 2026 homestead, the lookback uses the January 1 tax rolls for 2023, 2024 or 2025. File DR-501T with DR-501 by the annual March 1 deadline, extended to March 2 in 2026.

Protect your assessment savings before you move

Once the three-tax-year eligibility window closes, the prior SOH benefit generally can’t be transferred. The window uses January 1 tax rolls. A closing date can give you the wrong deadline. If you missed the filing deadline, contact the new county’s property appraiser promptly about late-filing options.

Florida homestead portability FAQ

Is Florida’s Save Our Homes cap 3% in 2026?

The statutory maximum is 3%, but the annual cap uses the lower of 3% or qualifying CPI. The 2026 figure is 2.7%. It applies to reassessments after the first homestead year, and assessed value cannot exceed just value.

How much can I transfer to a home of equal or higher value?

Subtract the prior home’s SOH assessed value from its just value. An eligible transfer carries that dollar difference to the new homestead, up to $500,000. The transferred amount reduces the new home’s initial assessment before exemptions.

How does portability work if I downsize?

Divide the old SOH benefit by the old just value, multiply that percentage by the new just value, and apply the $500,000 cap. For example, a $240,000 benefit on a $600,000 home gives a 40% discount, or a $160,000 transfer to a $400,000 home.

Which 2026 homestead exemptions apply to school taxes?

The first $25,000 of assessed value is exempt from both school and non-school millage. The inflation-adjusted exemption on assessed value above $50,000 is up to $26,411 and applies only to non-school millage.

When do I file DR-501T and how does the three-year window work?

For a new 2026 homestead, you must have received the old Florida homestead exemption on January 1 of 2023, 2024 or 2025 to pass the lookback. File DR-501T with DR-501 at the new county property appraiser by the annual March 1 deadline, extended to March 2 in 2026. Contact the appraiser about late-filing options.
