Frequently asked questions

What does California tax on RSU vests and stock sales?

Include the value of RSUs at vesting as wages. For shares later sold at a long-term gain, enter only the net gain above cost basis. California taxes long-term gains at ordinary income rates and applies its 1.3% uncapped SDI contribution to covered wages.

How does Washington tax long-term gains in 2026?

The 2026 deduction is $290,000 for a single filer or a married couple combined. The rate is 7% on the first $1 million of taxable Washington gains after the deduction and 9.9% on any excess. The calculator assumes the gains entered are eligible stock or similar investment gains allocated to Washington.

Can Texas property taxes offset the savings from moving?

Yes. For California to Texas, the calculator subtracts the difference between the two estimated annual property bills from the income and payroll tax savings. For an existing California home, use the current annual bill divided by market value to reflect the Proposition 13 assessment and local charges.

Can my former state tax RSUs after I move?

Yes. Some equity pay can stay taxable where you worked during the period from grant to vest. This calculator compares full-year resident scenarios. Review the sourcing rules, grant dates and workdays before applying the savings to a move year.

How does the calculator project the 10-year wealth gap?

It applies the same 2026 scenario in each of ten years and adds the signed annual savings at year-end. The balance grows at the entered net return, so a negative annual gap becomes a projected shortfall. One-time gains and future tax changes can materially change the estimate.
