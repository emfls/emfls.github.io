Frequently Asked Questions: Australian Bonus Withholding

What is ATO Schedule 5 and why must employers use it?

Schedule 5 is the ATO's official formula for tax withholding on back pay, commissions, bonuses, and similar payments. It prevents tax spike distortions by apportioning the bonus across the pay cycle rather than taxing it as a one-off regular pay spike.

Can my employer refuse to pay 12% super on my bonus?

Under the Superannuation Guarantee (Administration) Act 1992, bonuses that relate to work performed or general company incentives are Ordinary Time Earnings (OTE) and legally require 12% employer super contributions.

How are HECS-HELP repayments deducted from bonuses?

Under ATO formulas, if your annualized income exceeds $69,528, compulsory HECS repayments apply marginally at 15% on income up to $129,717, and 17% up to $186,050. This can result in higher immediate deductions from your bonus check.

When will I receive my overwithheld bonus tax back?

Any excess tax withheld by your employer during the financial year is reconciled when you lodge your Individual Tax Return via myGov after 30 June. ATO typically processes refunds within 10 to 14 business days.

What is the deadline for salary sacrificing a bonus into super?

Under ATO rules, an effective salary sacrifice agreement must be entered into before the bonus is earned or formally declared. Once the bonus is payable, it cannot be retrospectively sacrificed.
