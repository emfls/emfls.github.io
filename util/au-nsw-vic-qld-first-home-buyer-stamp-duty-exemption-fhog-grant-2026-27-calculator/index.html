Key takeaways

  • NSW offers zero duty on eligible homes up to A$800,000 and a discount below A$1 million.
  • Victoria offers zero duty up to A$600,000 and tapered duty through A$750,000.
  • Queensland offers zero duty on eligible new homes and residential vacant land with no value cap. An eligible new home valued below A$750,000 can also qualify for a A$30,000 grant.

How duty is estimated

  • NSW. The 2026–27 general schedule charges A$1.25, A$1.50, A$1.75, A$3.50, A$4.50 and A$5.50 per A$100 or part thereof, with breaks at A$18,000, A$38,000, A$103,000, A$387,000 and A$1.29 million. The minimum is A$20. A residential premium rate of A$7 per A$100 applies to the excess above A$3.87 million. For the concession, the discount scales down from the standard duty at the exemption threshold. Revenue NSW duty rates · Duties Act, section 78A · NSW grant
  • VIC. General duty uses 1.4%, 2.4% and 6% marginal brackets up to A$960,000, then 5.5% of the full value through A$2 million. Above A$2 million, add 6.5% of the excess to A$110,000. Eligible buyers in the first home taper pay a rising fraction of general duty. SRO duty rates · VIC duty relief · VIC grant
  • QLD. General duty is nil through A$5,000, then uses marginal rates of 1.5%, 3.5%, 4.5% and 5.75%, with upper breaks at A$75,000, A$540,000 and A$1 million. For eligible established homes above A$700,000, apply the published first home discount to the ordinary home rate. QRO duty rates · QLD duty relief · QLD grant

Estimates assume a wholly residential purchase by fully eligible buyers when the relevant box is checked. Queensland introduced updated citizenship and residency eligibility rules on 1 August 2026. The model excludes foreign buyer surcharges, mixed ownership, off-the-plan value adjustments and other concessions. The revenue office determines eligibility and final duty.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a duty exemption and a grant on the same home?

Yes, if you meet both sets of rules. Established homes can qualify for first home duty relief. The cash grant is limited to qualifying new homes and builds.

Why is the Queensland grant zero at exactly A$750,000?

The A$30,000 Queensland grant requires a value below A$750,000, including land and contract variations. An eligible new home at exactly A$750,000 can still receive full first home duty relief.

How do I enter a house-and-land package?

For a vacant land purchase with a separate building contract, enter the dutiable land value and the combined land and build value. For a completed new home purchased from a seller, select New home purchase and use the appropriate grant assessment value.

What happens to Queensland duty on an established home at A$800,000?

The extra first home discount ends at A$800,000. If you meet the ordinary home concession conditions, that rate still applies. For example, at A$850,000 it produces duty of A$24,100.

Does the calculator confirm I qualify?

The estimate assumes you meet the relevant rules when the checkbox is selected. Check the state revenue office requirements for residency, prior ownership, prior benefits and moving in, including any rules for your spouse or co-buyers.
