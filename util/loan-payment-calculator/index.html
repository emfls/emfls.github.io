What this estimate includes

The calculation assumes equal monthly end-of-period payments, a fixed note interest rate divided into 12 monthly periods, and standard unrounded monthly balance progression before final-payment rounding. Note rate reflects the contractual rate on principal; lender APR additionally incorporates financed finance charges. Property tax, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, escrow, and adjustable-rate resets are excluded. Confirm official disclosures with your lender; this is not financial advice.

FAQ

Does a zero-interest loan work?

Yes. When annual interest is 0%, principal is divided equally across the total number of monthly payments with zero interest.

How is remaining balance at my exit year handled in Offer Comparison?

At your chosen exit month, Offer Comparison sums monthly payments made, upfront fees/points paid, and the remaining principal payoff balance, then subtracts initial loan principal so both offers are measured on total net borrowing cost through the same exit date.

Why can a lender quote differ?

Real offers can include daily simple interest accrual, escrow reserves, PMI, prepaid interest, and lender-specific rounding rules.
