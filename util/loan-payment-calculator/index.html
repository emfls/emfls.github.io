What this estimate includes

The calculation assumes equal monthly payments, a fixed annual rate divided into 12 monthly periods, and no extra payments. It excludes fees, points, taxes, insurance, variable-rate changes, lender conventions, and rounding differences. Confirm actual terms with the lender; this is not financial advice.

FAQ

Does a zero-interest loan work?

Yes. The principal is divided equally across the number of monthly payments.

Why can a lender quote differ?

Real offers can include different compounding rules, fees, payment timing, insurance, taxes, and rounding.
