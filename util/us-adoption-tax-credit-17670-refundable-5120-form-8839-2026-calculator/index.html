2026 US Adoption Tax Credit Calculator

Estimate how much of your adoption credit could reduce this year's tax, increase your refund, or carry forward.

Key takeaways

  • The 2026 federal adoption credit is up to $17,670 per eligible child, including up to $5,120 refundable even with zero federal income tax.
  • MAGI above $265,080 reduces the credit. At $305,080 or more, the 2026 credit is zero.
  • A qualifying finalized special-needs adoption can start at the full credit base even with zero expenses.
  • Unused nonrefundable credit from 2026 can carry forward through tax year 2031.

Avoid losing unused credit

Keep your Form 8839 carryforward worksheet and record the amount used each year. Any unused portion of the 2026 balance expires after tax year 2031. Keep receipts and employer benefit records so you can remove expenses paid with excluded assistance before claiming the credit.

Adoption credit questions

Can I get a refundable adoption credit with zero federal income tax?

Yes. For 2026, up to $5,120 per eligible child can be refundable even with zero federal income tax. The amount depends on each child's eligible credit after the income reduction. Claim it through Form 8839 and complete Schedule 3-A (Form 1040) for the refundable credit.

How does MAGI affect the 2026 adoption credit?

The credit starts to decrease above $265,080 and reaches zero at $305,080. Draft Form 8839 applies the $5,120 ceiling to each child's credit after the reduction. The planning scenario on this page also scales down the ceiling, which can give a smaller refundable estimate.

When does a special-needs adoption qualify for the full credit base?

A finalized domestic adoption can qualify for the $17,670 credit base even with zero expenses if the child meets IRS requirements and a state or eligible tribal government has made the qualifying determination. The income phase-out still applies.

How does employer-provided adoption assistance affect qualified expenses?

Expenses covered by employer assistance excluded from income cannot also support the credit. You may claim both benefits for the same adoption using different expenses. Allocate expenses and benefits to each child when preparing Form 8839.

When does unused 2026 nonrefundable credit expire?

New unused nonrefundable credit from 2026 can be applied in tax years 2027 through 2031, subject to the credit limit in each year. Any balance left after 2031 expires. Save the Form 8839 carryforward worksheet for the next return.
