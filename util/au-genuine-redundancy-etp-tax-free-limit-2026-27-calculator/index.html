How the estimate works

The tax-free limit applies to qualifying severance and any qualifying notice pay together. The taxable balance is an excluded ETP and uses the life benefit ETP cap. If a payment fails the age test alone, its qualifying redundancy component can still be an excluded ETP.

Separate non-excluded ETPs use the smaller of the remaining ETP cap and the whole-of-income cap. This estimate applies the excluded ETP first and deducts it, other taxable income and taxable unused leave from the A$180,000 whole-of-income cap.

Estimates assume an Australian tax resident, a tax file number provided, a single payout received in 2026–27 within 12 months of termination, and no earlier ETPs. Eligibility can also depend on a younger compulsory retirement age, the terms of dismissal and arrangements for re-employment. The model excludes Medicare levy reductions or surcharge, tax offsets, study-loan repayments, older leave accruals and payroll rounding. For other terminations, leave tax is approximated using the incremental resident tax and standard levy on top of prior income because regular pay-cycle details are needed for payroll withholding.

The calculator estimates cash after tax at payout. Final tax can vary, especially if you earn more income later in the year or if your ordinary tax rate is below a concessional withholding rate.

Frequently asked questions

How much genuine redundancy pay is tax-free in 2026–27?

The limit is A$13,598 plus A$6,801 per full year of eligible service. Eight completed years gives a limit of A$68,006. If your qualifying payment is smaller, only the amount paid is tax-free. No income tax or Medicare levy applies to that portion.

Which age matters for redundancy and ETP tax?

For tax-free redundancy, you generally need to be under Age Pension age (67) at dismissal and meet the other ATO conditions. For ETP withholding, age on 30 June 2027 determines whether the rate up to the relevant cap is 17% (age 60 or older) or 32%. Amounts above the relevant cap use 47%.

Does payment in lieu of notice count towards the tax-free limit?

It can count if it meets the genuine redundancy payment conditions. If payroll classifies it as a separate non-excluded ETP, it uses the smaller remaining ETP and whole-of-income caps. The latter starts at A$180,000 and is reduced by other taxable amounts.

How are unused annual leave and long service leave taxed?

Unused annual leave and long service leave accrued after 17 August 1993 are calculated separately from the ETP. On genuine redundancy, the estimate uses 32% withholding including Medicare levy. Earlier accrual dates and other termination reasons can change the treatment.

How is tax saved versus ordinary income calculated?

The calculator adds the full gross payout to your pre-payout taxable income and calculates the extra tax using 2026–27 resident brackets plus a standard 2% Medicare levy. It then subtracts estimated payout tax. A negative number means estimated payout tax is higher than this benchmark.
