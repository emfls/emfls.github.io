What you give up when you buy a QLAC

Buying a QLAC trades liquid retirement assets for future income. If you die before payouts start, a life-only contract may pay nothing to heirs. A return-of-premium or survivor benefit can protect heirs or a spouse and may lower the quoted payment. A level payment also buys less as prices rise. Consider liquidity, contract terms and the insurer’s claims-paying ability alongside the projected tax deferral.

How the calculator projects withdrawals

Each RMD is the prior December 31 liquid balance divided by the IRS factor for that age. The model reserves any 2026 RMD before buying the annuity. It then applies growth to the liquid balance after each year’s modeled withdrawals. A married couple’s premium is allocated proportionally to each spouse’s available capacity. Two spouses share the same timeline, while their premium caps apply separately.

Once payouts begin, the model uses a full year of level payments and calculates RMDs on the remaining account separately. The tax estimate applies one marginal rate to fully taxable distributions. It excludes prior-year RMDs, delayed first RMDs, special spouse tables, plan retirement exceptions, optional annuity aggregation and future rate changes. Break-even compares gross nominal payouts with the premium and assumes continued payments at the quoted annual rate.

Frequently asked questions

Can I put $210,000 into a $300,000 traditional IRA QLAC?

Yes, if you have the full $210,000 of remaining lifetime capacity and enough eligible funds after any RMD due for the purchase year. SECURE 2.0 removed the former 25% balance limit, so $210,000 is 70% of a $300,000 balance.

Can married couples buy $420,000 of QLACs?

Yes, if each spouse separately has $210,000 of remaining lifetime capacity and sufficient eligible funds in their own accounts. One spouse cannot use the other spouse’s unused limit. Employer plan availability and rules can vary.

How does a QLAC change RMDs before age 85?

The value of a qualifying contract is excluded from the account balance used to calculate RMDs. A new purchase does not lower an RMD already based on the prior December 31 balance. Payouts must begin by the first day of the month after the 85th birthday and are modeled separately from liquid-account RMDs.

How can a QLAC affect Medicare IRMAA?

A smaller taxable RMD can move Medicare MAGI below a surcharge threshold. Taxable annuity payments can later raise MAGI and reverse that result. Medicare generally uses the tax return from two years before the premium year. This calculator applies 2026 thresholds as a benchmark to each projected year.

What does the longevity break-even age show?

It is the age at which estimated gross payments recover the premium in nominal dollars. At a 16% annual payout starting at age 85, the premium is recovered after 6.25 years, around age 91.25. The estimate excludes taxes, investment returns, inflation and death benefits.
