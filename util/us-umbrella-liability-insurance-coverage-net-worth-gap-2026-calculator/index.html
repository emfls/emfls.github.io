The catastrophic underlying coverage gap trap

Umbrella insurance does NOT cover from dollar zero. It sits on top of your underlying auto and homeowners policies. If your umbrella requires $250,000 in auto liability but your policy only has $50,000, you are personally liable for the $200,000 gap before umbrella coverage responds. Always align your primary policy limits.

Frequently asked questions

How much umbrella insurance do I need?

Your umbrella limit should equal or exceed your total exposed net worth (home equity above homestead cap, brokerage, cash, rentals) plus future garnishable wages.

Are 401(k) accounts protected from civil lawsuits?

Yes. Federal ERISA regulations grant virtually complete protection to qualified employer retirement accounts from civil creditors and lawsuit judgments.

What is an underlying coverage gap?

An underlying coverage gap occurs when primary auto or home limits are lower than the umbrella carrier's mandatory attachment threshold, leaving you to pay the gap out of pocket.

Does umbrella insurance cover legal defense costs?

Yes, umbrella insurance pays legal defense attorney fees outside of the policy coverage limit, protecting you from crippling litigation expenses.

Can umbrella insurance cover rental properties?

Yes, umbrella policies can extend over residential rental units, provided underlying landlord liability policies meet attachment guidelines.
