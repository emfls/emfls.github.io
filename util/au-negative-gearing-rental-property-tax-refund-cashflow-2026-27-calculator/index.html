Negative gearing FAQ

How is the estimated negative gearing tax saving calculated?

The calculator subtracts estimated tax with the rental result from estimated tax without it. It applies each marginal bracket to the income within that bracket. PAYG withholding, other credits and liabilities determine the refund or bill on your actual return.

How do Division 43 and Division 40 affect cashflow?

Division 43 covers eligible construction costs. Division 40 covers the decline in value of eligible depreciating assets. Both are entered as annual non-cash deductions and can reduce taxable rental income. The cashflow formula includes rent and deductible cash outlays.

What changes for established properties from 1 July 2027?

Subject to statutory exceptions, affected established homes acquired after 7:30 pm AEST on 12 May 2026 lose access to the wage offset from 2027–28. Losses can offset eligible residential property income and gains, with any unused balance carried forward. The calculator assumes one rental property and no other eligible property income or gains.

How much growth covers the annual holding cost?

Divide the annual after-tax cash shortfall by the property value. A $5,350 annual shortfall on a $750,000 property requires about 0.71% price growth before capital gains tax and transaction costs. A cash surplus gives a 0% holding-cost hurdle.

Does a rental loss reduce HECS-HELP, the Medicare levy surcharge or Child Care Subsidy income?

A deductible net rental loss can be added back as part of total net investment losses for HELP repayment income, Medicare levy surcharge income and the adjusted taxable income used for Child Care Subsidy. Each test uses its own income definition and other adjustments.
