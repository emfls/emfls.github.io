Parameter2026 treatment
STR definitionA Canadian residential property rented or offered for stays shorter than 90 consecutive days.
Daily complianceA day is non-compliant if the province or municipality prohibits STR operation at that location, or any applicable registration, licensing or permit requirement is unmet.
AAmounts otherwise deductible for use of the property as an STR during the tax year. CRA reports denied current expenses and denied CCA separately on T776.
B and CB counts non-compliant STR days. C counts all STR days, including days it was offered. For this calculator, C is 1–365 and B is 0–C.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a short-term rental day?

Count calendar days the home was rented or offered for stays shorter than 90 consecutive days. Include vacant days when the home was offered for those stays, and count each day only once.

Does a late licence restore earlier deductions?

For 2026, count the days when the property was offered or rented as a short-term rental and local or provincial rules prohibited it at that location or a required registration, licence or permit was missing. The December 31 relief applied only to 2024.

Can I owe tax on an Airbnb cash loss?

Yes. With $40,000 of revenue, $45,000 of otherwise deductible cash expenses and all STR days non-compliant, taxable rental income is $40,000 even though pre-tax cash flow is negative $5,000. At a 30% marginal rate, the estimated tax is $12,000.

How does Section 67.7 affect CCA?

Apply the same day fraction to otherwise eligible capital cost allowance (CCA). CRA reports current expense and CCA denials separately on T776. CCA reduces taxable income but has no cash outflow in this estimate.

What does the 90-day lease break-even rent mean?

It is the minimum monthly rent that matches or beats the non-compliant STR’s estimated after-tax cash flow, assuming 12 paid months and the annual lease expenses entered. Section 67.7 does not apply to periods rented and offered exclusively for 90 consecutive days or longer. Ordinary deduction rules and local rental requirements still apply.
