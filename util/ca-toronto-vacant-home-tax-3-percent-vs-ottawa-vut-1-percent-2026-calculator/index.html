Frequently asked questions

Which property value should I enter?

Use the residential assessed value from your property tax or MPAC assessment notice. On a $1 million assessment, a taxable vacancy costs $30,000 in Toronto and $10,000 in Hamilton or in Ottawa’s first vacant year.

How do the vacancy thresholds differ?

Toronto’s by-law uses more than six months. Ottawa uses more than 184 days and Hamilton uses more than 183 days. Check Toronto against the actual occupancy dates when your total is close to six months.

How does Ottawa’s vacancy tax increase?

Ottawa starts at 1% and adds one percentage point per consecutive vacant year up to 5%. After a year of occupancy, the rate resets to 1% for the next vacancy.

Can I skip the declaration if I live in the unit or qualify for an exemption?

File an annual declaration for an eligible property, including a principal residence or an exempt vacant property. A missed final deadline can result in a deemed-vacant bill. Ottawa charges $250 for late filing, Hamilton lists a $250 fee for its published filing cycle, and Toronto suspended its late-declaration fee.

What makes a Toronto tenancy qualify, and how is the gain calculated?

Toronto counts actual tenant occupancy of at least six months in the same tax year under written leases of at least 30 consecutive days per agreement. The calculator adds the Toronto vacancy tax saved to six months of rental income after incremental costs and estimated income tax.
