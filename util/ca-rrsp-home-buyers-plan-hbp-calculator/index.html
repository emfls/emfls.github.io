Frequently asked questions

When does a 2026 HBP withdrawal have to be repaid?

The 15-year schedule starts in 2031. First withdrawals in 2022 through 2028 begin repayment in the fifth following year. The standard rule starts in the second following year.

Can a couple withdraw $120,000, and what if we miss a repayment?

Two eligible participants can each withdraw up to $60,000 under the current limit. At $60,000 the first annual minimum is $4,000 per person. Include any shortfall as RRSP income on Line 12900 and estimate the extra tax at your own marginal rate.

How does the 90-day RRSP rule affect the refund estimate?

Contributions made in the 89 days before an HBP withdrawal can lose deductibility to the extent they exceed the RRSP’s value after withdrawal. Enter only the amount you can deduct. The estimated tax savings come from the contribution deduction.
