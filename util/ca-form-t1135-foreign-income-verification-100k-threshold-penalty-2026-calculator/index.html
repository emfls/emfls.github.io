Key takeaways

  • More than CAD $100,000 in total specified foreign property cost at any one time in the year triggers filing. Exactly $100,000 is below the filing trigger.
  • Part A is available below $250,000 if filing is required. Part B is required at $250,000 or more.
  • Ordinary late filing can cost $100 to $2,500 per return. Higher penalties require separate statutory conditions.

Official T1135 parameters for 2026

Rule2026 amount or condition
Filing triggerPeak combined specified foreign property cost over CAD $100,000 during the tax year.
Part APeak over $100,000 and under $250,000. The simplified method asks for property categories, up to three countries and totals for income and gains or losses.
Part BPeak of $250,000 or more. Detailed entries generally include maximum and year-end cost, income and gain or loss. Eligible Canadian dealer or trust company accounts may be grouped by country using the form’s Category 7 rules.
Standard filing penaltySection 162(7). $25 per day, with a $100 minimum and a $2,500 cap per late return.
Knowing or grossly negligent failureSection 162(10). $500 per counted month, or $1,000 when a formal demand was not complied with. Up to 24 months and reduced by the section 162(7) penalty.
Additional penaltySection 162(10.1). If liable under section 162(10) and the statutory month count exceeds 24, 5% of the year’s greatest combined cost, reduced by penalties under sections 162(7) and 162(10).

T1135 questions and answers

Does a U.S. ETF at a Canadian brokerage count?

Yes, U.S.-domiciled ETFs such as VOO and foreign shares such as AAPL or MSFT generally count in a non-registered account. Canadian-domiciled funds such as VFV, XEQT and ZSP are excluded even when the funds hold U.S. securities.

Where do registered plans, vacation homes and foreign crypto belong?

Put RRSP, RRIF, TFSA, FHSA, RESP and RDSP holdings under registered plans. Put a vacation home used primarily for personal enjoyment under personal-use property. A foreign rental held for profit generally counts. Use Other for qualifying foreign bonds, trust interests and foreign crypto holdings. Confirm the legal location and custody of crypto before classifying it.

How do I calculate the peak cost and convert foreign currency?

Use the highest combined CAD tax cost of all qualifying assets held at the same time. Foreign securities generally use adjusted cost base translated at acquisition, with later cost adjustments as applicable. Follow the form instructions for foreign bank balances and dealer-account reporting. Foreign income may still need to be reported even if no T1135 is due.

How does a 50/50 joint account affect the threshold?

An account with a CAD $180,000 qualifying cost contributes $90,000 per equal owner. Each owner also adds their own other qualifying holdings, so the account alone is below the filing threshold for both. Enter the full joint cost once and keep it out of the other categories.

Does every return more than 24 months late incur a 5% penalty?

No. The 5% top-up applies when section 162(10) liability exists and the applicable calendar-month count exceeds 24. It uses the highest aggregate specified foreign property cost for that year and subtracts the penalties under sections 162(7) and 162(10). Each missed return is calculated separately.
