Key takeaways

  • Employee deferral starts at $17,000. The SECURE 2.0 limit is $18,100 for eligible plans. Employers with up to 25 qualifying employees generally use it automatically. Eligible employers with 26–100 generally must elect it and offer a 4% match or 3% nonelective contribution.
  • Ages 60–63 can add $5,250. That brings the employee limit to $22,250 standard or $23,350 enhanced. Other eligible ages 50+ can add $4,000 or $3,850, respectively.
  • The first two years can change the tax bill. A taxable early distribution may incur a 25% additional tax. After two years, a cash withdrawal can still trigger 10%. A qualifying rollover can avoid the additional tax.

A $10,000 move could cost $2,500 in additional tax

Before the second anniversary of your employer’s first SIMPLE IRA deposit, sending pre-tax money to a traditional IRA, Roth IRA or 401(k) generally counts as a taxable distribution. If you’re under 59½ and no exception applies, the additional tax alone can be $2,500 on $10,000, on top of ordinary income tax. Confirm the date and receiving account with your custodian before moving the money.

SIMPLE IRA questions

Who can use the $18,100 SIMPLE IRA limit in 2026?

For employers that meet the SECURE 2.0 prior-plan requirement, the higher limit generally applies automatically with up to 25 qualifying employees. Employers with 26–100 generally must make a formal election and offer either a 4% match or a 3% nonelective contribution. Count employees who earned at least $5,000 in the preceding calendar year. A transition rule can temporarily preserve small-employer treatment.

What is the 2026 SIMPLE IRA catch-up at ages 50–59, 60–63 and 64+?

Use the age you reach by December 31, 2026. Ages 50–59 and 64+ can add $4,000 under the standard limit or $3,850 under the enhanced limit. Ages 60–63 can add $5,250 under either limit, for totals of $22,250 and $23,350 respectively.

Can my employer contribute if I defer nothing?

An eligible employee can receive a nonelective contribution with zero deferral. A matching contribution requires an employee deferral. A plan can also provide a uniform additional nonelective contribution for eligible employees earning at least $5,000, limited in 2026 to the lesser of 10% of compensation or $5,300.

When does the SIMPLE IRA two-year clock start?

Use the date your employer first deposited a contribution in your SIMPLE IRA. During the two-year window, an otherwise qualifying transfer can go to another SIMPLE IRA. A taxable withdrawal or a premature attempt to move the money to a different account can incur a 25% additional tax unless an exception applies.

What happens to withdrawals and rollovers after two years?

A cash withdrawal generally remains taxable and can carry a 10% additional tax before age 59½ unless an exception applies. A qualifying rollover to a traditional IRA or a 401(k) that accepts it is generally tax deferred. A qualifying Roth IRA conversion generally adds the pre-tax amount to ordinary income and carries no early-distribution tax on the amount converted.
