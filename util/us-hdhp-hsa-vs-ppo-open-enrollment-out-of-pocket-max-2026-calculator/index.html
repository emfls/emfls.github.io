⚠️ Key Open-Enrollment Insight: The "Guaranteed HDHP Buffer"

Before you incur a single dollar of medical care, an HDHP plan typically provides an immediate financial buffer from three distinct sources: lower annual payroll deductions, employer HSA contribution seed money, and triple tax savings (federal + state + 7.65% FICA payroll tax exemption). In many company plans, this buffer exceeds the difference between deductibles!
