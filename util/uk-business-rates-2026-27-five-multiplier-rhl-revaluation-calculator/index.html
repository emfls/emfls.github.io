Understanding the April 2026 England Business Rates Overhaul

The UK Government has enacted the most radical structural overhaul of non-domestic commercial property taxation in England since 1990. Commencing in April 2026, the antiquated two-tier system (comprising only the Small Business and Standard multipliers) is permanently replaced by a targeted Five-Multiplier Framework to protect the high street and rebalance liabilities toward large corporate distributions centers.

1. The Five Statutory Multipliers for 2026/27

Under the new statutory schedule, your business rates multiplier in the pound is determined by your open-market Rateable Value (RV) and trade classification:

  • Small Business RHL (38.2p / 0.382): For retail shops, high-street restaurants, cafes, pubs, hotels, and gyms with an RV under £51,000.
  • Standard RHL (43.0p / 0.430): For mid-sized retail and hospitality premises with an RV between £51,000 and £499,999.
  • Small Business Non-RHL (43.2p / 0.432): For small commercial offices, workshops, and local storage facilities with an RV under £51,000.
  • Standard Non-RHL (48.0p / 0.480): For general commercial properties, medium office buildings, and logistics hubs between £51,000 and £499,999.
  • High-Value Commercial Properties (50.8p / 0.508): Applies across all commercial sectors to properties with an RV of £500,000 or greater, ensuring large supermarkets and mega-warehouses contribute equitably to municipal funding.

2. Small Business Rate Relief (SBRR) Preservation

The landmark Small Business Rate Relief continues to operate alongside the new multipliers:

  • 100% Exemption (£0 bill): Available to businesses occupying a single commercial property in England with an RV not exceeding £12,000.
  • Tapered Relief: For single properties with an RV from £12,001 to £15,000, relief scales down proportionally at a rate of 1% for every £30 of rateable value.
