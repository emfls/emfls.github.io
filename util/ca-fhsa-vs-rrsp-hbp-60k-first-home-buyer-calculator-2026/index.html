How the estimate works

All three strategies start with the same annual new savings. The two HBP strategies can also draw from the existing RRSP balance you enter. The model adds savings at the start of each year, applies a constant return and assumes a year-end purchase and first HBP withdrawal. A final-year RRSP deposit compounds for the number of days you enter. New RRSP deposits are limited by the entered room and projected HBP withdrawal capacity.

The model multiplies eligible new contributions by your marginal tax rate. It assumes any resulting cash refund is kept for the home and arrives at the start of the next modeled year. The purchase-year refund therefore arrives after closing. Enter a net return appropriate for your savings as the same rate applies to any reinvested refunds and liquid savings, including money that may be held outside registered accounts.

This estimate assumes one HBP-eligible RRSP per buyer, sufficient tax payable to realize the modeled savings, and no early or additional HBP repayments. Verify eligibility and available contribution room before contributing. Investment returns, actual tax refunds and CRA-assessed repayments can differ.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my FHSA and the Home Buyers’ Plan for the same home?

Yes, if you qualify for each withdrawal. The combined principal can reach $100,000 per buyer or $200,000 across two eligible buyers. Qualifying FHSA growth and cash from reinvested tax refunds can add to the down payment. The RRSP HBP withdrawal itself is capped at $60,000 per person.

Does FHSA room build up before I open an account?

Room starts in the year you open your first FHSA. Up to $8,000 of unused room can carry into a later year, so standard annual room plus carryforward can reach $16,000, subject to the $40,000 lifetime limit.

When does a 2026 HBP withdrawal have to be repaid?

A first HBP withdrawal in 2026 has a first repayment year of 2031. The five-year start applies to first withdrawals from 2022 through 2028 inclusive. The repayment period is 15 years.

What if I contribute to my RRSP just before an HBP withdrawal?

For each RRSP you withdraw from, CRA can deny the deduction for the part of contributions made in the preceding 89 days that exceeds the market value left in that RRSP immediately after the withdrawal. Waiting 90 days avoids this restriction.

What happens if I skip a $4,000 HBP repayment?

The unpaid amount is generally included in taxable income for that year and reduces the HBP balance. If you pay none of a $4,000 minimum and your marginal rate is 32%, the estimated additional income tax is $1,280.
