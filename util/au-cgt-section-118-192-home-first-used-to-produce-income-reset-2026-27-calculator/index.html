Key takeaways

Home first. If the home was fully exempt before first income-producing use and the sale is now only partly exempt, an eligible Section 118-192 reset uses the market value on the first rental date. Growth before that date falls outside the reset gain. A qualifying six-year absence choice can leave the sale fully exempt.

Rental first. Keep the original eligible purchase costs and apportion the gain by non-main-residence days across the ownership period.

The estimate subtracts the tax on your other taxable income from the tax on that income plus the net taxable gain, then adds 2% of the gain for the standard Medicare levy. It assumes full-year Australian residency and applies the full levy. It excludes Medicare reductions, exemptions and surcharge, offsets, capital losses, capital works adjustments and indexation. Results are estimates for a whole dwelling, excluding mixed use, multiple absences, inheritances, overlapping home exemptions and special residency rules.

Use purchase and sale contract dates for the ordinary discount clock and sale tax year. Ownership days run to sale settlement in this model. In Scenario A the time apportioned after a reset starts on the first income-producing date. If you elect the absence rule, the model assumes you can claim this home throughout the covered period.

Before you rely on the savings

A lower first-rental valuation can raise the gain, and an early sale can forfeit the discount. Keep a defensible valuation and cost records. If you have another home, compare the exemption impact on both properties before choosing which home to claim. A registered tax agent can verify the valuation, costs and timing for your circumstances.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Section 118-192 market value reset compulsory?

For an eligible dwelling, the reset applies if it would have been fully exempt immediately before first income-producing use, that use began after 7:30 pm ACT legal time on 20 August 1996, and the sale is only partly exempt because of income-producing use.

Can the six-year absence rule make the sale fully exempt?

Yes. For one continuous rental absence, you can generally choose to keep treating the former home as your main residence for up to six calendar years if you do not also claim another home for that period. A fully exempt sale needs no market value reset.

How is the gain calculated if I rent the property out before moving in?

The original cost base applies. In the modelled timeline, divide the days from the start of ownership until moving in by the total ownership days, then multiply the gain by that fraction before any applicable discount.

When does the 50% CGT discount clock restart?

If the reset applies, the first income-producing use is the deemed acquisition date. For the discount, allow at least 12 months excluding the acquisition day and the CGT event day. A property sale generally triggers the event on the sale contract date.

Which property costs go into the reset cost base?

Start with the market value on the first day of income-producing use and add eligible later improvements and selling costs. The original purchase price, stamp duty and pre-rental improvements are replaced by the market value in this calculation. Keep evidence of the valuation and eligible costs.
