Key takeaways

  • REPS takes more than 750 hours and more than half of all personal service hours. One spouse must meet both tests individually. Material participation is evaluated for the rental activity.
  • An average customer stay of 7 days or less can qualify under the short-term rental exception. Material participation can make the loss nonpassive without REPS.
  • The passive rental allowance is up to $25,000 with active participation. It phases out from $100,000 to $150,000 of modified AGI for Single and Married Filing Jointly.

A deduction can be smaller than the loss you incur

Before you spend money expecting a W-2 offset, check your time records, basis and at-risk amount. Missing either annual REPS test or the applicable material-participation test can defer the deduction. A loss that exceeds the §461(l) limit can also leave less cash available this year.

How the estimate works

The §461(l) estimate assumes the entered activity is a trade or business and there are no other business gains or losses, passive income, prior-year losses, or other special limitations. Wages are excluded when figuring net business loss under §461(l), then used to show the maximum wage income the permitted deduction could offset. Any allowed deduction beyond wages can still affect other income or your NOL calculation.

Federal savings compare 2026 ordinary income tax before and after the allowed loss using IRS rate schedules. NIIT recomputes both the MAGI threshold and the investment-income base. The passive comparison treats the deductible rental loss as allocable to investment income. The state estimate assumes the state allows the same loss and applies the entered marginal rate up to the amount of state taxable income.

Use this estimate for planning. The model covers ordinary rates and excludes tax credits, AMT, payroll and self-employment taxes, preferential capital-gains rates, and changes to other deductions. Frequently asked questions

Can spouses combine hours to pass the 750-hour REPS test?

Each spouse on a joint return is tested separately. The same spouse must exceed 750 qualifying real estate hours and spend more than half of their personal service time in qualifying real estate work. Both spouses’ participation can count toward material participation in a particular activity.

Can I count hours across several rental properties?

Qualifying real estate work can count toward the annual REPS tests. For rental material participation, test each rental real estate interest separately unless a valid election treats all interests as one activity. The §1.469-9(g) election requires a statement with the return and generally binds future qualifying years.

How does the 7-day short-term rental exception work?

An average period of customer use of 7 days or less puts the activity outside the passive-loss definition of a rental activity. Material participation can then make a business loss nonpassive without REPS. Two tests are more than 500 hours, or more than 100 hours with at least as many hours as any other individual.

How much of a passive rental loss can I deduct at $120,000 of modified AGI?

For Single or Married Filing Jointly, $120,000 of §469 modified AGI gives a $15,000 maximum special allowance if you actively participate. The allowed amount also depends on your eligible loss. Suspended passive losses can generally be carried forward under the passive activity rules.

What happens to losses above the 2026 excess business loss threshold?

After applicable basis, at-risk and passive loss limits, Form 461 compares net business loss with the $256,000 Single or $512,000 joint threshold. The full excess becomes an NOL carryover. The 80% limit generally applies to the future-year deduction for post-2017 NOLs.
