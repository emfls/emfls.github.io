Frequently asked questions

How much of my overtime qualifies for the deduction?

Only the premium required by the Fair Labor Standards Act qualifies. At time and a half, the deductible premium is half the regular rate for each eligible overtime hour, or one-third of total time-and-a-half pay. The straight-time portion stays outside the deduction. Use W-2 Box 12, Code TT for 2026 employee reporting.

Which tips qualify for the 2026 deduction?

Voluntary cash, card and other electronic tips in an IRS-listed customarily tipped occupation can qualify when properly reported. Mandatory service charges do not qualify. Look for W-2 Box 12, Code TP for employee tips. Self-employed workers also face business eligibility rules and a net-income limit.

How does MAGI affect the overtime and tips deductions?

The draft 2026 Schedule 1-A reduces each capped deduction by $100 per full $1,000 of MAGI above $150,000, or above $300,000 on a joint return. Both deductions stop at zero. A smaller qualifying amount can phase out before the full cap does.

Can I claim these deductions with the standard deduction?

Yes. Eligible filers can claim both through Schedule 1-A for tax years 2025 through 2028 while taking the standard deduction or itemizing. Married taxpayers must file jointly, and each person claiming qualifying pay needs a valid Social Security number for employment.

Do the deductions remove payroll tax or guarantee a refund?

No. Social Security and Medicare still apply to covered overtime and tips. The estimate shows how much regular federal income tax could fall. Your actual refund or balance due also depends on withholding, payments and credits.
