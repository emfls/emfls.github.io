Frequently asked questions

How much VCT Income Tax relief can I claim in 2026/27?

Qualifying new VCT shares issued from 6 April 2026 attract 20% relief on up to £200,000 per tax year. That gives a £40,000 maximum, down from £60,000 in 2025/26, and your available UK Income Tax liability can reduce the amount further. Relief can repay tax you have already paid up to your liability.

How do sales within six months affect VCT relief?

Linked sales of existing shares in the same VCT, or a successor or predecessor VCT, can reduce the qualifying subscription. For a legally linked sale, deduct the consideration first, then apply the £200,000 annual maximum. The six months can run either side of the new subscription. Certain dividend reinvestments are excluded.

What happens if I sell VCT shares within five years?

An early sale can withdraw some or all of the relief attributable to the shares sold. HMRC guidance uses the original 30% or 20% rate on arm’s-length sale proceeds, subject to the relief received. The published section 266 wording states 30%. The Finance Bill 2026–27 proposes aligning it with the issue rate for shares issued from 6 April 2026.

Are VCT dividends and gains tax-free?

Qualifying VCT dividends and disposal gains are exempt within the £200,000 annual acquisition limit, which can include second-hand shares. The upfront Income Tax reduction is restricted to qualifying new subscriptions. A five-year dividend comparison assumes a constant payout and benchmark tax rate.

How do EIS and SEIS affect gains and losses?

EIS can defer an eligible gain on another asset. SEIS can exempt 50% of matched gains, subject to the amount supported by Income Tax relief. Both can exempt gains on the scheme shares when the conditions are met. Qualifying share loss relief may offset the loss remaining after upfront relief against income.
