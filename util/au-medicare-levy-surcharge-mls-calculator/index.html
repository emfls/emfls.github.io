Frequently asked questions

What are the 2026–27 MLS thresholds?

The single tier ceilings are AUD 105,000, 123,000 and 164,000. The family ceilings are AUD 210,000, 246,000 and 328,000, plus AUD 1,500 per MLS dependent child after the first. The four rates are 0%, 1%, 1.25% and 1.5%.

Does MLS apply only to income above the threshold?

The tier sets a rate on the full chargeable base. This generally includes taxable income and reportable fringe benefits, plus certain family trust amounts. Addbacks such as reportable super contributions and net investment losses can raise the tier without raising the surcharge base.

Which hospital premium should I compare against the surcharge?

Use the full-year net premium for appropriate private patient hospital cover. Extras-only cover does not qualify. Lifetime Health Cover can add 2% to the hospital premium per applicable year above age 30, subject to timing and exceptions. The separate base Medicare levy generally still applies.
